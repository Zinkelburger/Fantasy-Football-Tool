# Opportunity score comparison — September 15, 2026

## Conclusion

Keep the usage model. Our trailing average has lower next-calendar-week mean absolute error in all six matched comparisons, by 0.03–0.13 fantasy points. The margins are small and three of six player-bootstrap intervals include zero. These retrospective files do not establish universal forecasting superiority. No candidate gives a consistent next-week improvement.

The author is F4NT4SYF00TB4LLF4N; Subvertadown hosts the tables. His explanation confirms yards before catch for receivers. This accounts for a meaningful difference in what the models measure. A receiver can earn an uncompleted deep target in ours; completed air yards only enter his published feature definition. Our completed-air-yards candidate is a proxy, not a reconstruction of his code.

## Why this differs from the previous finding

The earlier script dropped every zero score. This check keeps a zero when nflverse records usage that week, and excludes zero placeholders without a corresponding usage row. It maps verified Kenny/Kenneth Gainwell, Josh/Joshua Palmer and Andrew/Drew Ogletree aliases. It checks player/week uniqueness. The files have different season coverage: 2024 RB/WR through Week 17, TE through Week 13; 2025 through Week 14. None is a full pair of completed seasons.

Our 2024 comparison fit uses 2021–2023; the 2025 comparison fit uses 2021–2024. The production model fitted on 2021–2025 is used only for descriptive agreement, never as the next-week test model. The alternative uses four RB opportunity counts, or receiver target counts, rushes and completed air yards. More features did not guarantee lower future error.

Unmatched nonzero records remain excluded. Reasons include different position classifications (e.g. fullbacks and Travis Hunter classified as CB), name differences and games lacking an eligible usage row. Coverage is restricted to the exported player list and games with usage, so DNP/zero-opportunity risk is not evaluated. The other author's training procedure and publication timestamps were not reconstructed. Bootstrap resamples players with all their cases together (1,000 repetitions, seed 39); intervals are exploratory, unadjusted for multiple comparisons and do not remove selection bias.

## Reproduce

Run `.venv-league-sim/bin/python research/opportunity-score/audit_public_scores.py --downloads /path/to/exports`. Uses cached 2021–2025 play-by-play and weekly stats. The JSON stores source SHA-256 hashes, coverage, exclusions, correlations, MAE, RMSE, bias, temporal holdout and candidate results. Source CSV files are left untouched and are not committed.

## Public explanation corrections

Expected points summarize recorded usage, including information observed during plays such as target depth and scrambles. They are not strictly pre-snap. The actual-minus-expected gap includes skill, randomness and omitted information. It is not all luck, and cannot be assumed to disappear. Six-point passing TDs need their own scoring fit and can reorder QBs.
