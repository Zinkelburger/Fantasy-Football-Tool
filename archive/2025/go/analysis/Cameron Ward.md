- Pass-volume risk: Low floor unless the Titans open up the passing game. In a run-heavy system, Ward’s fantasy floor is shaky week to week, with occasional spike weeks tied to shootouts or game scripts that force passing.

- Supporting cast uncertainty: Other Titans weapons are not clearly established in the discussion; Ayomanor is the only potential breakout piece highlighted. This limits sure-fire week-to-week reliability for Ward.

- Sentiment signals from Reddit data: The discussion framework is about late-round WR darts and mentions Ward in the context of Ayomanor’s potential rapport. No explicit praise/consensus for Ward itself; the value is speculative and tied to Ayomanor’s development.

- Draft/roster strategy:
  - Redraft: Treat Ward as a late-round, high-variance flyer or a Week 1/2 streamer only if preseason reports show strong early connection with Ayomanor and favorable Titans pass-volume signals.
  - Dynasty/keeper: Late-round QB with potential upside if Ward earns sustained starter role and Ayomanor develops into a reliable target.

- Red flags to monitor (preseason and early season): 
  - Early reports of Ward to Ayomanor chemistry.
  - Actual Titans pass attempts and efficiency in early games.
  - Any emergence of alternative weapons or offensive line issues affecting time to throw.

- Bottom line: Ward’s fantasy viability in 2025 hinges almost entirely on Ayomanor’s breakout and any shift toward a more pass-heavy Titans offense. Without that, he’s a risky, low-floor option. If Ayomanor hits and the Titans open the offense, Ward could offer a modest but real fantasy spike.
