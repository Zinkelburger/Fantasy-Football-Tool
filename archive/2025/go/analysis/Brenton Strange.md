Brenton Strange (JAC) – 2025 Fantasy Outlook (concise, actionable)

- Role/ Expectations
  - TE1 in name only for this note; real-world usage hinges on Evan Ingram staying healthy. Reddit signals Strange as a real fantasy sleeper with upside if he operates as the primary secondary TE.
  - 2024 data hint solid involvement: 0.099 first downs per route run (TE13), suggesting consistent route participation without being a target monster.

- Sentiment snapshot from Reddit
  - Widely viewed as undervalued and sleeper-worthy; multiple comments expect a productive year.
  - Seen as a strong late-round/add for Week 1 waivers; some think he should be drafted over in-line alternatives.
  - Compared favorably to Steelers TEs as a safer floor/plug-and-play option in some discussions.

- Usage/data-driven notes
  - Involvement level in 2024 was decent for a TE not in the top tier; not breakout, but trustworthy floor when used.
  - Proximity to targets expected in a Jaguars offense with Kirk/Ridley; upside driven by red-zone role and any Ingram-related volume shifts.

- Draft/roster strategy
  - Redraft/Fantasy: late-round stash or priority waiver target; treat as a tepid TE2 with upside rather than immediate TE1 upside.
  - If you roster Ingram: keep Strange on bench unless Ingram misses time; if Ingram is limited or out, Strange becomes a strong weekly streamer.
  - In best-ball formats, Strange is a sensible third TE/dart throw with weekly upside.

- Risk factors
  - Primary risk is Evan Ingram’s continued high target share; offense distribution may cap Strange’s ceiling.
  - Jaguars' pass volume volatility and competition from Kirk/Ridley could suppress Week-to-week spikes.

- Projections (rough, conservative to upside)
  - Baseline: ~40–60 targets, 300–450 yards, 2–4 TD; PPR ~60–90 points.
  - If Ingram misses time or usage shifts favorable: 60–75 targets, 450–650 yards, 4–7 TD; PPR ~105–140+ points.
  - Floor = low-end streamer; Ceiling = spike-week candidate in favorable matchups.

- Key discussion points to monitor (Reddit-driven)
  - Week 1 waiver trends: Strange among the most-added TEs; price to pay on waivers could define his immediate value.
  - Comparative value vs. Jonnu Smith and other 2nd-tier TEs; many view Strange as a safer floor play than some peers.
  - First downs per route run trend suggests he is involved enough to be usable when located on the field.

Bottom line: Brenton Strange is a realistic late-round/waiver-add option with a plausible path to usable fantasy weeks, especially if Ingram’s workload shifts or he misses time. Treat as a bench/stash with weekly streaming potential rather than a plug-and-play TE1.
