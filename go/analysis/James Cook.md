**James Cook** (BUF, RB, bye 7) — board rank 7

**Room sentiment:** Bullish with a well-defined ceiling cap — 64 mentions, and the loudest thread (445 upvotes) argues he'll surpass prime Dalvin Cook while the top replies inside that same thread take the argument apart.

- Consensus slot is firm: average rank 11.00 in the top-350 aggregate, Sleeper 12-man superflex ADP 2.04 (17.8 overall). A round-2 RB study called him "**RB7 ADP** — the safest pick in the group."
- The bull case is floor, not spike: "15-17 PPG range consistently," "probably the safest floor," attached to what one ranker calls the highest-scoring offense of the last five years. Two AMA guests named him their favorite 1/2-turn RB (one pairing him with Barkley, one ranking him ahead of Chase Brown and Jeanty).
- The bear case is specific and repeated: Josh Allen takes the rushing touchdowns, and Cook "didn't really catch many passes last year." The Dalvin thread's top rebuttal — in full PPR, Dalvin did 23.4 ppg to Cook's 17.4, in 14 games versus Cook's 17.
- Dissent worth hearing: "I'm a huge James Cook fan but I'm willing to bet his best fantasy season has already happened." One note recalls he held out most of last camp and produced anyway.

**Draft take:** Fine as the safe RB anchor at the 1/2 turn, but price him as a high-floor RB1, not a league winner — the ceiling case depends on receiving work that hasn't shown up yet.
