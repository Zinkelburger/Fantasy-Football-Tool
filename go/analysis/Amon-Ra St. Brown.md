**Amon-Ra St. Brown** (DET, WR, bye 6) — board rank 12

**Room sentiment:** Bullish and close to consensus — 55 mentions, almost none negative. The only live argument is Amon-Ra vs. JSN at the turn.

- Sits in Ian Hartitz's top WR tier ("stone cold baller WR1s") with Chase, Nacua and Smith-Njigba. The word that keeps recurring is *safe*: "the safest pick by far," and "probably the safest floor in the league given the offensive environment and his utilization as a Z/slot guy."
- The JSN comparison genuinely splits the room, but narrowly: "I'm taking Amon-Ra over JSN but not by much" against the reverse. One poster prefers Amon-Ra on ceiling "in his environment."
- Target competition (Jameson Williams, Gibbs, LaPorta) comes up constantly — but as a knock on his *teammates*, not on him: "Nobody is avoiding Amon-Ra or Drake London because of Gibbs' and Bijan's receiving work." One dissent notes "ARSB has Jamo, but he's an easy step below Pickens."
- Two minor bear notes: Detroit has a new OC, and one poster who faded him last year graded it "a bad call." Keeper threads are unanimous — keep him, including at $54 in a $200 auction.

**Draft take:** A round-1 pick you take without agonizing, especially in PPR. If you'd rather have JSN the room won't fight you, but don't pass him for a running back you're lukewarm on.
