**Emeka Egbuka** (TB, WR, bye 10) — board rank 46

**Room sentiment:** bullish and very loud (196 mentions), but with an unusually well-argued bear case — a genuine argument, not a pile-on.

- He is the consensus pick of the round-4 WR group. The 145-score thread's author lands on him, and reply after reply says "Egbuka and it isn't close." Tiered as a high-volume WR1/overqualified WR2.
- Bull case: Mike Evans is gone, leaving him the clear go-to target; the Bucs invested more in him than in McMillan; and he "did more in half a season than McMillan did in two." Supporters blame the second-half collapse on his hamstring, Godwin's re-injury, and Baker playing hurt.
- Bear case, repeated by several: the hype rests on a five-game stretch with five TDs while Godwin and McMillan were out; when the TDs stopped, "the scoring fell off a cliff." Add target competition (McMillan, a healthy Godwin, Tez Johnson), a new OC, Baker's injury and contract friction, and reports he often plays hurt.
- Price is the recurring complaint: ESPN ADP ~46.8, but posters report him going in the 3rd and "way above ADP" in best ball.

**Draft take:** Good pick at his ADP, a bad one if you reach into the 3rd. Don't pay for the five-game peak — pay for the vacated Evans targets.
