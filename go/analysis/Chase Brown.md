**Chase Brown** (CIN, RB, bye 6) — board rank 17

**Room sentiment:** Divided, and loudly so — 163 mentions, one of the most-argued players in the corpus. The top-voted takes are bullish ("probably my favorite second round pick," 270 upvotes), but an entire thread exists premised on him being the *lowest* RB people would tolerate as their RB1.

- Role is the bull case: finished RB8 last year with Burrow missing significant time, and posters repeatedly note there is "nothing at all behind Chase Brown in Cincy to take away the valuable touches." He was 6th among RBs in receiving yards (69 catches). Cited as averaging ~20-21 PPG in games Burrow started, and it's a contract year.
- The bear case is Burrow dependency: "without Burrow he is low end RB2 or flex; with Burrow he can finish RB1." Counterpoint from multiple posters: he was also good with Flacco, putting up 18/18/16/33/29/19 PPR games after Burrow returned.
- Real ADP disagreement. Underdog best ball has him RB9, ESPN closer to RB14. Some call him "a 1st round pick going in the late 2nd"; others say his ADP is too high, and a minority is simply unconvinced ("not convinced he's actually that good of an RB").

**Draft take:** A round-2 RB the room mostly wants but nobody agrees on the price for. Fine to pair with Ja'Marr Chase — the stacking thread landed on "don't overthink it."
