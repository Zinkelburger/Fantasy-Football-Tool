**Drake London** (ATL, WR, bye 11) — board rank 20

**Room sentiment:** Genuinely divided and loud about it — 45 mentions split between "the only WR value I love in the 2nd round" and "DRAKE LONDON IS NOT HIM." The Atlanta QB mess is what the argument turns on.

- Role is not in question. A Kyle Pitts post (51) notes there is "no competition apart from Drake London" for targets, and a commenter cites him leading all receivers in team red-zone target share over two seasons at 38.1%. Hartitz's tiers (233) put him in "overall WR1 upside."
- The bear case is the quarterback. Penix said he could be out another four weeks (89), Tua has a tight back, and Atlanta signed Cooper Rush. The top comment: "should I be worried about Drake London at ADP with this and the Tua news." A well-received reply argues the reverse — the news may hand you a discount.
- Durability is the second knock: he was pacing as a league-winner before getting hurt last year. ADP is unsettled — some rank him over CeeDee and Jefferson, others call that "objectively ridiculous."

**Draft take:** A late-2nd swing on target volume, not offense quality. If bad Penix news slides him, that discount is the reason to draft him — don't pay a top-15 price.
