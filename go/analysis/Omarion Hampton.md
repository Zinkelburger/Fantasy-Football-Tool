**Omarion Hampton** (LAC, RB, bye 7) — board rank 11

**Room sentiment:** Bullish but openly contested — 51 mentions, and the highest-scoring piece on him (score 121) literally labels him "the polarizing one" among second-round RBs.

- **Role is not in question.** The Training Camp Week 1 roundup (score 97) calls him "the clear-cut RB1 for the Chargers," with Keaton Mitchell "emerging as the backup, especially for passing down work." A separate high-score take (56) expects the Chargers to "play smash mouth" and sets Hampton up for a big year — while also predicting "another rb in this backfield will have stand alone value." A ranker describes him as a bellcow in a new McDaniel offense.
- **The bull case is loud.** He is a recurring answer in the "hill I'll die on" thread (top-3, top-4, top-5 RB finishes), the pick one ranker felt strongest about (score 23), and a guessed Matthew Berry ride-or-die.
- **The bear case is price, not talent.** "Omarion is in R1 which seems high to me" (score 3), and one Chargers optimist expects all their starters to beat ADP "minus maybe Omarion just because he's valued so high." One ADP duel ranks him behind Walker, Chase Brown and Achane.
- **Market:** RB8 ADP, consensus avg rank 17.67, 2.08 in a superflex sample. Keeper posts show him held at R2-R4. Note the competing thread that McConkey is "the No. 1 option" in this offense.

**Draft take:** A turn-of-the-second-round pick where the room's own framing is "impossible" versus Henry, Saquon, Chase Brown and Walker. Take him if he falls to the back half of round 2; paying a first-round price is where the pushback starts.
