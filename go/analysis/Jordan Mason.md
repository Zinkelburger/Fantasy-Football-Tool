**Jordan Mason** (MIN, RB, bye 6) — board rank 104

**Room sentiment:** Mildly bullish with real dissent — 25 mentions, most clustered in one "ambiguous backfields" thread. A popular late-round target, but a chunk of the room thinks the talent isn't there.

- The bull case is role. Hartitz's handcuff tiers (94) state it plainly: "Expecting Jordan Mason to lead the way in Minnesota and Aaron Jones to be a lesser 1.B/pass-down back." The top comment in the ambiguous-backfield thread (96): "I'd rather have Jordan Mason of anyone in Minnesota's backfield, and he's cheaper than AJ."
- The bear case is the player. A well-received reply (7): "Jordan Mason is just the definition of mediocre — even on the Niners he just took what the line gave him." Another notes he was underwhelming last year even when Aaron Jones went down.
- Some are explicitly waiting: "if training camp and/or preseason points to Jordan Mason, he will be someone worth having." No camp report in the corpus resolves it. He is cheap, and appears in "four late-round RBs you should be drafting."

**Draft take:** A 10th-12th round shot at a lead-back role at backup prices. Fine as an RB4 stash, but the Aaron Jones split is unresolved — don't draft him expecting a starter.
