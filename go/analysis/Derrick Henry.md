**Derrick Henry** (BAL, RB, bye 13) — board rank 13

**Room sentiment:** Divided and loud — 94 mentions, with "Derrick Henry feels like such a bargain" and "I refuse to be caught dead with the Derrick Henry fall-off year" showing up in the same threads.

- **The bear case is data, not vibes:** since 2020, every rushing-title winner (Henry in 2020, then Taylor, Jacobs, McCaffrey, Barkley) saw rushing yards drop by at least 848 the next season. He's 32, and posters bucket him with CMC and Jonathan Taylor as "the overused RBs from last season."
- **The same data names him as the exception:** per John Daigle, of 24 instances of an RB reaching 370+ touches since 2014, only 4 (16.6%) improved in PPG the next year — every one was Henry or McCaffrey. Skeptics are reduced to "at some point King Henry has to break down, the question is when."
- **Pricing:** the Ravens preview calls it an "old geezer discount" — RB11 in PPR despite four straight double-digit-TD seasons and 1,100+ rushing yards in each. Aggregate average rank 20.33; Sleeper superflex ADP 34.7. One poster notes Vegas expects the highest return of the Henry / Hampton / Barkley / Chase Brown / Walker group.
- **Format caveat:** no receiving work — one comparison calls his missing PPR catches the equivalent of Goff's missing rushing.

**Draft take:** Reasonable at his round-2 slot, and best in standard/half-PPR where the missing receptions cost least. Fade him on age and workload if that's your read, but nobody here has found anything wrong with the role itself.
