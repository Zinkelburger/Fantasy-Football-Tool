**Keaton Mitchell** (LAC, RB, bye 7) — board rank 154

**Room sentiment:** Low volume (19 mentions) but uniformly positive — he is a recurring name on "late-round RBs to target" lists, with no one in the corpus arguing against him. The only real skepticism is structural: it's a committee.

- **Role:** camp notes say Omarion Hampton is "the clear-cut RB1 for the Chargers, but Keaton Mitchell looks to be emerging as the backup, especially for passing down work." Multiple posters describe the room as an RBBC of Hampton, Kimani Vidal and Mitchell.
- **The bull case:** the highest-scored mention (131) notes the staff signed him out of free agency and that he "had an explosive run on 10.2% of runs which pairs well with the scheme," in an offense the room says "starts at the run game." He is also still only 24.
- **The cap:** Ian Hartitz files him in handcuff tier 5 — "Committee szn, but we're saying there's a chance" — paired with Vidal, meaning even the Hampton-injury path may not be a clean bellcow role.
- **Price:** ADP ~158; superflex tables have him 15.05, and one keeper post lists him at $2 / 16th round.

**Draft take:** A last-few-rounds pass-down flier and the most-cited Hampton handcuff. Worth a bench spot if you drafted Hampton; standalone value depends on him beating out Vidal.
