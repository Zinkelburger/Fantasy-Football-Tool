**Troy Franklin** (DEN, WR, bye 10) — board rank 243

**Room sentiment:** Bearish for 2026 despite a genuinely useful 2025 — 14 mentions, and he appears in almost all of them as a supporting detail in someone else's argument rather than as a target.

- The production is real and repeatedly cited: he had the 6th-most red zone targets in the league last year (Courtland Sutton was 5th), out-targeted Sutton for a majority of the season, and is listed at 6'3". One poster grants he "had some weeks where he popped and was serviceable enough as a WR2 to draw some defensive attention."
- The bear case is Jaylen Waddle's arrival. The framing that keeps coming up: Denver's spread-it-around problem existed "because they were working with Sutton and Troy Franklin as their best receivers," and now "the days of whack-a-mole with Troy Franklin, Pat Bryant, Marvin Mims are hopefully over."
- He shows up on a list of receivers not to draft, grouped with Doubs, Kupp, Shakir, Shaheed and Jeudy. The one bullish note is conditional and dynasty-flavored: if Courtland Sutton sees load management, "Troy Franklin and Pat Bryant dynasty stocks are up."
- ADP around 210-250 depending on source.

**Draft take:** Last-round or waiver name in redraft. His red zone volume is the case, but the room expects Waddle to absorb it — only worth a pick if Sutton news turns bad.
