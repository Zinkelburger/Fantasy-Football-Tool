**Geno Smith** (NYJ, QB, bye 13) — board rank 171

**Room sentiment:** Bearish on him as a fantasy asset, but the discussion is really about his teammates — 24 mentions, nearly all inside Breece Hall and Garrett Wilson threads.

- The hostile takes lead: "You will NOT catch me with anyone led by Frank Reich and Geno Smith" (31), and "I don't quite think you realize just how god awful Geno Smith was last year." Others note Seattle dumped him and won the Super Bowl while the team that added him became the worst in the league.
- The counterargument is relative, not absolute: he's "a massive upgrade from Justin Fields" and even performing worse than last year would still make him the best QB of Breece Hall's career statistically. Supporters expect better game script and more legitimate passing volume, with Hall's targets returning toward his 4.7/game career average from a career-low 2.6.
- Concrete downside for the Jets' rushing equity: the team goes from a QB duo that accounted for 600 rushing yards and 5 TDs to Geno, who had 40 rushing yards last year.
- Named risks beyond Geno himself: Frank Reich as OC (called "a bad OC" by one poster) and the Jets offensive line.

**Draft take:** Not a QB to roster — his ADP around 185 is charity as it is. He matters only as a modest bump to Hall and Wilson, and the room does not trust the Reich offense to deliver it.
