**Kyle Pitts** (ATL, TE, bye 11) — board rank 73

**Room sentiment:** Loudly bearish with a real contrarian minority — 49 mentions, most of them jokes. He is the top answer in both "do not draft no matter what" (282) and "hill you'll die on" (277). "Kyle Pitts 1.01" is a running gag across threads.

- The fatigue is earned in the room's telling: "I'm not falling for the Kyle Pitts trick for the 50th time," "always under delivers," and one poster noting he had half his points in a single game last year.
- The bull case is concrete though. He was second among TEs in routes run, put up 31 explosive plays, and posted his best season as a pro since his rookie year. New HC Stefanski is described as a coach "who utilizes his big, athletic TE." Hartitz has him in tier 3 ("fans, but at least one concern").
- Price is the actual argument. He's going ~104 overall on Underdog and 6.09 on Sleeper, prompting "is Kyle Pitts really an early-round player when he's going 104 overall?" Several people who hate him concede he's "fantastic at cost right now."
- Genuine risk in the corpus: Atlanta's QB room. Tua is dealing with a tight back, Penix still isn't practicing with a knee injury, and the Falcons signed Cooper Rush.

**Draft take:** The mockery is the reason he's cheap, and the volume is real. Take him as a TE1 only if you're comfortable with the Falcons QB situation resolving.
