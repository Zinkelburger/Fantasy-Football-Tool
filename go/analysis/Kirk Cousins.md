**Kirk Cousins** (LV, QB, bye 13) — board rank 185

**Room sentiment:** Bearish and mocking — 22 mentions, and the corpus treats him as a downgrade attached to other people's players rather than as a draftable QB.

- Confirmed role: camp notes report "Kirk Cousins has been named the starting quarterback of the Las Vegas Raiders." Klint Kubiak's quote is hedged, though — Cousins "is the starting QB, he's the guy," but Kubiak also says "I want Fernando and I want Aidan to push him," which the room reads as a live benching risk. One poster argues starting Cousins over Fernando Mendoza in week 1 would "instantly lose credibility."
- Physical concerns are specific: he was medically cleared two years ago but "still looked like he was operating at about 30% mobility," and "a shell of his former self initially, and never really got much better."
- The thin bull case: he "isn't a bad regular season QB, but older and very prone to picks," he's "still pretty decent for the slot receivers," and he was QB15 in 2020 when Klint Kubiak was his QB coach. Zac Robinson's Atlanta offense finished top 10 in yards and top 5 in passing with late-career Cousins.
- Downstream note: when someone said "Bowers will feast with Kirk," the reply was "Kyle Pitts 2.0."

**Draft take:** Do not draft him. His only relevance is as a mild discount on Raiders pass catchers, and the Mendoza-takes-over scenario is openly expected by parts of the room.
