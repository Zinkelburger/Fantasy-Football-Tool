Dylan Sampson (CLE) – RB3, 2025 Fantasy Outlook (Redraft/Dynasty)

- Baseline role
  - Competitor for the RB3 spot behind Jerome Ford; currently not projected as a true workhorse.
  - Standalone fantasy value is limited unlessJudkins signs or an in-season opportunity arises via injury or role change.

- Key sentiment from Reddit FF Hound analysis
  - Sampson is viewed as a potential beneficiary if Quinshon Judkins is not signed or unavailable; otherwise he’s a clear secondary option behind Ford.
  - The consensus warns against chasing media narratives about veterans being “the starter”—those notes are traps; Sampson’s upside rests on a Judkins absence or a true split backfield.
  - He’s a late-round/draft-niche pickup with upside in specific scenarios, not a core piece in standard redrafts.

- How the Judkins news affects him
  - If Judkins misses time or isn’t signed: Sampson’s value spikes as the obvious beneficiary; likely workload bump (handcuff-to-flex upside in a split backfield).
  - If Judkins signs and is healthy: Sampson’s role is secondary (behind Ford/Judkins); limited weekly floor, high variance, best as a late-round stash or DFS punt in deep leagues.

- ADP/market signals to watch
  - FF Hound notes Sampson as a “late in drafts” beneficiary when there’s Judkins uncertainty; a price increase is likely if Judkins news leans negative.
  - Dynasty mock data places Sampson (RB) as early as 2.11 in rookie-centric formats, signaling strong early hype but not necessarily reflective of redraft value. Treat as context, not baseline.
  - Expect volatility around camp reports; autodraft risk if you don’t actively monitor.

- College/backstory context (relevant to role)
  - Sampson reportedly benefited in college when light boxes were available; in Cleveland, his game would hinge on how the Browns deploy a potential split and how much pass-catching he can contribute.
  - Historically, Jerome Ford was the starter when available; Sampson’s ceiling scales with Judkins’ status and the degree of sharing in a healthy Browns backfield.

- Risk factors
  - High dependence on Judkins’ status; without him, Sampson’s path to consistent touches is clearer but still behind Ford.
  - Front-office/coach speak and preseason reports can swing his draft cost quickly; avoid autodraft exposure.
  - Limited upside in standard formats unless an injury or three-way committee tilt creates meaningful volume.

- Draft strategy recommendations
  - Redraft: only roster Sampson as a late-round lottery ticket or best-ball stash. If Judkins news swings negative, he becomes a priority add; have a clear exit if camp shows minimal involvement.
  - Dynasty: value as a very late-round flier or taxi-squad stash with possible long-term upside if he proves as a versatile depth piece or if Ford/Judkins’ roles shift.

- Quick takeaway
  - Sampson’s 2025 fantasy viability hinges almost entirely on Judkins’ status. In the best-case (Judkins out), he becomes a meaningful handcuff-turned-flex option. In the baseline (Judkins signs and plays), he remains a dart-throw, depth-chart depth piece with limited weekly upside. Monitor camp reports and avoid overpaying in drafts until Judkins’ status is clearer.
