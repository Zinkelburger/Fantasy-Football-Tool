**Matthew Stafford** (LAR, QB, bye 11) — board rank 116 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The reigning MVP coming off a QB3 fantasy season, and the room is arguing about how far the touchdowns regress rather than whether he is any good.

- No injury report anywhere in the corpus. What the room prices is age — he is 38 with a chronic back issue — while noting last year's back-problem panic proved overblown. He is widely expected to retire after this season, which the room reads as why LA drafted Ty Simpson in the first round.
- Role and situation are unchanged. He won MVP and finished QB3 in fantasy on an outlier touchdown year; one commenter's tally instead puts him first in total points, narrowly ahead of Drake Maye, but the room's repeated framing is QB3. The scoring pipeline is the short throw to Davante Adams — a commenter notes the two seasons with the most one-yard touchdown passes are 2020 Rodgers and 2026 Stafford, both with Adams, both leading the league in TD passes. That profit is explicitly conditional on his health at 38.
- The bear case has two halves. Touchdown regression: one commenter says there is very little chance he throws within five TDs of last season, to which the reply is that eight fewer would still have been QB5. And game script: the Rams defense took a large step up on paper, so LA can milk clock with Kyren Williams and Blake Corum after the third quarter and ask him to throw less — the counter being that nobody stopped the Adams red-zone look all year.
- Market: he goes about 120th and one poster took him in the 13th round where he "inexplicably fell," but others report him going as early as the 3rd and at 6.10 on a 14-team board, so the price is unstable league to league. The room's own read is that the market is overcorrecting from QB3 to barely rosterable. He is called an especially good target in 6-point passing-TD leagues, where one commenter argues Stafford around round 13 is essentially Josh Allen eleven rounds earlier.
- Near-term: Vegas has him as the Week 1 QB3 at 19.3, which a commenter questions because the Rams do not leave for Australia until the following week and he will be jet-lagged.

**Draft take:** Draft him as a late QB1 and pay up a round in 6-point passing-TD leagues — the touchdown rate regresses, but eight fewer scores still leaves a top-five quarterback at a double-digit-round price.
