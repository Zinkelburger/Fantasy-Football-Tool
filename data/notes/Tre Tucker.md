**Tre Tucker** (LV, WR, bye 13) — board rank 210 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the most-argued cheap receiver in the corpus — a real WR2 role on an offense the room does not believe can support one.

- Role: he is the Raiders' WR2 behind Brock Bowers, and Raiders fans say he has been a bigger focal point than Jalen Nailor through camp and the preseason. Dan Graziano (ESPN, 8/11) reports the 2023 third-round pick is probably the receiver in the Raiders group the team says can take the biggest step forward this year. He also appears on Ourlads' primary-returner chart among players who both return and should get decent offensive work.
- The bull case (9/2): Klint Kubiak's system needs a receiver to run the motion at its heart and that is Tucker at Z, with Kirk Cousins described as a tunnel-vision quarterback who locks onto two targets — Bowers plus one. Cited support: an 8/140/3 game last year and 29-43-389-4 over his first seven games.
- The bear case, argued at length by a Raiders fan (57 points): a 5'9" burner in the 19th/20th percentile against man and press last year, a career-high 57 catches on an 18% target share, "the Quentin Johnston of most teams." He expects Tucker and Nailor to split targets evenly and calls Tucker at best a serviceable WR4 spot start. A separate concentration knock: 28% of his half-PPR points came in one garbage-time game, and removing it drops him from WR37 to WR52.
- Market: the room pays roughly what the board says, and treats him as disposable either way — reported at 15.10 and 16.10 in 12-team leagues, "literally going to be people's last skill position pick," FantasyPros WR92 in dynasty. The stated plan is to draft him last, watch Week 1 and cut him if the role is not real; one owner notes 67/850/6 would make that a huge value.
- Sentiment: the most-discussed player in his thread (20 top-level comments, 473 upvotes), narrowly preferred to Malik Washington because "he's actually done something before." Comps run from Rashid Shaheed-lite and Darnell Mooney down to nothing; several say he simply will not be in weekly lineups.

**Draft take:** spend your last pick on him rather than trusting waivers, and cut him after Week 1 if the target share is not clearly above Nailor's.
