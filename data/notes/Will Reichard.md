**Will Reichard** (MIN, K, bye 6) — unranked on the board · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The room's favourite underrated kicker, and the case is leg strength plus a dome.

- The profile, from one commenter and unchallenged in the thread: two missed kicks all of last season, both from 50-plus; tied for the league lead with 11 made 50-yarders; a long of 62; and a perfect preseason including two more from beyond 50. Home games are indoors.
- The upside argument is offense-dependent. He was K6 last season in what the room calls a disaster offense, and the case for more rests on Kyler Murray running the Vikings offense in place of the 2025 Wentz and J.J. McCarthy mishmash producing more scoring drives — which holds only while Murray is starting and Minnesota actually improves on last year.
- Sentiment, 2026-09-04: "I feel like Reichard is always disrespected" was the best-received comment in the Week 1 kicker tiers thread, with a reply noting camera wires cost him two more made 50-yarders last season.
- Not unanimous — managers asked about dropping him for Spencer Schrader and about keeping him over Fairbairn, though the replies went his way ("Dropped Loop for Reichard. I'm a believer").
- Market: still a last-round kicker — "I will be taking kicker in the last round, hoping Reichard is still around" — against a 237.0 ADP, so the room's affection has not moved the price.

**Draft take:** Take him with your last pick; he is the rare kicker where the room's enthusiasm and the price still agree.
