**AJ Barner** (SEA, TE, bye 11) — board rank 174 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Healthy and ascending, with a role case stronger than his target volume.

- Finally healthy after multiple offseason surgeries, per Jeremy Fowler (ESPN, 8/11).
- Barner said on the record that Seattle will have two tight ends on the field "very, very often," with the position facilitating the motions and shifts that are a staple of the San Francisco offense OC Fleury came from. Fowler calls him an ascending tight end who should come into his own in Year 3, can do a bit of everything, and whose progress Fleury's offense should accelerate.
- The counterweight is volume and competition: about four targets a game, and Cooper prefers Elijah Arroyo's upside, expecting Arroyo in a big-slot role with Barner inline. He is named Seattle's tush-push guy, but a rebuttal notes he already held that role last season and was used on the goal line once all year, in a 30-7 game.
- Sentiment is quietly positive — called "criminally underrated" with a top-eight fantasy TE prediction, on the argument that a good in-line tight end who catches what he should and works underneath now clears the bar for a top-10 fantasy TE.
- Market: he sits on waivers in plenty of leagues and gets taken happily as a last pick; in dynasty superflex he went below his 3,541 market value in 68% of 153 trades.

**Draft take:** A last-round or waiver-wire tight end worth taking on the two-TE role, with the caveat that Arroyo is the one who could take the ceiling.
