**AJ Barner** (SEA, TE, bye 11) — board rank 174 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** a deep sleeper with a real, reported role — good blocker, rarely off the field, and the incumbent the room does not expect Elijah Arroyo to pass.

- Health: Jeremy Fowler (ESPN) reported on 8/11 that Barner is finally healthy after multiple offseason surgeries. No limitation reported since.
- The strongest role evidence is Barner's own, on the record: he said the Seahawks will have two tight ends on the field "very, very often", with the position facilitating the motions and shifts that are a staple of the San Francisco offense new OC Fleury came from. Fleury spent the last four years as the 49ers tight ends coach with Kittle. Fowler calls Barner an ascending tight end who should come into his own in Year 3 and whose progress the new offense should accelerate.
- Room read on the depth chart: Seattle loves him, he does everything well and is called a Kittle-level blocker, he was one of Darnold's favourite targets last season, and there is no established No. 2 behind JSN. Cooper is the dissent — he prefers Arroyo's upside and expects Arroyo in a big-slot role with Barner inline.
- What tempers it is volume. He averaged about four targets a game. He was named as Seattle's tush-push guy, and the rebuttal is that he already held that role last season and was used on the goal line exactly once all year, in a 30-7 game.
- No redraft market claims at all; the only price signal is dynasty, where a buy-low board has him going below his 3,541 market value in 68% of 153 superflex trades. Sentiment runs high for a player at this rank — "criminally underrated", predicted top-eight fantasy TE — but the same threads name him as a TE sitting on waivers.

**Draft take:** a last-round TE with a real two-TE role and a beat-confirmed clean bill of health; take him as the streamer you do not have to replace, not as a top-eight bet.
