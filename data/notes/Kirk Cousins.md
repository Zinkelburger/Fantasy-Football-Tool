**Kirk Cousins** (LV, QB, bye 13) — board rank 185 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** hard split on a 38-year-old — the same thread has him as PFF's QB13 and as Matt Ryan in Indianapolis.

- He is the Raiders' Week 1 starter. Klint Kubiak named him over No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza, having already opened camp with Cousins as QB1 on a heavy rep share; beat reporter Matt Zenitz had the naming coming on Aug 29. Commenters read the potential three-year deal, signed after the staff attended Mendoza's pro day, as Las Vegas planning for him to hold the job rather than keep the seat warm.
- The job is his until he loses it, and the room expects that to happen. The Mendoza threads cluster a takeover around weeks 5-9 if Las Vegas falls out of the race or Cousins gets hurt, one commenter thinks he realistically lasts five or six weeks rather than the eleven the team hopes for, and another listed him among the shakiest veteran starters in the league.
- Scheme fit is the bull case: Cousins knows Kubiak's offense from Minnesota and the room reads him as the ideal operator for a Shanahan-style West Coast attack — under center, play action, quick processing — and the team's best chance to be competitive. Bulls add a 5-2 finish throwing to practice-squad receivers, deep-ball efficiency and a run-first OC.
- The bears are specific: he looked bad in his 2025 starts, Atlanta benched him for Penix for a reason, and fans think the arm has lost velocity — which they read into how little the preseason plan attacked the middle of the field. A Commanders fan calls him a tunnel-vision quarterback who hyper-focuses on Bowers plus one other target, with his last full season at 3,500 yards in 14 games.

**Draft take:** a two-QB and superflex streamer with a midseason expiry date — do not draft him in one-QB and do not plan on him finishing the year.
