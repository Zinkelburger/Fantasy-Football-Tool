**Tetairoa McMillan** (CAR, WR, bye 5) — board rank 38 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The room fades him relative to London and Nico and can articulate why — proven-ness, not talent — while a slot-usage change is the live bull case.

- Health: nothing reported. The only injury claim is a commenter fading him on durability, saying he "can't seem to stay healthy," with no specific injury behind it.
- Role, and the reason to care: Carolina plans more slot work in year two. He ran only a 15% slot rate as a rookie and still went 70-1000-7 on a team that threw for about 3,300 yards. One commenter frames the change as roughly 10% to 30% slot work plus a 5% bump in team pass attempts, which he argues could push McMillan into the top 10. Chris Brazzell's injury clears the inside, and shorter targets from Bryce Young are a PPR positive.
- The counter is offense-level: low pass volume, which is why one ranker put him a tier below the receivers at his ADP in full PPR while naming him a half-PPR target as a high-TD deep threat. Loza ranks him first of the Ladd/Egbuka/Tee/Tet group on volume and calls him a floor pick, not an upside one.
- Market: WR20 around the 3rd/4th turn (ADP low 40s), reported picks in the 4th, at 5.03 and one outlier at 6.54 — the room pays about what the board says. The standing objection is that he goes above his rookie finish: WR16 in total points but WR23-25 at 12.4 per game. Twerski lists him among his ADP fades; Murchison names him the WR in his WR13-24 range he is least confident in and says he has barely drafted him.
- Sentiment, the sharpest item here: a 430-comment thread asked why he is faded next to London and Nico. The majority answer, at 1,901 upvotes, is that London and Nico are *also* discounted for quarterback play and are simply more proven, so a round of separation is fair. The dissent — "ignoring the noise," citing 70/1000/7 as a rookie floor — was second at 652.

**Draft take:** Fine at the 4th-round turn and a reach above it — you are buying a slot-usage projection, not production, against proven receivers the room has already argued are cheap for the same reason.
