**Jaxson Dart** (NYG, QB, bye 8) — board rank 92 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Polarizing, and a lot of the volume is about the person rather than the player — the two real football arguments are the play-callers and the injury risk.

- No current injury. The concussion narrative is overstated per the room: he has exactly one documented concussion, and commenters attribute the perception to refs sending him to the blue tent constantly and broadcasters not correcting it. He was the QB1 from Weeks 4-10 last year before that concussion. The forward-looking risk everyone names is play style — Twerski calls him a bigger injury risk than most quarterbacks and would take Caleb Williams instead if drafting only one, while Maher, who has him QB13 behind Caleb Williams and Trevor Lawrence, calls him the most volatile arm in the tier and the likeliest injury.
- The role concern is the coaching staff, not the depth chart. OC Matt Nagy said on Aug 13 that Dart is "super talented" but is having "struggles" in training camp — his second straight year learning a brand-new offense and his first working extensively from under center. The room expects the Nagy/Greg Roman/Brian Callahan staff to run a conservative under-center, run-heavy RPO and short-passing offense that caps his passing volume, and several call the whole Giants offense a do-not-draft zone.
- The bull case is a rushing floor of about 10 carries a game plus better weapons. As a rookie he posted 63.7% completions, 15 TD to 5 INT, a 91.7 rating, 13th in EPA per play and 17th in success rate while throwing to Jalin Hyatt and Wan'Dale Robinson; he now has Nabers and Likely. Top-three upside is conditional on Cam Skattebo and Malik Nabers staying healthy. Cooper wants a mobile quarterback in every late build and names Dart alongside Kyler Murray and Malik Willis on the same evidence: every QB running 5-plus times a game over 16-plus games has finished top 10 for a decade straight.
- Market: he goes in rounds 9-14, was the last quarterback off the board at 10.9 in one 14-team league, and goes undrafted in others — two commenters still predict a top-five scoring season, one of them conditioned on health. Dynasty runs the other way: QB12 on KeepTradeCut and repeatedly named the current sell, likened to Anthony Richardson and C.J. Stroud as a young QB whose top-10 price will not hold.
- Sentiment: 11 top-level fades in one thread, split between distrust of the Giants offense and personal dislike. The best-voted verdict on the camp reports was "Are the struggles named Matt Nagy?" at 1,134 points. Loza thinks he will crush on a points-per-game basis but finish behind Trevor Lawrence in total points.

**Draft take:** A round-10 QB2 with a real top-five weekly ceiling, an offense designed to suppress it and a body the room does not trust — pair him with a second quarterback rather than betting a season on him.
