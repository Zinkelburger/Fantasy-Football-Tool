**Marvin Harrison** (ARI, WR, bye 14) — board rank 81 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** hostile and split, with the negative side much louder — roughly fifteen mentions in one thread, uniformly down on him.

- Role, per a commenter relaying the coaching staff: Harrison is Arizona's primary X receiver, will be used in motion, and is expected to lead the team in targets — with McBride, Michael Wilson and Jeremiyah Love drawing targets in the same offense. The same commenter says he averaged roughly 15-18 fantasy points a game once Jacoby Brissett took over last season. Many still project him as the Cardinals' top receiver behind McBride, and the new LaFleur staff, coming from the league's best offense, is treated as a real upgrade on the departed Petzing scheme.
- Discount the one camp report you will see: the "different WR" line late in camp came from the RookieWatch aggregator account, which the room heavily discounted — commenters say it fabricates quotes and never sources anything, and several asked for it to be banned.
- Michael Wilson's extension is read as pressure on Harrison rather than confidence in him: Cardinals fans say nobody would be upset if the team moved off him, the fifth-year option is still undecided, and several think Wilson is simply the better player. The counter, at +38, is that when both were healthy Harrison was statistically better, and one commenter says Wilson's mid-tier contract moves Harrison up his board.
- The defence of him: only about a season and a half healthy, with a concussion, appendicitis and a heel issue last year and just two healthy games alongside Brissett. The scouting complaints recur across threads — loses at the catch point, body catches, cannot separate in and out of breaks. Production so far is roughly 850 yards a year.
- Sentiment cuts both ways but the volume is negative. "MHJ will be a top 10 receiver in PPR" was one thread's second-biggest entry at 156 points, and commenters note how much MHJ stock the sub holds; the pushback is that he has shown nothing to predict it. Nominated as a player the room has overcorrected on, the reply was "you're in for a rude awakening with MHJ".
- Market is dynasty-side: fourth most-traded in the week's roundup and declining, sold below his 5,279 market value in 64% of deals, a third straight roundup on the discount side. The price is roughly one future first, and buy-low framing is common. One commenter, calling it unpopular, says he will significantly outperform his ADP this year.

**Draft take:** a WR3 price on a player the room has given up on — take the discount as a bet on the LaFleur offense and a full Brissett season, and expect no help from sentiment if it goes wrong.
