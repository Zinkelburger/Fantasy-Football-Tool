**Marvin Harrison** (ARI, WR, bye 14) — board rank 81 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Split and hostile — Cardinals fans and the wider room lean negative on the player, while a sizeable minority argues the 2025 numbers are injury-distorted and the scheme has changed.

- No current injury. 2025 was three unrelated ailments, not one: a concussion, a foot/heel injury and an appendectomy. He played 12 games and several only partially — 60% of snaps in Week 13 before leaving, 50% in Week 16, 31-33% in Week 17, out Week 18 — which the room says distorts every full-season rate stat about him.
- Role, per the coaching staff as relayed in-thread (Aug 27): he is the primary X, will be used in motion, and is expected to lead the team in targets — that last only while McBride, Michael Wilson and Jeremiyah Love are also drawing targets in the same offense. Under new HC LaFleur, who calls the plays with Hackett as OC, the room projects him as the McVay-scheme X — the Davante Adams/Brandin Cooks isolation-and-red-zone job — with Wilson and McBride taking the Kupp/Nacua work near the line.
- The quarterback change is the bull case. Kyler Murray threw over 70% of his 2025 passes under 10 air yards and was last in the league at 26.3% deep-ball success while Harrison worked at a 13.6-yard aDOT. In his three healthy games with Jacoby Brissett he saw 6, 10 and 12 targets, a 38% first-read target share and about 18.7 expected fantasy points a game — a WR4 pace — while Michael Wilson saw 4, 4 and 7. That holds only while Brissett keeps the job.
- Underlying 2025, from the room's own numbers: 20.3% targets per route, 1.69 yards per route run, 60 first-read targets and 13 end-zone targets in 12 games, against a WR48 overall and WR36 points-per-game finish. His 5.5% drop rate ranked about 60th in the league — below Amon-Ra St. Brown, CeeDee Lamb, Ladd McConkey and Rashee Rice, with the highest aDOT of that group. Detractors answer with 4 drops on 73 targets and a 51.2% catch rate.
- One analyst's model has him WR21 against a FantasyPros ADP of WR31, projecting 120 targets, 1,046 yards, 7 TD and 12.3 half-PPR points a game.
- Market: he costs about 84th, WR31 on FantasyPros, and the room pays roughly that or less — taken at the 5/6 turn, in round 6 of a 12-team, as a WR4-WR5 in 10-team, $3 in an auction. Several say the discount is the whole appeal. In dynasty he is No. 4 most-traded and sold below his market value in 64% of deals, his third straight roundup on the discount side; the going price is roughly one future first.
- Sentiment is negative and loud. The substantive doubts are that he loses at the catch point, body catches, and does not accelerate in and out of breaks; the room repeatedly says Michael Wilson simply looks better. One caution: the widely-shared camp report that he looked like a "different WR" came from the RookieWatch aggregator account, which the room says fabricates quotes and glazes every player it posts.

**Draft take:** The market has already paid for the fade, so buy the round-6 price on a team's stated primary X with a new play-caller and Brissett throwing — but do not pay anything for the top-10 ceiling the sub keeps voting for.
