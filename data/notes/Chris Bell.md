**Chris Bell** (MIA, WR, bye 6) — board rank 267 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A last-pick rookie whose ACL rehab is running ahead of schedule into the thinnest receiver room in the league.

- **Activated off the non-football injury list on 8/17 and practising; he was never PUP-eligible because the knee injury came in college.** Coach Jeff Hafley said on Tuesday, per Joe Schad of the Palm Beach Post, that Bell is still working into football shape — he was cleared for full practice the previous week and played 14 offensive snaps in the preseason finale, catching 2 of 5 targets for 22 yards. Hartitz's read on 9/2 is that he was expected to be PUP-then-ramp with a return around mid-October and is instead already practising and set to be out there for Week 1.
- **The role is opportunity, not a job.** Miami traded Tutu Atwell to the Rams and, per Barry Jackson, is also moving on from Jalen Reagor, leaving three rookie draft picks, Malik Washington, Jalen Tolbert, Henning and Ryan Miller — with Jackson saying more moves are still expected there. The room's stash case is explicitly not Week 1 but weeks 4-8, once he is conditioned, and multiple commenters expect him to be the Dolphins' WR1 before the season is out. The activation buys practice reps, not snaps; a 21-point "temper expectations" post is the thread's own counterweight.
- Scouting splits sharply. The bull read calls him an A.J. Brown archetype, a big-bodied X who would have gone late first or early second without the knee. Hartitz names him his biggest darkhorse — A.J. Brown-esque film, a Miami team that will trail and pass more than expected, 100-plus projected targets, and the rookie most likely to lead the class in points outside Love and Price. Against that: one commenter says he drops too many easy catches and will be benched quickly, another scouting read calls him fast in a straight line but average across the board — no elite quickness, change of direction or route running — and a JAG. His route running is called raw by more than one poster.
- Market is two claims deep: a 17th-round keeper in a .5 PPR league and a 20th-round dart in a 10-teamer, which matches a board rank of 267.
- Miami fans are the high end on him — a 33-point comment says he was getting separation and chunk yardage against Falcons starters while out of shape and roughly 70% healthy — and Murchison wants him rostered as the year goes on as a candidate to matter during the fantasy playoff stretch.

**Draft take:** A final-round pick worth holding through September, since the case for him is weeks 4-8 in a receiver room nobody else has claimed.
