**Emeka Egbuka** (TB, WR, bye 10) — board rank 46 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A month of drafting has been spent arguing about a toe injury nobody has officially diagnosed, and the price still assumes he is healthy.

- **A toe injury from roughly mid-August is the whole story, and Tampa has never named the body part.** It has been reported only as a toe injury; "turf toe" is the room's inference, repeated so often it now reads as fact. HC Todd Bowles said on 8/29 he is "hopefully" ready for Week 1 and gave no return-to-practice date — the room's dominant read (top comment 542 points) is that Bowles is hiding turf toe, with a well-argued dissent that no report confirms it and that a grade 1 sprain is mild.
- The practice evidence is thin but positive. Bucs beat writer Rick Stroud saw him working with trainers on a side field on 9/2, running with no obvious problem. He is not on PUP, which would have meant no return before Week 4. Counterpoint in the same thread: side-field running proves little and toe sprains re-flare.
- Two medical reads, and they differ. FantasyDocs graded it a grade 1 toe sprain at one to two weeks, expecting Tampa to be cautious the way it was with his hamstring last season. A quoted doctor (Morse) on 9/4 is more guarded: progressing but not 100%, believed to play Week 1, roughly 25% re-injury risk, resolving by Week 4-6 absent setbacks, playing with a plantar plate in his shoe. Nothing official exists until the first Week 1 injury report.
- **Role is thin on evidence but clean in shape:** Tampa's WR1 with Mike Evans gone, in a new coordinator's offense — the OC was on the Rams staff during Cooper Kupp's record season and has said he wants to use Egbuka in that role. Scott Reynolds (Pewter Report) had him as Mayfield's primary red-zone target in early camp. The dissent is that he is WR1 by default and Chris Godwin still coexists. One keeper manager downgrades him because Bowles's comments on his role have been all over the place.
- **Market: rank 46 is what the room pays, and several say the injury is not priced in at all** — he goes at the 3rd/4th turn with DeVonta Smith, Waddle, McConkey and Tee Higgins, and the loudest thread on it is a fade at that cost ("DND", 175 points; "discount-only until he gets back on the field"). Analysts have moved him down but not off: Hartitz dropped him behind Waddle, McConkey and Zay Flowers; Cooper will only take him as a WR3 in a 2-WR league and moved him below Pickens and Rashee Rice; Maher has him WR24, essentially at consensus; Twerski still calls him the WR13-24 pick with the most likely upside at ADP.
- He drew 8 top-level questions in a Sept 2 AMA, the most of any player, all of them about the toe.

**Draft take:** Fine at a discount past the 4th, wrong at his current price — you are paying a healthy WR2 cost for a receiver whose team will not say what is wrong with him.
