**Emeka Egbuka** (TB, WR, bye 10) — board rank 46 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Unsettled a week from kickoff — the toe is the whole conversation, and he was the most-asked-about player in the Sept 2 AMA.

- **A toe injury from roughly three weeks ago that the team has never named.** The room calls it turf toe, but no report has confirmed the body part — only "toe." Bowles said on 8/29 that "hopefully" he is ready for Week 1, with no return-to-practice date, and the dominant read in that thread (top comment 542 pts) was that Bowles is hiding turf toe. Dissenters note it is not turf toe unless the big toe is involved, that the team called it stabilised, and that Tampa never used PUP, which would have ruled out a return before Week 4.
- **The practice evidence points to playing.** Bucs beat writer Rick Stroud saw him working with trainers on a side field on 9/02, running with no obvious problem with the toe. No official injury report exists until this week's practices — the first real read.
- **Two medical reads, both short of a clean bill.** FantasyDocs graded it a grade 1 toe sprain, one to two weeks to heal, and reads Tampa as being cautious the way it was with his hamstring last season. Dr. Morse on 9/04 says he is progressing but will not be 100%, is believed to play Week 1, carries roughly a 25% re-injury risk, and should have it resolved by Week 4-6 absent setbacks — playing with a plantar plate in his shoe.
- **Role is the bull case: Tampa's clear WR1** with Evans gone, and the new OC was on the Rams staff for Cooper Kupp's record season and has said he wants to use Egbuka the same way. Scott Reynolds (Pewter Report) had him as Mayfield's primary red-zone target in camp on 8/11 after an up-and-down start. That WR1 role holds only while Chris Godwin and the rest of the room stay healthy, and one dissenter calls it WR1 by default.
- **Market: the board rank matches where he goes, and the room thinks that is too high.** He lands at the 3rd/4th turn with DeVonta Smith, Waddle, McConkey and Tee Higgins, and the loudest complaint is that the injury is not priced in — you pay as if he is healthy. "DND" and "discount-only until he gets back on the field" carried the 8/29 thread. Analysts split: Hartitz dropped him behind Waddle, McConkey and Zay Flowers in that tier and Cooper moved him below George Pickens and Rashee Rice, while Twerski calls him the WR13-24 pick with the most upside at ADP and Maher has him WR24, essentially at consensus.
- **Sentiment:** the fade case is that a hot rookie start masked nine or ten mediocre games before he got hurt, and that Godwin outproduces him cheaper.

**Draft take:** Let him fall past the 4th — the role is real but you are paying full price for a toe that even the optimistic read keeps under 100% into Week 4-6.
