**Deshaun Watson** (CLE, QB, bye 11) — board rank 209 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Uniformly negative, and the room's stated reason to fade every Cleveland pass catcher and to stream defenses against the Browns.

- He is the Week 1 starter, and it is reported, not rumored: Jordan Schultz (08-24), per multiple sources, that Cleveland named him the starter, with Shedeur Sanders as the backup. That puts him under center Week 1 at Jacksonville, his first start since returning.
- The leash is the live question. A commenter relaying Monken (09-02) says the decision is "not a week-to-week decision"; Maher, a couple of weeks later, says the leash is going to be short — possibly two weeks short — and treats him as a stopgap QB3 rather than a superflex answer. One commenter puts better-than-50% odds he is not the starter by season's end; another says he does not last past Week 5; the 08-24 threads put near-zero odds on him starting all 17.
- The room reads the decision as financial, not football: roughly $230M already paid out and about $46M owed this year, and one commenter claims Stefanski was fired and Monken hired in January precisely because ownership wanted Watson starting. A separate read is sequencing — starting Watson first makes it easy and popular to bench him for Sanders after a few bad games, where starting Sanders first leaves no fallback.
- On the field, the room says he has looked horrid throughout camp and is past his prime coming off two Achilles tears. Several self-identified Browns fans treat the start as a placeholder and openly want Jacksonville to beat him out of the job.
- No market claims exist — nobody is drafting him. In a 1,322-point thread he drew 76 mentions and was called one of the worst quarterbacks in the league, fantasy-relevant only in 2QB/superflex, with many expecting a benching by Week 3-5. The most-upvoted forward-looking take (179 pts) is a 2022-Daniel-Jones season that forces an awkward extension decision.

**Draft take:** Undraftable outside superflex, where he is a two-to-five-week stopgap at best — the actionable use of this page is the other direction: fade Browns pass catchers and stream defenses against Cleveland.
