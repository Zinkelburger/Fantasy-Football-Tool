**Malachi Fields** (NYG, WR, bye 8) — board rank 223 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** a genuinely split preseason riser — the case for him is entirely a bet on how long Malik Nabers is limited, and the case against is that the Giants are deep at WR3-WR5.

- Role is conditional on Nabers, and the room knows it. Commenters have him as the Giants' WR2 only while Nabers is not fully healthy; if Nabers is ready, Nabers and Isaiah Likely draw coverage and Fields gets favourable looks, and if Nabers is not, one commenter (2026-08-30) calls him the Giants' best receiver and the one who plays the most snaps.
- Camp reports through 2026-08-23 had him pushing Darius Slayton for the WR2 job, with a preseason touchdown from Jaxson Dart already banked. The pushback in the same thread: Harbaugh wants 22 personnel, which means snaps with Nabers as the only receiver, and Likely eats targets.
- Snap ceiling is the live worry. One commenter warns the Giants rotate receivers with nobody over a 70% snap share until Nabers is up to speed. He is praised as a willing run blocker, which is the argument for him staying on the field anyway.
- Supporting notes from the room: route running crisper than expected for his size, and he mossed a defender in the end zone in preseason.
- Market: he is going undrafted in most rooms — the board has him 223 and the room's stated reason for passing is Giants WR depth, not the film. One commenter projects 500-600 yards, 50-55 catches and a main end-zone role worth 8-9 touchdowns, and says he should be getting De'Zhaun Stribling levels of hype; that is a single projection, not a consensus. A 2026-09-02 thread (59 upvotes) shows owners split between holding him purely as a Nabers handcuff and expecting real downfield and red-zone work.

**Draft take:** free last-pick swing on a Nabers-limited start to the season — draft him only if you can carry a bench spot through the first month, because the whole case dies the week Nabers is off a snap count.
