**Josh Jacobs** (GB, RB, bye 11) — board rank 24 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Availability is the whole conversation; the football case for him is intact and currently irrelevant.

- On the NFL commissioner's exempt list since 8/30 (per Adam Schefter), after the Brown County DA charged him with misdemeanor battery and criminal damage to property. Exempt-list placement is paid leave with no end date — he cannot play until the commissioner removes him, and any eventual suspension would be retroactive to 8/30. Court date is Nov 17.
- He is not reliably IR-stashable. ESPN briefly listed him Out, which opened an IR slot, then flipped him to DTD; managers report Sleeper and Yahoo would not allow him in IR slots at all. Check your platform before spending a pick on the stash.
- Packers GM Brian Gutekunst, 9/1: "I think that's to be seen, but I'm certainly hopeful that he will. I certainly would expect that. But we're going to let this process play out and we're going to be prepared either way." He also declined to rule out a release — "we'll let the process play out before making any kind of those decisions." The team says it has not seen the video. That is the only on-record read that points toward him staying, and it commits to nothing.
- The room's split runs on one hinge: whether the garage surveillance video becomes public. If it stays with police until trial, the common landing spot is a plea and a six-game personal-conduct ban with exempt time counting, which is a Week 5-7 return. If it leaks, the exempt-list precedent commenters cite (Peterson 15 games, Hardy 15, Peppers 7, Hunt 5, Foster 5 — only Peppers played meaningful snaps that same season) points at a lost year. Named analysts including Davenport, Silva and Harmon were quoted at above 50% that he never plays for Green Bay again, though Davenport pushed back on that framing: "I'm not at all saying he's certain to miss the season. I do think it's on the table."
- Role, when available, is elite and unchallenged: 100% of Green Bay's rush attempts inside the 5 before his Week 10 injury last season, at a 58.3% TD rate, plus real receiving work. One commenter following camp notes Green Bay is running more outside zone, which suits MarShawn Lloyd, and that LaFleur has talked about being less run-first — a mild dent, not a role loss.
- Market: he went in rounds 3-5 and for $31-45 in auctions right up to the news. Now drafters report taking him rounds 9-16 as an RB4/RB5 stash, or dropping him outright in no-IR leagues and planning to re-add him off waivers for the fantasy playoffs. Trade offers of 110-140 ADP players for him drew zero bites. A visible minority is fading him on moral grounds rather than football ones.

**Draft take:** Do not draft him as a starter at any price — he is a round 10-plus lottery ticket on the fantasy playoffs, and only in a league where you have a roster spot you genuinely don't need until Week 13.
