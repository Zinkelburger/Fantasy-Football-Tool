**Josh Jacobs** (GB, RB, bye 11) — board rank 24 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Unavailable indefinitely and priced as a stash; the only live argument is whether he plays at all in 2026.

- **Unavailable, no end date.** Schefter reported 8/30 that the NFL placed Jacobs on the commissioner exempt list after Brown County charged him with misdemeanor battery and criminal damage to property. That is paid leave — he cannot practise or play until the case resolves or Goodell reinstates him — and the first court date is November 17. He is not on IR, so in a league that will not slot him he costs an active roster spot. Schefter had already confirmed on 8/11 that the case was under league review, in the same batch as Puka Nacua's and Tyreek Hill's.
- **Length is genuinely unresolved, and the median has moved pessimistic.** The felony strangulation charge was dropped, leaving two misdemeanours; one faction reads that as 4-6 games with exempt time counting, citing Jabrill Peppers' 7 games. The other notes the list has no end date against a mid-November court date, so the floor is past six games with a real chance of the whole year. By 9/04 commenters are dropping him in 10-team leagues, and the most detailed read expects that if he returns it is late, in unknown shape and possibly not in Green Bay. The hinge everyone names is whether the garage surveillance video leaks.
- **Read the coaches as optimism, not status.** Gutekunst on 9/01: "I think that's to be seen, but I'm certainly hopeful that he will. I certainly would expect that. But we're going to let this process play out." He also says he has not seen the video, and on 8/26 said the Packers had been preparing for a suspension "in case that happens." LaFleur said the day before the exempt-list move that he expected Jacobs to play. The room treated both as unreliable — though Green Bay has signed no veteran back and would have saved roughly $10M against $3M dead by cutting him this summer, which several read as the team planning to wait him out.
- **Role, if he gets back to it.** Before his Week 10 injury last season he took 100% of Green Bay's rush attempts inside the 5-yard line at a 58.3% TD rate and averaged 19.6 FPG. The camp counterweight, from a commenter following practices: Green Bay is running more outside zone that suits MarShawn Lloyd, the line has not blocked the run well, and LaFleur splits carries by habit.
- **Market: the board rank is stale.** He went rounds 3-5 and $31-45 in auctions right up to the news; since, drafters report him lasting to rounds 9-16 as an RB4/RB5 stash, and some drop and re-add him off waivers. The top-voted read (24 pts) is that IR eligibility is the only reason to take him at all — a round 4-5 flier where the platform grants it (ESPN does; CBS and some leagues do not), a late dart where it does not.
- **Sentiment:** a real bloc is fading him on character grounds regardless of price, and Packers fans are lukewarm even setting the case aside — aging, regressed last season, on his last year in Green Bay anyway.

**Draft take:** Not a back you can plan around — take him only where an IR slot will hold him, no earlier than the late single-digit rounds, and otherwise leave him to waivers.
