**Josh Jacobs** (GB, RB, bye 11) — board rank 24 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** An off-field absence with no end date; the room has moved from pricing a suspension to pricing a lost season.

- **On the NFL commissioner exempt list since 8/30 — paid, but he cannot practice or play, with no fixed return date.** Schefter reported it after Brown County charged him with misdemeanor battery and criminal damage to property; a felony strangulation count was dropped. It runs until the criminal case resolves or the commissioner reinstates him. First court date is Nov 17.
- The exempt list is not a suspension, and "he'll get six games" is the room's most common error. Analyst Jakob Sanderson, in a Sept 1 AMA: placement and removal are at the commissioner's sole discretion, six games is the DV policy minimum that applies cleanly only if a plea lands before Week 6, and with the case unresolved the modal outcome is missing the whole season. The only precedent for mid-season removal before legal resolution is Jabrill Peppers in 2024 — 7 games, later acquitted; other exempt-list cases the room cites are Peterson 15, Hardy 15, Foster 5, Hunt 5.
- Green Bay has not moved on. GM Brian Gutekunst on 9/1, asked whether Jacobs plays for the Packers this year: "I think that's to be seen, but I'm certainly hopeful that he will... but we're going to let this process play out and we're going to be prepared either way." He says he has not seen the video. Matt LaFleur said the day before the placement that he expected Jacobs to play — coach optimism the room dismissed, and the practice record (none) is the only status that counts. Releasing him this summer would have saved roughly $10M against $3M dead and they signed no replacement back — read as the team planning to wait him out.
- The hinge everyone names is the garage surveillance video the police complaint says corroborates the assault; the top-voted read of the news (2,789 points) is that the league acted because it is about to leak.
- **Whether he is draftable is a roster-mechanics question.** He is not on IR. ESPN briefly listed him Out, making him IR-eligible there, then flipped him to DTD; managers report Sleeper, Yahoo and CBS refusing the IR slot. The 9/4 top comment (24 points) is blunt: IR eligibility is the only reason anyone drafts him. Without a free IR spot he burns an active roster spot all year.
- What he was is not in dispute: before his Week 10 injury last season he took 100% of Green Bay's rush attempts inside the 5-yard line at a 58.3% TD rate on them and finished RB6 in points per game.
- **Market: board rank 24 is weeks stale.** He went rounds 3-5 and $31-45 in auctions right up to the news; since, drafters report rounds 9-16, $15 auction prices, and outright drops in no-IR leagues, with one owner getting zero bites offering him for 110-140 ADP players. On waivers a 90-point comment says spend the whole FAAB budget; the counter is that the claim is cheap enough that it turns only on whether you believe he plays again.

**Draft take:** Not a pick at all in a league without a free IR slot; with one, a round 9-or-later stash on a fantasy-playoff return and nothing earlier.
