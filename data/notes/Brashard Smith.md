**Brashard Smith** (KC, RB, bye 5) — board rank 222 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A roster-move signal in August that the room talked itself out of by September.

- Role, consensus Aug 31: waiving Demercado is read as the Chiefs backing Smith. Commenters say he looked really good in camp and the preseason and has third-down back traits, and that the team chose youth over the veteran passing-down specialist. The condition attached to that claim matters as much as the news — it is a role behind Kenneth Walker, splitting the backup work with Emmett Johnson.
- The later and stronger read is colder (Sep 3): a commenter says Kansas City does not see Smith as a potential starter and keeps him as a change-of-pace back and kick returner, and the thread's OP is blunter — "Brashard Smith is not the guy" if Walker goes down. On the handcuff question that supersedes the Demercado inference.
- Why the room wanted him anyway: as a second-year back he showed real burst and, unlike Pacheco, patience letting blocks set up. His 2025 work was a play or two a game of gadget and end-around looks because the touches went to Pacheco and Kareem Hunt. The dissent is a commenter calling him a great route runner who "cannot spot a hole to save his life," and therefore very droppable.
- Return ability is part of the roster case (Aug 16) — commenters see it as making the incumbent returner's spot redundant. That keeps him employed; it does not score fantasy points.
- Market: listed among Sleeper's trending-down players, and a manager who cut him reports being surprised that no team with roster space would spend minimal FAAB or a late pick to add him.

**Draft take:** Not a real Walker handcuff — leave him undrafted and let someone else find out whether the change-of-pace role ever grows.
