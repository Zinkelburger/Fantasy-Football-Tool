**Joe Burrow** (CIN, QB, bye 6) — board rank 58

**Room sentiment:** Quiet and skeptical — only 20 mentions, and most use him as a variable in someone else's outlook (Chase Brown, Ja'Marr Chase) rather than as a QB target.

- The one projection cited puts him just under 4,000 passing yards. A camp roundup notes he "might be feeling some of the pressure of his recent injury struggles and the Bengals' underachieving record."
- Bear case is availability, not talent: he appears in the "do not draft no matter what" thread, and one poster says flatly "I'm not falling for Joe Burrow again."
- Bull case is thin but present — he's grouped with Hurts and Lamar in the elite-QB-advantage discussion, and posters cite the highest completion percentage in NFL history (68.5%).
- Downstream fact worth using: Chase Brown was a top-20 RB in 6 of 9 games without Burrow and averaged 11 ppg in those games, roughly RB20 pace. Cincinnati's skill players are priced on Burrow's health.

**Draft take:** Nobody in the room is excited to take him at cost. If you want him, get him as a mid-round QB with a cheap second QB behind him — and think twice about pairing him with Chase Brown, which doubles your exposure to the same injury.
