**Joe Burrow** (CIN, QB, bye 6) — board rank 58 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Bought in on the offense, faded on availability — nothing about the price argument is about his passing.

- No current injury. What the room argues about is protection: the bear case is that the offensive line crumbles and he gets hurt again, and the louder rebuttal, including from a season-ticket holder, is that last year's line was the best he has had, all five starters return, Risner upgraded Volson, and writers infer a bad line from sack totals plus his injury history. He had surgery early last season and missed roughly eight weeks, which commenters treat as the single swing factor on every Cincinnati fantasy asset.
- After a scuffle with the Bears defence in August, Ja'Marr Chase said on the record that whatever else the Bengals do, they had better protect Joe. No injury came of it; the room's worry was the line's willingness to defend him, and several commenters want Burrow to walk away from those and let the linemen handle it.
- Role: he throws it 50-plus times a game in what the room calls a top-three or top-four offense, and expects a Week 1 shootout with Tampa Bay. The standing scheme critique is under-center work — Burrow said on Chase's podcast that he had never taken a snap under center before LSU, coming from shotgun-only offenses, and commenters have connected that to camp reports of him struggling from under center this year and last.
- Market: ADP 52.9, and the room's line is that they take him if he falls to the 6th; reported picks land in the 5th and 6th, including over Mike Evans and Brian Thomas. He went QB7 in the most recent ESPN expert mock, behind Dart and Daniels, and one drafter got him in the early 8th. The stated reason is the missing rushing floor — 275 yards and 3 touchdowns is an elite line for him and only matches an average game from a running quarterback. Maher is the outlier in the other direction, and this is his single largest QB disagreement: 96th overall against a consensus of 49. In superflex he costs round 2, at 25% of a 12-team auction budget.
- Sentiment: the room mostly bought his "2026 feels different in my bones" line, with one popular take putting him on Josh Allen's tier when he, Chase and Higgins are all healthy. Bengals fans forcefully reject the national "Burrow wants out" narrative as manufactured. The dissent is availability alone — "he misses half the year or more whenever they roster him" — and one warning worth its own line: "Burrow has always lost everyone their week 1 matchups."

**Draft take:** Fine as your QB1 in the 6th and not before, and pair him with a streamable backup, because the fade on him is entirely about games played and the fade is correct.
