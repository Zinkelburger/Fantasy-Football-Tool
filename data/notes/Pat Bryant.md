**Pat Bryant** (DEN, WR, bye 10) — board rank 250 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** a well-liked cheap dart on the Denver WR3 job, with the whole case resting on somebody ahead of him getting hurt.

- His ceiling is somebody else's availability: he is the Denver WR3 behind Courtland Sutton and Jaylen Waddle, both currently healthy and both with injury histories, and the room's whole bull case is one of them missing time — the specific pitch is a Bryant run in the fantasy playoffs if either is banged up late.
- His own health is the second question. Commenters note he kept getting hurt last season just as his usage grew past depth-guy level, and that he left the Buffalo playoff game injured after three catches for 32 yards on three snaps. One commenter recalls, hedged with "IIRC," concussion trouble last year. Every WR3 projection in the corpus is explicitly conditioned on health.
- Role as of early September: mostly from the slot, and unlikely to see five-plus targets a game soon. The supporting evidence is real but thin — he out-snapped Troy Franklin late last season, took the first three targets and catches in the playoff game against Buffalo, was pulled from a preseason game before Franklin was, and Sean Payton talks him up. One Broncos fan reads that talking-up as Payton's habit with depth guys.
- An August claim that he might already be passing Sutton for the WR2 job came from a commenter relaying an unnamed report, and a Broncos fan answered he will believe it when he sees it. Waddle's arrival has since made that harder, not easier.
- Market: he is going far earlier than board rank 250 — a 14th-round keeper price in a .5 PPR keeper league, a round ahead of Chris Bell, with the room split on whether paying more for the smaller role makes sense. In the thread where he was the top ask, "Now do Pat Bryant" took 215 points; owners call him a cheap big-body investment in a good offense who is one injury from relevance, while conceding the camp drum has been beaten since camp opened.
- One commenter names him as the Denver receiver worth rostering ahead of Troy Franklin.

**Draft take:** a last-round stash that only pays if Sutton or Waddle goes down; take him at his board price, not at the 14th-round cost the room is paying.
