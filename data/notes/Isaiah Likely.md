**Isaiah Likely** (NYG, TE, bye 8) — board rank 101 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A cheap TE1 bet on contract and coaching relationship, argued against by four straight seasons of his own production.

- The bull case is money and the coach: a three-year, $40M deal that can reach $47.5M, roughly $13M a year and the fourth-highest AAV at the position, after following John Harbaugh from Baltimore. The room's line is that teams do not pay a tight end that to block.
- The snap-share bear case comes from camp coverage and preseason tracking: he played 6 of 16 first-team snaps in the preseason opener, all of them in 11 personnel, and was subbed for Theo Johnson and Chris Manhertz whenever New York went heavy. The room lands on roughly a 60% share as a move/F tight end.
- Treat the "Giants WR2" report carefully. The widely circulated claim — that he will be the WR2 behind Malik Nabers, that coaches love how he has embraced his relationship with Jaxson Dart, and that he could be Dart's top option if Nabers needs time off the ACL — is Graziano's bold-predictions column being run by blogs as reported fact, per the 827-upvote top comment. His No. 1-target camp standing was also measured while Nabers was out. Separately, one commenter says the word around camp is that Likely and not Malachi Fields is the real WR2, because of extra time with Dart including sitting in the quarterback film room.
- His own record is the strongest thing against him: 42-477-6 is his career high in four seasons, he was TE47 at 3.4 points a game last year behind Mark Andrews, his targets per route (22.3, 15.5, 21.8, 16.4%) and yards per route run (1.51, 1.62, 1.82, 1.40) have never sustained, and he graded 56.4 at PFF on limited 2025 snaps — below Theo Johnson. Cooper's read is that he finally has the opportunity the position rewards but is slow and needs heavy volume, the Zach Ertz path, and would draft a second tight end behind him.
- Market: roughly TE12, rounds 9-13 in 12-team PPR, with reported picks at 102 and 128 and several owners punting the position to get him. The board rank sits at the early edge of that; one league saw him go in the fifth, which a commenter calls drafting him at his ceiling. Hartitz has him around TE10 with Dalton Kincaid and would start him over George Kittle for at least a week. In dynasty he went below his 3,887 market value in 69% of 306 superflex trades, the highest trade count on that discount board.
- Sentiment is broadly long: a 79-upvote comment calls him badly undervalued and would not be shocked by a top-7 finish, several rank him fifth at the position, and one commenter projects 65-750-7 on 80-90 targets, which would have been TE4-5 last year. The counter, from the same thread, is that all of this is role and none of it is production.

**Draft take:** Fine as your TE1 if you punt the position to round 10 or later; anything in the single digits is paying for a role he has never converted.
