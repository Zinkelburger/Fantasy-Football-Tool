**Jacoby Brissett** (ARI, QB, bye 14) — board rank 201 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The room agrees he is the starter and disagrees on whether last year's passing volume was real or an artifact of a 1-11 stretch.

- Role: Arizona's starter, entering with a full offseason as the guy after taking over from Kyler Murray midway through 2025 and being paid following a holdout. Several commenters note he may not start all year, with Carson Beck behind him.
- The production, as the room states it: 12 starts averaging 26 of 40 for 280 yards, 1.9 TD and 0.67 INT — a 4,768/33/11 pace — at roughly 20 fantasy points a game, which they call top-8 in points per game.
- The discount on that number is the whole bear case: Arizona threw about 50 times a game with him under center, and the reframing is that this came from game script — he went 1-11 after taking over at 2-3, and Arizona was the only team in the league without a single 30-plus-carry game. A reply adds that the volume was inflated by the RB room being hurt, and the room calls 50 attempts a game unlikely to repeat.
- The volume case for this year, in a 33-upvote comment: two good receivers, a top pass-catching back in Jeremiyah Love, and a team that will be trailing in most games. One commenter drafted him in two leagues expecting 35-40 attempts a game. At 6'4" he can see and use the middle of the field, which Murray could not, and he keeps continuity under new playcaller LaFleur.
- Sentiment splits cleanly: one commenter calls him underrated and working with good offensive minds, and says Cardinals players with and without him are night and day; others use "the No. 2 or 3 option for Brissett" as shorthand for discounting Arizona's pass catchers against a Mahomes receiver.

**Draft take:** A last-round QB1 by volume whose floor depends on Arizona being bad again — stream him, do not plan around him.
