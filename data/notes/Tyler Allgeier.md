**Tyler Allgeier** (ARI, RB, bye 14) — board rank 112 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The room's most-recommended waiver add of the last month, entirely on Jeremiyah Love's ankle — and split on whether he keeps anything once Love is right.

- Availability is the whole story and it is Love's, not his: commenters treat Allgeier as the Week 1 Arizona backfield lead if Love cannot go with the high ankle sprain — "totally fine with Allgeier and Bam for week 1" (22 points) — and note he was signed in free agency to be the RB1 before Arizona drafted Love. He is the listed RB2 as of 2026-09-01.
- Where the room divides: some argue a tanking Arizona team lets him out-carry Love all year; others say he loses the job the moment Love is healthy. Maher takes the middle and prefers him to Chris Rodriguez as the Cardinals handcuff — RB1 if Love misses time, and still on the field for red-zone and goal-line work with Love healthy. Murchison agrees he is "already in line for split backfield work with Love" but only wants him as an end-of-bench RB4/5.
- The vulture case has a track record behind it. In Atlanta he matched Bijan Robinson's red-zone carries 32-32 in 2025, scored 8 TDs on 143 carries, took 4 scores from inside the 5 on 41% of the team's attempts, and stayed on the field over Bijan in short yardage because he does not fumble. He was paid $12.5M against Brian Robinson's $2.5M. One commenter puts him around RB20 on that profile.
- The fade is the offence, not the role. He is described as a short-yardage back with no receiving proficiency, not in Love's talent bracket, and boom-or-bust week to week. Cooper wants no part of him: a short-term committee lead on a team Vegas has winning 3.5 games.
- Market: he is going later than the board has him — reported as a rounds 13-14 sleeper, and preferred by one commenter to Washington in the 11th/12th as the more proven fill-in, though with tough early matchups. His price has been climbing since the Love injury ("Allgeir is gonna be a league winner this year", 124 points, with replies calling that overstated on a bad offence), and drafters report moving him up over the Rachaad White / J.K. Dobbins / Tyrone Tracy flex tier.
- Sentiment: Loza names him a late-round flyer she likes; the running joke ("Ill take Tyler alligiers", 103 upvotes) is about his inconsistency, not his role.

**Draft take:** Worth a real pick in the 12th-13th and a waiver claim anywhere he is still free — he is the Week 1 lead if Love's ankle costs him time and a goal-line vulture even if it does not.
