**Patrick Mahomes** (KC, QB, bye 5) — board rank 107 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Nobody doubts he plays Week 1; the argument is whether a QB who cannot run is still a fantasy asset.

- On track for Week 1 at Denver after the torn ACL and LCL that ended his 2025 at 6-7. Rapoport reported him on track on Aug 29 after a stellar camp; a Sept 4 beat piece by McDowell details a nine-month rehab with no shortcuts. He did not play in the Aug 28 preseason game but warmed up on the field wearing no left-knee brace, per Chiefs writer Derrick, and a commenter says he took every first-team rep in camp. Andy Reid at the season kickoff: "Watch out, because here we come" — coach messaging, not a practice report, and the room said so.
- The one genuine caveat is timing: he is not quite nine months post-surgery, a commenter notes the standard return-to-play mark lands the Friday before the opener, and he enters Week 1 with no preseason snaps. No limitation or setback has been reported.
- The fantasy consequence is the rushing, not the availability. About 20.3% of his fantasy points last season came from scrambling alone, and the room expects a pocket-passer year worth roughly 4.5-5 points a game less on the ground — conditional on how much he is cleared to run. Kansas City signing Kenneth Walker and bringing Bieniemy back is read as the offense deliberately shifting toward designed runs.
- The supporting-cast worry is the offensive line. Chiefs fans flag the backup tackles behind the starters as having zero NFL experience after the cuts; a counter notes most outlets have the line in the top half, PFF at 8th.
- Market says the discount is closing. Managers report him going well outside the top five QBs in nearly every redraft league, one took him as QB14 and was mocked for it, and a 77-upvote thread calls the buy-low window about to shut. Hartitz is the loudest dissent, ranking him behind both Goff and Stafford on the grounds that last season's boom was entirely added rushing. Twerski would draft him only in a two-QB-roster, play-the-matchups build.
- Sentiment in the Chiefs sub is confident about him and skeptical about everything around him: "talk is cheap", "I'll believe it when I see it", three lackluster seasons. The room's own prediction is a routine top-10 QB finish, and one commenter notes top-10 at QB is not an edge in a 12-team single-QB league.

**Draft take:** Fine as your QB1 at a round-9 price if you accept the passing-only version, but the rushing points that made him a difference-maker are the thing you are betting on and the knee is the reason not to.
