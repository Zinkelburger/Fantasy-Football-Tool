**Patrick Mahomes** (KC, QB, bye 5) — board rank 107 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Nobody doubts he plays Week 1; the fantasy question is whether a post-ACL pocket passer is worth anything more than a late pick.

- **On track for Week 1 at Denver after the knee injury — a torn ACL, with LCL involvement per one thread — that ended his 2025 season at 6-7.** Rapoport reported on 8/29 that he is on track after a strong training camp, and a 9/4 beat piece by McDowell details a nine-month rehab with no shortcuts. He did not play in the 8/28 preseason game but warmed up wearing no left-knee brace, per beat writer Derrick, and a commenter notes he took every first-team rep in camp. The one substantive caution: he is not quite nine months post-op, with the standard return-to-play mark landing the Friday before the opener, and he enters Week 1 with zero preseason snaps.
- **The role change is the actual fantasy news.** The room expects a pure pocket-passer season: roughly 4.5-5 fantasy points a game came from his legs last year, and one commenter puts 20.3% of his total fantasy points on scrambling alone. Kansas City signed Kenneth Walker specifically to beef up a run game that "threatened nobody", and Bieniemy is back with heavy 12 personnel and designed runs expected. A counter-argument in the same thread is that his scrambles were failed pass plays inflating KC's rush share, and the real split is closer to 70/30 pass.
- Confidence in him is not the issue; confidence in the pocket is. The recurring Chiefs-fan worry is pass protection after the tackle cuts, with backup tackles described as having zero NFL experience. His own preseason declaration — the Chiefs will be "a new version of the Chiefs that goes out there and dominates the same way the old one did" — got "talk is cheap" and "I'll believe it when I see it" from his own fanbase.
- **Market: board rank 107 matches a room that has stopped drafting him.** He is going as QB14, in the 8th to 12th rounds, at pick 155 of one 12-team as a second QB, and undrafted in a 10-team league where the room's read was that this is now normal. A 77-upvote thread says the buy-low window is about to close. Hartitz ranks him behind Goff and Stafford, breaking his year-by-year scoring down to show last season's boom came entirely from the rushing that is not coming back. Twerski will only take him in leagues where he rosters two QBs and plays matchups.
- The bounce-back case is a real-life one: he was the leading MVP candidate at midseason and the offense ranked 10th while he played, better by EPA and DVOA, before scoring around 150 total yards a game without him.

**Draft take:** A fine 10th-round second QB and a bad QB1 bet — the rushing points that made him a fantasy asset are the exact thing the knee takes away.
