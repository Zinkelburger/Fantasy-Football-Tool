**Patrick Mahomes** (KC, QB, bye 5) — board rank 107

**Room sentiment:** Quietly bearish-to-indifferent. 78 mentions, but almost all use him as a reference point for other players (Kenneth Walker, Rashee Rice, rookie QB development) rather than as a draft target.

- **Health is the headline.** Camp report: he "looks on track to play Week 1 following last year's season-ending ACL injury," and is described as fully cleared. Same report cautions: "While Mahomes appears mobile in camp, it's unlikely he's running as much as he was in 2025." One camp roundup notes he "has a clunky brace."
- **He said it himself.** Mahomes is quoted saying he'll limit his running: "I'm obviously going to try and take care of myself a little bit better than I did last season." That matters because rushing was propping up his QB scoring. One Chiefs fan: "Not interested in Mahomes (as a Chiefs fan!), no way he's running this year!"
- **Price is the only argument for him.** Ian Hartitz: "Patrick Mahomes is as cheap as ever." Repeatedly listed as a $1-$3 auction QB or a 9th-rounder alongside Purdy, Herbert, Dart and Goff. ADP 111.
- **Other bear points:** "not a Top-5 QB, probably not a Top-10, and hasn't been for a few seasons," and the offense "was starting to stagnate last season before Mahomes went down."

**Draft take:** A late-round QB2/streamer-priced starter, not a difference-maker. Take him only if you're pairing two cheap QBs.
