**Christian Watson** (GB, WR, bye 11) — board rank 67 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Nobody disputes he is Green Bay's best receiver when he plays; the entire argument is how many weeks that is, and the room has been having it for five years.

- Injury: no current designation. A commenter following Packers camp on Aug 27 says he is far removed from his early-career hamstring problems and is not concerned; another says he has apparently worked on the muscle imbalances behind the soft-tissue history. The room does not buy it — "usually healthy to start seasons and gets hurt once football starts", one expects 6-8 missed games, and Watson, Reed and Kraft missed 28 games combined last season. He is coming off a torn ACL the season before that.
- Role: the Packers' field-stretcher and clear first read — 28.6% first-read target share last year against Matthew Golden's 14.7%, and the man Jordan Love looks for on third down. Romeo Doubs and Dontayvion Wicks both left in free agency without being replaced, vacating 131 targets, and Green Bay signed Watson to a new deal. He averaged 13.2 PPR points per game (WR15 in PPG) and over 60 yards a game last season while working back from the ACL.
- The bear case on role, not health: Tucker Kraft and Golden soak up the vacated targets, Love spreads the ball, and the team still lacks a true WR1 — which is where the room caps this passing game. Per Albert Breer (SI) he is one of the top three receivers alongside Jayden Reed and Golden, but Breer sees Golden as the one with the best shot at breaking through.
- Market: the board has him at 67 and the analysts are consistently ahead of that. Maher has him 42nd overall against a consensus WR27 at 57 — one of three players he would take two rounds early — and calls him the best bet among Packers pass catchers, everything depending on health. Hartitz has him WR23 and names any Green Bay receiver his most confident WR25-48 bet, on a condensed rotation and more passing without Josh Jacobs — that last part only while Jacobs is out. Cooper is in at ADP and says Watson is the Packers receiver he mostly ends up with.
- Sentiment is the loudest negative signal on the page: the most-upvoted comment aimed at any single player in one thread (232 pts) is "Year 5 of this is his year for the Christian Watson truthers", with the rebuttal that he has never finished top-40 in PPR at a WR29-ish price. He was also faded about nine times as part of a blanket no-Packers-receivers position, and the most upvoted answer to a round-7 league-winner pitch calls him the new Will Fuller and is out at any price.

**Draft take:** The analysts are right that he is underpriced at the board's rank and the room is right that he will miss time — take him as a WR3 you can survive losing, not as the WR1 one drafter already regretted.
