**Kyle Pitts** (ATL, TE, bye 11) — board rank 73 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The room's running punchline for misplaced faith, and the pushback is that he already has two top-5 positional finishes and gets more hate than his record warrants.

- Atlanta's quarterback is the live variable and the room contradicts itself on which way it cuts. Pitts owners in one thread wanted Tua rather than Penix, because his Weeks 12-18 breakout came with Tua throwing; Murchison expects peaks and valleys **unless Penix is named the starter**. The only thing both sides agree on is that the situation is unresolved — one commenter ranked him behind Juwan Johnson for Week 1 purely because Atlanta's QB picture is murky, and another fades him at his mid-round price on quarterback grounds, grouped with Harold Fannin.
- Role is otherwise settled and favourable: Atlanta paid him ($17.66M a year) and added no competition for the No. 2 target job, and the bull case leans on Kevin Stefanski being top-6 in TE targets every year as a head coach. The rebuttal is that Njoku's 123 targets in 2023 came because Cleveland had no other receiving options, on offences that finished 32nd and 31st in scoring.
- The most load-bearing caveat is that his production has come when Drake London was hurt — **his target share depends on London being healthy and commanding coverage** — and Bijan Robinson takes targets too. Two commenters flatly will not assume he stays fantasy relevant.
- What he actually did: TE2 in total points last year but only 5th in points per game, 4th among TEs from Weeks 9-17 at 13.3 PPG, on 6-9 targets a game outside one outlier. One commenter re-litigates the season as a Kirk Cousins artifact — a single game against Tampa Bay accounted for 60% of his touchdowns and 20% of his yards — and notes that quarterback is now in Las Vegas.
- Market: the room pays considerably later than the board's rank 73. His ADP band is 65-75, but several report taking him in the 10th or with their last pick and being happy, and one drafter took him in the 7th specifically to stop streaming the position. Cooper names him his favourite early TE and his top target at cost, including as the auction answer over paying up for Bowers or McBride. In dynasty he is the highest-valued name on a buy-low board, acquired below his 5,652 superflex market value in 69% of 253 trades — while one commenter reports trying to sell him all offseason with nobody biting.
- Sentiment is uniformly negative and worth taking at face value given the backtest: four separate commenters named him a player they will never draft again ("Don't draft Kyle Pitts. Ever."), the most-repeated individual fade in a 400-comment thread; another thread's top TE comment calls his situation "horrible".

**Draft take:** Only at the 10th-round price the room actually pays, never at the board's 73 — the Atlanta quarterback question is unresolved and everything good about him is downstream of it.
