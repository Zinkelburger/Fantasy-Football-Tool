**Kyle Pitts** (ATL, TE, bye 11) — board rank 73 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Coming off his best season and paid for it, and the room's default posture is still ridicule.

- Role is the strongest part of his case: Atlanta extended him and added no competition for the No. 2 target role behind Drake London, and Kevin Stefanski has been top-6 in TE targets every year as a head coach. The condition attached every time it comes up is that most of last year's production came with London out — the bull case needs London healthy and Pitts still second in line.
- Quarterback is the swing and the room calls the situation murky. Murchison expects a peaks-and-valleys play unless Michael Penix is named the starter; Pitts owners wanted Tua, since the weeks 12-18 breakout came with Tua throwing. A separate commenter re-litigates 2025 as a Cousins artifact — one Tampa Bay game accounting for 60% of his touchdowns and 20% of his yards — with Cousins now in Las Vegas. The two accounts disagree on which passer drove it but agree that passer is not the one throwing to him now. One ranker put him behind Juwan Johnson for Week 1 on the QB murk alone.
- The Stefanski-loves-tight-ends argument gets pushback: Njoku's 123 targets in 2023 came because Cleveland had no other receiving options, on offences that finished 32nd and 31st in scoring. Commenters also describe Pitts, at $17.66M a year, as a big-slot receiver rather than a true tight end who blocks.
- Market: he was TE2 last year but only 5th in points per game, at 6-9 targets a game outside one outlier, and several report drafting him in the 10th or with their last pick and being fine with it — later than board rank 73 asks. Cooper is the loud exception: Pitts is his favourite early-draft TE and his top TE target at cost, including as the auction answer over paying up for Bowers or McBride. In dynasty he is the highest-valued name on the buy-low board, acquired below his 5,652 superflex value in 69% of 253 trades, and one owner reports trying to sell him all offseason with nobody biting.
- Sentiment is settled and negative — the top TE comment calls his situation "horrible," and he is the running punchline in a thread about takes gone wrong (six top-level mentions, +6 net, nobody making a case). The pushback notes he already has two top-5 finishes and gets more hate than his production warrants.

**Draft take:** Fine at the 10th-round price the room actually charges, wrong at board rank — anything above that is a bet on Atlanta naming a quarterback who throws to him.
