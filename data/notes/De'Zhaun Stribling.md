**De'Zhaun Stribling** (SF, WR, bye 8) — board rank 219 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The hype story of the week, and the most careful people in the thread think the report everyone is celebrating actually caps him.

- Healthy. His only injury note is a return to full participation in 49ers camp on 2026-08-09 after one limited practice, per 49ers.com's Brooke Evans, who had him "comfortable back in the offense".
- The sourced role claim is Matt Barrows of The Athletic (2026-09-03): the plan is for Stribling to be San Francisco's primary slot receiver and Mike Evans' understudy at X. Camp reports through August had him at or near the 49ers' WR1 and Brock Purdy's first look, "constantly open" relative to the other receivers.
- The best pushback is scheme math, not talent. San Francisco was near the bottom of the league in 11 personnel — 43% of plays, against 37% 21 personnel — which is roughly 28 snaps a game with a slot receiver on the field, so a pure slot role could mean under 50% of snaps. Behind CMC, Kittle and Evans and competing with Deebo, he is closer to a fifth option; one commenter says he should not be in a starting lineup while Evans is healthy, and Vegas props say the same for Week 1.
- What keeps him on the field is blocking. Shanahan reportedly wanted him for his run blocking off his college tape, which argues for heavy personnel snaps rather than three-WR sets only, and the room notes Shanahan benches young receivers who cannot block.- The bull case underneath the noise is an injury bet on the men in front of him — Evans banged up, Kittle ramping back from an Achilles — with several expecting him starting by Week 3-6. Twerski calls him one of the players he is irrationally confident in largely because every San Francisco skill player gets hurt.
- Market vs the board: this is the gap that matters. The board has him at 219; the room does not remotely pay that. FFPC ADP over the last three days is 83 — late sixth in a 12-team — reported picks have collapsed from the 13th round to the 8th in days, one auction went $9, and people are now discussing him in the 5th/6th, which a commenter calls "unequivocally BONKERS". Maher has him 65th overall / WR35 against a consensus of 120 / WR49, his single biggest WR disagreement inside the top 100.
- Sentiment is a runaway: a 1,461-point "this dude is going to eat", "I spent all my money at the strib club". The pushback is specific — victory laps before a snap, the sub does this to a random every year, and the article everyone is quoting describes a limited role.

**Draft take:** He is no longer a sleeper at a sixth-round price — draft him in the 8th or later where the slot-snap math still leaves room for profit, and pass in the 5th or 6th.
