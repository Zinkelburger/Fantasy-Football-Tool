**De'Von Achane** (MIA, RB, bye 6) — board rank 10 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Split down the middle on the offense around him, not on him — he was the second-most-nominated bust of the preseason, and the case against is entirely Miami.

- No injury reported. The only health mention is a general risk framing — commenters prefer Rice in a straight swap on durability grounds — with no specific issue behind it.
- The role is as concentrated as it gets: Dolphins fans say nobody else at running back did anything all camp and preseason, the room calls him the only piece of the Miami offense worth caring about, and the highest-voted answer to who leads Miami in receiving is "Devon Achane is wr1" at 93 points. He is signed long term as the bellcow.
- The live argument is his receiving work, and it is the whole valuation. Bears say Malik Willis scrambles rather than throwing the checkdown, that stacked boxes with no receiving threats suppress the rushing, and that a healthy Achane might not finish top-20 — ceiling high, floor "in the basement." Bulls say his receptions were designed screens rather than dump-offs, that Bobby Slowik, last year's passing-game coordinator, keeps deploying him as a route-runner from the backfield, and that Waddle's vacated targets flow to him. One bear cites Vegas props implying 58 receptions against last year's 67.
- The analysts split the same way. Dopp is still in but calls the risk much higher than last season — new OC, mobile QB, stacked boxes — with the receiving game still a cheat code. Loza named him her RB bust of the year. Hartitz flags the offense but notes the backs who overcame bad offenses were exactly the big-time pass-catchers Achane is. A Ringer host is lower than most while having no issue taking him over Bowers.
- The bear case has a number behind it worth taking seriously: one Fantasy Points ranker calls it 100% likely Miami has a bottom-5 scoring offense or under five wins, where the hit rate for a top-6 RB finish is very low. The rebuttal is his own record — Miami was 25th in scoring last year and he still posted 20.3 PPG, having led the NFL in yards per carry in two of three seasons.
- Sentiment is loudly negative and the room knows it: 18 top-level bust nominations for roughly 431 upvotes, 17 top-level fades in another thread. The top reply (385 points) is that he is the player most likely to be talked down all offseason and then score 30 in Week 1. Several add that his fantasy playoff schedule — Packers, Chargers, Bills — is brutal.

**Draft take:** He is worth the top-10 pick because the target share is real and unavoidable, but plan your playoff weeks around him rather than through him.
