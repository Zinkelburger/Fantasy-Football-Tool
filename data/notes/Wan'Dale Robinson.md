**Wan'Dale Robinson** (TEN, WR, bye 9) — board rank 97 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the one Titan this room will draft, and the argument is entirely about format — a PPR floor with no red-zone equity.

- Role is the slot, confirmed by the 9/03 news post, with a commenter backing it at a 68% slot route rate last season and 78% the year before, on 140 targets each year. A Titans fan relaying the beat writers says they constantly call him Cam Ward's security blanket and expects heavy volume behind a bad offensive line, with Ward hunting easy dump-offs rather than hero throws.
- The room's own case (4 top-level nominations, +273): chemistry with Ward — maybe the only one on the team who has it — and manufactured touches in space, so he does not depend on Ward's arm. The recurring caveat is that he only works in PPR and is a "scam merchant."
- The detailed structural case is Daboll's scheme feeding volume to undersized slot receivers: across Daboll's four Giants years the reception leaders were Richie James, Robinson, Nabers, Robinson, and Robinson had twice the targets of anyone but Nabers. Cited at a 28% target share with no proven target competition in Tennessee — but that last part only holds while Carnell Tate is eased in rather than taking share early.
- The bear case on usage, from 9/02: reports out of Tennessee have them in a ton of 12 personnel with Robinson off the field unless they go three-wide, and Calvin Ridley ahead of him on money. A Titans fan adds the room has never supported two fantasy receivers. Cooper lists him among the part-time slot players who could grow into a full-time role, the mold that produced JSN and CeeDee Lamb.
- Market: board rank 97 and the room agrees it is too cheap — he keeps falling in mocks, went 11.07 in one 12-team draft, and is called a late-round must-draft and "value flex." Two nominations at 54 upvotes value him around WR45 after a WR14 finish last year and say the truth is in between. Twerski names him a favorite last-round or early-waiver PPR pickup. The dissent: Hartitz would drop him for Terrance Ferguson in a 10-teamer, since Ferguson's value can skyrocket off one week and Robinson's cannot, and one manager plans to flip him for draft capital while Tate learns the offense.
- Sentiment is strongly positive with a vocal minority: "this sub doesn't appreciate Wan'Dale, therefore top-15 WR" sits at 371 points; the skeptic calling him receptions with no YAC is at -19 and was rebutted with 38% of his yards from YAC and 90+ catches in each of the last two seasons. Three predictions have him top-20 at the position and at least 20 spots above Tate.

**Draft take:** a full-PPR value at his price and close to unstartable in standard — draft him in PPR, fade him elsewhere, and sell if Tate takes the target share.
