**Lamar Jackson** (BAL, QB, bye 13) — board rank 33 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Ravens fans are bullish on a rebound but deliberately guarded — "After last season, I will not allow myself to be hyped up" — and the fade case is age, not talent.

- No injury report of any kind in the corpus. The only health claim is a fan on 2026-08-29 describing him as healthy alongside a healthy Derrick Henry and defense; no practice report either way.
- **New coordinator Declan Doyle is the story, and there is real reporting under it.** Jeremy Fowler (ESPN, 8/11) said Doyle's offense wants chunk plays, and that Jackson worked from under center often and threw downfield aggressively, sometimes off play action. Commenters add that Lamar is at his best from under center, so the room reads the scheme change as a fit rather than a hurdle, and notes his best seasons have come in a first year with a new OC.
- The team is saying the same thing, which is worth exactly what team talk is worth. Doyle said publicly (9/3) he has "really enjoyed working with" Lamar; Jackson said on the record the same day that defenses "aren't prepared" for what the new offense has in store; DC Anthony Weaver, asked on the team podcast how Lamar has taken to Doyle versus Monken and Roman, said he fits within any system and he expects him to thrive. The room discounted all of it as what any franchise says about a $260M quarterback and treats the quotes as lip service until it shows up on the field.
- The mechanism the room agrees on: his rushing and passing feed each other. When the scramble threat was reduced last year the Baltimore line got overwhelmed — his production is tied to being able to run, not to volume.
- Market: Maher has him bunched with Drake Maye and Jalen Hurts in the 75-79 range against an ECR of 36, part of a thesis that quarterbacks are the most over-drafted asset in 2026. That is the only price claim on him and it sits well behind his board rank. Loza is on the other side, taking him over Joe Burrow in round 5 on rushing upside and schedule.
- Sentiment splits cleanly: three "too low" nominations (+38) on last season being an outlier and reports having him healthy, against ten top-level fades (+11) arguing his value has always come from rushing, that he is approaching 30, and that this may be the start of a slow decline. The Ravens room separately and forcefully rejects both the "he's just a runner" and "hard to coach" framings.

**Draft take:** buy the scheme change, not a first-year-OC premium — the rushing-decline fade is the only argument on him with content, so take him where the market's QB discount puts him rather than at his board rank.
