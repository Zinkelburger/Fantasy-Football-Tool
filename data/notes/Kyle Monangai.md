**Kyle Monangai** (CHI, RB, bye 10) — board rank 71 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A handcuff who is himself hurt — the room spent two weeks pricing him as the Bears backfield's second real asset and then noticed he may not be available to be one.

- Availability, his own, is the first problem. He left Bears practice on Aug 16 with a knee injury — commenters say Grady Jarrett tackled him in a no-pads drill where tackling was not allowed. Bears beat reporter Pearson ruled him week-to-week on Sept 1, a longer timeline than the day-to-day tags two defensive teammates got the same day. The in-room description is a hyperextension with no ligament damage, no IR, and side work the day before Sept 3. One commenter says a possible small fracture is being downplayed and could affect him all year — that is a single commenter with no source. As of Sept 4 he is still listed as hurt with no timeline or designation. Several say the handcuff bid on him is unsafe because he may not play Week 1.
- Availability, D'Andre Swift's, is what the pick is actually about. Monangai has no standalone role: he is the direct Chicago handcuff and nothing else, and the room proved it on Sept 4 — his value spiked for the hours Swift's status was unknown and collapsed when Schefter called Swift's practice exit cramping. Swift is fine. Roschon Johnson is the competing contingency bet, and one commenter is holding only until the Monangai-vs-Johnson pecking order is settled.
- What the role would be if it came: Cooper calls him one of the best "handcuff plus" options available, on the grounds that Chicago leans on whoever is healthier and Monangai out-snapped Swift in one game last year when Swift was returning from injury. One commenter's frame if Swift misses time is "rookie season Saquon Barkley Monangai coming up". Others pitch drafting the pair on the theory that Ben Johnson's backfield as a whole is a championship asset.
- Market: he was the cheaper way into the Bears backfield that several commenters had been targeting all offseason instead of Swift, and one names him with Blake Corum as barely even a handcuff because the role is that real. In dynasty, a 7th-round NFL pick now valued as an early second on KTC — one commenter expects Chicago to add another back and suggests midseason as the sell point.

**Draft take:** The board's rank of 71 prices a healthy handcuff; he is week-to-week with a hyperextended knee and Swift is fine, so let someone else pay and take him a round or two later.
