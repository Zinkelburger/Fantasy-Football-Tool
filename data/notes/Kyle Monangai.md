**Kyle Monangai** (CHI, RB, bye 10) — board rank 71 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** a handcuff who may not be available for the injury he exists to cover.

- He is hurt, and the timeline is soft. He left Bears practice on 2026-08-16 with a knee injury — commenters say Grady Jarrett tackled him in a no-pads drill where tackling was not allowed — and was ruled week-to-week on 09-01 by Bears beat reporter Pearson, a longer tag than the day-to-day given to two defensive teammates the same day. The room's read on 09-03 is a hyperextension with no ligament damage about three weeks earlier, no IR, and side work the day before; one commenter reads it as needing about a week of rest and Hartitz agrees. He was still described as banged up on 09-04. The board rank of 71 knows none of this.
- His status is moving the market on his own starter. Commenters say the books had posted no Week 1 line for D'Andre Swift because Monangai's availability was unclear, and one credits Monangai's under-discussed injury directly for Swift's projection climbing.
- Role is contingent and nothing else. He is the direct Chicago handcuff, with Roschon Johnson the competing bet — his value spiked for the hours Swift's practice exit on 09-04 was unexplained and collapsed when Schefter called it cramping. Cooper calls him one of the best "handcuff plus" options: Chicago leans on whoever is healthier, and Monangai out-snapped Swift in a game last season when Swift was returning from injury. Several commenters said plainly during that scare that the handcuff bid was unsafe because Monangai may not play either.
- Market: he was the cheaper way into the Bears backfield that several commenters had been targeting all offseason instead of Swift, named with Blake Corum as barely even a handcuff because the role is real. In dynasty he is a 7th-round NFL pick valued as an early 2nd on KTC, with one commenter expecting Chicago to add another back and suggesting midseason as the sell point.

**Draft take:** draft him as the Swift handcuff and nothing more, and only once he is practicing — a week-to-week knee is not a bench spot worth holding through September.
