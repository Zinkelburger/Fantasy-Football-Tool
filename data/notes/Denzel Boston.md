**Denzel Boston** (CLE, WR, bye 11) — board rank 169 · as of 2026-09-04

- Camp, per beat report: Ben Solak of ESPN (8/11) said watching Boston move made it immediately evident he has sufficient speed and that everything else looks as promised — and that the last time he left a camp feeling that way about a rookie pass catcher was Sam LaPorta at 2023 Lions camp. - Role: as of Aug 21 he is second in the Browns' receiver pecking order behind KC Concepcion on preseason usage — conditional on Concepcion holding the WR1 job — though one commenter argues the gap is negligible since the two were drafted within 15 picks of each other. A Sept 2 projection has him in a Cedric Tillman-type role, a better version, with Jerry Jeudy moving to Z; a Browns fan tempers it, saying the rookie receivers are talented but need a year to adjust to NFL speed and physicality behind a bottom-five offensive line. The newest read (9/4) is that he is explicitly not a taxi stash because he is already a top-three receiver on that depth chart.
- The ceiling argument is a quarterback argument, and the room has mostly settled it: a 169-point comment says he only thrives once Cleveland has an actual quarterback, i.e. 2027, and another calls him a keeper for next year rather than a piece for this one. His own owner's best case is a Tillman-style peak — heavy hype, a nice three-week stretch, then nothing.
- Market is dynasty rookie drafts, not redraft. He goes at the 1.08-2.04 range: one manager took Ty Simpson at 1.08 and got Boston at 2.01 calling him his next-best option, another took Coleman at 1.09 over him, and he consistently goes ahead of De'Zhaun Stribling. Maher names him a favorite late-round flier at WR58; Hartitz picks him as the best long-term add over Tre Tucker, Khalil Shakir and Caleb Douglas on the chance he becomes an outright alpha, and says an equal Week 1 line from all of them leaves Boston with the most trade value. The thread's film evaluator keeps him tiers above Stribling even after Stribling's camp hype.

**Draft take:** the deep-bench receiver to take if you take one — a named ESPN camp comp and a top-three depth-chart spot beat everything else at board rank 169, but the payoff year is 2027.
