**Matthew Golden** (GB, WR, bye 11) — board rank 157 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The clearest split in the Packers receiver room — a real vacated-snap case against a bust-rate argument the sub loudly rejected.

- Injury: nothing current. Mike Spofford (Packers.com) reported Golden (toe) did not practise on Aug 9; that is the only injury claim on him and it has not recurred.
- Role: with Romeo Doubs and Dontayvion Wicks both gone, commenters have him as Green Bay's full-time boundary X in two-WR sets, citing 72% two-wide usage as a rookie and 1,240 vacated snaps, and say he is on the field over Jayden Reed in two-receiver looks while Reed stays a pure slot. An Aug 27 camp report has him looking really good and taking over Doubs' former high-volume role. Per Albert Breer (SI), of the Packers' top three receivers — Christian Watson, Reed and Golden — Golden has the best shot at breaking through, with elite hands and downfield speed.
- The condition on all of it is the two men ahead of him. Skeptics say he is still the third option behind Watson and Tucker Kraft, and back it with first-read target share: 14.7% for Golden against Watson's 28.6%. Cooper expects a part-time field-stretcher — a mystery-box bench pick he does not want near a starting lineup early. The room's standing caveat is that all Green Bay rookie receivers start slow, Davante Adams and Jordy Nelson included, so the discount should be temporary. Blunt dissent exists too: "Golden's big problem is that he sucks."
- Market: the board has him at 157 and the room is taking him earlier than that — rounds 10-11, WR31-40 at his highest, $2 in one auction, and ahead of Josh Downs, DK Metcalf and Jaylen Warren in a dynasty startup. Maher has him 85th overall against a consensus of 123 and calls him a late-round dart in an offense consensus treats as an unrankable committee; Sanderson names him his biggest ADP return among WR25-48, a low floor with a ceiling worth attacking; Twerski calls him a favourite last-round or early-waiver pickup. Hartitz is the moderate at WR41.
- Sentiment: an analyst piece argues he is a likely bust — of 101 Day 1-2 receivers since 2011 with under 525 rookie receiving yards, 85 never had a 12 ppg season, and he adds that 4.31-or-faster combine receivers bust 95% of the time; Golden ran 4.29 with 361 yards. The room rejected it at 558 upvotes as cherry-picked, with the top replies (235, 209, 157 pts) noting his best game came in the playoffs, Doubs and Wicks are gone, and Green Bay has always brought rookie receivers along slowly.

**Draft take:** Take him in the 10th-11th where the room is paying, not earlier — the vacated snaps are real but he is the third read behind Watson and Kraft, which is a bench profile until something changes.
