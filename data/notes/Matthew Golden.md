**Matthew Golden** (GB, WR, bye 11) — board rank 157 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the cheapest way into the Green Bay passing game, and the room defended him at 558 upvotes when an analyst called him a bust.

- Health: the only report is Mike Spofford (Packers.com) having Golden (toe) out of practice on 2026-08-09. Nothing since.
- Role is the reason to be interested. With Romeo Doubs and Dontayvion Wicks gone and Jayden Reed a pure slot player, commenters have Golden as the full-time boundary X in two-WR sets, citing 72% two-wide usage as a rookie and 1,240 vacated snaps; a camp report on 08-27 has him looking really good and taking over Doubs's high-volume role. Albert Breer (SI, 08-11) picked him out of the Watson/Reed/Golden trio as the one with the best shot at breaking through, on elite hands and downfield speed. The check on all of it: he is still third in line behind Christian Watson and Tucker Kraft, and had a 14.7% first-read target share to Watson's 28.6%. Cooper wants him only as a bench mystery box — a part-time field-stretcher, not a starter early.
- The bust case was published and rejected. An analyst article argued that of 101 Day 1-2 receivers since 2011 under 525 rookie receiving yards, 85 never had a 12-ppg season, and that combine receivers at 4.31 or faster bust 95% of the time — Golden ran 4.29 with 361 yards. The room's rebuttal, at 558 upvotes with top replies at 235, 209 and 157 points, is that the sample is cherry-picked and context-free: his best game came in the playoffs, Doubs and Wicks are gone, and Green Bay has always brought rookie receivers along slowly (Nelson, Cobb and Adams all under that threshold).
- Market: 10th-11th round, WR31-40 at his highest, with drafters reporting $2 in an auction and a near-last-pick fall in a 10-team Yahoo league — cheap enough that missing costs nothing, which is the room's whole framing. Maher has him 85th overall against a consensus of 123 and calls him a nice late dart in an offense consensus treats as an unrankable committee; Twerski names him a favorite last-round or early-waiver pickup; Hartitz has him WR41 as the Green Bay fallback behind Watson and Reed.

**Draft take:** last-round dart, and the right one of the cheap Packers receivers if you believe the two-WR-set snaps are actually his.
