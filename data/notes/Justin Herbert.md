**Justin Herbert** (LAC, QB, bye 7) — board rank 88 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** a top-6 quarterback the room takes in round 8 or 9, faded only for reasons that are not about football.

- Kris Rhim from camp (8/9): Herbert "continues to be sharp and on target with his receivers" and looks comfortable with the timing of the new Chargers offense, with receivers getting the ball early and in space. Buyers cite that scheme as reliably manufacturing big games for him.
- The one substantive reservation is the offensive line: a commenter asks whether the starting center has already gone down and says last year's line problems put him off entirely. Nothing in the claims confirms or resolves that injury question.
- He is 28, with almost 500 rushing yards and 2 rushing TDs last year, and one of four quarterbacks with five-plus 50-yard pass attempts last season. Vegas Week 1 full-PPR projections have him as the QB1 at 19.5 points.
- Market: ADP 72.8, board rank 88, and the room's most consistent 8/9-turn target — real picks reported at the 7th, 8th, 9th and 12th rounds and at 116 overall in a 10-man, with one manager who punted the position entirely landing him in the 12th of a 12-team double-flex. He went 7.3 on one 14-team board. One commenter plants a flag on a career year and a rise to top-3 dynasty QB; the pushback is that the breakout is already priced in and top-3 for a pocket passer is a stretch.
- Sentiment: four nominations for 11 upvotes, one predicting a top-6 finish and another citing 10-1 MVP odds as evidence he is mispriced. He was faded about four times in one thread and every stated reason was non-football — his girlfriend, one bad Week 11 against Jacksonville — with no usage or scheme argument offered against him anywhere in it. A commenter expecting a top-5 Chargers offense is drafting only Herbert, Quentin Johnston and Mitchell off it, calling the tight end room too ambiguous to pay for.

**Draft take:** take him at the 8/9 turn as your only quarterback — the fades in this room are personal, not analytical, and the offensive line is the only thing worth watching.
