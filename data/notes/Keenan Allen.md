**Keenan Allen** (IND, WR, bye 13) — board rank 256 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A short-window volume play whose real risk is legal, not physical, and the room mostly expects the legal risk to land after the season.

- **Availability is a discipline question, not a health one.** Allen was arrested overnight on 8/30 in Indianapolis for driving while intoxicated — Stephen Holder, citing Marion County sheriff's records, reported the arrest and a Sept. 16 court date — and was charged the same day with two misdemeanours, DUI at 0.08-or-above and DUI endangering a person. The Colts said only that they are aware and gathering information. On 9/01 the GM said Allen will play Week 1.
- **No suspension announced, and the room does not expect one this season.** Consensus (54 upvotes) is that the conduct policy's three-game first-offense DUI penalty follows a conviction or plea, not an arrest, and that his lawyer pushes proceedings past the season — precedent cited is Jordan Addison's three-game ban served a full year after his arrest, and beat writer Nathan Brown notes last year's three comparable cases took 9+ months. All conditional on the Sept. 16 outcome; several commenters, noting he was found in a parked, non-running car, expect dismissal.
- **Role: the short-area/slot target in a thin room, on a one-year deal.** Jordan Schultz reported the signing at up to $8.32M, reuniting him with Shane Steichen, and the room reads him into the underneath and red-zone work Michael Pittman vacated — "he won't lead your team in yards, but he'll be the reason Pierce does" (114 pts). He made the 53 on 8/31; top comment, 95 points: "Why Indy kept Keenan Allen: have you seen our WR room?"
- **The volume case is conditional and shrinking.** The target-share reflex was built on Alec Pierce (PUP), Josh Downs (calf) and Tyler Warren (groin) all being limited. Maher on 9/03 has him WR76, eight spots below ECR, flagging that Pierce is already back to full practice and calling him a short-term volume bet rather than a season-long asset. A commenter notes he missed all of camp and had one practice into a new route tree, capping him early regardless of discipline.
- **Market:** a final-round stash or waiver add, and the room thinks that price sits below his opening-weeks role — a few argue top-24 WR upside at that cost. Board rank 256 and the room's late-round talk agree; nobody is paying up.
- **Sentiment:** Colts fans are disgusted with the arrest while assuming it costs him nothing on the field. A preseason clip drew "slow as is," countered by the view that nobody else on the roster catches that ball.

**Draft take:** Last-round flier only — the early target volume is real while Downs is limited, but Pierce's return shrinks it and a Sept. 16 court date means you are also holding a suspension you cannot schedule.
