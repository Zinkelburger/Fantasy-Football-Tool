**Keenan Allen** (IND, WR, bye 13) — board rank 256 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A DUI arrest that the room expects to cost him nothing this season, and a target role that only exists while the receivers ahead of him are hurt.

- **Arrested overnight on 8/30 in Indianapolis and formally charged the same day with two misdemeanors — DUI with BAC 0.08 or above, and DUI endangering a person.** Stephen Holder, citing Marion County sheriff's records, reported the arrest and a 3:49pm release, with a court date set for Sept 16. He was found in the driver's seat of a parked, non-running car. The Colts' statement said only that they are aware and gathering information. One commenter calls it a felony charge; the beat reporting says two misdemeanors, so treat the felony framing as wrong.
- **The room's read (54 upvotes) is that he misses no games this season.** The NFL's conduct policy sets a three-game suspension for a first-offense DUI, but discipline follows a conviction or plea rather than an arrest, and comparable cases last year took nine-plus months — Jordan Addison's three-game ban was served a full year after his arrest. The Colts GM said on 9/1 that Allen will play Week 1. His one-year deal expires after this season, which is likely before any suspension lands.
- **His role is entirely a function of who else is hurt.** He signed 8/18 on a one-year deal worth up to $8.32M (Schultz), reuniting with Shane Steichen, and made the 53 — the top comment on why is "have you seen our WR room?" (95 points). With Alec Pierce on PUP, Josh Downs's calf and Tyler Warren's groin, the room's reflex was that Allen soaks up the short-area targets by default. That window is closing: Maher noted on 9/3 that Pierce has already returned to full practice, has Allen at WR76 — eight spots below ECR — and calls him a short-term volume bet usable only while Downs is limited. A 9/3 commenter calls him expendable now that the room around him is healthy.
- What he is: a 34-year-old slot/underneath separator stepping into roughly the role Michael Pittman vacated, projected as a chain-mover at around 700 yards and the third or fourth option. "He won't lead your team in yards, but he'll be the reason Pierce does" (114 points). He led the Chargers in receptions last year at 81/122/777/4. He also missed all of training camp and had one practice before the arrest, so timing and conditioning limit him early regardless.
- Market: at a board rank of 256 he is a final-round pick or a waiver add, and that is where the room puts him — several say they will spend a last pick as insurance behind an injured Colt.

**Draft take:** A final-round stash or a waiver add only, and one whose value shrinks every time an Indianapolis receiver returns to full practice.
