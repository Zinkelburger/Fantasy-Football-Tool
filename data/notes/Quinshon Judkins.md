**Quinshon Judkins** (CLE, RB, bye 11) — board rank 42

**Room sentiment:** Almost nobody disputes the talent or the volume; the argument, across 154 mentions, is entirely about the Browns and the leg.

- **Rookie usage is the bull case:** 18.3 touches per game (11th-most among all players) and 16.4 carries per game (8th-most), which posters extrapolate to 300+ touches over 17 games. He was RB13 from Weeks 2-7 with no training camp. The dead-zone thread calls him "the clearest workhorse of the group" at RB23.
- **Concrete camp news:** full go after the season-ending leg injury, "off to a fast start," and RB coach Deuce Staley says he "can play all three downs." That matters because he played only 18% of third-down snaps last year — though he still ran 135 routes and caught 26 balls.
- **The bear case:** 3.6 YPC behind stacked boxes, near-consensus that the Browns will be bad and playing from behind (Myles Garrett gone), and Dylan Sampson as passing-down competition — the two split targets exactly 26/26 in their 13 shared games. A high-scoring "do not draft" post fades him purely on the injury.
- **What's actually changed:** new OC Todd Monken (described as run-heavy), a rebuilt offensive line including LT Spencer Fano, and added WR talent. Bears counter that five linemen who've never played together isn't an upgrade yet.
- **Price:** ADP ~43-46, near Skattebo, Montgomery and Tuten.

**Draft take:** A 4th/5th-round volume bet where you are explicitly accepting bad game script. Fine as an RB2 at cost; if the third-down role reports hold up through preseason, he's the best value in that tier.
