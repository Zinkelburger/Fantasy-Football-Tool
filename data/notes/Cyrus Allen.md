**Cyrus Allen** (KC, WR, bye 5) — board rank 262 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The Chiefs' camp-hype pick of the year, and the sub is openly aware that is exactly what it is.

- Healthy. He picked up a shin/practice injury in early August at the same time as Xavier Worthy and returned a few days sooner; Charles Goldman (A to Z Sports) had him back as an apparent full participant on 2026-08-11.
- The camp reporting is real, not just fan noise. Per Pete Sweeney (KC Star), Mahomes' first two completions of an early team blitz period both went to Allen, including a contested back-shoulder grab isolated one-on-one; Goldman says he seems to make strides every day while noting fans have been burned by camp hype before. A commenter relays praise from Andy Reid and Mahomes, and another says he was reportedly Mahomes' favourite target throughout camp.
- The role argument is complement, not displacement: the room calls him Kansas City's only true slot receiver and a man-beating deep threat, where Rashee Rice is a zone-beater — so he could pass Xavier Worthy without needing to pass Rice. Steve Smith Sr has twice said Allen will be Kansas City's WR1 before long, and the room treats that as evidence against Smith rather than for Allen.
- The bear case is specific and comes from someone reading practice reports: Allen is not dominating camp — Rice and Kelce still take more targets in 7-on-7 and 11-on-11 — the hype is inflated because Rice and Worthy were sitting and he had to play WR1 in practice, and he opens the season third or fourth behind Rice, Worthy and Dont'e Thornton while fighting Jalen Royals for WR4. The broader room read is that he is mostly irrelevant for 2026 redraft and one of the more dropped players after Week 1, since Andy Reid rarely uses rookie WRs in year one.
- Market vs the board: the board has him 262 and redraft agrees — a last-rounds lottery ticket that only makes sense on a deep bench, and Twerski's pick for best final bench stash over Tre Tucker, George Holani and Chris Bell. One practical objection: Kansas City plays Monday night in Week 1, so you cannot see his usage before you would have to drop him. Dynasty is a different market entirely — pre-hype he went in rookie fourths and fifths or for $2-7 FAAB; since, one owner spent 51% of his season FAAB, and he is valued at a late 2nd on KTC. A 20-upvote comment says sell one share now, on the reasoning that there is still a 70-80% chance he is never more than a bye-week dart.

**Draft take:** Worth a final redraft pick if your bench is deep, but in dynasty he is a sell at a late-2nd price, not a buy.
