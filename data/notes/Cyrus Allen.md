**Cyrus Allen** (KC, WR, bye 5) — board rank 262 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The loudest camp-hype name in the corpus, and self-aware about it — the dynasty price has run miles ahead of a depth-chart role the room still argues is WR4.

- Injury: picked up a shin issue in practice around Aug 1 and was back sooner than Xavier Worthy; per Charles Goldman (A to Z Sports) he was a full participant again on Aug 11. Nothing since.
- The camp reports are real. Per Pete Sweeney (KC Star), Mahomes' first two completions of the Aug 11 team blitz period both went to Allen, including a contested back-shoulder grab isolated 1-on-1; Goldman wrote it "seems like Allen makes strides every day" while noting fans have been burned by KC camp hype before. Matt Harmon charts him as the best man-coverage beater on the roster, and the room's structural case is that he is the Chiefs' only true slot receiver, complementing Rashee Rice rather than competing with him.
- The bear case is the depth chart, and it is the better-sourced side. He is fighting for WR4 behind Rice, Worthy and Dont'e Thornton, only while both Rice and Worthy return healthy — his practice reps have been inflated precisely because they were sitting. A commenter who reads the actual practice reports says Rice and Travis Kelce still take more 7-on-7 and 11-on-11 targets and that Kansas City did not draft a receiver until the fifth round, so the staff does not share Reddit's view. Later single-commenter camp reports (Sept 2-4) say Mahomes loves him and expects him up from WR3, citing Rice's legal situation.
- Steve Smith Sr has twice said Allen will be Kansas City's WR1 — the room treats that as evidence against Smith, not for Allen, given his ranking record.
- Market: the cost exploded on the hype. Pre-hype he went in dynasty rookie fourths and fifths or for $2-$7 of FAAB; since, one owner spent 51% of a season's FAAB, another traded a 2027 fourth to take him, and he is valued at a late second on KTC. The 20-upvote sell-high line is that there is still a 70-80% chance he is never more than a bye-week dart throw. Practical redraft note: Kansas City plays Monday night in Week 1, so he cannot be your last pick ahead of a kicker — you never see the usage before you'd need the spot.
- Sentiment: the most-nominated "last pick who wins your league" name in one thread (15 top-level comments, 53 upvotes), but the redraft consensus is that he is mostly irrelevant for 2026 because Andy Reid rarely uses rookie receivers in year one. The dissent notes rookie Rashee Rice earned a role over the final 10 games at a 1200/12 pace.

**Draft take:** Final-bench dynasty stash only — at WR4 on the depth chart with Rice and Worthy healthy, the price has already outrun the evidence, and a redraft roster cannot afford to hold him through a Monday-night Week 1.
