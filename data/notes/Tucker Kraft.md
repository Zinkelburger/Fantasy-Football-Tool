**Tucker Kraft** (GB, TE, bye 11) — board rank 77 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The ACL return has gone about as well as one can, and the only live question is whether Green Bay caps his snaps early anyway.

- **Coming back from an ACL tear and, per Packers beat reporters, on a normal timeline.** Rob Demovsky and others said in mid-August that he looks like a player who was never hurt and should be fully cleared for Week 1; that day he ran first-team 11-on-11 for the first time and finished 5 catches on 5 targets with a TD. He was practising without full contact in early August and took part in joint 11-on-11s in late August.
- **The snap-count worry is Kraft's own words, not a team decision.** On the record 8/16: "They're going to take into account my rep range probably until halfway through the season, and then they're going to just pat me on the ass and send me out there" — while saying in the next breath that he hopes not to be on a rep count in Week 1 and that the only thing holding him back is Gutekunst trying to slow him down. A later, paraphrased Kraft quote saying he is done with ramp-up talk and good to go was relayed on 9/2 but nobody produced the link, so treat it as unconfirmed. The best-supported read is the 695-point reply: any limit is nominal and he plays around 85% anyway, since Packers limits on Christian Watson and Micah Parsons lasted about a game. A commenter's 3-4 week snap limit is the honest downside.
- The depth chart forces him onto the field regardless: TE2 Luke Musgrave is out long term with a neck injury and the TE3 saw six targets all last season, so Jonnu Smith was signed as depth rather than as a threat. He plays near the goal line and on third down.
- His unresolved extension is not an availability risk — he said on camera it will not affect whether he plays and that he will not hold out. The room reads the delay as guarantee structure, with a dissenter arguing only the front office knows the knee's real condition.
- Role: arguably the best receiver on his team, which is the profile you want at the position. He was 2nd in TE fantasy points per game before the injury, a YAC weapon and Jordan Love's underneath option, with Doubs and Wicks gone. The bear case in the same thread: only 5.5 targets a game with 6 TDs in 8 games on a team 28th in pass rate, and Cooper's read that his deployment leans on play-action and screens — a Kittle profile, not a Kelce one, which costs him in PPR.
- **Market: board rank 77 is behind the room.** Reported prices are the 6th to 8th round, with "Kraft isn't going as TE9 anymore, more like 5 or 6" at 8 upvotes and drafters saying the round-9 tier is stale; auction reports run $2-4, occasionally $6. The room mostly defends the 6th-8th as the highest-ceiling non-elite tight end behind Bowers, McBride and Loveland.

**Draft take:** Take him in the 6th or 7th as the last tight end worth a real pick, and plan on a light first month rather than a lost one.
