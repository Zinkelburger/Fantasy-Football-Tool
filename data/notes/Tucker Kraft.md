**Tucker Kraft** (GB, TE, bye 11) — board rank 77 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The consensus buy in the position's second tier, priced down purely on the knee — and the knee story has trended the right way since mid-August.

- **Coming back from an ACL tear roughly ten months ago, and the strongest sources are positive.** Packers beat reporters including Rob Demovsky said in mid-August he "looks like a player who was never hurt" and should be fully cleared for Week 1 on a normal ACL timeline; that day he ran first-team 11-on-11 for the first time and finished 5-of-5 with a touchdown. A 9/03 beat report has Kraft and the coach both describing him as as healthy as last season.
- **The snap-count question is not fully closed.** Kraft said on the record on 8/16 that the Packers "are going to take into account my rep range probably until halfway through the season," then in the same breath hoped to avoid a rep count in Week 1 and blamed Gutekunst for slowing him down. The room's read, at 695 upvotes, is that any limit is nominal — Green Bay's Watson and Parsons limits lasted about a game — and that the headline was aggregator noise on the same reporting. A commenter repeated the pitch-count line a week later, and Hartitz agreed he may be snap-limited for three to four weeks. The fade case survives either way: one detailed version calls him a mid-season buy rather than a draft pick, and skeptics group him with Kittle as an ACL return that needs the following year.
- **The contract is not an availability risk.** Kraft said on camera he will not hold out and the unresolved deal will not affect whether he plays; the room reads the extension delay as guarantees, not health, and Gutekunst has said for months a deal is being worked on.
- **Role is secure and the depth behind him is thin.** The Packers signed Jonnu Smith, which the room reads as a response to Luke Musgrave's long-term neck injury rather than a threat — the TE3 saw six targets all last year — so Kraft plays near the goal line and on third down. With Doubs and Wicks gone he is one of Green Bay's top two targets alongside Watson, and the room expects red-zone share to grow if the Packers pass more without Jacobs.
- **The usage bear case is separate from the knee:** 5.5 targets a game with 6 TDs in 8 games on a team 28th in pass rate. Cooper has him around TE5 in standard but lower in PPR for exactly that — a play-action and screen profile closer to Kittle than Kelce.
- **Market** has him in the 6th to 8th rounds, roughly where the board has him, and repeatedly calls him the best non-elite TE value — "the last TE worth a real pick before the position flattens." The gap is in auctions, where he keeps going for $2-4 when nominated late.

**Draft take:** Take him in the 6th or 7th as your starting TE and accept a slow September; at $2-4 in an auction he is the clearest value at the position.
