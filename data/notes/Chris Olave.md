**Chris Olave** (NO, WR, bye 8) — board rank 29 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The analysts are unanimously above his price and the room is unanimously scared of his head — those two facts are the whole page.

- Injury: no current designation. Per Jeff Nowak (Aug 9) he was busy in Saints team drills with "no sign of any ill effects from the wrist issue". What the room actually fades is the concussion history from 2024 plus what owners describe as multiple scares this preseason — a commenter fading him at pick 29 says he is one concussion away from his career being over, and another is trying to move him inside larger deals rather than hold the risk.
- Role: the Saints' clear WR1 with no established WR2, and while Jordyn Tyson is on IR he is the unquestioned alpha — "let Olave eat" — with target share consolidating until Tyson returns. Supporters cite 19.1 PPR points per game from weeks 7-18 last year and 20.2 from week 10 on, after Rashid Shaheed was traded, playing with Tyler Shough. The longer version of the case: 18.2 FPG (WR6) in the games Shough started from week 8, 20.2 (WR3) after the Shaheed trade, 27.7 (WR1) in weeks 15-17, on 100 catches from 156 targets. One commenter warns the Shough connection is strong enough that most targets and red-zone looks went his way with Juwan Johnson taking a back seat. New Orleans also upgraded the offensive line, and he signed a 4yr/$124M deal this offseason.
- The skeptic's answer is that he has never dominated at that level over a full season, Tyson takes targets back on return, and the small-sample splits are propped up by a 37-point Jets game. One commenter notes New Orleans' top two receivers are both among the most injury-prone in the league.
- Market: the board has him at 29 and the named analysts are all higher — Maher has him 18th overall and WR8 against a consensus of 24, the player he is furthest ahead of the room on in his Pickens/Olave/Smith cluster; Hartitz has him WR11; Murchison has been pounding the table all summer as one of two picks to beat ADP and would take him back for Drake London; Cooper ranks him ahead of Malik Nabers. But actual drafts are going the other way: one drafter took him at pick 45, another reports him slipping to 4.04 even after the Tyson news. Health is the stated reason for the discount.
- Sentiment: 13 top-level fades in the biggest fade thread, overwhelmingly concussion-driven — one hit at his price ends a season.

**Draft take:** Let the room's concussion fear work for you — he is the alpha in New Orleans while Tyson is on IR and drafters are letting him fall past pick 40, which is a price the analysts say is a round and a half too cheap.
