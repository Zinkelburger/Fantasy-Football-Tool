**Chris Rodriguez** (JAC, RB, bye 7) — board rank 146 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the room agrees he takes the valuable short-yardage touches from Bhayshul Tuten and disagrees only on whether that is worth a roster spot.

- Role, per beat report (8/26): the Jaguars' best pass-protecting back with LeQuint Allen hurt — he got the nod for the end-of-game drill, which mostly meant pass protection for Trevor Lawrence. Read it as conditional. The passing-down role reverts when Allen returns, and Shipley framed the stock boost as early-season only, and only if Allen cannot resume his normal third-down work.
- The durable part of the role is goal line and short yardage, which the room has expected consistently since 11 August. Hartitz names him as one of the Jacksonville backs taking the valuable touches — targets and goal-line carries — out of Tuten's larger snap share; Cooper says the same. He does not catch passes, and one skeptic puts his career snap share at 30-35% and calls him touchdown-dependent.
- The newest read (9/4) is that most of the room still expects him to lose the job. Defenders cite 4.5 yards per carry on 100 carries in Washington last year; detractors answer that this was about 38 yards a game and that he never broke through a talent-poor committee. One commenter's framing: CRod will challenge Tuten more than Holani challenges Price.
- Analysts split on ceiling. Dopp says he is highly thought of by the staff, should have a legit role immediately as the RB2, and is his stash over Woody Marks and Tank Bigsby. Maher calls him a JAG with a clearer path to season-long playing time but a lower ceiling, and sees no scenario where he clears a 50% snap share. Beat coverage notes his Kentucky history with Liam Coen could give him the upper hand over Tuten.
- Market: going around round 12, roughly where board rank 146 has him, and several commenters call that far too cheap for a co-starter who becomes the full-time back if Tuten gets hurt.

**Draft take:** a last-two-rounds pick if you own Tuten and a defensible one if you don't, but he is a short-yardage specialist, not a starter, unless Tuten gets hurt.
