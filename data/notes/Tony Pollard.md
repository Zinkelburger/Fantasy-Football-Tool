**Tony Pollard** (TEN, RB, bye 9) — board rank 70 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The room has turned on his price, and the preseason snap counts are the reason.

- Role is genuinely disputed and nothing from the team or a beat writer settles it. One commenter (Sept 3) has him locked into the Titans' No. 1 back role for the year with Tyjae Spears only a change-of-pace, and another says he took all the first-team preseason snaps and should be a workhorse. The upvoted rebuttal (Sept 2) cites Jared Smola's Aug 24 preseason splits putting Pollard behind Spears at 42% of snaps and 31% of routes, and says he simply is not a workhorse back.
- No injury or availability claims.
- The other role item is a scenario, not news: a commenter (Aug 18) predicts Tennessee trades him to San Francisco — last year of the deal, minimal dead money, a new head coach who just drafted a back — but only if Christian McCaffrey creates the need.
- Market is where the room is loudest, and it is paying about what the board asks and hating it: a 105-upvote comment says he was already a bad pick at 100 and there is no chance at 73, a 21-upvote reply never wants to hear anyone sell a 31-year-old Pollard again, and "nobody will trade for him as anything more than a bag of chips." Twerski (Sept 3) uses him as this year's example of the floor player he no longer drafts at all.
- Sentiment, both sides: the defence is four straight top-24 finishes with 1,000-plus rushing yards and a strong fantasy playoff run, a fine boring flex if you swung for upside early; the prosecution is that he was a workhorse last season and still averaged five half-PPR points a week. An Aug 27 fade takes the whole Tennessee backfield on a projected bottom-five offensive line and Cam Ward, preferring Spears far cheaper as a PPR cheat code.

**Draft take:** Let him fall — the preseason splits say committee, the room says the price is wrong, and no reported role change argues the other way.
