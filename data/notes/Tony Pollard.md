**Tony Pollard** (TEN, RB, bye 9) — board rank 70 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Nobody disputes he has a role; the room disputes paying round-6 for it, and the objection is loud.

- The workload picture is contested. One commenter has him locked into the Titans' No. 1 job for the year with Tyjae Spears as a change-of-pace back, and another says he took all the first-team preseason snaps. The upvoted rebuttal cites Jared Smola's 24 August preseason splits putting Pollard *behind* Spears at 42% of snaps and 31% of routes — **so treat the workhorse framing as unsettled, split with Spears**.
- The offence around him is the other split: some see a better line than last season plus Cam Ward; one OP fades the whole Tennessee backfield on a projected bottom-5 line and would rather have Spears, "a PPR cheat code", far cheaper.
- Market is where the room is emphatic. His ADP of 73 is the single biggest objection raised: a 105-upvote comment says he was a bad pick at 100 and there is no chance at 73, and a 21-upvote reply says he never wants to hear anyone sell him a 31-year-old Pollard again. He also has no trade value — "nobody will trade for him as anything more than a bag of chips." The board rank of 70 sits right in the middle of that complaint.
- The defence, from a minority: top-24 in each of the last four seasons with 1,000+ rushing yards, a strong run through last year's fantasy playoffs, and a fine boring flex if you swung for upside early. The prosecution's number is that he was a workhorse last season and still averaged five half-PPR points a week. An earlier thread noted his yards per carry fell 1.2 when he moved from backup to full-time starter in Dallas, so the Tennessee decline was never as steep as the reputation.
- Sentiment: Twerski uses him as this year's example of the floor player he no longer drafts. A Ringer host says he "has more juice than people remember" but still takes Warren over him. He is separately nominated as this year's fake-safe mid-round pick, the successor to Calvin Ridley and Mike Evans.

**Draft take:** Let someone else pay pick 70 — he is a floor-only flex whose own preseason snap share does not support the workhorse price.
