**Jordyn Tyson** (NO, WR, bye 8) — board rank 83 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A rookie the room liked and now treats purely as an IR stash, with the durability record the main thing being argued about.

- New Orleans placed Tyson on IR on 30 August with a hamstring injury; he misses at least the first four games of his rookie season. The room reads the timeline as roughly four games to two months, with a return around mid-October. Individual commenters range wider than that — "out two months", "out for most of the year" — but none of them are sourced, and the official four-game minimum is what the reporting supports.
- Durability is the actual bear case, not this one injury. Commenters note he has not had a healthy season in four years, that the hamstring is recurring, and that he played 33 of 51 possible college games — evidence of a pattern rather than bad luck. "A player with chronically injured hamstrings already injuring a hamstring in camp is an awful sign" is how one drafter put it; the top reply on the point drew 39 points, and one commenter raised a Kevin White bust comp.
- His absence is the reason the room expects concentrated early-season target volume in New Orleans: Juwan Johnson has been the second-most-targeted pass catcher without him, and Chris Olave faces very little target competition. Commenters expect Johnson's share to shrink when Tyson returns, and add that Tyson will not come straight back to a 90% snap share.
- Role when he plays is barely reported. Saints beat writer Nick Underhill's only camp verdict was "Jordyn Tyson is a dawg", and Jeff Nowak wrote in early August that the "Jordyn Tyson and Tyler Shough connection is building." One manager expects him to surprise late, reasoning that Shough is poor and Olave is likely to miss games.
- Market: the room takes him around the 12th round, or last, far behind his board rank, and names him the top-priority IR stash ahead of Charbonnet. The IR news moved his dynasty rookie-draft cost sharply — he had been going top-5 and two commenters report him lasting to 1.11, behind Love, Price, Concepcion, Lemon, Tate, Coleman, Stribling, Washington, Mendoza and Sadiq. Buyers call that a discount on a year that was already redraft-irrelevant.
- Sentiment is thin and defensive: named twice as a possible Treylon Burks arc, with the pushback that "if you drafted him, this kind of potential start is expected."

**Draft take:** IR-stash only in redraft, and the first name to stash if you have the slot; in dynasty the IR discount is the buying window, but price in a hamstring that has never stayed quiet.
