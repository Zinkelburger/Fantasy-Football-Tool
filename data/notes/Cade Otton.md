**Cade Otton** (TB, TE, bye 10) — board rank 179 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Nobody's favourite and nobody can name a replacement — the argument is about whether a locked-in boring job is worth a pick.

- Role, Sept 2: he is Tampa's TE1 with no competition, a 94% offensive snap share over three years, and a new three-year, $30M extension with $20M guaranteed. Two straight years of 59 catches on 81-plus targets. The claim is that Mike Evans leaving means a red-zone bump and top-12 upside at a bottom-of-the-board price.
- The Sept 1 thread ran the same fact both ways: defenders cite the near-full snap share, two seasons near 59 catches and 600 yards, Baker Mayfield's trust, and the vacated Evans targets; detractors say he falls flat whenever the plan actually runs through him.
- Against it, Cooper (Sept 1) has him off his board as a late tight end — he would need multiple Tampa receiver injuries to become a top-two target. That is the sharpest dissent and it is one analyst against a consensus.
- Sentiment, Sept 1: two top-level comments cut him, but the loud replies are pushback — "why would you drop Otton", "I'd smash the waivers if I saw him even in 1TE" — and the owners cutting him mostly roster five or six tight ends.
- No injury or practice claim on him anywhere.

**Draft take:** A fine last-round starting tight end if you punted the position — the snap share and the extension are real, the ceiling case is not.
