**Puka Nacua** (LAR, WR, bye 11) — board rank 6 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Two separate availability clouds, neither of them resolved in the corpus, over a receiver nobody argues with on the field.

- **He is not on the commissioner exempt list, and as of 8/30 Schefter reported the NFL was still reviewing his matter** alongside Alaric Jackson's and Rashod Bateman's, with no players added to the list that day. The underlying facts in-thread: he bit a woman in a limo, his attorney acknowledges the bite and calls it horseplay, no criminal charges have been filed, a civil suit is pending, and the LA city attorney has it in pre-filing diversion. An attorney commenter (94 points) predicts a confidential settlement and no charges; the room's estimate of missed games runs zero to two.
- A second story broke days before the season: abuse allegations from an ex-girlfriend via a since-deleted TikTok, using the word "abuse" without naming him or describing an incident, amplified by the NY Post. The room (1,115-point thread, top comment 2,049) reads it as vague clickbait and expects no discipline — no charges, no arrest, no new video, and the older biting video has been public for months without league action. Consensus is he plays Week 1. Murchison is the dissent worth recording: he says there is real fear the exempt list could be coming and told people to watch for weekend news.
- **The injury is the underreported half.** Rapoport had him leaving practice on 8/11 with minor groin soreness, the Rams being cautious, and McVay saying he was not yet sure what it was. By 8/25 he had missed all of camp to that point, doing sideline work, with the Rams reportedly unworried; Maher still described him as "banged up" on 9/3. Nothing in the corpus reports him back to full practice — that is a gap, not a clearance.
- Role, thinly covered: the Rams' YAC engine, mutually protective with Davante Adams so defenses cannot key on either, with Stafford filtering targets to both; one commenter puts 13-personnel goal-line looks on Adams instead.
- **Market: board rank 6 is roughly ADP, and live drafts are pushing him below it.** He went 1.02 and 1.06 in reported drafts and fell to pick 12 in another where the drafter still thought that was high; a 248-point comment is simply "He fell so far in my draft that I had to take him". Several drafters at 1.05-1.09 took JSN or Lamar instead, citing an 0-2 start being hard to overcome. Yahoo still ranks him WR2.
- The fade is almost entirely off-field — he was the second-highest bust vote-getter (25 top-level comments, ~1,612 upvotes) on character rather than production, and multiple faders concede in the same breath that he is a fantasy WR1 and took him anyway when he fell.

**Draft take:** Do not reach at rank 6 with the groin unaccounted for and a league review still open, but take him without hesitation if the room's nerves push him past pick 8.
