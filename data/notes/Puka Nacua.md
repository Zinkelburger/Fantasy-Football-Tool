**Puka Nacua** (LAR, WR, bye 11) — board rank 6 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A consensus top-two fantasy receiver carrying an unresolved league review and a groin, and the room is split on whether that is a discount or a trap.

- **Availability, off-field: still under NFL review, not on the commissioner exempt list.** Schefter reported on 8/30 that no players were added to the exempt list that day and that the league is still reviewing Nacua, alongside Alaric Jackson and Rashod Bateman, with no timeline. The underlying matter: he bit a woman in a limo — his attorney acknowledges the bite and calls it horseplay — no criminal charges, a civil suit pending, the LA city attorney at pre-filing diversion. The room's suspension estimate spans zero to two games; an attorney commenter (94 pts) predicts a confidential settlement and no charges. Cooper twice cited Nacua's absence from the exempt list as the yardstick for how much more seriously the league treated Josh Jacobs.
- **A second, vaguer allegation surfaced 9/02 and the room dismissed it.** A since-deleted ex-girlfriend TikTok used the word "abuse" without naming him or describing an incident, amplified by the NY Post. The largest thread (1,115 pts, top comment 2,049) reads it as clickbait and expects no discipline — no charges, no arrest, no new video, and the older biting video public for months without league action. Murchison the same day said there was real fear of the exempt list and told people to watch the weekend; Twerski would still draft him as a top-two WR. Nothing since has changed the 8/30 beat report.
- **Availability, physical: a groin that has never been declared clean.** He left practice early on 8/11 with head trainer Reggie Scott, McVay saying he did not yet know the issue; Rapoport reported it the same day as minor groin soreness with the team being cautious. By 8/25 he still had not practised for a while and was doing sideline work, with the Rams reportedly unworried about missed time. Maher still called him "banged up" in early September.
- **No role claims at all** — nobody is arguing about his usage; the entire discussion is availability. Some read the Rams reacquiring Atwell as an injury or suspension signal; others say the room behind Nacua and Davante Adams is simply thin. No report confirms either.
- **Market:** he went 1.02 and 1.06 in reported drafts, roughly where board rank 6 puts him, but multiple drafters passed at 1.05-1.09 for JSN or Lamar, and "He fell so far in my draft that I had to take him" hit 248 points. A minority pushes him into round 2 on cumulative off-field risk.
- **Sentiment:** the most-nominated fade in one thread (16 top-level comments, 247 upvotes), almost entirely off-field, with faders conceding in the same breath that he is a fantasy WR1 and that they took him anyway when he fell.

**Draft take:** Worth a top-six pick if you can absorb a one-to-two game absence, but let him come to you at the back of round 1 rather than reaching — the league review is overdue and unresolved and the groin has never been declared clean.
