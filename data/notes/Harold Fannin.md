**Harold Fannin** (CLE, TE, bye 11) — board rank 63 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A cheap TE1 last year whose price has caught up while his quarterback situation got worse — the room is uneasy rather than negative.

- The quarterback is the swing factor and the room reads it as a downgrade. Keeper and dynasty owners say the Watson decision hurts his value and note he came alive during Shedeur Sanders' starts last year — **the fade is conditional on Sanders not taking the job back**. One commenter fades him on quarterback grounds alongside Kyle Pitts. Hartitz takes the other side: he has Fannin TE8, ahead of Juwan Johnson, expecting Cleveland to make the most of the Watson experience through screens and lower-aDOT targets. The room's general version of that is "a bad QB checks down to the TE more often."
- Target competition is the second question. The bull case is that Cleveland's pass-catcher room — Concepcion, Boston, Jeudy — is unproven, so he still commands a solid share. The bear case is that Watson historically has not used tight ends much and that rookie KC Concepcion is the most direct competitor for his exact routes, both working short-to-intermediate. One commenter expects him to cede those easy targets to Concepcion, weakening his floor — **only while Concepcion holds the underneath role**.
- What he did: 11.7 PPR points per game last year after going undrafted, and 6th among tight ends from Weeks 9-17 at 12.6 PPG. One commenter tracked the tell in Week 1 — 70% of snaps, 60% route participation and all the red-zone snaps. A separate evaluator calls him the easy TE1 of his class and uses him as the benchmark this year's rookie tight ends fall short of.
- Market: he goes later than the board's rank 63 — around TE6 in the 8th-9th round, priced around round 8, in the $5-6 auction range, with one commenter expecting Browns QB unease to push him down further. He is named with LaPorta and Kraft among preferred mid-round TE targets. One drafter took him only as a consolation after Tyler Warren went a pick early.
- Sentiment: a Ringer host will not take him, or Judkins, at any price because the vibes on that offence are off. He is called unlikely to repeat as a top-10 TE, though one commenter notes he is going too late to be a real bust; one suggestion is to let someone else draft him and buy a few weeks in after the expected slow start.

**Draft take:** Fine at the 8th-to-9th-round price the room actually pays, not at the board's 63 — and if you miss him, the room's own advice is to buy after the slow start.
