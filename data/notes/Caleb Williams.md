**Caleb Williams** (CHI, QB, bye 10) — board rank 85 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The analysts are warmer than the room — rankers put him in the QB7-QB12 band while r/fantasyfootball fades the whole Chicago offence as priced above a mediocre 2025.

- No injury or availability claim. The one role change worth knowing came Sept 3: Chicago lost the coordinator who oversaw last season's production to Baltimore, cited there as the credential for the Ravens' new playcaller. Ben Johnson remains the reason the bulls give — Hartitz (Sept 2) expects a year-three leap in year two of Johnson's offence, with targets centralised on three players and plenty of multi-TE sets, good for Loveland, Burden and Odunze alike.
- The statistical case against him is the completion rate: 58.1% last season, second-worst among qualified starters, below Dillon Gabriel and Cam Ward and barely above J.J. McCarthy and Shedeur Sanders. The defence in the same thread cites 4,000 yards, 20 TDs to 7 INTs, around a 90 rating and upper-half EPA, arguing the low rate reflects deep shots.
- The most detailed bear case is from a rival fanbase (Sept 1) and is about the tape, not the situation: average play they call mediocre with an insane highlight reel, throws sailed over open receivers, flat-out bad through three quarters on average, and the biggest throws coming off secondary busts. Dissenters there expect year-two-under-Johnson improvement.
- Analyst ranks bracket the argument. Maher (Sept 3) has him QB7 and prefers him to Trevor Lawrence and Jaxson Dart at the same price on the best combination of rushing upside and passing ceiling plus his playcaller — but he is below ECR on all three and would not reach for the tier. Loza has him QB12, nervous about consistency, citing the 4th-hardest QB strength of schedule and erratic 2025 play, while still expecting QB1 numbers. Twerski has Williams and Dart back-to-back and takes Williams if rostering only one.
- Market and fades: about eight top-level fades on Sept 1, mostly the Madden cover curse and general Bears scepticism rather than play, plus several commenters holding zero shares of any Chicago skill player on price. Room price and board rank are in the same place, so there is no discount here. The bull scenario commenters float — 4,500 yards and 30-plus TDs — is one commenter's projection, not a consensus.

**Draft take:** A mid-QB1 you take only at his price in the 7th-8th — the ceiling case rests on a staff that just lost the coordinator who oversaw last year's numbers, and nobody in the corpus argues for reaching.
