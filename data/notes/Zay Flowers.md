**Zay Flowers** (BAL, WR, bye 13) — board rank 45

**Room sentiment:** Sharply divided, and the split is the story — 53 mentions. He headlines a 273-upvote "must-have WRs" post and sits in Hartitz's tier 3, yet he draws more "never again" votes in the do-not-draft thread than almost anyone in this batch.

- Role is not in question: cited at a 29.9% target share and 86.5% snap rate, tethered to Lamar Jackson, and he finished as WR7 in both total and PPG last year. ADP 42.7 with a 974.5-yard projection.
- The bull case is scheme change: Baltimore goes from Greg Roman — "only 4 teams in football passed less often" — to a Ben Johnson-tree playcaller (OC Declan Doyle, HC Jesse Minter), lost Isaiah Likely, and added little receiving competition. One poster thinks a "JSN-style leap" is live.
- The bear case is weekly usability, not season totals: "go look at his weekly finishes and tell me you'd feel comfortable having that be your locked-in starter." Posters claim Baltimore "straight up does not use Flowers in the red zone," and question a 5'9" receiver taking red-zone work from Derrick Henry.
- The sharpest line in the thread: "the people who fight for Zay Flowers are never the people who owned him."

**Draft take:** A round-4 volume bet with a documented consistency problem. Fine as a WR2 if your build already has ceiling elsewhere; avoid if you need him to be a weekly difference-maker.
