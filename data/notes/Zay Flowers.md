**Zay Flowers** (BAL, WR, bye 13) — board rank 45 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Back at practice after a month of missed work, with the real argument being that a WR7 finish was a miserable week-to-week hold.

- **Returned to practice on 9/3 — his first practice in two weeks, listed as a quad — per beat reporter Cordell Woodland.** Before that: Jamison Hensley (ESPN) reported him out on 8/31 with an undisclosed lower-body injury, and on 9/2 Jeff Zrebiec had him as the only Raven not practising, though doing sprints and a workout, with Hensley noting full-length sprints usually mean a player is very close. HC Minter said on 8/31 that it is nothing major and Flowers "should be ready to go" for Week 1. The body part is reported three different ways across the corpus — quad, lower-body, and Zrebiec's belief that it is hamstring soreness picked up in the Vikings joint practice, which he was explicit was a belief and not team-confirmed.
- **The risk is rust, not availability.** He has had maybe one or two full practices since getting hurt roughly three weeks earlier, and the room's stated worry is that he opens short of his normal snap and target load rather than that he misses the opener.
- **Role: undisputed WR1, and freshly paid.** Baltimore extended him at roughly $27M/yr guaranteed, the highest-paid receiver of this offseason though only 15th in league AAV. Isaiah Likely is gone, Rashod Bateman is described as in trouble, and the projected WR2 is a third-round rookie, so there is no obvious competitor for targets. He is used inside and in space out of the slot but moves anywhere. New OC Declan Doyle arrives from Chicago's high play-action rate, which matters because Flowers leads all WRs in yards per route run on play action over the last two seasons at 4.60 (citing Ryan Heath); Flowers has praised Doyle's install as full of routes "to help you get open".
- The production case: WR7 last season on about 1,200 yards, 7th in receiving yards and 16th in targets, in the league's lowest-volume passing offense (roughly 3,200 team passing yards) with Lamar Jackson missing time, and one missed game in three years. No volume increase is needed to justify the price.
- **The fade is the most-nominated in its thread — 25 top-level comments, 168 combined upvotes — and it is about the weekly experience,** not the finish: he was WR44 in full-PPR points per game from weeks 2-13, with a best weekly finish of WR23 and only five games over 15 PPR points, so the WR7 came out of Week 1 and Week 18 spikes.

Loza ranks him first of Flowers/McConkey/Waddle/Higgins and is unbothered by the 7th-hardest schedule; Twerski keeps him at $16 over Drake London at $30; Hartitz has him WR15, just behind Waddle.
