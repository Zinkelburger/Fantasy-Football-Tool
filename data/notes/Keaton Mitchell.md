**Keaton Mitchell** (LAC, RB, bye 7) — board rank 154 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Analysts like the 8-10-touch change-of-pace role at the price; the fanbase that watched him most does not believe in him, and he is hurt right now.

- Injury first, Sept 1: a commenter says he is already injured, and cites that as the reason not to move off Kimani Vidal yet. Commenter-level, no timeline, nothing since.
- Role: commenters expect the Chargers brought him in for the Achane-rookie-year change-of-pace job, which one calls "Keaton Mitchell hopium" (Aug 25). The newest read (Sept 4) is the speed case in Mike McDaniel's offense — 6.3 yards per carry on 100-plus NFL carries, and what McDaniel did with De'Von Achane — but even the supporters say that if Omarion Hampton went down he would get real work rather than take over, and the dissent is that he needs both Hampton and Vidal out to matter.
- Analysts split on what that is worth. Hartitz does not expect top-24 numbers but can see an RB3 season, arguing the Chargers offense, play-caller and back archetype optimize 8-10 touches, and calls him a great later-round pick behind an anchor RB. Murchison wants him only as an end-of-bench RB4/5 roster filler and deep stash. Twerski names him among his must-grab handcuffs and favourite late-round RB pickups.
- The loudest negative comes from the team he left in free agency: Ravens fans largely agree he lost a step, had drop issues, was a one-trick pony and a terrible pass protector, and is injury prone. A minority still think he looked better than the back Baltimore kept.
- Sentiment runs both ways and both sides are thin: two commenters predict a top-10 back season and one insists he outscores Hampton outright, while the manager who kept him over Vidal calls him a roster clogger held on inertia rather than expected role.

**Draft take:** One late-round roster spot for the 8-10-touch role at most — he is hurt now, and the top-10 talk is two commenters rather than a projection.
