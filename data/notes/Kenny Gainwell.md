**Kenny Gainwell** (TB, RB, bye 10) — board rank 96 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** strongly bullish and unusually well-sourced for a backup — the debate is over his profile, not his role.

- Role, per beat report: Adam Slivon of PewterReport wrote (8/9) that Gainwell had one of his most explosive camp practices, that he "has not toted the rock much" for the Bucs but "has emerged as a dynamic receiving back." That is a passing-down role, not early-down volume, and it is the cleanest source on him.
- The room's version matches. Bucs fans (Aug 31) converge on about a 30% touch share with a 40-45% ceiling, Bucky Irving at 50-55% and a sprinkle for Sean Tucker; they describe Gainwell as a former slot receiver, the best route-runner and pass protector in the room, "check down central." One projects 450+ receiving yards on 70+ catches. Hartitz pegs him near a 35% snap share but taking the valuable touches — targets and goal-line work — out of Irving's share; Cooper is higher than consensus and says the role holds regardless of Irving's health.
- Only while Irving is healthy: the same Bucs commenters say his share climbs if Irving misses time, and the room flags him as a full-PPR league winner in that scenario.
- The contract is the room's main evidence: two years, $14M with $9M guaranteed, roughly the 23rd highest-paid back, against about $2M for Rachaad White — read as Tampa planning a real complementary role. His arrival is also why Tucker's snaps shrink and why Tucker became a cut candidate.
- The split on what he is: one side cites the 2025 Pittsburgh finish — outsnapped Jaylen Warren in 7 of his last 9 games from Week 11, RB12 or better in 6 of 8 from Weeks 11-18, team MVP votes, 187 touches for 1,000 scrimmage yards and 8 TDs. The other calls that an outlier inflated by a Rodgers checkdown offense and points at a 2025 average depth of target around 0.6 yards against CMC's 2.5-3.5. He has never been an NFL RB1 in five years.
- The sharpest argument against the hype (9/3): in Irving's rookie season Rachaad White had 195 touches for 1,000 yards and 9 TDs alongside him, so a productive 1B does not mean Irving loses anything.
- Market: board and room agree. Commenters target him near ADP 96 and "as late as he's going"; dissenters call him a PPR scam artist. Loza would start him over TreVeyon Henderson in Week 1.

**Draft take:** a genuine PPR flex at his round-8 price on pass-down volume alone — worth the pick in full PPR, much less so in standard where the aDOT complaint bites.
