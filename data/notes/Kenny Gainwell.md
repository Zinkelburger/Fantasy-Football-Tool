**Kenny Gainwell** (TB, RB, bye 10) — board rank 96

**Room sentiment:** The most-argued player in this batch — 162 mentions, genuinely divided, and mostly a proxy war over Bucky Irving.

- Facts the room keeps citing: Tampa let Rachaad White walk (1 year, $2M) and signed Gainwell within 24 hours of free agency at 2 years/~$14M (~$9M guaranteed). Bowles "is already calling it a 1A/1B backfield." Last year in Pittsburgh he was RB7 in PPG after Week 11, reigning team MVP, ~1,000 scrimmage yards and 8 TDs in 17 games, with heavy pass-game volume (73 targets in one comment, 85 in another) and 8 goal-line carries.
- Camp reporting cuts against the takeover thesis: "Irving was given the most carries on Friday morning, as Kenny Gainwell was primarily utilized as a receiver out of the backfield."
- Bulls: "Gainwell might be the best value of anyone right now," "my highest-exposure player in Best Ball," "at worst an elite handcuff." The contract is read as the clearest signal the team wants to split the load.
- Bears, and there are many: 27, a career backup on his third team in five seasons, and last year's production leaned on Aaron Rodgers checkdowns. Sean Tucker also owns the goal line, capping TDs.

**Draft take:** Draft him at ADP (~96) as a PPR pass-down flex, which the reporting supports. The league-winning version requires a Bucky injury — pay for the role, not the ceiling.
