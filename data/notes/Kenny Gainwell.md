**Kenny Gainwell** (TB, RB, bye 10) — board rank 96 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A passing-down back the room likes on money and role, not on any expectation that Bucky Irving loses the job.

- His standalone case is capped by Irving's health, and Bucs fans put numbers on it: ceiling 40-45% of touches, more likely near 30%, with Irving at 50-55% and a sprinkle for Sean Tucker. The same commenters say his share climbs if Irving misses time, and one thread flags him as a full-PPR league winner in that scenario.
- The role itself is defined and not contingent: passing-down and pass-protection back who spells Irving and sometimes lines up beside him. A former slot receiver, described as the best route-runner and pass protector of the Bucs backs, "check down central". Adam Slivon (PewterReport, 8/9) reported one of his most explosive camp practices and wrote that he "has not toted the rock much" but "has emerged as a dynamic receiving back" — passing downs, not early-down volume. Hartitz pegs him around a 35% snap share but taking the valuable touches, targets and goal-line work, out of Irving's share; Cooper is higher than consensus and says he could have a meaningful role regardless of Irving's health.
- The money supports the role: two years, $14M with $9M guaranteed, roughly the 23rd highest-paid running back, against about $2M for Rachaad White. The room reads that as a real complementary job rather than a three-touch backup.
- The bear read is specific. He has never been an NFL RB1 in five years, sitting behind Sanders, Swift, Barkley and Warren. His 2025 average depth of target was around 0.6 yards — a dump-off back at the line of scrimmage, against CMC's 2.5-3.5 — and his Pittsburgh finish, outsnapping Jaylen Warren in 7 of his last 9 games from Week 11 and scoring RB12 or better in 6 of 8 from weeks 11-18, is called an outlier inflated by Rodgers' checkdown offense.
- The best argument that he does not break Irving: in Irving's rookie season Rachaad White took 195 touches for 1,000 yards and 9 touchdowns alongside him, which is roughly what Gainwell did last year (187 touches, 1,000 scrimmage yards, 8 TDs). Irving can produce with a 1B.
- Market is one claim: commenters actively targeting him near his ADP of 96 — the same neighbourhood as the board rank — and taking him "as late as he's going", while dissenters call him a PPR scam artist.

**Draft take:** Worth a round-9 pick in full PPR for the receiving role alone, and he becomes a starter the week Bucky Irving misses one.
