**J.J. McCarthy** (MIN, QB, bye 6) — board rank 252 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The most-discussed name in several threads and almost entirely as a negative reference point — he lost the job, and the live question is whether he is on the roster.

- Availability is genuinely open. Alec Lewis projects he will not make the Vikings' 53-man roster — and the room's caveat, at 32 upvotes, is that Lewis does not say he is hearing it or citing sources, so it reads as a projection rather than a report. One commenter puts it at roughly 20% traded and near zero cut; dissenters note cutting him costs about $6M more in dead cap than keeping him, and that no top-10 quarterback has been cut on his rookie deal without a replacement in place.
- Role: he has officially lost the Minnesota starting job to Kyler Murray and is at best the backup. The room's most-cited evidence that the coaches are out of patience is that Minnesota played Carson Wentz over him last season through a dislocated shoulder, a torn labrum and a fractured socket. He gets on the field only while Murray struggles or gets hurt, and even then one commenter doubts he holds it — the reply is that he looked halfway decent late last year, is still very young, and Murray is neither a sure thing nor durable. The room expects last year's production collapse to return with him.
- The evaluation behind that is unsparing: he was a net negative to a 9-8 offense, Minnesota chose him over Sam Darnold on sunk-cost draft capital, and one poster notes Murray has only 10-15 career games below McCarthy's per-game passing average.
- Market: the price has collapsed to nothing. Managers report turning down a 2026 first going into Week 1 last year, a 2026 second four to six weeks in, and Jaxon Smith-Njigba straight up in superflex — and now one traded him for Jacory Croskey-Merritt plus Jordan Mason and calls it a steal, another got Mac Jones plus a pick swap, a third simply dropped him, and one says you cannot move him at all in a league whose members have eyes. In one league he was cut and nobody claimed him.
- Sentiment is the loudest fade in the corpus and worth taking seriously as such: the 771-point top comment on the roster-cut thread is "that seems impossible but he sure was bad"; "the argument is that JJ McCarthy wasn't a QB" carried 260 upvotes across five mentions; "a brick that the Vikings dressed up as a quarterback". Cooper names him his own miss from last year — the scheme and opportunity were there and the talent wasn't, and might never be.

**Draft take:** Not rosterable in any format that requires him to play — he is a backup who may not survive cutdowns, and the dynasty holds are lottery tickets kept only because he is taxi eligible.
