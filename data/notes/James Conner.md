**James Conner** (ARI, RB, bye 14) — board rank 152 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** He is on IR to open the season and the board's rank does not know it.

- Availability: the Cardinals placed Conner on injured reserve with a designation to return, per John Gambadoro (Arizona Sports 98.7) on Aug 30, so he misses at least the first four games. He had been hurt with no timetable since mid-August, which is why the room directed waiver claims at Trey Benson's replacement rather than at him, and he is named among the few fantasy-relevant players starting the season on IR alongside Jordyn Tyson and Zach Charbonnet. One commenter says calling him fantasy-relevant this year is a stretch and stashed him anyway.
- The injury is on top of age. He is 31, coming off what the room repeatedly calls a major injury — one commenter refers to a broken ankle — and multiple threads describe him as probably done. Even his backers hedge everything with "assuming he gets healthy and isn't just washed".
- Role on return, and it is diminished. The top comment on the IR news (47 pts) frames the designated-to-return move as Arizona letting him recover and rebuild trade value rather than cutting him; a second commenter expects at most a goal-line/power-back role behind the young backs. Arizona released Benson and restructured then extended Conner, which the room reads as the team valuing his veteran leadership and pass-game versatility — but Jeremiyah Love, Tyler Allgeier and a 31-year-old's ankle are all telling owners to move on. Before the IR move, with Love out on a high ankle sprain, he was the downhill veteran the offense leaned on.
- The one real skill claim: he is the direct counterexample to the idea that Kyler Murray does not throw to running backs — he lived off Murray's dumpoffs in Arizona, which drew 20 upvotes as the rebuttal in its thread.
- Sentiment: six top-level comments (97 upvotes) speculate Green Bay trades for him if Lloyd, Brooks and Johnson do not work out. That is speculation with nothing reported behind it, and Twerski's answer covers it — he is not worth an IR slot, because he needs to be traded *and* get healthy for it to matter, which is too many ifs.

**Draft take:** Leave him on the wire in redraft — he is on IR through at least Week 4 at 31 years old, and the best case anyone describes is a goal-line role behind two younger backs.
