**A.J. Brown** (NE, WR, bye 11) — board rank 28

**Room sentiment:** Mostly bullish with a real dissenting minority — 97 mentions, and he shows up in both "bet my season on this year" and "do not draft no matter what" threads.

- **New situation is the whole story:** the Eagles traded him to New England for a 2028 first and a 2027 fifth. The room likes the fit — "Drake Maye needed an Alpha" — and notes McDaniels' history of funneling his offense to his best playmakers. Camp chatter says the Patriots "are loving life with AJ Brown."
- **Where he ranks:** Ian Hartitz slots him WR6 and in his "overall WR1 upside" tier with Jefferson, Lamb, Nico Collins and London (score 233). ADP is late-2nd/early-3rd (28 Yahoo, 26 ESPN); multiple posters call that too cheap.
- **The bear case:** he's coming off a down year — 78/1000/7, roughly WR11 and ~15 PPG. Skeptics point to a "confirmed degenerative knee," Maye's tendency to spread the ball (Diggs out, Doubs added as a real WR2), and a harder schedule. One Pats fan says outright he is "going way too high."
- **Knock-on effect:** the room expects DeVonta Smith and Dallas Goedert to absorb the vacated Philly targets.

**Draft take:** A 2nd/3rd-round WR with legitimate top-5 range in a better passing offense. The consensus is that he's underpriced; the honest counter is that you're paying for a scheme change plus a knee, so don't reach past his ADP.
