**Mark Andrews** (BAL, TE, bye 13) — board rank 109 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A 30-year-old whose camp reports were bad and whose own fanbase says he is cooked, still priced as a late-round TE1.

- Role: with Isaiah Likely and Charlie Kolar both gone he is again the red-zone security blanket, and the bull case is arithmetic — if Lamar Jackson throws 30-plus touchdowns they have to go somewhere and Zay Flowers does not catch many. That is conditional on Jackson staying healthy.
- The counter, from a self-identified Ravens fan and seconded by a second commenter: he is cooked and will be used situationally on third downs and in the red zone, making him entirely TD-dependent and near-impossible to time week to week — fine in a TD-only format, disappointing otherwise. An 08-23 thread describes a rough camp and calls him "absolutely toast" with the old regime gone, arguing Baltimore's receivers benefit.
- Volume has already fallen: he has not cleared 70 targets in either of the last two seasons, and commenters say his 2025 points were heavily skewed by a few boom games.
- Contract context, from commenters rather than reporting: Baltimore offered Likely a deal first, Likely wanted more, and the Ravens went back to Andrews. One reads that as the front office knowing something; another says it only explains why Likely never got a chance.
- Market: he goes 120-135 overall, roughly in line with board rank 109, and is a stated late-round target for several drafters, usually paired with Jake Ferguson or Chig Okonkwo. Maher ranks him TE15, a spot below Juwan Johnson at TE14, and notes Andrews is drafted nearly three rounds earlier than Johnson — he prefers Johnson at cost. Cooper takes Andrews (and Kelce) as the safe-but-boring half of a two-TE build, pairing him with a high-risk bench TE, and allows he could be elite in this scheme.
- Sentiment: three commenters name him the correct drop to hold Josh Jacobs in a 10-team league, arguing better tight ends hit waivers most weeks. Another thread treats him as a nostalgia gamble worth a 10th-round pick at most and warns against punting TE on the hope that he or Kelce turns back the clock.

**Draft take:** Touchdowns are the entire case and the camp reports are bad — 10th round or later, and never at the three-round premium the room pays over Juwan Johnson.
