**Josh Allen** (BUF, QB, bye 7) — board rank 18 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the one quarterback this room will pay up for, and the one it argues about paying up for every single year.

- No availability concern. Commenters note he has never had a major injury; one recounts that he played last postseason banged up — a foot injury from a Myles Garrett pressure, worsened in the wild-card game, plus a knee — and still nearly beat Denver. That is retrospective and self-labelled speculation, not a current status.
- Situation changed: Sean McDermott is gone and Joe Brady is head coach while keeping playcalling duties. Fans expect a more open offense, blaming McDermott for conservative, predictable calls and a refusal to stretch the field, and note Buffalo added a receiver who can separate. Skeptics answer that Brady was the problem.
- His rushing floor is a usage fact, not a talent claim: he holds the all-time QB rushing touchdown record and took 17 carries inside the 5 last year, which the room treats as a hard cap on James Cook's touchdown ceiling. One commenter frames the Allen-over-Lamar rushing-TD edge as exactly that — Buffalo gives Allen the goal-line sneaks while Baltimore hands off inside the five to Derrick Henry.
- The knock on Buffalo receivers is also the case for him: he spreads the ball around, Bills WRs never produce as hoped, and Diggs was the last receiver good enough to command a real target share there. The Fantasy Footballers laughed off the receiving-corps worry when ranking offenses — Buffalo finished 4th last year with a worse group because of him.
- Market, single-QB: the room does not actually pay board rank 18 for him. Real reported cost is rounds 2-3 of a 12-team, one pick at 14, one at 3.x that the room called a steal, and one in the middle of the 4th in a 14-team. Several call spending an early-first pick on him over the elite RB/WR still on the board a reach. Maher, who has him 44th overall, is the only ranker with a quarterback inside his top 50.
- Market, superflex: he is close to unanimous as the 1.01 — 22 top-level comments for 329 upvotes, led by "in 12-team-plus SF, taking anyone but Allen would be terrible process" (128 points) — but that is eroding. He cost 32% of budget in a 12-team SF auction, third behind Gibbs (35%) and Bijan (33%), and several posters ran value-above-replacement math putting him at 1.03 (Allen 6.9 weekly points above baseline, Gibbs 10.1).
- The endorsement stack is as strong as it gets and adds nothing to price: NFL players ranked him the #2 overall player, execs/coaches/scouts, AP writers and PFF all have him as the #1 QB, and he is the betting MVP favourite around +550/+600. The only remaining knock the room accepts is that he has no Super Bowl win — a team result.
- The bust case is positional cost, not the player, and the rebuttal is the one number that matters: the gap from Allen to QB6 has exceeded 50 points every year for six seasons, and he has six straight 40-touchdown seasons. Dopp is fading him specifically in ESPN's knockout format, where Buffalo opens against Houston and Allen scored 8.1 fantasy points there last year.

**Draft take:** the automatic 1.01 in superflex; in single-QB let him fall to the 2/3 turn and take him there rather than paying his board rank.
