**Jonathon Brooks** (CAR, RB, bye 5) — board rank 127 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Two ACL tears, twelve career touches and an ADP the room itself calls out of control.

- **He has not practised with the team since the cut-down deadline, and the only status anyone has is the coach's.** Panthers HC Dave Canales called it "some soreness", declined to name the body part, and said on Wednesday 9/2: "I'm very optimistic he'll be available for Week 1" against Chicago on Sept 13. Beat writer Joe Person had him working on the side rather than practising on 9/1, which Canales called normal maintenance; as of 9/3 he was still not back at practice. Take the quote as a coach's forecast and the practice record as the fact.
- The history is the reason this matters: back-to-back ACL surgeries on the same right knee (November 2023 in college, again about a year later), a lost 2025, and 12 career NFL touches — 9 carries at 2.2 yards a pop — plus roughly 8 preseason carries this summer at 3.6 YPC against Jacksonville's second and third stringers.
- Two medical voices in the corpus, both more optimistic than the room. A physical therapist is not fading him: he is 21 months post-op on a revision ACL at Week 1, close to the ~24-month mark where research shows re-injury risk drops, the surgery was done by Dr. Neal ElAttrache, and there is no significant difference in re-injury rates between primary and revision ACLs. An orthopaedic surgeon adds that the 2023 reconstruction was likely done without a lateral extra-articular procedure — that literature is only about a year old — which may be why the graft failed.
- **Role: a share, not the job.** Canales has said the Carolina backfield will be a committee at worst, so Brooks does not have to beat Chuba Hubbard out to get touches — but Hubbard leads it, and the takeover case is a slow burn. Rico Dowdle is gone, there is no rookie competition, and Loza notes Hubbard is dealing with a hamstring issue. A commenter insisting Brooks is the locked-in passing-down back produced no evidence when pressed.
- **Market: board rank 127 badly understates what he costs.** He is going around RB34 in the 7th or 8th round, frequently ahead of Hubbard, with reports as high as 5.09 in a 14-team and 7.06 in a 12-team. The analysts split hard on whether that is right: Murchison would go as early as the middle of round 4; Twerski has him on his sleeper and "irrationally confident" lists and will reach from round 7; Hartitz calls him overdrafted and caps him at rounds 8-9, citing an unimpressive preseason, a banged-up Carolina line and a possible bottom-10 offense; Loza was high on him in May and now will not draft him before double digits. Dynasty owners report they cannot get a single 2nd for him.
- The room's tone is harsh: "How anyone can be optimistic about this myth of a player is beyond me" at 122 points, and a 1,006-point reminder that he has 9 career carries.

**Draft take:** A round 8-plus dart and nothing sooner; at the round-5-to-7 price the room is actually paying you are buying a committee share from a back who has not finished an NFL game.
