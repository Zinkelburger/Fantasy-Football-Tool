**Rashod Bateman** (BAL, WR, bye 13) — board rank 260 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A late-round WR3 whose real variable is a league investigation, not his route running.

- The availability risk is legal, not medical. A Baltimore Banner feature (8/31) lays out domestic-violence allegations by the mother of his child, including an arrest over a windshield damaged with a screwdriver. Per Schefter (8/30) the league is still reviewing the matter and he was not placed on the commissioner exempt list — available for now, with an open suspension risk hanging over the season.
- The accuser formally recanted the physical-abuse allegation in writing a week or two before 8/31, admitting she made false statements and injured herself; the room's most upvoted read (51 points) is that this guts the case. The vehicle-damage arrest is not recanted, and commenters note Maryland prosecutorial discretion survives a recantation. The Ravens room is split on whether anything lands this season — the arrest was June 3, so the league has had the file for months — and much of the wider room still expects a short suspension whenever the facts settle.
- Role: he still opens as the outside starter opposite Ja'Kobi Lane and is on the field for more packages early as the established pro, but the room calls this a make-or-break year — he must produce to keep Lane from taking the job, and one commenter expects him traded before his deal runs to 2029. Conditional on both a suspension and Lane's development.
- The standing case for him is fit rather than ability: he gets open, but Lamar Jackson likes to go off-script while Bateman is an in-time route runner, so the two have no connection. New coaching and a healthy Jackson are the cited reasons that could change.
- Cooper expected him to be Baltimore's WR2 and won't rule it out, citing weirdness around him and Lane flashing — but Bateman goes undrafted in Cooper's leagues.

**Draft take:** Leave him on the wire — a suspension is still live, Lane is coming for the job, and nothing about the Jackson fit has actually changed.
