**C.J. Stroud** (HOU, QB, bye 8) — board rank 166 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A cheap late quarterback the room is arguing about on merit, with the contract standoff and the offensive line as the two live facts.

- No availability issue; the news is contractual. Jeremy Fowler (ESPN) reports he will not sign an extension before the season — Houston offered north of $50M a year, Stroud is holding out for close to $60M and betting on himself — and Houston beat writer Aaron Wilson confirmed both the over-$50M offer and Stroud's $60M floor. An earlier $70M figure circulating in the thread was called false. Houston has already exercised his 2027 club option, so they can hold him two more seasons without the franchise tag.
- The supporting cast is the fantasy question. Last year he played behind a line ranked 32nd and 31st in pass and rush block win rate, with a bottom-five running back room, Tank Dell out, Nico Collins banged up and a first-year playcaller. The line was invested in this offseason but has already suffered losses, and Texans fans read GM Caserio calling rookie guard Keylan Rutledge the team's best offensive lineman two ways — proof the group is still thin, or a sign it is finally improving after five bad years. Several call the rookie-plus-Ersery left side protecting his blind side scary. The deep threat is also unsettled while Tank Dell is out: Boutte is meant to restore it, but commenters including Patriots fans caution that Stroud lacks Drake Maye's arm strength and that Boutte's production leaned on Maye's deep ball.
- Beat writer Aaron Wilson had him sharp at the 8/26 practice — multiple connections with Nico Collins, touchdowns to Jaylin Noel, rookie TE Marlin Klein and Lewis Bond — in year two of Caley's offense with a new quarterback coach. Texans fans temper it: the reports amount to the offense not getting its ass kicked as badly as last year.
- The statistical defense is what the room upvoted most: from weeks 4-18 he was 4th in adjusted EPA per play, 5th in EPA per play and 11th in success rate, with 17 touchdowns to 5 interceptions and a 97.2 rating, and year three beat year two in touchdowns, interceptions, yards, completion percentage, yards per attempt and rating. A separate thread has him 9th-11th in EPA per dropback on a 3,041-yard, 19-8 season at 64.5% while missing about 3.75 games, and commenters citing The Athletic Football Show make the same point.
- The bear case, loud but outvoted: he collapses under pressure, threw four first-half interceptions with the season on the line, and one commenter calls it a Knoblauch-style yips case. Texans fans are lukewarm, their top comment saying both sides are content to let him play the year out. His lack of rushing production has always capped him as a low-end QB1, and one commenter notes the perverse risk that a better supporting cast lowers his fantasy totals.
- Market: he goes very late — reported at an ADP of 142.4 in mid-August and taken in the 12th round in one draft, in the same range as the board rank — and is named as a quarterback worth taking there specifically to beat that price.

**Draft take:** A fine last-round QB2 or streaming target on the line investment and the EPA record, but he has never rushed enough to be a set-and-forget QB1 and the room's own fan base is not sold.
