That is a team-produced interview, so weigh it as such.

The counter is that Taylor is the truer tight end and could lock a role in before that happens. Beyond Sadiq, he is low in the pecking order on a team expected to feature the run and target Garrett Wilson first.
- Sentiment from roughly ten commenters is mixed but holding: several call him an easy TE3 stash or waiver add, two would take him over Greg Dulcich now, one predicts he is the tight end to own in three years — against others saying he is barely rosterable with a ceiling capped by Sadiq.

**Draft take:** A last-pick or waiver TE3 to hold through Sadiq's absence, not a tight end you plan a season around.
