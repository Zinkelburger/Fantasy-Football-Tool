**Fernando Mendoza** (LV, QB, bye 13) — board rank 199 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A stash, not a starter — the Raiders have said out loud they intend to sit him, and the only live question is when that ends.

- He opens the season on the bench behind Kirk Cousins, and the team has been explicit. QB coach Mike Sullivan says his development will be "a long process" with "no shortcut to it"; Kubiak, GM Spytek and Brady have all said they intend to sit the rookie; Mendoza himself said he has a lot to learn after struggling against vanilla preseason looks.
- The room still expects a takeover during the season, with guesses clustering around weeks 5-9 (Las Vegas byes in week 13) and r/raiders arguing he plays once the team is out of the race — one guess is as early as weeks 3-5. All of it is conditional on Cousins staying healthy and effective. The minority view is that signing Cousins to a multi-year deal after attending Mendoza's pro day means they will not rush him at all.
- On the tape, Ryan Fitzpatrick praises his reads and willingness to make aggressive throws, and the thread notes every former-QB analyst is saying the same despite an up-and-down preseason box score. The counterweight in the same thread: that aggression already produced two preseason interceptions, and how often he took hits behind the Raiders line is itself a reason to sit him.
- Market: purely a superflex and dynasty stash — draft him for 2027, hold him if your starters have early byes. A commenter reports the Fantasy Pros dynasty crew were unanimous earlier this offseason that you must take him 1.01 in superflex rookie drafts, and calls that more indefensible than taking Anthony Richardson over Bijan Robinson.

**Draft take:** Roster him only where a bench spot is free and 2027 matters; he is not a 2026 fantasy starter until Cousins stops being one.
