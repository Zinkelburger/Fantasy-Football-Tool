**David Montgomery** (HOU, RB, bye 8) — board rank 48 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The sub's favourite value RB all offseason, now priced like it — the argument has narrowed to how much Woody Marks takes.

- No injury news. He did not play the preseason exhibitions while Marks did, which the room reads as a veteran kept fresh rather than a problem; he has missed 10 games in seven seasons.
- Role is the note, and the sources disagree. Houston traded a 4th plus an interior lineman for him, gave him a new contract, and his coach has publicly called him a three-down back in a room with little competition — the team's stated problem last season was having nobody who could punch it in near the goal line. Against that, Jonathan Alexander of the Houston Chronicle (9/3, repackaging an Aug. 23 report) expects a fairly even hot-hand split with Marks.
- The room largely rejects the even split: the majority expect him to lead, take most carries and nearly all goal-line and red-zone work, several projecting a 60-70% snap share, and even posters arguing for a real Marks role concede the red-zone work. Cooper calls the split report overblown and names Montgomery the latest back he would be comfortable starting as an RB1. Maher expects him to be the immediate short-yardage and goal-line option but does not project a workhorse, with Marks involved on passing downs. Fantasy Points relayed Texans insider sourcing — not beat reporters — that Houston wants him in a 2024 Joe Mixon role.
- Bear case: he is 29 on his third team, running behind a line commenters call the worst in the NFL (PFF 32nd, though others say Houston fixed pieces and camp reports are positive), with Stroud regressed and Marks good enough as a receiver to take real work.
- Market: rounds 4-5 in 12-team redraft, and no longer a secret — several call the 4th a reach and the 5th fair, and a minority won't pay at all over committee risk. Maher has him RB22, four spots ahead of consensus, a solid RB2 with double-digit touchdown upside off 4.5 yards per carry and 8 rushing scores on a career-low 158 attempts. Loza has him as a middle-round must-have who keeps falling to her. Hartitz changes his mind "by the hour" in the round-5 range and elsewhere lumps him with Judkins as a dead-zone profile; Twerski fades him outright; Murchison has barely drafted him — high usage floor, very low ceiling.
- Sentiment: "People are sleeping on David Montgomery per usual" was the second-biggest answer in its thread at 286 points, and he drew ~305 upvotes as a "too low" nomination elsewhere — though Texans fans themselves rejected single-season-touchdown-record talk as homer stuff.

**Draft take:** Draft him in the 5th and pass in the 4th — the goal-line role is as close to reported as this range offers, but the offense caps the ceiling.
