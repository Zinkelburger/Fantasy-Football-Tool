**Jared Goff** (DET, QB, bye 6) — board rank 126 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The room's consensus late-round QB1 finish, and the standing objection is not his baseline but his lack of a ceiling.

- The production is stable and documented: over 4,500 yards and 30 TDs in each of the last three seasons, which Hartitz notes against a league that has managed six such seasons total over that span. He ranks Goff ahead of Stafford and Mahomes and calls him a darkhorse MVP candidate at about 35-1 on the easy schedule and a defence that should be mediocre again.
- The 2026 role argument is specific: without David Montgomery vulturing the red zone, a chunk of roughly ten rushing TDs should convert to passing TDs, with LaPorta on the field helping. Commenters call the Detroit offence extremely QB-proof, with the weapons around him being Gibbs, Amon-Ra St. Brown, LaPorta and Jameson Williams, in a dome.
- The knock is variance, not baseline: commenters say he can go half a season without a turnover and then hand over four or five in one game, pointing to three interceptions and a fumble in the divisional loss to Washington. The other knock is structural — zero rushing upside, and he is rarely among the top-50 rostered players on championship teams, which is why one ranking has him QB16 despite three or four straight top-10 finishes. He gains real value in 6-point passing TD leagues.
- Market: he goes about 130th and later in practice — reported in the 11th round or later in both of one poster's drafts, the 12th in a competitive 12-team after five running backs, and "the 13th-14th round" in the thread where he drew the most upvotes of any name (10 nominations, 159 points). That is cheaper than Stafford, Purdy and Nix, whom the same posters rank behind him. One warning worth keeping: if several managers start rostering backup quarterbacks, the Goff tier becomes the Bryce Young tier.
- Sentiment: Vegas had him as the Week 1 QB2 at 19.5, and the most-upvoted comment there (+131) was "Goff is gonna be a Top-10 QB for the fifth year in a row and nobody cares." The counter, worth the weight: he has finished top-10 by PPG only once in four years among QBs with 12+ games, and his 2025 QB8 came partly from playing Week 18. One commenter adds that two years past Ben Johnson, projecting the Lions to trend down is reasonable.

**Draft take:** The best late-round quarterback on this board — take him in the 11th or 12th, and value him up in 6-point passing TD leagues where the missing rushing floor matters least.
