**KC Concepcion** (CLE, WR, bye 11) — board rank 138 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the room likes the player and does not trust the quarterbacks throwing to him, and that gap is the entire file.

- Beat writer @AkronJackson named him MVP of the 8/21 joint practice against Buffalo and said he will be a focal point of the Browns offense when the season starts. Ben Solak (ESPN, 8/11) separately warned not to let Cleveland's first-round receiver fade in Denzel Boston's spotlight.
- The room reads him as the de facto WR1: first preseason game usage put him ahead of Boston, with better separation, better YAC and easier manufactured touches. Dissent is that this rests on one preseason game and Boston was drafted only about 15 picks later.
- Hartitz calls him flexable in Week 1 at WR42 — he loved the preseason usage on the reverse and the pop pass and expects Todd Monken to scheme him roughly 8 targets and a carry a game "to work around the black hole at QB."
- Return work is a live add: two commenters list him as a likely punt returner who also gets offensive snaps, and one would move him up a round in return-yardage formats.
- The one conditional worth carrying: a commenter calls him the best long-term receiver of this tier but says he needs a Cleveland quarterback change first, which is why that commenter takes Stribling ahead of him now.
- Market: the board has him 138th and the room drafts him round 10 or later, while Maher has him 78th overall and WR42 — 38 spots above ECR — and uses that gap in trade arguments. He is also the name commenters would rather have than a defense at the 10/11 turn, and one Ringer host would take him at 1.07 in a dynasty rookie draft, ahead of Boston and Jonah Coleman.
- Sentiment: four top-level Browns-breakout nominations, every one hedged on the quarterbacks. One NC State fan expects the Browns to break him this year; another says he only thrives once Cleveland has a real QB in 2027. The dynasty room treats Concepcion versus Makai Lemon as a coin flip with Tyson and Tate a tier above, and several posters say he would have gone third in class with a Buffalo landing spot.

**Draft take:** the round-10 price is paying for Cleveland's quarterbacks, not for the receiver — take him there every time, and accept that the payoff may be 2027.
