**Malik Nabers** (NYG, WR, bye 8) — board rank 35 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Talent nobody disputes attached to a knee and an offense both of which the room does dispute; the fade is about the Giants, not about him.

- Availability is the whole question and it is still open. He is back from a season-ending ACL tear with a follow-up cleanup knee procedure — the cleanup is a room claim with no beat source attached to it. HC John Harbaugh, via Jordan Raanan (2026-09-01), says Nabers took "most of the team reps" in practice, that the workload plan varies day to day, and that Week 1 is "trending in that direction". Beat report has him on the field at practice on 2026-09-02, stretching alongside newly signed Odell Beckham Jr.
- Nabers himself will not commit. Speaking to Raanan on 9/1 he said he feels "good" but would not say whether he plays Week 1; on 9/3 he would only say he is "focused on returning this season", which one commenter reads as deliberate vagueness aimed at Dallas rather than as a timeline.
- Expect him active but capped. The Giants room expects a managed snap count in the opener — guesses run from decoy snaps to 20-50% of his usual share, against a normal ~99% — with a run-leaning offense early. One poster's line is the honest one: it is smoke and mirrors until he actually draws 10-15 targets in a game. A single dissenter thinks he sits until Week 3.
- Hartitz notes the most recent updates have not been as optimistic as the earlier ones, while still expecting him to play Week 1 with easing-in talk.
- Market vs the board: the board has him at 35 and the room mostly pays that — several independent recaps have him going at the 2/3 turn, Cooper and Twerski both take him in the third — but a run of managers report getting him in the 3rd or even 4th on the ACL and Giants worries, which the thread calls insane value. The dynasty buy window is described as already closed; his price recovered.
- Sentiment is the sharpest negative here: 10 top-level fades for 201 upvotes, and the single most-cited reason is the Giants' offensive coaching rather than Nabers. A Ringer host: he "could be WR1. He could be WR100 and barely play."

**Draft take:** Take him in the third and plan on a limited Week 1 — the price already pays for the knee, and the risk you are actually buying is the Giants' offense, not the rehab.
