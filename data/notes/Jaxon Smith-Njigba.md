**Jaxon Smith-Njigba** (SEA, WR, bye 11) — board rank 8 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A settled top-five receiver; the only live argument is whether last year's leap repeats, and nobody argues his role changes.

- Role: Seahawks fans attribute the leap to opportunity rather than a change in the player — with DK Metcalf gone he became the undisputed WR1 on what commenters call an absurd target share, and won Offensive Player of the Year.
- The repeat risks the room actually names: a less condensed target share, a large OC downgrade, doubt that last year's efficiency and target rates hold, and Rashid Shaheed's improving chemistry with Darnold. One commenter adds that he sat out roughly eight fourth quarters in 2025 because Seattle was blowing teams out, so his production may track the Seahawks defense more than people expect.
- Market: Yahoo ranks him WR4; Twerski has him sixth overall behind Gibbs, Chase, Bijan, Puka and Amon-Ra, and names Amon-Ra and JSN the only two receivers he would refuse to pass on at picks 8 and 13. He was 5th on the player-voted NFL Top 100 after going unranked a year ago, the highest wide receiver on the list. In practice he has been falling to picks 8-10, with a high of 1.03; taking him at 8-9 in PPR is called a no-brainer and several say he is a steal in the mid-first because "people hate JSN for fantasy reasons." Board rank 8 is exactly where he goes.
- Keeper leagues: at a 5th-round keeper cost he is the near-unanimous answer over Javonte Williams in the 10th and Parker Washington in the 15th, five separate commenters saying so, and one says it holds even with boosted rushing scoring.

**Draft take:** Take him anywhere in the top ten without thinking about it — the only case against him in the whole corpus is generic regression.
