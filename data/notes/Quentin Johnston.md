**Quentin Johnston** (LAC, WR, bye 7) — board rank 113 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** One of the most consistently underpriced names in the corpus — 37 mentions in one thread and near-unanimous "way too low."

- Healthy. He had health problems from roughly Week 5 through Week 16 last season, and the Chargers passing game shortened in his absence.
- The role case is vacated volume: Keenan Allen is gone and his 122 short and intermediate targets, the team lead, are up for grabs. The room's own counterpoint is that Allen's under routes and Johnston's X role barely overlap, so the spill goes more to Ladd McConkey, Tre' Harris and the tight ends. What is not disputed is that he is the X — he was a WR1 for the first month of last season on 37 targets in four games before the injury and the offensive line collapse, and posted 2025 games of 24.9, 23.8, 20.4, 16.1, 15.3, 14.9 and 14.8 as a deep threat. Chargers fans say McDaniel has been working with him specifically on his release and route stems, and the line is healthy again.
- Analysts have him ahead of his cost: Maher has him 69th overall and WR36; Hartitz has him WR35, ahead of Michael Pittman and Romeo Doubs, and calls him the best asset in a Johnston-for-KC Concepcion trade.
- Market is where the disagreement lives. He drafts in rounds 8-11 with an ADP near 118 — 11.08 in one 12-team league, where the owner then dropped him and someone picked him back up for free — while KeepTradeCut prices him alongside Terry McLaurin, Matthew Golden, David Montgomery and Omar Cooper. A 51-upvote nomination calls that a real market inefficiency created by the national consensus still treating McConkey as the unquestioned Chargers WR1. One Ringer host's favourite double-digit-round value is "Quentin Johnston in the 10th," and a commenter says he reaches for him in every draft.
- Sentiment is bullish and should be discounted as such: predictions of a WR1 season, a top-15 finish, and outscoring McConkey, plus the argument that he is the live counterexample to the 525-yard-rookie rule. The room's own bear case is only that he is boom-or-bust and has been a meme — the drops reputation is now called a lazy take. One holder admits he needs the breakout because he has too much QJ stock.

**Draft take:** Draft him a round or two ahead of his ADP — 950 yards and 8-10 touchdowns is a reasonable ask for the X in an offense that just lost 122 targets, and the round-9-to-11 price does not require it.
