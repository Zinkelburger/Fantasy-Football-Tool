**Quentin Johnston** (LAC, WR, bye 7) — board rank 113 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A near-unanimous "way too low" — 37 mentions in one thread with the only bear case being that he is boom-or-bust.

- Health: no current injury. He had health problems from roughly Week 5 through Week 16 last season, and the Chargers passing game shortened while he was out.
- Role: the beneficiary of Keenan Allen's departure, with a caveat the room raises itself. He was a WR1 for the first month of last season — 37 targets in four games — before the injury and the offensive line collapse. Commenters say the staff has been working with him specifically on his release and route stems, and that Ladd McConkey is his only real competition, with 950 yards and 8-10 TDs reachable. The counterpoint is that Allen's 122 vacated targets came on short under routes that barely overlap his X role, so the spill goes more to McConkey, Tre' Harris and the tight ends.
- Market: the analysts are well ahead of the board here. Maher has him 69th overall and WR36; Hartitz has him WR35, ahead of Michael Pittman and Romeo Doubs. The room takes him in rounds 8-11 against an ADP of 100-120, one commenter says he reaches for him in every draft, and a Ringer host's favourite double-digit-round value was "I like Quentin Johnston in the 10th." In dynasty he is fetching Isaac TeSlaa plus a 2027 3rd, and holders are split on whether to sell.
- Sentiment is the loudest buy in the corpus: he is the room's live counterexample to the 525-yard-rookie rule, called one of the best number twos in football, an open-field force and a quiet red-zone weapon, with predictions of a WR1 season and better production than McConkey.

**Draft take:** Take him in the 9th or 10th as your WR4 — two analysts price him around WR35 against a rank-113 board and the whole room agrees, which makes the reach cheap and the miss survivable.
