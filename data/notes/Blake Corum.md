**Blake Corum** (LAR, RB, bye 11) — board rank 82 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The room likes him as one of the best secondary backs to own and is openly dismissive of the "1A" story attached to him — "we do this every year."

- His case is Kyren Williams' availability, and Kyren is healthy, 26, and one year into a three-year, $33M extension with $23M guaranteed — there is no injury or role news working in Corum's favour right now. Everything below is what he is worth while that holds.
- He is the clear No. 2 in Los Angeles, not a co-starter. The 2025 split was roughly 65-35: 159 touches to Kyren's 309, with a snap share flat all year between 25% and 40% and settling near 35%, per commenters posting the game logs. The late-season carry bump came from game script rather than a role change.
- Pass protection is the stated cap. The room's read is that McVay's one requirement of a starting back is blocking, that Corum barely played as a rookie because he could not, and that he is still the worse blocker and receiver — a one-and-two-down back. That is why the volume question keeps resolving the same way.
- The usage he does have is genuine: Weeks 13-18 of 2025 he ran 63 times for 405 yards and 5 TDs with double-digit attempts in 8 of the final 12 games, against Kyren's 89-456-4 and 12 catches over the same span. In the three playoff games the carry split was 11-13, 6-21 and 9-10 — two of three close games near even. His 5.1 yards per carry beat Kyren by about 0.3 — but on roughly 0.7 more yards before contact, which the room reads as blocking and script, not a talent gap.
- The "could emerge as the 1A" line has a traceable source and it is not reporting: it comes from Dan Graziano's ESPN bold-predictions piece covering all 32 teams. The thread's top comment (1,817 points) and its 789-point reply say aggregators recycled a prediction as a report. Treat it accordingly.
- Contingent value is the point. He is signed for two more years and sits ahead of any other Rams back, and the pre-season idea that Hunter would supplant him is treated as wrong. Cooper calls him a "handcuff plus" you can start in a pinch; one commenter names him the biggest beneficiary of a dominant Rams defence, as the back who closes out blowouts. Twerski expects the split to frustrate owners of both.
- Market: costs about 85th overall against Kyren around 21st. Some argue for him in round 10 as a flex-capable handcuff; others report the price has already moved past that and call him a mid-round pick now — closer to where the board has him than to a late-round dart. One commenter prefers him to Tony Pollard despite the higher price, on the grounds that even a zero-production season leaves a handcuff tradeable while Pollard's value evaporates.

**Draft take:** Take him around the 8th as the best contingent RB on the board — a weekly flex with league-winning upside if Kyren misses time — but do not pay for a timeshare the last three seasons say is not coming.
