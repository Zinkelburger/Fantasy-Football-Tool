**Omarion Hampton** (LAC, RB, bye 7) — board rank 11 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** genuinely two-sided at a first-to-second-round price — one thread's biggest bloc says he is going too low, the next day's says he is a fade.

- No current injury. He did break his ankle last season, recounted by one commenter as the injury that wrecked his roster, and that is why the usage numbers below come from nine games.
- The live question is the committee, and the head coach has put it on record: Mike McDaniel has said Hampton will not always be the team's lead back and that he wants a committee. That is the best-sourced line on the page, and it is also a coach forecasting rather than reporting a fact. The most upvoted rebuttal calls the hot-hand talk textbook coach speak and says Keaton Mitchell and Kimani Vidal would be fighting over scraps if Hampton is healthy; the bust case is that Mitchell genuinely takes passing downs and third-and-short.
- The usage case: 80.0% of team rush attempts inside the 5 last season (RB4) and five-plus receptions in four of nine games, now with line and coaching upgrades, on a 17-game pace of 256.7 fantasy points that would be the second-best rookie RB season in five years. Cooper ranks him ahead of Achane, Henry and Kyren Williams on opportunity, because this scheme runs the back on a ton of receiver routes. Hartitz has him RB10, ahead of Jeanty, trusting the Chargers offense more than Las Vegas.
- Market: mid-to-late second in 10-team formats, around picks 16-24 — roughly what the board says. The real split is scoring format. Maher has him RB9 and 16th overall in PPR but RB14 and 37th in non-PPR, and calls him the one top back he would let someone else take in standard, because so much of the case is receiving work. Two commenters suggest selling him in-season while Chargers hype peaks — that is trade value, not draft cost.
- Sentiment: nine top-level nominations and about 167 upvotes for "not high enough" — best back on the team, elite quarterback, improved line, and a new OC said to have taken the job because of him, with several reporting they took him over Kenneth Walker. The same week brought twelve top-level fades over committee fears, and a keeper room declined him at $26 against Chase Brown at $11 as an unproven sophomore.

**Draft take:** take him at his board rank in PPR and let someone else have him in standard — the receiving role is most of the price, and the head coach has already said out loud that this is a committee.
