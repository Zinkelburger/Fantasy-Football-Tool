**Trevor Lawrence** (JAC, QB, bye 7) — board rank 89 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the room's favourite wait-on-QB target, bought on an eight-game finish it half-suspects was schedule.

- The role story is scheme fit, not usage: a feature story reports Lawrence admitted mid-season that the game felt too fast and Liam Coen simplified the offense rather than benching him, which fans credit for the late run and read as room for more in year two of the scheme — a contrast they draw explicitly with the Pederson staff.
- The one skill dispute: a commenter argues he rarely attempts 30-plus-yard throws and that arcing, timing-based deep balls are a weakness, his deep completion rate flattered by broken plays. Others push back and call him one of the better deep-ball passers. Unresolved in the thread.
- Market is where the volume is, and it is consistent: ADP 79.6 in mid-August, the most-discussed QB in that thread at 17 mentions, with real picks in the 8th round and at 136 overall in a 10-man. The stated plan is to be the last manager to take a quarterback and land him; one commenter targets him every time because the round 7-8 stretch of RBs and WRs is so good. Another will pass on Allen, Jackson and Maye in rounds 2-4 and take Purdy, Goff or Lawrence in round 9-10, noting the per-game gap between Lawrence and Allen last year was about 1.4 points. Murchison used him as proof the top-6 superflex QB matters less than people think — he let the 1/2 turn pass and still came away with Lawrence and Bo Nix. He is QB8 in dynasty, which one commenter calls too low.
- Sentiment: Jaguars fans are bullish on an eight-game finish they do not think was a fluke — the most-cited number extrapolates it to 334/536, 4,384 yards, 45 pass TD, 13 INT plus 444 rush yards and 9 rush TDs over 17 games, which the room calls MVP production. Ten nominations for 31 upvotes elsewhere, with one predicting a QB3 finish. The pushback is twofold: the late run came against an easy schedule (rebutted at 26 points with him putting up around 20 against good defences too, and the easy schedule continuing), and rushing regression — 9 rushing TDs last year against 14 total in his first four seasons, on no real increase in rushing volume. Fantasy managers who like the Jacksonville offense mostly say he is the only Jaguar they would confidently start.

**Draft take:** the right quarterback to wait for — take him at the 8/9 turn as your only QB and discount the extrapolated MVP line, which rests on nine rushing touchdowns that are unlikely to repeat.
