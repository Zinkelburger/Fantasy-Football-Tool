**Trevor Lawrence** (JAC, QB, bye 7) — board rank 89

**Room sentiment:** Bullish on high volume (39 mentions) — one of the room's favorite late-round QBs and the default answer to "which Jaguar do I want?"

- Production the room keeps citing: QB5 last year at 19.9 PPG, and one post lists him as a full-season top-4 QB "only 25 points behind Josh Allen" despite going as a late-round flier.
- Repeatedly named among the best QBs in rounds 8-10 with Dak, Purdy and Herbert. Across the Jaguars WR threads the recurring conclusion is "Trevor Lawrence plus whoever's the cheapest is the way." One model gives him a 26% chance at a top-6 finish. Rushing matters: 9 rushing TDs last year, used heavily at the goal line.
- Bear case is coherent: rushing-TD regression — "never gonna sniff half those 9 rushing TD again in career" — plus "Trevor Lawrence is not a top 16 fantasy QB this year." His career-best stretch coincided with Parker Washington's Weeks 15-18 breakout, so part of the bull case is betting that continues.

**Draft take:** The cheapest way to buy this offense — take him in rounds 8-10 rather than paying up for the WRs in front of him. Don't project 9 rushing touchdowns forward.
