**Trevor Lawrence** (JAC, QB, bye 7) — board rank 89 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Jaguars fans are bullish off an eight-game finish they do not think was a fluke, and the fantasy room likes him mainly because he keeps falling.

- No injury or job questions. The role story is scheme: a 9/3 feature reports Lawrence admitted mid-season the game felt too fast and Liam Coen simplified the offense rather than benching him. Fans credit that for the late run and read it as room for more once he is fully comfortable — a contrast they draw explicitly with the Doug Pederson staff.
- The skill knock in-thread: one commenter argues he rarely attempts 30+ yard throws and that arcing, timing-based deep balls are a weakness, his deep completion rate flattered by broken plays. Others push back and call him one of the better deep-ball passers. Unresolved.
- Fans' number, and it is an extrapolation rather than a projection: those eight games over a 17-game pace come to 4,384 yards, 45 pass TD, 13 INT plus 444 rush yards and 9 rush TDs. Treat it as the bull case stated at full stretch, not as a forecast. The same room expects the Jacksonville offense to be good but too spread out for most of its pieces to matter in fantasy, and names Lawrence as the one Jaguar they would confidently start.
- Market: the board's 89 is roughly where he goes, and the room's argument is that you should not pay more anywhere. One commenter passes on Allen, Lamar and Maye in rounds 2-4 to take Purdy, Goff or Lawrence around round 9-10, noting the per-game gap between Lawrence and Allen last year was about 1.4 points. Murchison let the 1/2 turn pass in a superflex draft and still ended with Lawrence and Bo Nix; Loza calls him such a value she would roster him and Jaxson Dart; a Ringer host took him over Caleb Williams and Herbert because "he's the one that falls in drafts I see, pretty consistently."

**Draft take:** the late-round QB1 to plan around — let the top six go and take him in the 9th or 10th.
