**Khalil Shakir** (BUF, WR, bye 7) — board rank 135 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** respected as a player, discounted as an asset — the Buffalo target share was already flat, and DJ Moore just arrived.

- Availability first, and it is unresolved. He is coming back from tightrope ankle surgery, and on 9/2 Matt Zenitz reported the Bills are optimistic he will be available for Week 1 despite an undisclosed issue — the nature of the injury is not stated anywhere. A commenter the same day says flatly that he is injured, with no detail, timeline or source. One owner is already asking whether the ankle alone makes him a cut candidate.
- Role, per beat reporting: he led Buffalo at a 20% target share last season, ahead of Coleman at 12%, Kincaid at 10% and Knox at 10% — one of the flattest distributions in the league, which is the context for the Moore trade. He is described as a slot-only player who does not overlap with Moore's skill set, though others push back that Buffalo moves him around the formation rather than boxing him inside.
- The optimistic version — Moore's vertical threat pulling coverage and returning Shakir to more downfield routes than he ran in 2025 — depends entirely on Moore drawing that attention. The competing scenario the room floats, moving Shakir to the boundary opposite Moore, runs into the fact that he and Kincaid already occupy the inside.
- Market: DraftKings set his receptions line at 62.5. Cooper uses him as the example of a slot player capped by three-receiver deployment. Sentiment is a contrarian take that he outscores Moore, and a downvoted one that the two are redundant and Buffalo still lacks a true WR1; rival fans call him a top-five YAC receiver.

**Draft take:** confirm the Week 1 status before you draft him — a slot receiver on a flat target tree with a new alpha ahead of him and an undisclosed issue is a bench pick, not a WR4 you plan around.
