**Chuba Hubbard** (CAR, RB, bye 5) — board rank 78 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Reddit likes him more than the market does — a cheap probable starter with an agreed-on low ceiling, and the room notices that gap itself is a warning.

- Hamstring, trending the right way: head coach Dave Canales called it a "legit" hamstring strain confirmed by MRI and the team listed him week-to-week in camp, but he was back at practice — in full — on Sept 1 per beat writer Mike Kaye, and he told people he could have played the day after. Treat Week 1 as on track; a Sept 3 commenter still describing him as "dealing with" the hamstring is the weaker source and looks like lag.
- Role is his while he is healthy: the majority read is that he is Carolina's lead back, a team captain on a four-year extension and the room's only reliable pass protector, and Jonathon Brooks still is not practicing. Commenters project 60-70% of backfield touches — conditional on Brooks not clearly outplaying him once healthy, which the room does not expect soon.
- The dissent is real and worth pricing: he lost the job to Rico Dowdle last year, had only three games with 15+ touches, and Canales has never called it anything but a committee. Detractors call him a plodder who "runs in mud" — under 4.0 ypc in four seasons with 100+ carries.
- Not a zero in the passing game but not a receiving back: 73 catches on 93 targets across his last 30 games, roughly three targets a game, and that assumes Brooks is not healthy enough to take passing-down work.
- Market: drafters report getting him in rounds 7 through 9, one for $4 at auction, and the room calls round 9 "fantastic value" — though he has already climbed toward round 8 on the practice news. Both camps agree the ceiling is capped: bottom-third offense, a beat-up O-line, no explosive-play ability.

**Draft take:** Take him in the eighth or ninth as a floor-y RB3 with startable weeks while Brooks is out, but do not pay up for the ceiling — the room's own bulls concede there isn't one.
