**Chuba Hubbard** (CAR, RB, bye 5) — board rank 78 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The room has swung to him hard as Jonathon Brooks stayed off the practice field, and is aware that being the reddit-consensus value is itself a warning.

- Healthy and on track. He was back at practice on 2026-09-01 in full, per Panthers beat writer Mike Kaye, and the thread treats him as a Week 1 go. Dave Canales had called the hamstring a "legit" strain confirmed by MRI, suffered in a one-on-one drill, with the team listing him week-to-week during camp; Hubbard said he could have played the day after and the staff held him out. Later commenter mentions of "a hamstring issue" (9/3) are lag on the practice report, not new news.
- The backfield is his while he is healthy. Carolina named him an offensive captain alongside Bryce Young, he is on a four-year extension, he is the room's only reliable pass protector, and he retook lead work in the playoffs last year. Brooks still is not practising, and commenters say Hubbard is one Brooks setback from 80% of snaps — effectively already the case.
- Carry the condition: Canales has only ever called this a committee, and the room's own projection is 60-70% of touches, sliding toward a 50/50 split if Brooks proves healthy and effective. The dissent is that he lost the job to Rico Dowdle last year and had three games with 15+ touches.
- He is not a zero in the passing game — 73 catches on 93 targets across his last 30 games, roughly three targets a game — but that is modest, not a receiving role.
- Market vs the board: the board has him 78 and the room pays later, reporting him at 8.05, in the 7th-to-9th round, $4 in an auction, and "Chubba's adp is currently at round 9" at 168 points. That gap is the case for him. Note the counter-signal a commenter raises: reddit is all-in while cross-platform ADP is down on him.
- Sentiment splits on ceiling, not floor. Detractors call him a plodder — under 4.0 ypc in four 100-carry seasons, a bottom-third Vegas offense projection, a beat-up line, a 14-yard long run all last year. Backers cite the 2024 PPR RB14 finish with 1,200 yards and 10 TDs behind a bad line, and project RB20-24. Both camps agree the ceiling is low.

**Draft take:** Draft him in the 8th or 9th as your RB3 — a captain'd, extended, pass-protecting starter at that cost is a big-hit/small-miss bet even if the ceiling is capped.
