**Cam Skattebo** (NYG, RB, bye 8) — board rank 37 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Fully recovered and the named RB1, with a fade case built entirely on the way he plays rather than on anything currently wrong with him.

- **Healthy. He has been practising in full since late August**, getting into camp scuffles, and one commenter cites him doing a backflip at Fan Fest. The only in-camp scare was 8/7, when Jordan Raanan reported he limped off after a collision and stretched his left leg — the opposite leg from last year's injury — before HC Harbaugh said it was "nothing serious at all".
- Last season ended in Week 7 with a dislocated ankle on a hip-drop tackle. Kay Adams reported he said he considered retirement afterwards; commenters who watched the full clip call it a thoughtful answer the tweet flattened, and the room's read is that it was a moment in the ambulance — he was walking around team HQ right after and at a WWE show at MSG about a week later. He was RB7 from weeks 2-7 before it.
- **The durability worry is stylistic and the room is fatalistic about it** — the top comment in one thread is "No chance this guy survives the season" at 1,766 points, and a running joke across several threads is that his contact-seeking play guarantees a concussion. The rebuttal that keeps getting upvoted: the injury was a borderline hip-drop tackle on a pass play, and he missed about 1 of 45 college games. One commenter notes he has never had an officially diagnosed concussion.
- **Role: the Giants' RB1, named there early by the head coach**, with Najee Harris signed as the back who takes touches when Skattebo is off the field, plus Tyrone Tracy and Devin Singletary in the room. Dan Duggan (The Athletic) had him as the first back up in team periods on most days back in August but figured a time share with Tracy as the 1-B. The room expects a run-heavy offense — Patrick Ricard at fullback, Greg Roman as run-game coordinator, a better line — but not a three-down workhorse, since he does not play special teams and the staff wants a rotation behind him. One cap on the ceiling: Jaxson Dart's rushing takes goal-line and short-yardage work.
- The other noise is disciplinary, not usage. Harbaugh called him out publicly on 8/27 for energy that was "infectious in a bad way" after a 15-yard penalty and a taunting scuffle; a practice fight with CB Greg Newsome was defused by Newsome himself ("brothers fight, we were cool right after it"), and beat writer Pat Leonard is pushing a counter-narrative about his act running thin. No depth-chart change followed any of it.
- Market is thin: rounds 4/5 in 12-team drafts, with several saying they are out at that cost and keeper owners at the 7th, 8th and 12th calling it an easy hold. One commenter names him this year's sell-high.

**Draft take:** Take him in the 4th as a lead back who is healthy right now, and accept that you are buying about twelve games rather than seventeen.
