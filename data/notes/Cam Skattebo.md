**Cam Skattebo** (NYG, RB, bye 8) — board rank 37 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The role is not in question and the health is — the whole debate is whether a contact-seeking back coming off a dislocated ankle finishes the season.

- **Fully recovered as far as anyone can tell.** He is practising in full, getting into camp scuffles, and one commenter cites a backflip at Fan Fest; no lingering limitation has been reported. He limped off an 8/07 practice after a collision (Jordan Raanan), but it was the opposite leg from last year's injury and Harbaugh called it "nothing serious at all."
- **The injury was a season-ending dislocated ankle in Week 7 on a hip-drop tackle, with ligament damage.** Kay Adams reported he had considered retirement; commenters who watched the full clip call it a thoughtful answer about a moment in the ambulance — he was walking around team HQ right after and at a WWE show at MSG about a week later. A paraphrase, not news.
- **Re-injury is the real bear case and the room is fatalistic about it.** "No chance this guy survives the season" was a 1,766-point top comment, and the running joke everywhere is that his style guarantees he gets hurt. The most-upvoted rebuttal: it was a borderline hip-drop tackle on a pass play and he missed about 1 of 45 college games — elevated risk, but modest.
- **He is the Giants' RB1,** named there very early by the head coach, and the room expects him run into the ground: the team loves him, he has chemistry with Jaxson Dart, and he catches a lot of passes. He was RB7 from weeks 2-7 before going down, on an offense that added Patrick Ricard and Greg Roman as run-game coordinator and looks very run-heavy behind a better line.
- **The cap on that workload is real, and one beat writer put a number on it.** Dan Duggan (The Athletic) had Skattebo first up in team periods most days but figured it for a time share, Skattebo the 1-A and Tyrone Tracy the 1-B, with Tracy taking most two-minute snaps. Najee Harris is read as insurance for exactly this player, with Devin Singletary also in the room, and the room does not see a three-down workhorse. One commenter argues Dart's rushing threat takes goal-line and short-yardage work while Dart is the starter.
- **Market data is thin and stale** — the only price claim, from 8/07, has him in rounds 4/5 in 12-teamers, roughly where the board has him. Several drafters said flatly they are out at that cost; keeper owners at rounds 7, 8 and 12 all call it an easy hold. One commenter names him this year's sell-high.
- **Noise to discount:** Harbaugh called his energy "infectious in a bad way" after a practice penalty, beat writer Pat Leonard is pushing a story that his act is running thin, and the practice fight with Greg Newsome ended with Newsome saying "brothers fight, we were cool right after it." No depth-chart consequence in any of it.

**Draft take:** He is the fourth-round pick whose price already reflects the upside and none of the injury risk — take him only if you are comfortable holding a backfield handcuff you will probably need.
