**Mike Washington** (LV, RB, bye 13) — board rank 161 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A pure Ashton Jeanty handcuff whose price spiked on the ankle injury and has been deflating since Jeanty's Week 1 outlook improved.

- His entire role is contingent on Jeanty. He took most of the carries in Raiders practice on 8/23 after Jeanty left with an injury, with the staff praising his practice and preseason work, and he was kept as one of only three Raiders backs on the 53-man roster. Jeanty avoiding IR is what narrows his path to a starting workload.
- The direction of travel matters more than any single claim. Through late August the room had him startable in non-shallow leagues and the top waiver target in the league; by 9/1 Sanderson would pass on him now that Jeanty looks good for Week 1, preferring Tank Bigsby or Emmett Johnson for better contingent value, and by 9/3 managers are holding him only until Jeanty's Week 1 status settles, with one listing him as the drop.
- With a healthy Jeanty the room calls him a handcuff who will never threaten the backfield, expecting Kubiak to give him a few extra snaps easing Jeanty back; one dissenter puts a 4th-round rookie around 35%. The minority arguing for standalone value is modest about it: Hartitz says he could eat into Jeanty's work mainly to be annoying rather than to build value for himself, and calls him top of the handcuff pile and not more. Cooper rates him a "handcuff plus" and ranks him second among handcuffs, above Tyler Allgeier, on explosiveness and what Klint Kubiak and Rick Dennison can scheme. Murchison: "think of him as a lesser Blake Corum. He'll have a role, but not to the extent of Corum."
- The profile is a 4th-round rookie with good camp reports, highlight runs in both preseason games and nearly 8 yards a carry in preseason — against a serious fumbling problem, roughly 13 lost fumbles in two college seasons, and limited pass-catching, with Dylan Laube and Dare Ogunbowale expected to take passing downs. Skeptics note he was under 3 YPC in his last preseason game outside one long run, and that he was a real-life 122nd overall pick who did not reach 1,000 yards until his fifth college year.
- Market has been chaotic and is now settling downward. During the injury window reported prices ranged from a 5th-round pick — a 587-point comment called that an ACL-only price — through the 8th-12th round, down to last pick or still on waivers, with $27 in one auction against $33 for Jeanty. In dynasty he went round 15 of startups at RB53 before the news, briefly fetched a 2029 1st plus two 3rds, and settled at a 2027 2nd being a fair-to-generous price. Three top-level comments now treat him as a sell, and Twerski keeps him on his must-grab handcuff list but places him in the last two rounds.
- Sentiment peaked on the day of the injury — 22 top-level comments for 363 upvotes with "league winner" repeated — and skeptics called drafting him ahead of Rico Dowdle or Jaylen Warren a negative-EV swing.

**Draft take:** Draft him in the last two rounds if you own Jeanty and not otherwise — the standalone case died when Jeanty's Week 1 status stopped being in doubt.
