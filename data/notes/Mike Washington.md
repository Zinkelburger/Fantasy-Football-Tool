**Mike Washington** (LV, RB, bye 13) — board rank 161 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the handcuff of the moment — 8 top-level nominations carrying 600 upvotes — and the room knows it is a handcuff; the split is over whether he holds weekly value once Jeanty is fully back.

- Ashton Jeanty's ankle is the whole story. Per beat report, Washington took most of the carries in Raiders practice on 8/23 after Jeanty left with the injury, and the staff has been praising his practice and preseason work. Jeanty avoided IR at final cuts on 8/31, which narrows Washington's path to a starting workload rather than closing it — commenters expect a capped Week 1 for Jeanty, with one wanting an outright 60/40 or 70/30 split favouring Washington while Jeanty is eased in, and some would start Washington even if Jeanty plays.
- Role while Jeanty is out: head of a committee, not a bellcow. He is one of only three Raiders backs on the 53, with Dylan Laube and Dare Ogunbowale expected to take passing-down work. The loudest replies insist Vegas signs or trades for a veteran rather than hand a 4th-round rookie a full workload, and a dissenter puts a healthy-Jeanty Washington at about 35% of the work.
- Role once Jeanty is healthy: mostly a pure handcuff who "will never threaten the backfield," with a minority — including one commenter who compared his speed and acceleration to Achane and said he catches well, contradicting earlier can't-catch takes — arguing for a weekly role regardless. Cooper ranks him second among all handcuffs, prefers him to Tyler Allgeier, and calls him a "handcuff plus"; Hartitz says he could eat into Jeanty's work but mainly to be annoying, top of the handcuff pile and no more; Murchison: "think of him as a lesser Blake Corum. He'll have a role, but not to the extent of Corum." A Sept 3 thread prefers Emmett Johnson as the better version of the same bet, on the grounds that Kansas City is a better backfield to inherit than Las Vegas.
- The knocks are consistent: a 4th-round rookie with a serious fumbling problem (~13 lost fumbles in two college seasons) and limited pass-catching, who did not reach 1,000 yards until his fifth college year. Skeptics point at a preseason game under 3 YPC outside one long run and compare the hype to Zamir White.
- Market: the board has him at 161 and the room pays far more, erratically. Reports run from the 8th-12th round down to the last pick or waivers, with a 587-point top comment reporting a 5th-round selection the room called an ACL-only price; Jeanty drafters treat him as a must-own pair, and one auction had him at $27 against Jeanty's $33. Twerski wants him in the last two rounds. Dynasty spiked from round 15 / RB53 to rookie 2.04-2.05, and three top-level comments now treat him as a sell — one already moved him for a 2027 2nd plus a player "due to the Jeanty concerns."

**Draft take:** buy the cheapest insurance on a top-10 back you may already own — take the 8th-12th round price, never the 5th, because the standalone case dies the day Jeanty's ankle stops being a story.
