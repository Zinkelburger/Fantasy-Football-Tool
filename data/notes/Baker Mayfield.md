**Baker Mayfield** (TB, QB, bye 10) — board rank 139 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Bucs fans treat him as an unreplaceable starter entering the season healthy; the fantasy room barely drafts him.

- Health, and it is the whole story of his 2025: commenters explaining Tampa's late-season collapse say he played through a knee bone bruise and then an AC joint injury and was "a shell of himself." The flip side, from another commenter, is that he was routinely a full practice participant through a separated shoulder, a bruised heel and that knee bruise. Every recent thread premises that he enters 2026 healthy; one commenter (09-04) still asks whether he stays on the field all season.
- Role: no competition and no insurance. Rick Stroud's reporting and the 08-28 thread are entirely about who wins QB2, and Jordan Schultz reported (08-30) that Tampa gave the job to UDFA rookie Jalon Daniels over Jake Browning. The room's line is that the season is over if the 31-year-old goes down. The framing that the rookie is pressure on Mayfield got voted down — he has been openly mentoring Daniels.
- The live on-field concern, from Bucs fans (09-03): he scrambled on seemingly every play last season and kept doing it after the line solidified mid-year, which they read as a habit formed under early-season pressure rather than a protection problem now.
- The case for him: genuine first-half MVP talk last season before the injuries, improved line health, and a Week 1 shootout at Cincinnati where the room notes his career numbers against the Bengals. He is playing the last year before he needs a contract.
- Market is nearly empty — one Vegas Week 1 quarterback board has him 13th, just outside the posted top 12, which a commenter calls low for a game with a 50.5 total. Five nominations for 11 upvotes in one late-QB thread, including going undrafted in a league; the pushback is that Tampa's play-calling is among the league's most predictable.

**Draft take:** A last-round or waiver quarterback with a real Week 1 matchup — healthy and unchallenged, but nothing here argues for spending a pick ahead of the other punt options.
