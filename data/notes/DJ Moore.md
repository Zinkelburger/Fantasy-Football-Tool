**DJ Moore** (BUF, WR, bye 7) — board rank 51 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The most-nominated undervalued player in the corpus, with a real and repeated counter-case that he is a target-spread victim who has disappointed managers before.

- **Healthy.** A preseason scare against Carolina on 8/16 came to nothing — a commenter relayed OC Joe Brady saying Moore would have gone back in had it been a real game — and a 9/02 comment notes he dodged any camp injury scares.
- **Role is the case for him:** Buffalo's clear No. 1 target, reunited with Brady, who had him for two seasons in Carolina and took 31-36% of the team's receiving yards there. Ben Solak (ESPN) asked around the Bills about his usage and heard a lot about deep and intermediate-breaking routes, with Buffalo expecting to be more productive off play-action given the running game and Moore first up for downfield one-on-ones. Camp reports are described by fans as the team's best in years, and the room expects heavy 13 personnel with Moore as the lone receiver. Red-zone work is expected to go elsewhere.
- **The role is locked by money, not just the depth chart** — commenters note the dead-cap hit makes moving on before February 2028 impractical, so he is Allen's top target for at least two seasons. The caveat carried through the threads is that a healthy Khalil Shakir takes targets; one downvoted dissenter argues Moore and Shakir are the same player. One commenter also disputed the OP's claim that Buffalo extended him on a restructured deal — "The Bills did not extend DJM" — and the thread never resolved it, so treat the extension as unconfirmed.
- **The bear case is distribution, not talent.** Buffalo's shares last year: Shakir 20%, Coleman 12%, Kincaid 10%, Knox 10%; no Bills receiver has finished top-30 since Diggs' WR9 in 2023, and Allen has not thrown to one man for 1,000 yards since 2022, with touchdowns vultured by Allen and James Cook. The rebuttal is that Chicago's shares were nearly as flat (Odunze 17%, Moore 16%, Loveland 15%), so his dip was distribution rather than decline.
- **Price is moving against you.** Drafters report late 4th to 5.05 and above consolidated ADP in every draft, with concrete picks from 3.01 to 7.07 and $20+ in auctions; the two highest-voted replies (994 and 639 pts) call the platform ADP a mirage and say he is the most reached-for player they have seen. Maher has him 35th overall, 20 spots ahead of ECR. Books set his receptions line at 59.5, below Shakir's 62.5, which one bettor likes as a prop and dislikes as a fantasy signal.
- **The fade is durable and worth hearing:** a long record of disappointing managers in Carolina and Chicago, and an effort objection — "Ah, the Allen Robinson Defense" at 170 points, with commenters who watched every Bears game saying he ran wrong routes and quit on some. The popular comp is "2024 Amari Cooper with more athleticism" rather than a high-end WR1.

**Draft take:** Take him where the board has him and no earlier — the WR1 role is real and the price has already caught up, so paying a 4th-rounder for the hype is buying the room's opinion rather than the discount.
