**Dallas Goedert** (PHI, TE, bye 10) — board rank 119 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The room's favourite cheap veteran tight end this offseason, on one argument — A.J. Brown's targets have to go somewhere.

- No current injury, but the room prices his history heavily: a 158-comment thread was entirely a contingency plan for him missing time, with "if Goedert had to miss 2 games" drawing 164 upvotes. Philadelphia's reported interest in Ertz is read the same way — insurance for a Goedert injury, not a threat to his snaps, since the signing only makes sense if he goes down.
- He is the unquestioned Eagles TE1, back on a one-year, $7M deal, with blockers behind him: Mundt is the only reliable blocker and is still rated ahead of rookie Eli Stowers as a receiver, and Stowers was drafted as an eventual replacement rather than an immediate one. Commenters note Goedert himself blocked badly last year, from age or the attrition of an injury-filled career.
- The role case: with A.J. Brown gone to New England, the room expects him to be Philadelphia's second passing option behind DeVonta Smith, at least early while Makai Lemon develops, and notes Hurts leans on trusted targets. He is the Eagles' best red-zone option on passing plays and was already a consistent goal-line target when Philadelphia threw in the end zone, and he has historically produced when Brown was out. Cooper says that for the first time ever he is not fading Goedert — a clear path to being a top-2 target, because Lemon and Dontayvion Wicks are nowhere near known commodities next to Brown.
- The counters come from the same threads: touchdown regression, a feast-or-famine profile with a lower floor than Juwan Johnson, an Eagles offense that may go run-heavy, and Lemon taking the vacated share sooner than expected. One dissenter thinks Wicks, not Goedert, ends up the second option. The room also treats 2026 as likely his last year in Philadelphia.
- Market: he goes around 120th, and Stowers hype pushed him cheaper still — one manager got him in round 12 of a TE-premium startup, another dropped him outright expecting Stowers to take the job. The room now calls that one of the best cheap veteran buys of the offseason.

**Draft take:** Take him as your starting tight end in the double-digit rounds — the vacated A.J. Brown targets and the goal-line role are worth far more than pick 120, and the only real risk is the games he misses.
