**Jaylen Warren** (PIT, RB, bye 9) — board rank 61 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** favourable but shallow — a lot of role argument, one market datapoint, and no bust chatter.

- First on the Steelers' official depth chart and started the first drive of the last preseason game; Rico Dowdle was not declared the starter. With Najee Harris gone the room is Warren, Dowdle, Nichols and Homer, and Steelers fans treat Warren as the entrenched lead back.
- Passing downs are the case. He is described as one of the best pass-blocking backs in the league, Dowdle is said to be poor in protection, and Rodgers needs it — commenters expect that to convert into snaps. He also has a chance to inherit Kenny Gainwell's targets, which one Ringer host calls the game changer and the reason he prefers Warren to Tony Pollard for RB2 upside.
- The best-sourced read is a shrug. Ben Solak (ESPN), from conversations in Pittsburgh, is confident the 2026 Steelers keep the 2025 identity — running the ball out of heavy sets — and says he cannot get a lean between Warren and Dowdle, only that both will be used enough to be playable in any given week.
- The pushback: Cooper expects the workload to take a hit, noting Vegas set Dowdle's rushing-yards over/under above Warren's and that the Steelers OC said back usage would be decided week to week by game plan and blocking scheme. Older threads describe Warren as a career part-timer behind Harris — the reason Gainwell saw so many snaps — and one commenter notes he has dealt with injuries every season, though the counter is that Tomlin, whose preference for availability over efficiency kept Warren on the bench, has now stepped down.
- Market: taken as a low-end RB2 in rounds 6-7, a touch later than his board rank; the defence is that he and TreVeyon Henderson project close enough to high-end RB2s that the gap is smaller than the WR15-to-WR30 gap.

**Draft take:** worth his board rank as the depth-chart leader and pass-down back, but draft him as an RB2 — the ceiling needs Dowdle to lose the early-down split, and the reporting says that split is week to week.
