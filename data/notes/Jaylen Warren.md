**Jaylen Warren** (PIT, RB, bye 9) — board rank 61 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The named starter and, most of the room thinks, the better back — inside a committee nobody can predict week to week.

- First on the Steelers' official depth chart and started the first drive of the last preseason game; Rico Dowdle was not declared the starter.
- Ben Solak (ESPN, 8/11) says from conversations in Pittsburgh that the 2026 Steelers identity will be the 2025 one — running out of heavy sets — and that he cannot get a lean between Warren and Dowdle, but is confident both will be used enough to be playable in any given week.
- The bull case is pass protection. Warren is described as one of the best pass-blocking backs in the league, Dowdle was not good in protection, and the room expects Rodgers to reward that with snaps. A Ringer host prefers Warren to Tony Pollard specifically because Warren has a chance to inherit all of Kenny Gainwell's targets, which he calls the game changer.
- Cooper is the counter: he expects the workload to take a hit, noting Vegas set Dowdle's rushing-yards over/under higher than Warren's and that the Steelers OC said back usage would be decided week to week by game plan and blocking scheme. The Steelers also pay the two almost identically.
- The history cuts against the "entrenched lead back" framing — Warren was second string to Najee Harris for multiple seasons and is described as a part-time back, which is why Gainwell saw so many snaps last year. One commenter argues Tomlin's preference for availability over efficiency is what kept him behind plodders, and Tomlin has now stepped down. Another notes he has dealt with injuries every season.
- Market is thin: one claim has him going as a low-end RB2 in rounds 6-7, slightly later than the board rank, with one owner arguing he and TreVeyon Henderson project close enough to high-end RB2s that the gap is smaller than the WR15-to-WR30 gap.

**Draft take:** Worth the round 6-7 price as the pass-catching, pass-blocking half of a two-man room, but this is a timeshare and the beat coverage says so.
