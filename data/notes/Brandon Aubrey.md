**Brandon Aubrey** (DAL, K, bye 14) — unranked on the board · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the single exception to "never draft a kicker" — and the room knows it is reaching, and defends the reach.

- Market, and it is the whole story: against a 129.0 ADP, reported picks in one 2026-08-30 thread ran to 1.09, the 8th round (twice, both mocks), the 10th, 11.11, the 12th and the 13th. He was also reported going in the 9th round of a home league. The room routinely pays well above that ADP.
- The justification offered is numeric, not sentimental: the top two kickers were worth roughly 4 points per game over the 12th-ranked kicker last year, comparable to the 4.5 points over replacement the top two defenses gave, so paying up is defended rather than mocked. Backers also cite him as equal to WR6 or RB16 in standard scoring last season.
- Keeper and roster-management value: advisers say to hold him at a 12th-round keeper price, especially with long-kick bonuses, because he never lasts to the 13th in 10-team drafts. One commenter who drops his kicker every Tuesday to free a waiver spot makes an explicit exception — "assuming I don't have a Brandon Aubrey or similar".
- Sentiment: "sometimes he's like an extra flex player" is the room's summary, with several saying he is the only reason they would break a wait-on-kickers rule.

**Draft take:** the one kicker worth a real pick, but only in long-kick-bonus or keeper leagues — everywhere else let someone else reach in the 9th and take your kicker last.
