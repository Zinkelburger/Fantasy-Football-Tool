**Ja'Marr Chase** (CIN, WR, bye 6) — board rank 3 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A top-three pick with a knee everyone is watching — the scare has been priced in and then mostly priced back out.

- **Left knee, still managed.** He left the Aug 25 practice after landing awkwardly on an underthrown ball and hobbled off under his own power; Ben Baby reported he was back on the sideline unsupervised, walking normally, in good spirits and reenacting the landing with teammates. Chase called it "a little hyperextension" and said he could have kept practising. He sat the next day after tweaking the same knee, notably wearing no brace, which the room read as precautionary.
- The latest word from the team is limitation, not doubt: Zac Taylor told Ben Baby on Sept 1 that Chase will be limited in practice through the week but that he "feels good about him." No one has suggested Week 1 is in question, and as of Sept 3 analysts describe him simply as banged up.
- The one thing nobody has produced is imaging. The reassuring reads — hyperextension without structural damage, sprain not tear — come from commenters watching video, not from a medical source or a team release.
- Role has one small wrinkle worth a mention: a Bengals fan reports he has been working short and goal-line fades, and reads his 23-TD talk as a plan to take touchdown equity from the backs.
- Market never really moved. He briefly slid on the afternoon the news broke — reports of him going late 1st, 3rd, even 4th — but that was a one-day discount. He is going 1.01 in a lot of leagues, is FantasyPros' consensus No. 1 in PPR and Yahoo's No. 1 WR, and the board agrees at rank 3. Cooper wants whichever of Gibbs, Bijan and Chase is left at picks 1-3; Twerski has him tied with Bijan behind Gibbs; Murchison would not let him pass at 1.03 and would pay $69 of a $200 auction budget. Hartitz is the dissent, taking McCaffrey at 4 partly on the knee.
- The room pushed back hard on one drafter who passed at pick five for a fully healthy Jaxon Smith-Njigba: Chase is a take-regardless at the top of round one, and apart from one lost season has no real injury history. The underlying risk several keep naming is Burrow's health, not Chase's.

**Draft take:** Still a top-three pick — the knee is a limitation on practice reps, not on Week 1, and if a skittish league lets him slide past four, take him.
