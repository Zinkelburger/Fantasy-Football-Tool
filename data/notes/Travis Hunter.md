**Travis Hunter** (JAC, WR, bye 7) — board rank 197 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** still a two-way player, still not a startable fantasy receiver, and the room has largely stopped pretending otherwise.

- Snap count is the whole story. John Shipley (SI Jaguars) after day 9 of camp: Hunter has been the WR4 in snap count for Jacksonville. Michael DiRocco (ESPN, 8/11): 14 snaps in 11-on-11 work — 8 on defense, 6 on offense — with two catches on three targets.
- A commenter on 9/02 cites what he calls solid reporting that Hunter will not be doing much if anything at all on offense this year, and wonders whether Coen is bluffing. That is a commenter relaying reporting, not a beat report itself, but it is the most recent read on his offensive usage and it is negative.
- The earlier consensus was more generous: a real if not huge share of the slot and Z snaps, rotating with Brian Thomas as the third receiver and competing for fifth or sixth in team targets, or an F/Z on limited snaps. One OP does not expect more than 40% of offensive snaps and would not boost him above ADP even in IDP.
- The forward-looking case is a contract argument, not a usage one: a commenter says a full-time pivot to receiver in year 3 is inevitable if Hunter wants a receiver's extension, given Jacksonville's roster and cap situation — he is being taxied as a two-way player who is not yet startable.
- Market and sentiment are thin and both retrospective: one manager reports turning down Breece Hall, Chris Olave, a 2025 1st and a 2025 2nd for Hunter in last year's rookie draft and now lists him among his sell-high regrets. Three top-level predictions (~28 points) have him ending the year as the highest-ranked Jaguars receiver on KTC or simply becoming a viable fantasy WR.

**Draft take:** a redraft non-entity at WR4 snaps — leave him to dynasty managers betting on the year-3 receiver pivot.
