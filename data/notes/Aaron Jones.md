**Aaron Jones** (MIN, RB, bye 6) — board rank 99 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the room is out on the player and only defends the price — an aging back losing early-down work to Jordan Mason.

- No current injury reported. The availability case against him is age and history: 31, among the ten oldest active NFL running backs, and commenters expect him to miss multiple games and leave others early if he is treated as a starter. Mason drafters say plainly they are buying that chance.
- Role, per the room's read of Minnesota's preseason: Jones was still the first back on the field with the starting offense, and is expected to keep the passing-down and pass-protection work because Mason is described as one of the league's worst pass-blocking backs. In the one preseason game both played, carries alternated Jones-Mason-Jones-Mason-Mason-Jones. This holds only while Mason's protection stays a weakness.
- The "Mason has taken the job" story traces to a Rotowire opinion piece projecting a smaller Jones role after Mason's camp; commenters repeatedly note it carries no coaching-staff or beat-writer quote. A Vikings fan's reply is that Mason is already the starter and Jones already the backup, so there is nothing left to overtake.
- Volume, per commenters: 1,915 career touches against the ~2,000-touch cliff the room applies, and 28 catches in 12 games last season — roughly 5 PPR points a game before touchdowns. Projections in-thread range from 10-13 touches and 3-4 catches a week (an RB4) to at least 200 touches if he stays healthy.
- Market: the room drafts him in the 13th-15th round of 12-14 team leagues — RB40 by one count, pick 122 in a 14-team half PPR, second-to-last round in another, and $1 of a $200 auction budget where Mason went $6. That is well past board rank 99; several say the price makes him a free look rather than an avoid.
- Sentiment: 19 top-level comments for a combined 260 upvotes, led by a 143-point "easy avoid — old RB, bad offense, good backup who could easily overtake him." The dissent is about cost, not about the player.

**Draft take:** let him fall to the last two rounds and take him only as a cheap bet on Mason's pass protection never improving — never as a roster's RB3.
