**Geno Smith** (NYJ, QB, bye 13) — board rank 171 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Loud and split — the Jets side calls him a clear upgrade, the skeptics and his old fanbase call him washed.

- Role: he is the Jets' starter, and the room's entire Garrett Wilson case rests on Geno being the best quarterback Wilson has ever had. Supporters cite a 4,300-yard, 30-touchdown season and four straight years of 50+ QBR before a 34 QBR with the Raiders, which they blame on a broken offensive line and inept coaching (Sep 3). Jets fans call him miles better than Fields, Tyrod Taylor and Brady Cook.
- The skeptic version uses the same Raiders season: nearly 36, one good Seattle year, looked washed, and now paired with Frank Reich. Twerski says plainly that his unwillingness to draft Garrett Wilson at cost is the measure of his confidence in Geno. One commenter taking a rookie QB stash does not see Geno starting the whole year, "either due to talent or injury" (Sep 4) — an opinion, not a report; there is no injury claim on him in the corpus.
- Protection is not obviously fixed: the Jets trading for ex-Raiders center Jordan Meredith drew "reunited," "can't escape him," and "the man is gunna protect Geno again." The room rates Meredith a fringe backup guard who will nonetheless start for the Jets, so it reads as no upgrade.
- The strongest-consensus opinion on him is negative and comes from the people who watched him most: r/Seahawks overwhelmingly rejects the idea he could have replicated Darnold's season, roughly ten commenters independently calling him a bridge QB who throws red-zone interceptions with the game on the line and a poor leader who blamed receivers. The top reply (44 pts) says flatly they do not win with him. Elsewhere he is referred to only in the past tense — "Geno with the yips behind an incredibly bad offensive line," leading the league in interceptions — as the baseline Kirk Cousins has to beat.
- The dissent worth keeping: a commenter would not be shocked if he is decent, arguing last season's numbers were a coaching artifact behind what he calls the worst offensive line the league has ever seen, and a dynasty thread nominates him as likely to beat market expectations — but only if the acquisition cost is not a 2nd.

**Draft take:** Late QB2 or streamer only — the room's most settled view of him is the negative one, and that is the read the backtest says to trust.
