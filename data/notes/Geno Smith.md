**Geno Smith** (NYJ, QB, bye 13) — board rank 171 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** He matters here mostly as the input to Garrett Wilson and Breece Hall, and the room is split on whether last year was the line or the outlier.

- Role: the Jets' starter, with a commenter drafting a rookie QB stash on the view that Geno does not start all seventeen games "either due to talent or injury" (2026-09-04). That is one commenter, not a report.
- The bull case is Sanderson's, and it is conditional: he thinks Geno is better than consensus **if he is protected**, that this offensive line can do it where last year's could not, and that Geno is far better for the fantasy production around him than Fields was — which is why Sanderson holds Breece Hall and Garrett Wilson. Supporters cite a 4,300-yard, 30-TD season and four straight years of 50+ QBR before a 34 QBR in Las Vegas, blamed on a broken line and coaching.
- The protection premise is doing a lot of work and the room does not believe it. The Jets trading for ex-Raiders centre Jordan Meredith was treated as a joke at Geno's expense — "the man is gunna protect Geno again" — with the room rating Meredith a fringe backup guard who will nonetheless start.
- The fade: he is nearly 36, had one good Seattle season, and led the league in interceptions behind Las Vegas' line. r/Seahawks rejected outright the idea he could have matched Darnold's season — roughly ten commenters independently call him a bridge QB who throws red-zone interceptions with the game on the line, and the top reply (44 points) says flatly they do not win with him. Twerski frames it from the other end: his unwillingness to draft Garrett Wilson at cost *is* his confidence in Geno.

**Draft take:** Not a fantasy quarterback you start — the reason to have an opinion on him is whether you are paying up for Garrett Wilson, and the room's evidence says shade that price down.
