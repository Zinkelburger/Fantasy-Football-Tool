**CeeDee Lamb** (DAL, WR, bye 14) — board rank 15 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Split between "hurt, not bad" and "most overrated player every year" — 17 nominations in one thread drew the weakest agreement of any heavily-named player in it.

- No current injury. The room attributes his 2025 dip to a high ankle sprain and three missed games rather than decline: he was on a 98-1,400-4 pace in the games he played, with air yards and target share better than his points, and commenters note that injury historically depresses production in the season it occurs and that his touchdown variance ran against him. He is 27.
- Role is unchanged — the alpha in a pass-friendly Dallas offense with the same quarterback he has had for years, with finishes of WR5, WR1 and WR8 since 2022, a 400-plus PPR-point season on the résumé, and a WR18 last year explained by the missed games and lost touchdowns. Bulls expect a return to 7-10 TDs over 17 games.
- The live 2026 question is George Pickens, and the room did not resolve it. Pickens is named repeatedly as a target thief with Ferguson behind him; one commenter says Lamb's dominant fantasy years came on a share he will not repeat, another suggests he may not even be the best receiver on his own team, and one manager passed on keeping him in superflex specifically over the split. The bull answer is that Dallas throws at high volume behind a suspect defense and that offense supports two WR1s. A secondary worry is a Dallas run game cutting pass volume.
- Market: he goes 15th, at the back of round 1 or early round 2, and two commenters say they would pass at that cost and prefer Pickens at his cheaper ADP — "You can't lose with any of those picks, except Lamb. I don't like him for his ADP this year." Where he wins is the direct comparison with Justin Jefferson at the same price: the room takes Lamb, and the stated tiebreaker is trusting Dak more than Kyler Murray.
- Sentiment: 14 top-level fades in one thread, citing repeated failure to return a first-round cost, injuries, perceived disengagement once Dallas falls out of contention, and the Pickens split — against another thread where he was the second-most-nominated pick at 1.09 and the room's "safest" option, "the only proven one whose situation is clear."

**Draft take:** Take him at the 1/2 turn as the safest receiver in that range — last year's WR18 was three missed games and touchdown luck, and the Pickens worry is real but unproven.
