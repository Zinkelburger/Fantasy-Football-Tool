**Zach Charbonnet** (SEA, RB, bye 11) — board rank 120 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Everyone expects him back and almost nobody expects him back on time or at full speed; the argument is whether a late, slower Charbonnet takes a job Jadarian Price will already hold.

- Starting the season on PUP after tearing his ACL in January with surgery in February. GM John Schneider said at the 53-man press conference that he was always going on PUP — standard track, not ahead of schedule, and no setback reported. Camp reports within the previous month had floated a Week 1 return; the PUP move contradicted them.
- He is first eligible in Week 5. Seahawks fans put him at nine to ten months post-op in November and most commonly guess a Week 12 return after the bye, with a minimum of six games missed; the fanbase's own baseline is the last few games and a playoff run rather than early-season work. A 31-upvote comment argues February surgery makes him a bad bet even for the fantasy playoffs, and skeptics note backs returning midseason from an ACL come back with lower yards per carry and need weeks to reach speed.
- Seattle paid him — around the ninth-highest-paid back in the league — and one fan frames him as the reason the team let Kenneth Walker leave. He held roughly a 50% snap share alongside Walker, has been used at the goal line, and is proven as a pass catcher and pass protector. He is in a contract year.
- The counter is that Seattle still spent a first-round pick on Price rather than a stopgap, and that the dominant read for this season is a change-of-pace role behind Price rather than the old even split — a real timeshare only if Price disappoints, and only from whenever he is actually back. Dissenters expect Seattle's habit of splitting carries to reassert itself by the fantasy playoffs.
- The room prices him purely as roster mechanics: an 11th-to-14th-round PUP/IR stash, several taking him with a next-to-last pick or "essentially free" and recommending him over a defense or kicker. The dissent is that without an IR slot, nine weeks of a bench spot for an RB3 is not worth it. In dynasty superflex he traded below his market value in 70% of 178 deals.

**Draft take:** A last-two-picks IR stash and nothing more in redraft — if you have no IR slot, let him go, and if you do, understand you are buying November and December.
