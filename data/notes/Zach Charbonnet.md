**Zach Charbonnet** (SEA, RB, bye 11) — board rank 120 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Treated as an IR slot rather than a roster spot — the argument is only about whether he returns in time to matter.

- He starts the season on PUP. GM John Schneider said at the 53-man press conference that Charbonnet "was always going on PUP", which puts him on the standard track rather than ahead of schedule and contradicts the camp chatter from earlier in August that had floated a Week 1 return. He tore the ACL in January, with surgery around 20 February after swelling went down.
- Timeline, per the room's arithmetic on that surgery date: nine months post-op is roughly 20 November, first return eligibility is Week 5, and the most common guess is Week 12 after the bye, with a minimum of six games missed. One commenter working a 2021 study notes 64.5% of NFL backs return and average 14.5 months. The consensus read is that he is at best 80-90% by the fantasy playoffs, and a minority doubt he plays at all.
- One late-August claim relayed local beat reporters "pumping" that he is ahead of schedule with a September or October return. Treat it as unconfirmed — the same thread called a six-month ACL return a major anomaly, and Schneider's PUP comment is both later and the stronger source.
- Role on return: per Destin, he pairs with Jadarian Price and pushes Holani to third string, so his return is the threat to Price rather than to Holani. Seattle's staff is said to love him for short yardage and ball security — "guarantee you 3 yards and protects the ball" — and he took the goal-line work in 2025, when he held roughly a 50% snap share with Kenneth Walker still on the roster. He was paid to stay, around the ninth-highest-paid back in the league, and is in a contract year.
- The counter is that Seattle spent a first-round pick on Price rather than a stopgap, that Charbonnet returns late and not at full speed into a job a starting rookie already holds, and that the team has no reason to sit a productive Price for him. The dominant read is change-of-pace behind Price this year, with a real timeshare only if Price disappoints.
- Market: the room drafts him in the 11th to 14th round, later than his board rank, explicitly as a PUP/IR stash and a next-to-last pick — several say they will not spend a bench spot on him without an IR slot. Twerski's version is that he is worth the stash only because it costs a roster spot you were not using. In dynasty superflex he went below his 3,818 market value in 70% of 178 trades.

**Draft take:** Only draftable if you have an IR slot — spend a last pick there and stream the roster spot, but do not carry him on a bench through October.
