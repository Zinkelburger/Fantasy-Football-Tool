**Zach Charbonnet** (SEA, RB, bye 11) — board rank 120 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Thin and one-note — the whole discussion is about when he comes back, and the room has already repriced him as a stash rather than a roster piece.

- He is on PUP to start the season (Seattle, 2026-08-30), so he misses at least the opening stretch. The board's rank does not reflect that. He tore his ACL cleanly in January, about seven and a half months before the designation; camp reports within the prior month had floated a Week 1 return, and the PUP move overrides them.
- Return timing is a guess, trending later. Room estimates run Week 8-9 through Week 13-15, with Week 12 after the bye the most common answer and six games the floor. One commenter puts it at 6-8 weeks missed with Seattle giving vague answers, and doubts he reaches full form this season. Skeptics note backs returning mid-season from an ACL typically come back slower and need weeks to get to speed.
- The role he returns to is smaller than the one he left. The dominant read is change-of-pace work behind Jadarian Price rather than the old even split, with a real timeshare only if Price disappoints; one commenter expects a three-way split with Price and Holani. Dissenters think Seattle's habit of splitting carries reasserts itself by the fantasy playoffs. He is in a contract year.
- Market has him as a late-round stash: rounds 11-14, several taking him with their next-to-last pick, and the room prefers stashes like him over a defense or kicker with the final picks. The dissent is practical — nine weeks of a bench spot for an RB3 is not worth it without an IR slot.

**Draft take:** Only draft him if you have an IR slot; on PUP with a Week 12-ish return into a backup role, he is a last-two-picks stash and nothing you can start.
