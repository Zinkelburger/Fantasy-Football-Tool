**Tee Higgins** (CIN, WR, bye 6) — board rank 41 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Nobody is down on him, but nobody is excited either — he has flown under the radar as the safe, capped WR2 in a two-receiver offense.

- Heel contusion, limited through the week of Sept 1 per Ben Baby, with Zac Taylor saying he does not consider it a major concern for Week 1. Nobody in the room argues the injury itself is serious.
- What the news did was reactivate his history. Drafters who already took him say they are scared, one asked whether to slide him, and a heavily upvoted joke has him reliably missing four to six games mid-season before returning healthy for the fantasy playoffs. The recurring knock is that he rarely plays a full season and often plays hurt then taps out — he finished as high as WR5 in 2024 at 18.5 PPR PPG, and missed 5 games doing it.
- Role is the structural cap: the top comment in one thread argues he is worth less in fantasy precisely because Chase is there, since the Cincinnati target hierarchy limits his volume. The flip side is real contingent value — he is the direct beneficiary if Chase is limited or used as a decoy.
- Market sits right about where the board does. Yahoo has him WR14; one commenter calls a 4th-round ADP a smash; a 542-point comment named him the risk-averse alternative to Emeka Egbuka in the same range, which drew a 431-point reply joking that Higgins as the safe pick is funny in itself. Loza ranks him last of Flowers, McConkey, Waddle and Higgins, calling him a floor pick moving from a No. 2 to a No. 1 receiver overall.
- The fade is durability plus Chase-driven week-to-week volatility — 10 top-level fades in one thread, and one manager who paid WR4 value already shopping him.

**Draft take:** Fine at the back of the fourth as a stable WR2, but he is a floor pick with an injury history, not the league-winner the Chase-injury threads briefly made him.
