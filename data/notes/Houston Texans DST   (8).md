**Houston Texans DST** (HOU, DST, bye 8) — unranked on the board · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The room's second-favourite set-and-forget defense behind the Rams, and the argument against it is price, not personnel.

- The unit case, from a thread the room largely agreed with (Aug 30): All-Pro corner Derek Stingley plus Pro Bowlers Lassiter and Bullock, PFF's No. 3 safety in Jalen Pitre, the Anderson/Hunter edge duo and Pro Bowl linebacker Azeez Al-Shaair; the acknowledged weakness is defensive tackle. Houston scored about 173 fantasy points, roughly 10 a game, in 2025.
- Week 1 is milder than the reputation: a reader-built weekly team-defense projection (Sep 3) has Houston 10th of 32 at 6.9 points, one of only two teams the ranker holds well below expert consensus — though his number is only 0.4 points under theirs.
- Market: going rounds 10-11 in the drafts reported here against a 139.0 ADP. Backers say Houston single-handedly won them multiple games last year, and even the thread's critic of early defense picks concedes round 11 is not a terrible reach. An article quoted in another thread names Houston and Denver as the two defenses somebody in your league will reach for, its premise being that streaming matchups beats paying up for a name.
- The counter-case, from the same rooms: one OP listed Houston with Seattle as defenses to let your leaguemates take, and several commenters took Houston anyway rather than hunt waivers all year — "Just gimme texans and i'll be on my way". The substantive knock is that Houston's own offense caps the defense's fantasy ceiling no matter how good the unit is.

**Draft take:** If you are going to pay for a defense at all, this is the defensible version of that bet — but you are buying the unit, not the game script, so round 11 is the price, not round 9.
