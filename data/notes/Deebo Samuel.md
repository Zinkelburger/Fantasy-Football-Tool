**Deebo Samuel** (SF, WR, bye 8) — board rank 162 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the room is mostly writing him off as washed while quietly conceding he has more paths to weekly value than anything else at his price.

- He is back with San Francisco for 2026 on a one-year deal, which the room treats as settled — one commenter noted "Deebo is returning to the 49ers this season and no one said a thing" (30 upvotes). No injury is reported; a mid-August aside called him the only 49er staying healthy right now.
- Role is deliberately undefined and that is the point. David Lombardi (@LombardiHimself, 8/11) said he never saw Samuel split the position-drill portion of practice between running backs and receivers in his first 49ers stint, and Samuel did exactly that on 8/11 — a role even more versatile than last time. Commenters split him three ways: an H-back, the Z starter, and a return to the RB2/screen role.
- The two conditionals are the value. If McCaffrey misses time, commenters expect Shanahan to hand him 5-10 carries a game as a hybrid, eating into whichever backup takes over; while Kittle is out or limited coming back from the Achilles, one expects "a heavy dose of Deebo, CMC and Tonges" early. He gains target share if a receiver goes down instead. He knows and is trusted in the offense, which is why the room thinks Shanahan leans on him in the early games.
- One commenter says he has kick-return incentives written into his contract, expects him to take return work because of them, and projects near-WR2 numbers in return-yardage scoring, particularly early.
- Market is thin: one commenter still likes him at ADP but ranks him behind Stribling on camp reports, and another asks why consensus is so low on him and Diggs. Nobody reports an actual draft price.
- Sentiment is the loudest thing here and it is negative: "washed 2 years ago," "hasn't been good since 2021," "approximately 74 years old," "the corpse of Deebo," with his injury history cited as a reason 49ers receiver depth is thinner than the depth chart suggests. Rams fans in a rivalry thread pile on with no usage detail behind it. The contrarian pocket is small — a bare-name nomination at 24 upvotes as a post-150 flier, and one commenter expecting him to crush for five weeks and then fade, taking that at ADP anyway.

**Draft take:** a late-round flier whose case is entirely conditional on a 49ers injury, and the crush-then-fade read is the honest one — draft him to start early, not to hold all year.
