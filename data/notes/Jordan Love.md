**Jordan Love** (GB, QB, bye 11) — board rank 136 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** an efficiency quarterback the room believes in and a volume quarterback nobody has seen yet — the 2026 case is entirely whether LaFleur throws more.

- No reported injury. Durability is named as his top risk by a well-upvoted commenter, whose framing is that the team contends whenever he is on the field.
- **The volume case is conditional on Josh Jacobs being off the field.** Twerski calls him a solid early-season QB option specifically because Jacobs is suspended, and rates Green Bay the offense he is most confident in among Minnesota, Kansas City, Green Bay, Jacksonville and Denver. Packers fans expect his attempts to rise with no established RB1 on the roster: the rate stats have been strong while the counting stats stayed mid because LaFleur had a bellcow and wanted to run — 15th in completion percentage, 13th in yards, 14th in TDs last year; he threw 579 times in his 4,100-yard, 36-TD season and under 439 in each of the last two.
- **The coach has said the words; treat them as a plan, not a result.** LaFleur told beat reporters Green Bay will play with much more pace after finishing last in possessions per game, has talked about letting Love rip, and separately called him the best QB he has worked with at making line adjustments. On 2026-08-29 Love called the second-half plays himself off a wristband and the offense produced, with LaFleur taking back only the last drive. Green Bay has also signalled more 11 personnel, and Tucker Kraft, Christian Watson and Jayden Reed never played a snap together last year.
- The caps are specific: an offensive line commenters call suspect apart from one lineman and say will not allow MVP-level play, no true WR1, and a habit of forcing deep shots — Packers-watchers say he repeatedly missed an open Golden downfield last season.
- Market: a QB2 price. Dopp expects Green Bay to lean on his arm more than anticipated and sees top-10 in the cards (he was QB5 as a first-year starter) but still drafts him only in the QB13-16 range, an upside case that assumes Jacobs misses the season.
- Sentiment: the Packers room is loudly bullish — Micah Parsons publicly predicted an MVP, and Hasselbeck says he believes in Love "more than most", with the next step being the boring, unspectacular parts. The bear reads are efficiency without wins, a worse playoff record than Dak Prescott, and rival fans calling the optimism inherited credit from decades of Green Bay Hall of Fame quarterbacks.

**Draft take:** draft him as the best QB2 on the board while Jacobs is out — the top-10 outcome needs LaFleur to actually raise the attempts, so do not pay for it as your starter.
