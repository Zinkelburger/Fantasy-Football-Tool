**Jordan Love** (GB, QB, bye 11) — board rank 136 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the efficiency has been there for three years and the volume has not; this is a bet that Green Bay finally lets him throw.

- No injury. Durability is named as his top risk by a well-upvoted commenter, on the argument that the team contends whenever he is on the field — no specific report behind it.
- The volume case is the whole note, and it now has real support. Josh Jacobs is off the roster — Twerski says suspended — leaving no established RB1, and Matt LaFleur told beat reporters Green Bay will play with much more pace after finishing last in possessions per game. Commenters say LaFleur has already talked about letting Love rip. Green Bay has also signalled more 11 personnel with a condensed receiver room. He threw 579 times in his 4,100-yard, 36-TD season and under 439 in each of the last two.
- Everything above is conditional on LaFleur actually changing the plan, and the room says so. His 2025 counting stats were mid — 15th in completion percentage, 13th in yards, 14th in touchdowns — while the rate stats were not: ESPN Total QBR of 9th in 2023, 5th in 2024 and 3rd (72.7) in 2025. The ceiling knocks are the offensive line, which commenters say will not allow MVP-level play, and the absence of a true WR1.
- LaFleur said recently that Love is the best quarterback he has worked with at making adjustments at the line — a real skill note, traced in a feature from a first-start weakness to a strength, with Aaron Rodgers credited as mentor. In a preseason game Love called the plays himself in the second half wearing a playbook wristband and the unit produced; the Packers room's reaction was a 1,665-point thread with no dissent.
- Supporting cast is a question the room raises rather than answers: Tucker Kraft, Christian Watson and Jayden Reed never played a snap together last year, Kraft is back from injury, and the new receiver starters replace defections rather than upgrade.
- Market: he goes earlier than board rank 136 — ADP 119.4 with a reported pick in the 9th. Analysts treat him as a QB2 with upside: Twerski likes him early in the season specifically because Jacobs is unavailable and rates Green Bay the offense he is most confident in; Dopp sees top-10 in the cards (Love was QB5 as a first-year starter) but still only drafts him in the QB13-16 range.
- Sentiment is a Packers-fan bull case and should be discounted: Micah Parsons publicly predicted an MVP, and Matt Hasselbeck says he is a believer "more than most," with the next step being the boring, unspectacular parts. The hostile read from Lions fans is that his first great season will be his first.

**Draft take:** the best late-round QB2 on the board given the Jacobs vacancy — draft him in the 9th-11th as a streaming upgrade, not as a QB1 you commit to.
