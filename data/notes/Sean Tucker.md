**Sean Tucker** (TB, RB, bye 10) — board rank 180 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The roster question is settled and the answer was a pay cut — he is Tampa's third back and his own team said so with money.

- Availability: he is on the team and healthy. Greg Auman and Pewter Report had him in danger of being cut or traded on Aug 28 over a ~$3.5M cap hit for an RB3; instead, per Jeremy Fowler on Aug 30, he agreed to a one-year, $2.5M fully guaranteed deal to stay in Tampa Bay after being due $3.52M on a restricted tender, and he made the 53. The room reads that as a demotion, not security — roughly a 30% pay cut taken after free agency dried up, with the Bucs making clear he is not a priority.
- Role: third back behind Bucky Irving and Kenny Gainwell, getting "a sprinkle" of the work while both are healthy. His path to touches is an injury to one of them and nothing else — one commenter's summary is that Tampa's actions define him: he already had his opportunity, they signed Gainwell, and they restructured his contract to save money.
- The goal-line job is the one thing that made him fantasy-relevant and it is not secure. He scored seven rushing touchdowns last season and took snaps inside the five from Irving, but the room's explanation is Irving's shoulder injury rather than any short-yardage skill, and they do not expect it to repeat with Irving healthy and more dynamic in the red zone. Hartitz says it has become "a bit more iffy" whether he keeps that role — which is exactly why Irving's own price improved.
- Pass protection is the stated cap on his usage: commenters rewatched a game and saw him miss his man on a blitz that got the quarterback hit, and argue it limits the situations Tampa can use him in. Beyond that, with Tucker on the field the run play was telegraphed, because he is not there to block on play action. One dissenter calls the missed blocks overblown, and supporters cite elite burst and home-run speed.
- Sentiment: he was cut in five top-level comments in one thread, and the owners still holding say they are waiting only on a trade out of Tampa or an injury to another Bucs back. DynastyFF is bearish — the consensus reply to the signing is that the sub keeps wish-casting him as an RB1 somewhere and it is "probably not in the cards". He is the room's own benchmark for wishful thinking: one 150-yard game a year and nothing in the other sixteen, and nobody can tell which week.

**Draft take:** Not draftable outside deep leagues — he is the third back on his own team's cap sheet, and even the goal-line role that made him interesting is expected to go back to a healthy Bucky Irving.
