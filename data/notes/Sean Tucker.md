**Sean Tucker** (TB, RB, bye 10) — board rank 180 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the roster question got settled and the role question got settled against him.

- He is on the team. Jeremy Fowler reported on 2026-08-30 that Tucker agreed to a one-year, $2.5M fully guaranteed deal after being due $3.52M on a restricted tender, and a commenter confirms he made the 53. That ended a real scare — Greg Auman and Pewter Report had him in danger of being cut or traded on 08-28, with the room expecting a cut-and-re-sign to the practice squad.
- The room reads the contract as a demotion, not security: roughly a $1M, 30% pay cut taken after free agency dried up, which commenters say makes plain he is the third back behind Bucky Irving and Kenny Gainwell. A correction on 09-04 confirms Tucker, not Chris Rodriguez, is the third man in that committee.
- Pass protection is the stated cap on his usage and the argument is specific: commenters rewatched a game and saw him miss his man on a blitz that got the quarterback hit, and note the run play is telegraphed with him on the field because he is not there to block on play action. One dissenter calls the missed blocks overblown. The room's own summary is an explosive, decisive runner held to RB3 by blocking and hands.
- The goal-line role was his only standalone value and it is going away. He scored seven rushing touchdowns last season and took snaps inside the five, but commenters attribute that to Irving's shoulder injury rather than any short-yardage skill, and Hartitz says it has become "a bit more iffy" that he keeps it — the reason Irving's price improved. Beyond that, Tampa added Gainwell and restructured his deal to save money, leaving an injury ahead of him as the only path to touches.
- Sentiment: five top-level comments cut him, and the holders say they are waiting only on a trade out of Tampa or a Bucs injury. DynastyFF is bearish and calls the recurring RB1 wish-casting "probably not in the cards", framing him as a deep-league hold and a shallow-bench cut.

**Draft take:** not a draft pick — a waiver name to remember the week Bucky Irving's shoulder shows up on an injury report.
