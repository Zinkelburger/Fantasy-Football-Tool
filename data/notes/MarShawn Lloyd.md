**MarShawn Lloyd** (GB, RB, bye 11) — board rank 189 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The most-traded name in fantasy for a week straight — a back with seven career touches who inherited a starting job, and a room that does not believe he can keep it upright.

- Healthy right now, and that is the whole story. Commenters say he has been healthy through camp, looked good in the preseason, and appears fully recovered; Maher's counterpoint is that we have barely seen him in an NFL uniform and his positive scouting impressions date to the Senior Bowl over two years ago. The dynasty room is blunter — "made of glass", seven career touches, specialist visits for compensation issues dating back to 2020, and one comparison to Trey Benson with even less on-field evidence.
- His value exists because Josh Jacobs is on the commissioner exempt list, and it evaporates if that changes. With Jacobs out, the consensus is Lloyd gets first crack as the lead back; Green Bay traded for Kaleb Johnson the same day, which most read as a depth move, and Chris Brooks mixes in. Others expect a three-way committee with no true RB1.
- There is a case that survives Jacobs's return. A camp report has Lloyd with the freshest legs in the NFL and fitting the outside-zone scheme Green Bay is now running, expected to see real usage even with a healthy Jacobs; a Packers fan adds that no serious competition was brought in for the RB2 job and there is an easy contract out on Jacobs after this season. Twerski expects him to hold a role either way, as long as he stays healthy, and would start him over Jadarian Price in Week 1.
- Board and room are wildly apart and the room is where the price is. The board still has him at 189. Pre-news he went rounds 13-15 or for $1-2 in auctions; the day the news broke he went rounds 9-11 and for $28 in one auction, and drafters have since taken him in rounds 6-8. Maher has him RB26 and 66th overall, 23 spots ahead of ECR; Twerski and Hartitz land around the 6th-7th; Murchison says the price has passed what he will pay and tells owners to sell into the excitement. A vocal group calls round 6 far too high and says round 10 or later.
- The sell-high framing is the most useful thing in the file. He is the single most-traded player DynastyDealer has recorded — 2,265 deals in seven days, roughly double the next name — trading at fair value, with owners flipping him for DJ Moore, David Montgomery and KC Concepcion. Four separate comments name him as the sell-high, comparing him directly to Trey Benson a year ago.

**Draft take:** Start him Week 1 if you have him, but he is a sell, not a hold — the job is Jacobs's the moment Jacobs is reinstated, and the market is paying you round-6 money for a back with seven career touches.
