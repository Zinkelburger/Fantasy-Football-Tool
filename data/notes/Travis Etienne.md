**Travis Etienne** (NO, RB, bye 8) — board rank 32 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Treated as the Saints' lead back at a mid-round price, with the only serious argument being how much Alvin Kamara takes back once he is healthy.

- New Orleans listed him co-first-team with Alvin Kamara on the depth chart (8/30), which is why the room says pay a premium for the upside but not bellcow pricing yet. His four-year contract is read as the team locking him in as the long-term lead back.
- Kamara is the condition on almost every bullish claim. Several of the strongest role reads explicitly assume Kamara opens the season out or on limited snaps. Murchison makes the counter directly: Etienne looks intriguing right now only because the backs behind him are hurt, and the original worry returns once Kamara is back and the third-down role shrinks — he has not been selecting Etienne much.
- Kellen Moore's offense is the other half of the case: heavy screen usage, designed work for backs, the best offensive line Etienne has played behind, and no meaningful depth behind him. The counter raised in the same thread is that Moore's offenses target running backs at a lower rate than Liam Coen's did.
- Per Jeff Nowak (8/11), Etienne had a few nice moments in Saints practice including an effective 10-plus-yard screen in the final red zone set — on the day Audric Estime got his first extended run with the 1s.
- A heavily upvoted correction worth carrying: Jacksonville letting him walk says nothing about their view of him or of Bhayshul Tuten, because the Jaguars were already over next year's cap and could not carry a premium back's contract.
- Market: Twerski calls him the RB13-24 pick with the most likely upside at ADP and the mid-round back he is most confident in, ahead of Swift and Tuten; Dopp says the Skattebo/Etienne/Swift range in rounds 5-6 is the latest he would wait for an RB1; Hartitz wants him as an RB2 at worst. Drafters report taking him at or just after pick 32 and as late as picks 45-63 — the room mostly pays a little later than the board rank.
- Sentiment: 11 top-level "too low" nominations and roughly 652 upvotes in one thread. The fade case is efficiency — one OP's 382-point-objected-to argument is that Etienne has been among the league's least efficient rushers over two seasons, less efficient than Kamara, with 2025 value resting on receiving and 6 of 13 touchdowns coming through the air.

**Draft take:** A fine RB2 in the fifth, not a bell cow price — the volume case shrinks the week Kamara is back on the field.
