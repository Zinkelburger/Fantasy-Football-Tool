**Tyrone Tracy** (NYG, RB, bye 8) — board rank 121 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** His stock fell through the offseason — the room's favorite unsexy value in early August, an easy drop by early September.

- His value is Cam Skattebo insurance, and Skattebo is healthy and handling three downs — which leaves Tracy with no standalone role and, now, real roster risk. After New York signed Najee Harris, two commenters (09-03) call Tracy the Giants' RB3 and an easy drop. An 08-23 claim already had him on the chopping block ahead of Devin Singletary as cuts approached, with another listing him among backs Vegas could sign off waivers.
- Role: contested and trending down. A Giants fan (09-03) says the coaches who drafted him are gone and that Brian Daboll's replacement staff favors Singletary — Tracy is the most explosive of the four backs, but the work gets spread. A Ringer host, pushed on Tracy "not even being the backup at this point," said he still thinks Tracy ends up playing ahead of Singletary and Harris, but that the split is fluid.
- The strongest source on the page is older and now stale: Dan Duggan (The Athletic, 08-11) had Tracy taking the majority of two-minute snaps and projected a time share with Cam Skattebo the 1-A and Tracy the 1-B.
- The two-sided case: 1,000+ all-purpose yards in each of his two seasons, a 150-yard rookie game, and 14+ carries every week but one from Week 10 on after Skattebo went down — against four rookie fumbles, only three 20-yard runs and poor blocking, including a missed block that got Jaxson Dart flattened, cited as part of why the Giants added a better pass protector.
- The stated exit condition: he becomes droppable if Harris or Singletary start taking the work behind Skattebo.
- Market is all from 08-07, before the Harris signing — a 409-point comment calling him "such a great unsexy pick," one bestball drafter reporting him as his second-highest-owned player across ~95 drafts, and expectations his price would rise. Nothing has repriced him upward since.

**Draft take:** The value case died with the Najee Harris signing — with Skattebo healthy he is a third back on a staff that did not draft him, worth a last-round pick only if you own Skattebo.
