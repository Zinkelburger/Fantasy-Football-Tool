**Kimani Vidal** (LAC, RB, bye 7) — board rank 186 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The room agrees on exactly what he is — the real Omarion Hampton handcuff, with no standalone value.

- Everything here depends on Hampton, and nothing in the corpus reports Hampton as hurt. Murchison puts it flatly: Vidal "only has value if Hampton is out." A 09-04 commenter goes further and calls him the useless one of the Vidal/Keaton Mitchell pair until and unless Hampton goes down.
- Role: the Chargers' RB3, and consensus is that he will not be used much with all three backs healthy; his plausible jobs are short yardage or spelling Hampton for a breather.
- If Hampton does miss time he steps into a sizeable grind-it-out role while Keaton Mitchell stays a change-of-pace, home-run back rather than becoming a must-start. He is getting good pass-protection buzz, one poster says he looked smoother than Hampton in relief last season, and one owner regrets cutting him last year for exactly this reason.
- The dissent is about how much even that is worth: one commenter says he would still not start Vidal if Hampton went down, as happened in 2025.
- Sentiment, one claim and an outlier: a commenter predicts he scores the most fantasy points of any Chargers back, expecting the veterans rather than Hampton to carry that backfield.

**Draft take:** Hampton is healthy and this is a pure contingency — draft Vidal only if you own Hampton.
