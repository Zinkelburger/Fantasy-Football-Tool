**Alec Pierce** (IND, WR, bye 13) — board rank 94 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Genuinely split — the health scare is resolving and the depth chart is his, but a loud group thinks a 20-plus ADOT deep threat cannot turn a WR1 role into weekly points.

- Health has turned. He missed training camp with an ankle/heel issue, was activated off PUP on Aug 27 (Schefter), returned to practice Tuesday Sept 1 per Colts writer Jake Arthur, and by Sept 3 one analyst had him back at full practice. Later commenters still calling him "banged up" are lagging the practice report, not adding to it.
- The room's caveat is worth keeping: coming off PUP is a starting point, not a clearance, and several expect a slow ramp or a missed early game while he gets back to cutting and contact. One commenter who says he performs the procedure put soft-tissue ankle cleanups at 6-12 weeks and bony work at about six months, had Pierce roughly 5.5 months out, and expects him to be fine; another noted he has missed only three games in four years.
- With Michael Pittman gone he is the depth-chart WR1 and the favorite to lead Indianapolis in receiving yards and touchdowns, on a 4yr/$114M deal ($84M guaranteed) that commenters say carries a top-five cap hit at the position. Backers see a widening route tree and steadier week-to-week usage; dissenters point out his ADOT has been 20-plus two straight years, he has never been a target earner at any level, and that Josh Downs and Tyler Warren project ahead of him in targets.
- Keenan Allen is the other variable. One commenter expects Allen to absorb the outside work Pierce was paid for; the more upvoted reply says Allen is a slot player now and that his underneath and third-down volume pulls coverage off Pierce's deep routes. Unresolved, and it only matters if Allen actually lines up outside.
- Market and board agree here rather than disagree: the top take in one thread (217 pts) is that his ADP fall makes him a value in ESPN rounds 8-9, with one drafter getting him at 88 — right about where the board has him.
- Sentiment is the weakest column and the loudest. He appears on several fade lists with no reasons attached, and asked for a player who is cooked whose ADP does not show it, a Ringer host answered Alec Pierce. The bull case is mostly bare nominations plus "he'll be great after the first couple of weeks."

**Draft take:** Take him in the 8th as the WR1 on a thin depth chart with the ankle behind him, but expect a slow first fortnight and do not pay up on the WR20 upside case — the target-share math has never supported it.
