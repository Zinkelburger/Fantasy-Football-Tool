**Kyler Murray** (MIN, QB, bye 6) — board rank 153 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Named the Vikings' starter and treated as merely adequate — "the definition of mid" — but a clear upgrade on last year's Minnesota quarterbacking.

- Availability is the room's central objection, and it drives the fade on his receivers too: one full season in the last five, roughly 10 games a season over the last four, and 12.75 passing TDs averaged across them. He is 29, and a commenter notes he has played more than 11 games once in four years. The detailed rebuttal counts only three injuries in seven seasons — a torn ACL spanning two years, a two-game ankle sprain, and a foot injury that commenter reads as a pretext for a benching — and argues he is no more injury-prone than any quarterback.
- The job is settled. He was named the Vikings' starter per ESPN sources (Aug 11); the room read it as a foregone conclusion from side-by-side drill clips and noted Minnesota did not keep up the illusion of a competition after one preseason game. Earlier, Will Ragatz had him "the heavy favorite to eventually win" it (Aug 9), and HC Kevin O'Connell said that day that Murray is ahead of where he expected on the mental side of the offense — a coach's read on progress, not a performance report.
- Bull case is rushing plus structure: every quarterback in the last decade who ran five times a game across 16-plus games finished top 10, he was on pace for about 100 carries last year, and he has finished top-10 in every season he played 14 games, including a top-3. Career 17-game pace is 4,622 total yards and 30 total TDs, and the Minnesota roster is the best he has had.
- Bear case is equally specific: in 2025 he threw over 70% of his attempts under 10 air yards and had the league's worst deep-ball success rate at 26.3%. Post-ACL he bails on clean pockets, will not step up and drive timing routes, carries a very low depth of target and has become a low-200s yards-per-game passer. He lost the Arizona job to Jacoby Brissett, who made that same offense explosive overnight. The room also flags that Minnesota hired a run-game coordinator and drafted a fullback intending to run more, and that a foot injury may have cost him quickness — without the legs he is a bottom-tier fantasy QB.
- His vet-minimum Minnesota deal is not evidence he is finished: Arizona still owes him $36.8M this season and any new salary only offsets that, so signing for the minimum costs him nothing. Two heavily upvoted comments correct the OP on exactly this.
- Market: about QB17 at pick 158, and as late as round 12 in 10-team leagues, which several call close to free given a history of QB4-QB12 per-game finishes. He was called the best ADP value on the board all summer in best ball at QB18, and Cooper says that in the late-QB range he "mostly just focused on getting Kyler Murray."

**Draft take:** Rushing upside at a QB17 price with a real top-10 season if he plays 15 games — take him as the cheap half of a two-quarterback build, never as your only one.
