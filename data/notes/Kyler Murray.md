**Kyler Murray** (MIN, QB, bye 6) — board rank 153 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Treated as the definition of mid and drafted anyway, because the price is nearly free and the legs still count.

- Role: he is Minnesota's starter over J.J. McCarthy, signed for about $1.3M. Will Ragatz reported on 08-09 that despite ups and downs he "remains the heavy favorite to eventually win" the job, and HC Kevin O'Connell said the same day that Murray is probably ahead of where he expected him to be on the mental side of learning the offense. The room now treats the job as his.
- The risks are specific: Minnesota hired a run-game coordinator and drafted a fullback intending to run more, and he played through a foot injury last year and may have lost quickness — without his legs, commenters say, he is a bottom quarterback. On health more broadly the detailed rebuttal counts three injuries in seven seasons and argues he is no more fragile than any QB, though managers still avoid Justin Jefferson over it.
- The upside is the pairing: his scramble-drill and downfield tendencies fit Jefferson and Jordan Addison, and Twerski is buying him feeding Jefferson given the quality of quarterbacks Jefferson has succeeded with — while ranking the Vikings offense last of the five he covered. The skeptics are blunt: cannot play on schedule, cannot throw over the middle, a long-declining deep ball, never consistently good at 29.
- Market: around QB17 near his board rank, and as late as round 12 in 10-team leagues, which several call close to free for a quarterback with a history of QB4-to-QB12 per-game finishes. Cooper "mostly just focused on getting Kyler Murray" in the late-QB range as the cheap upside half of a two-QB build.

**Draft take:** Take him as your only quarterback in round 12 or later — the rushing floor and Jefferson make him a top-12 outcome at a bottom-20 price, and the run-heavy staff is the reason not to pay a round earlier.
