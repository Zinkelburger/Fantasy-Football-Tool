**George Holani** (SEA, RB, bye 11) — board rank 183 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Camp-hype waiver name the room mostly does not believe, and every claim about him is conditioned on Zach Charbonnet being out.

- Role, and its condition: while Charbonnet is out, Holani is Seattle's back behind Jadarian Price. The Sept 4 consensus grants the camp hype and is not sold on it, rating him a weaker threat to Price's touches than Chris Rodriguez is to Tuten's.
- The one report (Aug 31) has him getting a heavy dose of work behind Price, which the room reads as third-down and clear-passing-down usage on his pass protection and system knowledge — conditional on Price's pass-protection development and Charbonnet's return from PUP. The context against it: he was the RB2 through last season's playoff run and got nine touches across those three games.
- The loudest bull claim and its rebuttal sit in the same thread (Aug 30): a commenter claiming heavy Seahawks-media exposure insists Holani starts the first couple of games as the trusted veteran who knows the system and can pass protect against New England, capping Price near 35-50% of snaps early. The room largely rejected it — 25 career carries, and he ran with the 2s all preseason.
- Analyst interest is real but small: Murchison lists him among deep RB stashes for early-season flexibility while Charbonnet is out and says of that group "Holani has the most immediate value"; Loza names him her hottest waiver target after Week 1.
- Sentiment is a fade despite the drumbeat: "No one is buying Holani" drew 20 upvotes, holders report dropping him or leaving him on waivers, and one calls him a one-week stash to drop for a kicker. He is also the third name in a Seattle committee the room expects to be a mess to own.

**Draft take:** Waiver name, not a draft pick — let someone else spend the roster spot and claim him in Week 2 if the snaps are actually there.
