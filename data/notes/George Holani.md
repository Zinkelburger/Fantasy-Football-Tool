**George Holani** (SEA, RB, bye 11) — board rank 183 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** an offseason drumbeat about a role, and a room that has read the drumbeat and refuses to buy it.

- Zach Charbonnet is out, and every version of the Holani case is conditional on that. An NBC Sports fantasy item on 8/31 reported the Seahawks backfield will have a "heavy dose" of Holani, with a beat reporter posting separately on his role; commenters read that as third-down and clear-passing-down work on his pass protection and system knowledge, and expect him to open ahead of or alongside rookie Jadarian Price because a rookie will not be asked to start against a top-5 defensive line. That read is also conditional on Price's pass-protection development and Charbonnet's return from PUP.
- The strongest form of the claim came from one commenter claiming heavy Seahawks-media exposure (8/30): Holani starts the first couple of games as the trusted veteran, capping Price near 35-50% of snaps early. The room largely rejected it, noting he ran with the 2s all preseason.
- The rebuttal to the reported goal-line role is his record: an undrafted third-year back with 28 career touches, 83 yards at 3.3 ypc, 3 catches for 14 yards and exactly one career carry inside the 10. He was the RB2 through last season's playoff run and got nine touches across those three games, though he did play real snaps in the NFCCG (3 catches, 27 yards) and the Super Bowl. Supporters note the staff likes him and he has a full season plus two preseasons in the system.
- By 9/04 the room treats him as a weaker threat to Price's touches than Chris Rodriguez is to Tuten's, and as the third name in a Seattle committee they expect to be a mess to own.
- Market: he costs a last-round pick — 197th, free on most waiver wires — and several make exactly that the argument for him. Others invert it: if the reported goal-line role were real he would not be going this late, which they treat as evidence against the report.
- Sentiment: "No one is buying Holani" drew 20 upvotes, several holders have dropped him, one calls him a one-week stash to drop for a kicker. Against that, he is actively being added off waivers in real leagues, one manager dropped Brian Robinson to join the hype, and Loza names him her hottest waiver target after Week 1.

**Draft take:** free is the right price for a reported role nobody in the room believes — take him in the last round or off waivers, and let Week 1 settle it.
