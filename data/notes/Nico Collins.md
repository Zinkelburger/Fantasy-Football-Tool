**Nico Collins** (HOU, WR, bye 8) — board rank 26 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Unanimous on the role — the unquestioned Houston alpha — and the discount attached to him is for durability and the offense around him, not for anything he does.

- Healthy, with durability as the standing knock: he has never played a full season, one commenter counts roughly 20 games missed in five seasons ("dinged up a lot") against 6 in 4 for Drake London, and the room repeats a 2-4 games-a-year expectation. He was the WR1 in 2024 until he went down.
- The role is as consolidated as it gets. Three straight top-10 WR finishes by points per game (WR7, WR7, WR9), 15.1 PPG in 2025, and over a quarter of Houston's receiving yards — with 15-point weeks still coming when Davis Mills was starting. He has lined up at X on roughly 80% of snaps and in the slot about 20% over the last three seasons; the room's upside case is that ~15% more slot time puts him in overall WR1 contention, with a top-5 season the realistic ceiling given the quarterback. The 8/26 practice report has him as the clear focal point of the passing game, hit multiple times by Stroud.
- Targets are consolidating further right now, and temporarily: Tank Dell is out at least four games and Jayden Higgins is hurt, leaving Kayshon Boutte as effectively the only other listed receiver. Bears say his share drops back when Dell returns; one commenter who would have called him a sell-high before the Higgins injury now says he would only sell after a big game.
- The bear case is entirely environmental — a bad offensive line limiting the deep shots he is best at, a run-heavy low-play-volume offense with David Montgomery added, and C.J. Stroud. An 88-point fade notes his best weeks last year came with Davis Mills throwing; the rebuttal is that Houston being down to four healthy receivers makes him a steal.
- Market: he goes 26th and the room pays about that — late 2nd to early 3rd in 12-team, end of round 3 in a 10-teamer, right around Chris Olave at the 2/3 turn, with the room split on the order. The recurring read is that the Stroud discount is already in the price: managers report buying him for Marvin Harrison plus a 1st, and for Emeka Egbuka plus a 2027 2nd, at 129 upvotes' worth of agreement that he is a mispriced top-5-upside WR. Twerski takes him first overall in a one-year keeper ahead of Olave and Jeremiyah Love. One commenter calls A.J. Brown going ahead of him on Sleeper backwards, given Collins is in his physical prime without the aging questions.
- Asked to choose between Collins and George Pickens at 2.11 the room leans Collins, the stated reason being that he is the unambiguous WR1 on his own depth chart.

**Draft take:** Take him at the 2/3 turn — you are paying a discount for C.J. Stroud and for games missed on a receiver who has finished top-10 by PPG three years running and currently has almost no competition for targets.
