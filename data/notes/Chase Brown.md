**Chase Brown** (CIN, RB, bye 6) — board rank 17 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Nobody disputes the job; the entire argument is whether the ceiling is top-five or merely top-ten, and it turns on Joe Burrow.

- Workload is unthreatened. Bengals fans call running back the team's worst position group — an aging Samaje Perine in a niche role, Tahj Brooks not panning out — and the room expects "another 300 touch season". He out-touched Perine 115 to 62 over the last six games of 2025.
- The receiving role is what separates him: 88 targets last year, top-5 among running backs, against Kenneth Walker's 36. Ben Solak (ESPN) saw multiple Bengals snaps of Brown in the slot and flying across the formation in jet motion, and notes 45 slot snaps and 25 out wide in 2025, with Cincinnati preferring to use him as a pass catcher rather than a pass protector. That makes him script-proof in a high-volume passing offense behind a bad defense.
- Every top-five case is conditioned on Burrow. He was fine in Week 1 last year, dropped off for eight weeks after Burrow's surgery, then averaged 22.3 PPG as the overall RB2 from weeks 13-18 on roughly a 63% snap share once Burrow returned. Joe Flacco having a full offseason with the team is the stated mitigation.
- The ceiling caps are real and come from his own fan base: Zac Taylor's system will never be run-heavy, the back is at best the third most important skill player behind Ja'Marr Chase and Tee Higgins, and Brown has not been a home-run threat. He averaged 13.6 carries a game in 2025 and still finished a top PPR back, which the room offers as proof RB1 scoring does not need a bellcow share. Cincinnati also did nothing to improve a questionable offensive line, and the thread's read is that Bengals receiver injury news is not a Brown boost — he is better off with Chase and Higgins healthy.
- Contract context, for dynasty only: he plays 2026 as a pending unrestricted free agent, and the fan room broadly expects Cincinnati not to extend him, citing his 5th-round pedigree, his age, the Joe Mixon extension and the cap going to Amarius Mims and a Burrow deal.
- Market: he goes around 16th overall, matching the board, and reports have him off the board ahead of Saquon Barkley, Achane and Josh Jacobs about 90% of the time, though ESPN boards reportedly take Omarion Hampton first. Hartitz names Brown, James Cook and Barkley his big three from the late-first/second-round cluster and ranks Brown ahead of Walker and Hampton; Murchison has been pounding the table on him all summer; Dopp gives him a real shot at a top-5 finish, citing over 21 PPG from Week 8 on, RB12 two years ago and RB8 last year. The dissent is that the hype is unearned — long touchdown droughts and good games mostly against bad defenses.
- Sentiment is unusually self-aware: thirteen top-level fades from commenters who say outright that he is correctly valued, has no competition for carries and will see light boxes, and still cannot bring themselves to draft him.

**Draft take:** Take him at the 1-2 turn as the safest receiving-back bet in that tier, and price the top-five outcome as a bet on Burrow's health rather than on Brown.
