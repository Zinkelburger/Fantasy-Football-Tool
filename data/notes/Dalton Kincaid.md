**Room sentiment:** The room likes the player and does not trust the body — every argument here is about availability, not ability.

- He is healthy as of 2026-09-03, and the nomination in that thread says health is his only issue. One commenter did ask about a pinky injury and got no answer. Against that, another notes his games played have trended down every year of his career and prefers Freiermuth or Juwan Johnson on health grounds alone.

- Snap-share risk is smaller than it looks. Dawson Knox only shares the field in two-tight-end sets and is called irrelevant to Kincaid's snaps if the knee holds; the real snap drag is that Kincaid leaves the field on run downs because he does not block well.

- Market: Hartitz rates him around TE10 and wants him or Isaiah Likely in the round 7-10 range as the fallback if he missed on Bowers or Loveland early; Cooper puts him in the tier he waits for in 10-12 team leagues after taking seven to nine backs and receivers first. One report has him going in the 11th, purely as Kittle insurance.
- Sentiment is warm — 50 upvotes on "really feeling a Kincaid year", with drafters citing glowing beat reports all summer and Josh Allen's passer rating when targeting him. The counter is burnout: low floor, no boom weeks, hurt every year.

**Draft take:** Take him around round 8-9 as a mid-tier TE1 if you punted the position, and budget for two or three missed games rather than pretending they will not happen.
