**Jadarian Price** (SEA, RB, bye 11) — board rank 79 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A first-round rookie with the Seattle backfield to himself for now, and a room that mostly disbelieves the one beat projection that says otherwise.

- **He is healthy, and the availability that matters is Zach Charbonnet's — Charbonnet is out on PUP rehabbing an ACL and Price has the job while he is.** Price missed a couple of early-August practices with leg soreness (HC Mike Macdonald, via Jeremy Fowler: not serious, back in a few days) and returned limited on 8/11 per Corbin K. Smith. He took no preseason carries at all, and Seattle's staff said that was because they lacked the depth to risk an injury to him — the opposite of a committee signal. He has an Achilles tear in his college history.
- **Nobody disputes he is the lead back on paper; the fight is over how much of the backfield he gets.** Seattle took him 32nd overall and GM John Schneider said they stayed put specifically for him because he stood alone on their board. George Holani is the only real competition and has 25 carries in two seasons, with one career carry inside the 10. Emanuel Wilson mixes in, and AJ Barner is the tush-push option.
- **The caps are real and both are named.** His pass protection is unproven, and the room expects a rookie to lose third downs over it until he is dependable. And Macdonald's history is committee: he told Fowler in August the rotation would feature "two, possibly three guys in a game plan", while adding that Price will be hard to keep off the field and "doesn't feel like a rookie". Kenneth Walker and Charbonnet split all of 2025 and were both largely unstartable.
- The one concrete projection is a beat writer's, and the room rejected it almost unanimously: Destin has Price on first and second downs with Holani on third downs, short yardage and the goal line, and pairs it with 200 carries for 850 yards, 25 catches for 190 and 8 total TDs — about RB25 last season. That drew 28 top-level comments and 1,009 upvotes against it, led by "might be the steal of the draft, I refuse to believe this" (854). An NBC Sports item on 8/31 separately called for a "heavy dose" of Holani; posters reading both land on roughly 55/45, or 50-30-20 with Wilson.
- Context: 280 carries for 1,692 yards and 21 TDs at Notre Dame while splitting with Jeremiyah Love, camp reports that he "looked like a WR out of the backfield", and Arizona and Washington in Weeks 2-3.
- **Market: board rank 79 is well behind the room.** Reported snake prices cluster in rounds 4-7 of 10- and 12-team drafts — picks 54, 60, 65-68 — and he is climbing toward the 4/5 turn, going roughly a full round ahead of Bhayshul Tuten. Auctions run $13 to $39 by format. Maher names him his favourite upside RB in the RB13-24 range and credits the missed preseason for keeping the price sane; Murchison trusts him only as an RB3 because of the expected split.
- The room's caveat is uniform: happy at cost, nervous about Charbonnet's return, several saying to sell four or five weeks in.

**Draft take:** Draft him in the 5th as a starter for the first half of the season, with a plan to sell before Charbonnet is back rather than after.
