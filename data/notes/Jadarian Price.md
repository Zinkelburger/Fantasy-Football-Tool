**Jadarian Price** (SEA, RB, bye 11) — board rank 79 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Split but leaning bullish — a first-round back with the job by default, priced like the room already knows it.

- **Health is the settled question, not the job.** He missed a week-plus of camp with leg soreness — Jeremy Fowler relayed Mike Macdonald calling it not serious with a return expected in days — and Corbin K. Smith had him back on a side field in limited work on 8/11. He then took no preseason snaps; commenters divide on whether that was a real setback or Seattle protecting a known starter, and one flags an Achilles tear in his college history. Nothing since 9/02 says he is limited.
- **He has the Seattle backfield to himself only while Zach Charbonnet is out.** Every role claim carries that condition: Charbonnet is expected back, the staff likes him, and a return cuts Price's share late in the season.
- **Draft capital is the room's anchor, and the coach's language is the caution.** GM John Schneider said Seattle's trade-down options fell apart and they stayed at No. 32 specifically for Price, who "stood alone" on their board. But Macdonald told Fowler the back rotation will feature "two, possibly three guys in a game plan" — while adding that Price will be hard to keep off the field and "doesn't feel like a rookie." Fowler also reported he had "highly impressed" Seattle in camp. Kenneth Walker signed elsewhere; George Holani (25 carries in two seasons) is the only other back.
- **Third downs are not his.** He has no passing-down work in his profile and unproven pass protection, so the room expects Holani on obvious passing downs until that changes. AJ Barner is Seattle's tush-push and goal-line option, which caps the touchdown share.
- **Analysts split on the ceiling:** Maher calls him his favourite upside RB in the RB13-24 ADP range and notes the missed preseason kept his price sane; Murchison says he will deliver RB2 weeks but Macdonald's committee habit caps consistency, so he only trusts him as an RB3; Twerski is lukewarm and would rather spend the pick on Burden or Kraft.
- **The room pays more than the board says.** He is going around picks 65-68 in 12-team leagues since the Charbonnet news and is climbing toward the 4/5 turn, a full round ahead of Bhayshul Tuten — so treat the board rank as stale on price, not as a discount. Weeks 2-3 draw Arizona and Washington, which the room reads as a hot start.
- **Sentiment is 50/50:** bulls call an RB24-25 finish his floor as the starter on the defending champion, bears say a 5th-6th round price is steep for a back who did not play much in college.

**Draft take:** Draft him at the 4/5 turn as your RB2 with eyes open — the volume is real while Charbonnet is out, and the exit is Charbonnet's return, not a bust.
