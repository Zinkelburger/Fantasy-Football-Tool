**Terrance Ferguson** (LAR, TE, bye 11) — board rank 202 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The room's designated last-round tight end dart — real hype, an honest path, and a crowded tight end room standing in front of it.

- Role: second-year Rams tight end whose whole case is target consolidation. The Rams were 5th in the NFL in tight end targets last year at roughly 150 but split them among three or four tight ends; the bull argument (95-upvote nomination) is that his route tree and role are expanding, that McVay is talking about interchangeable bigger personnel packages, and that 70-80 targets is reachable. Only while: he wins the lion's share of those targets — Colby Parkinson, Higbee and Davis Allen are all still there.
- The coach's word is secondhand: a commenter says McVay spoke about him very highly this offseason as the offense's third option. Treat that as relayed praise, not a role report — there is no direct beat reporting on his snap share anywhere in the corpus.
- The evaluation: YAC vision, speed and route running for a tight end, breaks arm tackles, finds space downfield — but poor blocking, and the evaluator's worry is that he becomes a big slot receiver, in which case his real target competition is Puka Nacua and Davante Adams rather than the other tight ends. He had 231 receiving yards as a rookie.
- The contingent case is the one the analysts actually make. Cooper calls him a handcuff to two players at once — if either Adams or Nacua goes down he could be the WR2 while qualifying at tight end — and made him the cover of his contingent-upside piece; commenters asked who to grab if Nacua sits name him twice, on the logic that receivers have no handcuffs but tight ends do. The counter, from managers deciding whether to cut him, is that even the optimistic outcome is a volatile back-end TE1. Maher has him TE16, well above ECR, and still leans Juwan Johnson over him as a stash.
- Market is tight and consistent: last two rounds (Twerski), next-to-final round (Murchison), last 2-3 rounds as your TE2 behind a Likely/Goedert type (Hartitz), rounds 10-11 as a see-what-Week-1-looks-like play (Sanderson). Reported as going as late as the 15th, with posters starting him over Chig Okonkwo or Mark Andrews in zero-TE builds. One commenter warns he "has been getting some steam lately" and may be the hype tight end to cash out on.

**Draft take:** End your draft with him instead of a second real tight end and decide after Week 1 — you are paying nothing for a snap-share bet plus a Nacua/Adams contingency.
