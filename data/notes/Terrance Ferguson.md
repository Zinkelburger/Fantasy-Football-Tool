**Terrance Ferguson** (LAR, TE, bye 11) — board rank 202 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the analysts like him more than his own managers do — three of them rank him above consensus while roster-crunch threads debate cutting him for a handcuff.

- Role, and it is conditional. A commenter relays that Sean McVay spoke very highly of him this offseason as the third option in the Rams offense, and one commenter fully expects him to be LA's most relevant third pass catcher, ahead of Colby Parkinson, Higbee and Davis Allen. Cooper's framing is the sharpest version: Ferguson is the handcuff to two players at once — if either Davante Adams or Puka Nacua goes down he could be the team's WR2 while still qualifying at tight end. Asked who to grab in LA if Nacua sits, commenters named Ferguson twice on the logic that receivers have no handcuffs but tight ends do.
- The gate on all of it is the depth chart. Maher has him TE16 and considerably above ECR but says his path still requires emerging from a crowded Rams tight end rotation, which is why he leans Juwan Johnson over him as a stash; managers in a Sept 3 thread say the Rams have a lot of tight ends and other weapons and that even his optimistic outcome is a volatile back-end TE1 rather than a breakout.
- Scouting, from one evaluator (Aug 30): YAC vision, speed and route running for a tight end, breaks arm tackles, finds space downfield — but poor blocking, with the worry that he settles in as a big slot, in which case his real target competition is Nacua and Adams. A separate commenter cites an apparent rapport with Matthew Stafford as his reason to expect a breakout.
- Market: everyone prices him at the very end of the draft, roughly where the board has him. Hartitz is bullish but has him as your TE2 behind a Likely/Goedert type, taken in the last 2-3 rounds in non-TE-premium, and notes his value would skyrocket off one solid Thursday Week 1 game; Murchison says comfortably draftable in the next-to-final round; Twerski would take him only in the last two. One commenter says he "has been getting some steam lately" and may be the current version of a hype-driven tight end to cash out on.
- Sentiment splits the same way: two separate predictions have him top-eight or top-10 at the position and he is a repeated last-round TE dart alongside Likely, Goedert and Strange, while an Aug 27 thread asks whether it is already over for him and the reply is to sell for anything before the market catches on. One redraft bull would still sell him in dynasty during a good year, rating Max Klare the more well-rounded prospect.

**Draft take:** last-two-rounds TE2 whose realistic path runs through a Nacua or Adams absence — a fine final pick, never a reason to skip a starting tight end.
