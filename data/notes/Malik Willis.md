**Malik Willis** (MIA, QB, bye 6) — board rank 147 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Discussed less as a fantasy QB than as the variable that decides De'Von Achane's season, and every claim on him is commenter-level.

- Role is not in dispute: he is Miami's starter and a clear upgrade on the previous one, behind an improved offensive line and with three rookie receivers commenters say look good. No injury or availability claim anywhere in the corpus.
- The usage caveat is the note. He has never thrown more than 61 attempts in a season (155 career passes), his career high in a game is 21 attempts, and he is described as a bottom-five check-down-rate quarterback — a scrambler, not a dumper — in an offense the room projects around 20 attempts a game. Commenters read that as confirmation Miami is run-first by design.
- The knock-on, and the reason he matters on draft day: a dissenting commenter says Willis running instead of throwing checkdowns costs Achane receptions, and Dopp (Sep 3) turns it into a job condition — if Willis fails to get Achane involved, "he will not last long" at QB there. He is also named as the gating factor on every Dolphins receiver, though one commenter argues the room underestimates him and that Miami's WR1 has a WR3 floor if Willis is even somewhat good.
- Weapons cut against the upgrade case: commenters call the receiver room WR3/WR4 types with rookies forced into WR1 and WR2 roles, and say Miami cannot give him what he needs to prove himself. The only pushback is that those rookies already beat what was there.
- Sentiment is warmer than the projections. He carried three dark-horse nominations worth about 50 upvotes (Aug 23) on the argument that even a 3-14 Dolphins season can produce a fantasy-winning passer, with a Packers fan calling him composed and sharp; the room elsewhere holds him up as the reclamation-project template — "the worst QB I've ever seen when he was on the Titans, now he's about to be a superstar." Counters: Miami has 60% of its cap on players not on the roster, and one commenter simply does not see him lasting the year.
- Market: drafted as a second QB behind a starter precisely because he runs and Miami is expected to trail — a garbage-time hedge, and named as the round 11-13 QB archetype the data says can help (Sep 4).

**Draft take:** A last-round QB2 hedge with real rushing floor, and his bigger draft-day use is as the argument against Achane's receiving projection.
