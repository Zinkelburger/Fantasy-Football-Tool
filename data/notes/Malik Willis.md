**Malik Willis** (MIA, QB, bye 6) — board rank 147 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The room's favourite last-pick quarterback lottery ticket, bought on rushing volume behind a bad team rather than on anything he has thrown.

- He is Miami's starter and, per the room, an upgrade on Tua specifically for the run game. Dopp calls him a much more mobile new starter and adds that if he fails to get De'Von Achane involved he will not last long at quarterback there.
- The case is rushing, and it has numbers: Vegas projects him for the most rushing yards of any QB this season, and his three Green Bay starts produced 13 points (QB20), 25.4 (QB6) and 31.5 (QB3) on 6, 6 and 9 carries with 3 rushing TDs. Miami being bad is treated as helping — more trailing, more point-chasing. Two commenters project a top-7 to top-15 finish. The comparison offered is this year's Justin Fields — a QB making his living with his legs.
- The sample is tiny. Six career starts, 155 pass attempts across four years, never more than 61 in a season and a career high of 21 in a game — which is why the room dismisses his second-lowest check-down rate as noise. Bears note he was a third-round pick who never won the Titans job.
- The strongest documented negative is what he does to his running back's receiving work: Tennessee's RB target share ranked 6th without him and 26th with him in 2022-23; Green Bay's ranked 22nd without and 32nd with him in 2024-25. Across 2022-25 the league averaged 5.8 RB targets a game; in Willis's six starts it was 2.7. The rebuttal is that the only backs he has played with are Derrick Henry and Josh Jacobs, neither a receiver, so the pattern may be circumstance rather than design.
- The receiver room is the cap. Commenters call it WR3/WR4 types with rookies forced into WR1 and WR2 roles, on an offence projected around 20 attempts a game, and he is named as the gating factor on every Dolphins pass-catcher. One commenter argues the room underestimates him and that if he is even somewhat good Miami's WR1 has a WR3 floor with league-winner upside.
- Market: goes about 154th, taken as a last-pick lottery ticket or QB3 stash — one report has him in the 15th — with six nominations for 39 upvotes on the argument that he costs less than Nix, Purdy and Stafford with more upside. In superflex he goes rounds 8-9 as a QB2, in the tier people wait for alongside Shough, Darnold, Mayfield and Daniel Jones — several want him as insurance behind an injury-prone QB1 like Lamar rather than as their own starter.
- Sentiment: three dark-horse nominations for 50 upvotes, on the argument that even a 3-14 Dolphins season can produce a fantasy-winning passer.

**Draft take:** Worth your last pick as a rushing-upside QB2, best behind an injury-risk starter — but if you roster Achane, understand you are buying the thing that suppresses his targets.
