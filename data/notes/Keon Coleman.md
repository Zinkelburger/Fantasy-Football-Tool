He held a 12% target share last season as a possession receiver. Supporters want him moved inside — most pre-draft projections had him as a big slot and boundary usage has never worked — while detractors say his problem is separation and nickel corners cover him just as well. The other path offered is DJ Moore drawing CB1s and leaving him on a CB2/CB3, only while Moore is healthy in the WR1 role.
- **Sentiment is the loudest thing here and it is negative.** On 8/30 the room calls him a JAG or a borderline bust as often as it defends him, and the top comment (102 pts) mocks him being seen leaving his driveway at 11:30 for a 1:00 kickoff; the 9/02 split-fanbase thread's top comment is "maybe, but only if he can do the basics of being a pro like showing up on time" (63 pts). His 2025 Week 1 against Baltimore, cited as proof of upside, gets discounted: seven of eight catches and 95 of 112 yards came in the fourth quarter against prevent coverage. Supporters lean on his age (just turned 23) and the year-three window under Joe Brady, and the most upvoted pro argument anywhere is simply that Josh Allen is his quarterback.
- **Market: the sell window shut a year ago** — managers say it was the week after that Week 1 game; one turned down an early 2027 second, another says nobody was buying even then.

**Draft take:** Fade him — the snaps are contested, the reported development is unverified, and the only bull case anyone can name is his quarterback.
