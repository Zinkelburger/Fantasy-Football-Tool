**Jalen Hurts** (PHI, QB, bye 10) — board rank 69 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A former consensus tier-1 quarterback the room now argues over — the bet is a new offense fixing a bad one, and the rushing floor is why he stays draftable.

- Health and mobility: two beat reports on 08-11 point the same way. Jeremy Fowler (ESPN) has him looking like a viable dual threat again — designed runs executed multiple times on a light practice day, a team source calling him "quicker and faster." Brooks Kubena (The Athletic) says this is the most the Eagles have shown him running bootlegs from under center. A commenter reporting him running less in camp (08-26) is the weaker read, and Mannion was quoted the same day saying they will lean on his rushing.
- Role: a brand-new offense under newly promoted OC Sean Mannion after a year with a play-caller the room repeatedly calls one of the worst in recent memory. Mannion has said publicly they want Hurts to run more. The scheme asks for timed throws and work over the middle, which is the room's actual question — his comfort zone has been safe sideline throws — and commenters expect growing pains through roughly weeks 1-4.
- The 2025 baseline the fade rests on: only 454 pass attempts, well under the ~550 of a top passing offense, with eight games under 200 passing yards and four under 150. He was still 7th in the league with 25 touchdown passes and has not finished below QB9 in five years.
- If you also roster Saquon Barkley, this is the conflict: Hurts is the standing goal-line vulture. One Hurts owner reports 4 rushing TDs in the first three games last year and only 4 after, blamed on interior line surgeries and the Tush Push being solved.
- Market: ADP 64.3, with a commenter expecting him to slide below cost on negative perception in casual leagues and others taking him rounds 6-7. Maher has him 75-79 against an ECR of 61. ESPN's Dopp calls him the scariest tier-1 quarterback and is out at ADP — falling efficiency, a less effective tush push, a team that wants Barkley more, A.J. Brown gone — while granting the rushing upside.
- Eagles fans cluster their projections at 3,300-4,200 pass yards and 25-35 total touchdowns, mostly a bounce-back; a minority sees a benching. One commenter's 55% odds that this is his last season in Philadelphia is speculation with no reported benching or trade behind it.

**Draft take:** Take him rounds 6-7 as your QB1 and expect an ugly first month — two beat reports say the rushing floor is intact, and the price already includes last year's collapse.
