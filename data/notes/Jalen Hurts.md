**Jalen Hurts** (PHI, QB, bye 10) — board rank 69 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** split between "top-10 every year, wrecked by a bad play caller" and "average QB the league has solved," with nobody arguing his fantasy usage changes either way.

- The rushing question is the whole fantasy case, and the beat reporting is favorable. Jeremy Fowler of ESPN (8/11): Hurts looks like a viable dual threat again — he executed a designed run multiple times on a light practice day and looks to be moving well, with a team source agreeing he looks "quicker and faster." Brooks Kubena of The Athletic, same day: this is the most the Eagles have shown Hurts running bootlegs from under center and the most defenders have chased him laterally.
- The room's later read (8/26) that he was running less in camp is weaker-sourced than that and is split on its own terms — real change, or practice focus — with OC Sean Mannion quoted saying they will lean on his rushing. Take the direction from the beat reports and treat the camp-volume chatter as unresolved. One commenter argues he runs less because he is playing for an extension; the rest of that thread projects 500-645 rush yards and 8-10 rushing TDs.
- Role change is the story: a brand-new offense under newly promoted OC Mannion after a year with a play caller the room repeatedly calls one of the worst in recent memory. Consensus expects him asked to throw over the middle far more, with growing pains through roughly weeks 1-4. The volume caveat cited against him: he threw only 454 times last year, well under the ~550 of a top passing offense, with eight games under 200 yards and four under 150. One owner reports 4 rushing TDs in the first three games and only 4 more after, blamed on interior OL surgeries and the Tush Push being solved.
- Contract noise, speculation only — no reported benching or trade. One commenter puts roughly 55% odds this is his last season in Philadelphia; others in-thread call a benching unlikely because the team keeps winning with him.
- Market: ESPN's Dopp calls him the scariest tier-1 QB and is out at ADP — efficiency down, tush push less effective, the team wants Saquon Barkley more, A.J. Brown gone — while granting the rushing upside. Maher has him 75-79 against an ECR of 61. Both analysts sit below the market; board rank 69 is between them. The counter: top-10 every year as a starter, QB9 in a bad scheme behind a banged-up line, and a play caller who wants the tush push back.

**Draft take:** a real discount on a perennial top-10 QB and the beat reports say the legs are back — take him at rank 69, and only fade if you think Mannion keeps him in the pocket.
