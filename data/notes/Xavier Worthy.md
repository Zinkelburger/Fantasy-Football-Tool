**Xavier Worthy** (KC, WR, bye 5) — board rank 128 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** written off — grouped with Skyy Moore and Mecole Hardman as bad Chiefs picks, with a small post-hype camp arguing the shoulder explains the down year.

- Health is the whole file and the arc is messy. He was out to start camp, and on 8/11 Charles Goldman of A to Z Sports had him still "in some sort of load-management ramp-up" with the shoulder; commenters that week said he had picked up a second injury about ten days earlier, and mid-August threads described him as sidelined, which is the stated opening for Cyrus Allen. By 9/3 the room says he is fully healthy. Nobody has reported a limitation since Goldman's note.
- The injury doubles as the bull case: commenters say he was hurt on the first series of Week 1 last year and played the season through it — one says a torn labrum — in a bad offense that also lost Mahomes late.
- Role is capped rather than unclear. He is the WR2 behind Rashee Rice and the room's read is that his ceiling stays capped while Rice is healthy and available; his best game last season was 17.1 points, which one commenter calls fringe-WR3 territory. Hartitz notes he looks healthy enough to work underneath, which Andy Reid has talked about, and flags it as a risk to Rice's volume rather than a promotion for Worthy.
- The deep role is conditional on blocking: the case for Worthy and Thornton on sideline shots assumes the Chiefs line holds up long enough to throw them.
- Market and board agree. He is priced as a round 10-11 pick — one commenter says he went undrafted entirely in his league — for boom/bust output behind a quarterback who spreads targets thin. Dopp calls him a post-hype sleeper who keeps falling to him; Murchison only wants him in best ball.

**Draft take:** a round-10 lottery ticket on the shoulder being the whole story, and only worth it if you can afford the weeks Rice eats — do not move him up.
