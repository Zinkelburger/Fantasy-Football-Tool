**George Kittle** (SF, TE, bye 8) — board rank 102

**Room sentiment:** Quiet for a name this big — the health news knocked him out of the elite-TE conversation without producing a fade consensus.

- The hard facts, from a camp/age roundup: he turns 33 in October and tore his achilles in January. That lands him in a fourth tier described as "top-5 upside after a Kona Big wave or 3" — a hope, not a plan.
- Bull case is opportunity. San Francisco doesn't expect Ricky Pearsall to contribute in 2026, which produced "George Kittle TE1 SZN" replies. A separate analysis notes alpha TEs like Kittle play more snaps in 2WR/heavy sets, where the offense is more productive and their target share is higher. A punt-TE study names him and Kelce as the two tight ends where the opportunity cost has historically been justified — 60 explosive plays on 260 receptions (23.08%).
- Bear case: "I'm staying far away from George Kittle in all formats," plus an argument that he blocks a lot on a team with many weapons, making him unreliable week to week. Market: ADP ~89-108, projected 724.5 yards, around TE9 in one aggregate.

**Draft take:** A discounted upside TE, not a set-and-forget one. Reasonable in the 9th if you punted the position; don't pay the pre-injury price.
