**Chris Godwin** (TB, WR, bye 10) — board rank 87 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The most-nominated value in the draft-community threads, with a real and specific injury history as the whole of the bear case.

- **Healthy, per the team.** GM Jason Licht said that by the Dolphins game Godwin finally looked like himself again, and that he finished last season healthy rather than carrying anything into this one (9/02); camp reports have him in top form. That supersedes the room's lingering worry, which is history rather than a current status: a dislocated ankle and fractured fibula on top of a torn ACL with an MCL sprain, and "his constant injuries scare me" was the top comment (83 pts) in a Bucs thread on 9/03. He turns 31 right after the Super Bowl.
- **Locked into the slot, and it does not depend on the injuries around him.** Beat writer Greg Auman, who covered the Bucs for a decade, says Godwin and Emeka Egbuka are both set for full snap shares when healthy and that Godwin is "pretty locked in" on the slot, with McMillan wide in 3-WR sets; Maher adds that Tampa expects him to keep the primary slot role even once Egbuka and McMillan return, because Mike Evans' departure leaves the targets. A competing commenter reads camp as slot-only with McMillan the primary X. Scott Reynolds (Pewter Report) had him the most-targeted Bucs receiver in an 8/11 practice, working the middle for first downs with clear Mayfield chemistry.
- **The near-term bump is separate and conditional.** Egbuka may be playing through turf toe; if he misses Week 1, Godwin is the target beneficiary against Cincinnati. Treat that as ceiling, not the case.
- **Market: the board is at his ADP, the analysts are a full tier higher.** Drafters report getting him in the 8th, 9th, 10th and 13th rounds and being thrilled, and multiple call his ADP "criminally low." Maher has him WR30 and 54th overall, 25 spots ahead of ECR, explicitly as a season-long asset rather than an injury fill-in; Cooper calls him his favourite player in the draft at ADP and notes he was fantasy WR2 behind only Ja'Marr Chase the last time he was healthy, in 2024; Murchison names him the WR25-48 pick he is most confident in. One commenter argues the ADP is held down by platform rankings, not by the expert community.
- **Sentiment:** overwhelmingly positive and unusually concentrated — ten top-level nominations and roughly 641 upvotes in one value thread, five more (+309) in another. The dissent is age 31 and durability, never role; the rebuttal is that his 2025 drop-off was Baker Mayfield playing hurt. The honest bear number in-thread is a ceiling of 5 catches for 50 and roughly 700 yards.

**Draft take:** Take him at or slightly before board rank 87 — the slot role is confirmed by the beat, Evans' targets are vacant, and everything the room is discounting is a chart of past surgeries rather than a current status.
