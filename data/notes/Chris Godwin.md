**Chris Godwin** (TB, WR, bye 10) — board rank 87 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The most-cited value pick of the late summer, with the dissent about age and injury history rather than about role.

- Health is the whole case, and it currently reads clean. GM Jason Licht said Godwin finally looked like himself again by the Dolphins game and ended last season healthy rather than carrying the ankle into this one; camp reports have him in top form. The bear case the room keeps returning to is the medical file — a dislocated ankle and fractured fibula on top of a torn ACL with MCL sprain, and he turns 31 right after the Super Bowl. "His constant injuries scare me" was the top comment in the Bucs thread at 83 points.
- Role is the slot, and it is locked. Beat writer Greg Auman, who covered Tampa for a decade, says Godwin and Emeka Egbuka are both set for full snap shares, with Godwin sliding inside in 3-WR sets and McMillan coming in wide, and calls him "pretty locked in" on the slot. Maher expects him to keep the primary slot role even once Egbuka and Jalen McMillan are back. One commenter reads camp reports as making him slot-only with McMillan the primary X.
- Mike Evans is gone and Egbuka may be playing through turf toe, which raises his early-season ceiling rather than being the case for him — he is the named target beneficiary if Egbuka cannot go Week 1. Scott Reynolds (Pewter Report) had him the most active Bucs receiver on 8/11, working the middle of the field for first downs with clear Baker Mayfield chemistry.
- Before the ankle injury he was the overall WR1 through five games (50-576-5), and the room says he passed the eye test on his return around Week 12.
- Market: platforms price him WR38-45 while drafters report taking him in the 8th, 9th, 10th and even 13th rounds — roughly where his board rank sits, and every analyst voice in the corpus is higher than that. Maher has him WR30 and 54th overall, 25 spots ahead of ECR, and says his age is what makes him cheap. Cooper calls him his favorite player in the entire draft at ADP and his most confident WR25-48 pick, noting Godwin finished fantasy WR2 behind only Ja'Marr Chase the last time he was healthy in 2024; Murchison answered the same question with the same name.
- Sentiment: ten top-level nominations and roughly 641 upvotes in one value thread, and five in another (+309). The minority fade is "one of the most chronically hurt WRs out there"; the rebuttal is that his 2025 drop-off was Mayfield playing hurt.

**Draft take:** Take him a round early rather than waiting — a healthy, locked slot role in a room that lost Mike Evans is worth more than the 8th, and the injury history is already fully in the price.
