**Bo Nix** (DEN, QB, bye 10) — board rank 118 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The room's most-named undervalued quarterback — 12 top-level nominations for 119 upvotes in one thread — and the argument is about price, not projection.

- He is coming off a broken ankle from last season, described in-thread as persistent. No current designation or timeline is reported anywhere in the corpus. The stated worry is that it makes him run less, which is where most of his fantasy edge comes from, and one commenter expects him to check down more; a top-5 call in another thread was conditioned explicitly on the ankle being a non-issue. One drafter says the injury, plus Denver's receiver health, steered him off Jaylen Waddle at his pick.
- Role and environment are the case: back-to-back QB7 finishes, about 3,900 passing yards in 2025 with a solid TD/INT ratio, near the league lead in pass attempts even behind an elite defense, Sean Payton, an offensive line the room rates the best in the argument, and a rushing floor Goff, Purdy and Stafford do not have. Jaylen Waddle has been added alongside Sutton, Franklin and Mims, which is the stated reason several expect a step up as a passer.
- Market is the whole story. He goes as the QB13-QB14 around pick 120 — the 6th round of a 14-team in one posted board, the 13th round in another league — after finishing QB7 in each of his first two seasons. The room repeatedly calls that the cheapest QB1 in the pool and names him the fallback in at least five separate draft plans. Cooper ranks him ahead of Baker Mayfield, Malik Willis and Tyler Shough but behind Brock Purdy, while admitting he does not draft much Nix.
- The dissent is weekly reliability, and it comes from someone who owned him: around 5 points entering the fourth quarter, with the production arriving on late comebacks. Others name him the likeliest regression candidate at the position.

**Draft take:** The best value at quarterback on this board — take him as your QB1 in the double-digit rounds and accept that the points arrive late and in bursts.
