**Brian Thomas** (JAC, WR, bye 7) — board rank 75 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The room thinks a bad second season has people treating him as if he was never good, and it is buying the dip louder than it is defending the price.

- Healthy now, after playing hurt through 2025 — which the room gives as the reason he averaged only around 10 PPR points a game, with a bounce-back to 13 PPG as the stated bar. One side of that argument notes his snap share only fell after a Week 9 ankle sprain.
- The room's other explanation for last season is not football: his best friend Kyren Lacy died before the season, and commenters describe him playing scared over the middle, dropping passes and never building chemistry with Trevor Lawrence.
- Role is the Jacksonville X, which plays essentially every snap outside jumbo and goal-line packages, while Parker Washington is the F and only plays in 11 personnel. With Strange healthy and two tight ends drafted, the room expects far less than last year's 70%-plus 11-personnel rate, which caps Washington's snaps rather than his.
- The bear case is target competition and quarterback fit: too many mouths in Jacksonville with Travis Hunter added and Jakobi Meyers producing, and a passer whose skill set does not maximise his deep speed. He ranked 3rd in the league in GO routes from Week 9 onward, which one commenter reads as a sacrificial X pulling coverage deep to open the middle for Washington unless the downfield connection with Lawrence returns.
- Camp reporting is positive: John Shipley (SI Jaguars) had him with his best day of camp — a deep touchdown plus "running crisper routes, not putting many balls on the ground," looking "more and more like the 2024 version of himself than the 2025 version."
- Market: commenters say he should not be going in the 5th/6th round and one reports getting him in the 11th. The dynasty collapse is the headline — he peaked at the 1/2 turn in startups and now trades for a single future first, is No. 5 most-traded at 1,234 deals, and is acquired below his market value in 64% of them. He is currently valued one to two rounds behind Parker Washington in both redraft and dynasty, which one detailed poster calls crazy and thinks should be reversed by 6-12 spots. One commenter would wait until the Week 7 bye to buy, because the opening schedule likely puts Ward, Surtain, Gonzalez, Quinyon and Stingley on him.
- Sentiment cuts both ways in the same week: the most-nominated buy-the-dip name in one thread (10 top-level comments) and 11 top-level fades in another, all recoil from a second-round pick that disappointed.

**Draft take:** Buy him at his current cost — the room's two explanations for 2025 are an ankle and a bereavement, and the every-down X job is still his — but expect the opening schedule to hold the price down before it moves.
