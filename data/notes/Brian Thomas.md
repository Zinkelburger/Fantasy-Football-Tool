- Market: he goes around the 5th-6th round, roughly where board rank 75 has him, and the room is split on whether that is too rich — one drafter reports getting him in the 11th, so league-to-league variance is large, and the ranker who ate his 2025 miss now has him in the 8th. In dynasty he is the No. 5 most-traded asset (1,234 deals) and declining, acquired below his 4,770 market value in 64% of trades, priced in the same tier as Marvin Harrison and trading near 1:1 against him with the edge on quarterback and situation.
- The fade side, plainly: eleven top-level fades on Sept 1, essentially all recoil from a second-round pick that disappointed, with commenters saying he has to prove it and noting Reddit's enthusiasm has moved to Parker Washington.

**Draft take:** buy the discount if he falls past the 6th — the bust post-mortem is about last year's circumstances and the beat writer says the 2024 version is showing up again, but do not pay a pre-2025 price.
