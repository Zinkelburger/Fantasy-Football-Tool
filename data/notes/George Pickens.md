**George Pickens** (DAL, WR, bye 14) — board rank 27 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The room is arguing about who the Cowboys' WR1 actually is, and a real minority has settled on Pickens — the reservation is effort, not role.

- No injury claims. Role is trending up: commenters relaying unnamed camp reports (08-31) say he has been the better of the two Dallas receivers this summer and could be the team's WR1 over CeeDee Lamb. The only direct source in the corpus is the Cowboys' own site (Practice Points, 8/11), which had him with another strong practice in an up-and-down day for the offense, capping a good camp in his second year in Dallas. He is on $27M this year.
- He is now the standing reason to fade Lamb: named repeatedly as real target competition, with one commenter saying there is a chance Lamb is not even the best receiver on the team and another calling Pickens "the true 1." The room's own counter is well argued — an elite receiver gaining a stud WR2 historically loses target share but gains longer drives, less defensive attention and more scoring chances, citing Puka with Davante Adams and Ja'Marr Chase with a healthy Tee Higgins. Others simply note that Dallas throws for 4,500+ yards and both can eat.
- Nobody resolves the split. One commenter argues he was a WR1 with Lamb on the field and THE WR1 without him; the rebuttal is that the splits show they did take from each other last season. Lamb's health is the hinge.
- The reservation is effort, not role. Even a commenter backing Nico Collins over him at 2.11 concedes Pickens is "awesome when he wants to try," and one dissenter notes he visibly gave up on plays last year. The counterweight, which the room uses as a general argument elsewhere: he was checked out in Pittsburgh and then turned in a WR1-level season the moment he changed teams.
- Market: the board's rank 27 and the room agree. Drafters report taking him at the 2/3 turn in 10- to 14-team leagues as the early-WR anchor after a first-round back, with one slip to the early 4th of a 10-man. Maher has him 22nd overall and WR10, exactly at ECR — no edge either way — and is fine with him over Malik Nabers. The room repeatedly takes him over A.J. Brown in the same range on age and upside.
- Sentiment: about 15 fade mentions, on Lamb's target competition, the even-year Dak injury worry and a price much higher than last year — several faders say they had him last season, he crushed it, and they still will not re-draft at cost. In dynasty only: more than one commenter expects him to leave Dallas in free agency after this season.

**Draft take:** Take him at the 2/3 turn — the camp chatter has him beating CeeDee Lamb out and the analysts price him exactly at consensus, so the only thing you are paying up for is a receiver whose floor is his own effort.
