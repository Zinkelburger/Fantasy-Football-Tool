**George Pickens** (DAL, WR, bye 14) — board rank 27

**Room sentiment:** Mildly positive with a real skeptic wing — 17 mentions, mostly ranking-list appearances rather than argument, and the pushback is about price, not role.

- **Role looks locked:** camp notes have Ryan Flournoy as the WR3 behind CeeDee Lamb and Pickens. One rankings writeup puts Lamb at a 25% target share and Pickens right behind at 23%, framing Dak Prescott as having two legitimate No. 1 options.
- **Where the market has him:** Hartitz tier 3 ("high-volume WR1s and overqualified WR2s"); aggregate average rank 23.67; one projection set lists ADP 28.0 for 999.5 receiving yards; Sleeper superflex ADP 33.7. A Top-300 writeup: "safe to take here but could have another insane year / will see how target share is."
- **Bear case:** Hartitz has Pickens lower than most — "I just think he's NFC Tee Higgins," preferring cleaner WR1s in their own offense. Another poster: "I really don't get how CeeDee and George Pickens can both be in the top 10 receivers." A Fantasy Life/Yahoo analyst says he's been actively avoiding him. Counterpoint: a "WR3 tax" piece argues a WR2 on a great offense beats a WR1 on a bad one, naming Pickens.

**Draft take:** Fair at his rank, not a discount. If you want the Cowboys passing game the room considers Lamb the cleaner buy; take Pickens only if you're comfortable the 23% target share holds.
