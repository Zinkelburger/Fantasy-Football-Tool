**Tyler Warren** (IND, TE, bye 13) — board rank 60 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the Colts' best pass catcher by a wide margin, with a groin that took until September to settle.

- Back at practice, and only recently. He went down on a route on 2026-08-19 clutching his groin, was shut down in camp with what was reported as an abductor strain, and returned to practice on 2026-09-01 per Colts writer Jake Arthur. Reporting at the time said he "is going to be fine" and would not miss much if any time, and a Ringer host said flatly he will be fine and "is gonna eat". The live flag: no Week 1 Vegas prop had been posted for him as of 09-02, which the room read as uncertainty — he "will supposedly play but only just rejoined practicing in full" — and a keeper-league owner who watched the shutdown is not convinced of a full ramp-up. Flare-up risk was noted when it happened.
- Role is the strongest thing on his page. Commenters have him as the Colts' likeliest team target leader, ahead of Josh Downs and clear of Alec Pierce, roughly even with Pierce for the team touchdown lead, and the primary red-zone threat. With Michael Pittman traded, Downs and Pierce both coming off injuries and 34-year-old Keenan Allen next up, a Colts fan's projection puts a 26% target share (about 140 targets) in play, off an 82-970-6 pace in his full games with Daniel Jones. Cooper says he can finish as the overall TE1 — he was already TE4 — and adds real gadget usage as a converted quarterback: tush push, wildcat, end-arounds, a double pass.
- Keenan Allen, not the groin, is the specific threat. Warren's aDOT was about 6 and Allen's about 8, both living on short first-read targets, and Warren generates little YAC, so the argument is that Allen slices his volume without changing his role; one commenter expects him to "get Keenan'd". The counter is that Allen caps only the ceiling and Warren stays the best receiving option by a distance. Jake Arthur also had him as one of the most-mentioned players by both sides after the Colts-Patriots joint practice, essentially carrying the passing game late and drawing praise from Shane Steichen.
- Market: reported anywhere from the early 4th to the 7th round. A Colts fan calls the early 4th indefensible with Kittle or Kelce available late on a team that wants to run 40 times a game; several call the 7th solid value and one calls even that too high. He also turns up on fade lists specifically because of the Allen signing.

**Draft take:** pay the 5th-to-7th round price most of the room is reporting rather than the 4th — the groin is behind him, and Allen is what caps the weekly ceiling.
