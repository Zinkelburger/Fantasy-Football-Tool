**Tyler Warren** (IND, TE, bye 13) — board rank 60 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A settled target-hog role with a groin that cost him most of August, and a price the room is split on paying.

- Injury: he went down on a route in 11-on-11 on Aug 19, got up clutching his groin and left practice; the strain was reported Aug 20 with a source saying he "is going to be fine" and would not miss much time. He was then shut down in camp through Aug 26, the third of Indianapolis' top pass catchers banged up under three weeks from the opener. Per Jake Arthur he returned to practice Tuesday Sept 1. As of Sept 2 he had no Week 1 Vegas prop posted, which the room reads as uncertainty rather than a ranking — he "will supposedly play but only just rejoined practising in full". Otherwise the room has him a top-12 TE.
- Role: with Michael Pittman gone he is the Colts' best receiving option by a wide margin and the most likely team target leader, ahead of Josh Downs and clear of Alec Pierce, and about 50/50 with Pierce for the team touchdown lead. Per Jake Arthur, he carried the passing game in the late-game drill at the Colts-Patriots joint practice and was praised by HC Shane Steichen afterward. He finished TE4 as a rookie and Cooper thinks he can finish TE1 overall, pointing at gadget usage from his QB background — tush push, wildcat, end-arounds, a double pass. One Colts fan's projection: he was on an 82-970-6 pace on 110 targets in his full games with Daniel Jones, and a 26% target share is reachable now.
- The Keenan Allen signing is the live disagreement. One side says the banged-up receiver room means the passing game runs through Warren regardless; the other says Allen is precisely the threat, since Warren's aDOT was around 6 and Allen's around 8, both living on short first-read targets, and Warren generates little YAC — so the volume gets sliced without the role changing. One commenter expects him to "get Keenan'd"; the room's summary is that Allen caps the ceiling, not the floor.
- Everything here is conditional on the quarterback. He was a beast in 2025 until Daniel Jones got hurt, and QB-dependent volatility is what depressed his scoring; if Indianapolis struggles with Jones off the Achilles his value goes with it, though one commenter notes that could just mean more screens and quick throws.
- Market: the board has him at 60 and the room is paying more than that — reported going 45-50 overall, pick 57 in a 14-team, the early 4th on Yahoo. Buyers want him in rounds 7-8 and will not reach past it; a Colts fan calls the early 4th indefensible with Kittle or Kelce available around round 13.

**Draft take:** Wait for him — the role is real but the room's own buyers cap out in rounds 7-8, and paying the early 4th for a tight end who just resumed full practice is the version of this pick that loses.
