**TreVeyon Henderson** (NE, RB, bye 11) — board rank 43 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the sub is net negative on him and several posters say so outright; pass protection is the whole argument.

- Week 1 is unresolved. He is still rehabbing an ankle hurt slipping on a cut in practice in late August: per Mike Reiss on 2026-09-01 he was walking normally in the locker room but was not yet cleared for full practice participation. Reports call it not serious, and Loza (09-03) still says the ankle plus a tough Week 1 matchup is enough to start Kenny Gainwell over him to open the season. The board rank of 43 does not price that.
- Role is capped by pass protection and everyone involved knows it. He was benched late last season for blocking and vision, and under Vrabel a back who cannot pass block does not see the field; two commenters who follow the team say he gets ice-boxed whenever Rhamondre Stevenson is healthy. Matt Waldman flagged the flaw coming out of college — drops his head — and the rookie year bore it out. The counter, from 09-03: several reports had him improving and one negative report overwrote them all. Greg Bedard, high on him a year ago, charted 10 team-drill rushes in July and had seven stuffed at the line, blaming the run blocking in part.
- The production case is real and conditional. He posted roughly 1,000-1,100 total yards and 10 touchdowns as a rookie, out-carried Stevenson, and went from 6.1 carries a game over the first seven weeks to 13.7 over the last ten at 15.2 half-PPR points a game — but that stretch overlapped Stevenson's absence, and he was RB19, RB4 and RB4 in the three games Stevenson missed. The rebuttal is that his YPC rode untouched breakaways against bottom-tier run defenses and he went down on first contact; across 18 shared games Stevenson averaged 13.0 FPG to his 8.3.
- Market: about a 6.07 ADP (RB24-28), two to three rounds ahead of Mason, Corum and Brooks, and sliding on the ankle news — $7 in an auction where Stevenson went $12, with drafters reporting rounds 7-8. Dynasty has him as the steepest discount on the most-traded board, acquired below market value in 66% of matched-format trades.
- Sentiment: the highest-voted answer in one bust thread (723 pts) is that he may not even be the starter and costs too much for a split backfield, with a 165-point reply calling him massively overrated. The defense (38 upvotes) is that he outproduced every rookie back but Jeanty and gets his blocking flaws picked apart while others' are ignored.

**Draft take:** he is a bet on Stevenson getting hurt that currently costs a 6th — pay the 7th-8th others are reporting, and not before the ankle clears.
