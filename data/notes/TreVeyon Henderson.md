**TreVeyon Henderson** (NE, RB, bye 11) — board rank 43 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The sub is net negative and says so — the pass-protection knock is now the consensus read on why he does not hold a workload, and the price has been sliding for two weeks.

- Injury: rehabbing an ankle hurt slipping on a cut in practice in late August. Per Mike Reiss on Sept 1 he was walking normally in the locker room but was not yet cleared for full practice participation; Week 1 availability is unresolved. Loza (Sept 3) says the ankle worries her about his volume in a tough opener and suggests starting Kenny Gainwell over him. Reports in-room describe it as not serious.
- Role: the lead job is Rhamondre Stevenson's to lose, and the reason given is pass protection. Matt Waldman flagged it coming out of college (drops his head, wouldn't work in the NFL); commenters say he was benched late last season for blocking and vision, and that under Vrabel a back who cannot pass block does not play. Greg Bedard, who was high on him a year ago, charted 10 team-drill rushing attempts in July and had 7 stuffed at the line of scrimmage. Two commenters describe him as ice-boxed whenever Stevenson is healthy.
- The contingency is where his value actually lives: he was RB19, RB4 and RB4 in the three games Stevenson missed, and his rookie usage ramped from 43 carries over the first seven weeks to 137 over the last ten (15.2 half-PPR points per game across that stretch) — a stretch that overlapped Stevenson's absence, which is the immediate rebuttal every time it is cited. Best case with both healthy, per the room, is a 50/50 committee with no goal-line work; one commenter expects 60/40 Stevenson.
- The stat fight is unresolved and worth knowing both sides of. Henderson led Stevenson in rushing yards, yards per carry, attempts and receptions in 2025 and posted roughly 1,000-1,100 total yards and 10 TDs as a rookie; Stevenson led in EPA, yards from scrimmage, YPRR, explosive-play rate, missed tackles forced per attempt and yards after contact, and averaged 13.0 FPG to Henderson's 8.3 across the 18 games they shared.
- Market: the board has him at 43 while the room is buying him in rounds 7-8 — he went $7 in an auction where Stevenson went $12, and his average ADP is quoted around 6.07 (RB24-28). That gap is the decision. In dynasty he is the steepest discount on the most-traded board, bought below market in 66% of matched trades.
- Sentiment: the highest-voted answer in the biggest bust thread (723 pts) is that he may not even be the starter and costs too much for a split backfield. Defenders argue the room overcorrected.

**Draft take:** Do not pay the board's round-4 price; take him in rounds 7-8 where the room is actually letting him go, as a Stevenson-injury bet with a hurt ankle attached.
