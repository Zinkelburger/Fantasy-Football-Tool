**Tyler Loop** (BAL, K, bye 13) — unranked on the board · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Turning hard against him — managers are dropping him, and the job security the sub was confident about a week ago has thinned.

- Job at risk, 2026-09-02: two commenters say Baltimore has since signed Jake Moody and that Loop could be cut if he keeps struggling. No beat writer is quoted for it, but it directly reverses the 2026-08-29 read that the team had pointedly declined to bring in competition.
- That earlier read: the Ravens brought no kicker into camp, which the sub took as commitment, and the top-voted view was that competition would only damage his confidence. The same thread still expected a short leash — misses in the first two games and a veteran arrives quickly.
- HC Minter after the third preseason game, to reporters including Hensley: "You want to see makes in games. And so, it's something we'll continue to look at... He has had a good camp overall, but again, it comes down to making the kicks and the pressure situations in the game." That is a coach declining to commit, not a vote of confidence.
- The kicking itself: 4-for-7 this preseason, 2-for-5 from 45+, and 1-for-3 in the third game — missed a 58, made a 51, missed a 51 — with one commenter blaming a bad hold from new holder Eckley for at least one miss. As a 2025 rookie he hit 88% of field goals and 44-of-46 extra points, but 1-for-4 from 50+; he was 96.7% inside 50 against a ~91.5% league average, with 65% of his attempts from inside 39. Zrebiec reported on 2026-08-28 that he converted from 60 and 65 in warmups, missed back-to-back from 70, then made one from 74 — the room's answer being that warmups prove leg strength, and the doubt is accuracy at distance in games, not power.
- Zrebiec on the Baltimore Collective podcast: the Ravens have significant faith in him, no team gives up a vested kicker, and the market only deepens after cut-down day, so he does not expect a change before Week 3 or 4 if Loop keeps kicking this way. That is the strongest source on this page, and it is a floor rather than an endorsement.
- Sentiment: he is being cut for Schrader, Pineiro and Reichard, with three top-level nominations in the Schrader thread — "Except for Tyler Loop. Dude sucks at long kicks." r/ravens is harsher: "cooked", "bottom 10 kicker in the league". The bull case is volume, not accuracy: "ravens offense is going to put up so many points and be in the final third often".

**Draft take:** Draft a different kicker — the leg is fine, the job is not, and the best a beat writer offers is that Baltimore probably waits until Week 3 or 4.
