**Dontayvion Wicks** (PHI, WR, bye 10) — board rank 253 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the best-sourced late-round receiver on the board — four separate beat writers on the same thing — with one honest objection: hands.

- Jeremy Fowler (ESPN, 8/11): Wicks is more than a camp standout and the former Packer should be a big factor in the Eagles passing game; multiple people with the team brought him up unprompted, and he has the length to high-point the ball with Hurts throwing a good deep ball. Dave Zangaro (NBC Sports Philadelphia, same day): one of Hurts' best throws of the day was a perfectly timed out route to Wicks, and the two have been impressively on time together. Brandon Lee Gowton (Bleeding Green Nation, 8/9): "It's clear that Hurts and Wicks have chemistry," which matters because Hurts leans into targets he trusts.
- The room's read as of 9/04 (two top-level comments): the camp drumbeat has consistently had him as the clear WR2 with chemistry built with Hurts, and the change of scenery should help a talented but inconsistent player stuck in an offense that did not throw enough. The caveat everyone attaches is "if he can catch."
- Circumstance is doing work: Philadelphia's new OC came from Green Bay and specifically requested him, A.J. Brown has been traded and Makai Lemon is hurt, so he is projected a full-time starter at least to open the season. One commenter thinks he, not Goedert, is the more likely second target.
- The bear case is real and stated twice: he is a WR3/WR4 being asked to play a No. 2 role. Hartitz has a hard time seeing him as more than WR3 behind DeVonta Smith and Lemon and would cut him first among deep keeper stashes. Others counter that Lemon was drafted to start and will take the job soon, with a reply citing film analyst Brett Whitefield arguing Lemon will struggle to take it given his college tape. The bull prediction in-thread is 800-plus receiving yards beating out Lemon.
- Market: board rank 253 and going very late, with one commenter saying this is the cheapest he will be for a while because everything lined up this offseason. Twerski takes him for a final roster spot over Adonai Mitchell and Caleb Douglas. The 8/11 room was already buying — "If Wicks actually breaks out I'm going to feel like a genius. I have way too many shares."

**Draft take:** the highest-conviction beat-reported dart on the board — take him with a final roster spot and start him while Lemon is out, but he is a rental if Lemon gets healthy.
