**Luther Burden** (CHI, WR, bye 10) — board rank 39 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The loudest split on the board — heavy "too high" voting all summer against an equally loud analyst contingent, with the groin injury now mostly behind him.

- **Groin injury from the 8/08 practice, and he is back.** Rapoport reported a short absence on 8/09; Matt Zenitz (CBS) put it at about a month with a hope of being ready for the opener. Brad Biggs of the Tribune had him moving well at practice on 8/29, Bears beat reporter Pearson had him practising on 9/01, and by 9/03 drafters report him in full practice with nobody disputing availability. Ben Johnson said on 8/30 that with Burden and Zavion Thomas back, neither bubble receiver made the 53. Treat the earlier "out until at least the start of the season" comments as lag.
- **The residual risk is reps, not the injury.** The room's own point is that missing most of camp cost him playing time last season, and Hartitz would only flex him in Week 1 if he gets through the practice week with no designation.
- **Role is the slot/flanker in Ben Johnson's offense** — the Amon-Ra St. Brown job, YAC as the selling point — with DJ Moore's ~90 targets vacated and his snap share no longer shared with Moore. The bear case is that he is the third target behind Colston Loveland and Rome Odunze; a top reply (111 pts) floated 120/115/110 targets, which lands him around WR25, roughly where he is drafted. Chicago's heavy 3-TE sets are the other cap. He gains if Odunze or Swift misses time.
- **Ben Johnson has talked him up all offseason** — "wired different", comps to St. Brown and Jarvis Landry, wanting the ball in his hands as much as possible — and the room discounts it hard; the top reply (38 pts) is that Johnson buys stock in all his players.
- **Market: his cost fell on the injury and has not fully recovered.** Reported buys in the mid-5th, the 6th, the 7th and as late as the 8th in a 10-team keeper, against a pre-injury near-automatic 4th. Maher has him 34th overall / WR19 against an ADP of 53 and calls him a round-5 value; Twerski prefers him to Odunze "by a comfortable margin"; Murchison would start him as a Week 1 flex if healthy. Odunze is now going ahead of him in several live drafts.
- **Sentiment is negative in volume and that is the sharper signal:** 17 bust nominations (~195 upvotes) on 8/25 and 14 more (~180) on 9/02, all citing too many mouths in Chicago, Caleb Williams's accuracy, and a six-game sample propped up by five touchdowns and one game against a DB who did not make a 53. The bull side is analyst-heavy — Ringer hosts, Hartitz, Twerski.

**Draft take:** Healthy and cheaper than he was in July — take him in the 5th or later, but do not pay the pre-injury 4th-round price the analysts are arguing for.
