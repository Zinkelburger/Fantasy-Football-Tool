**Rashee Rice** (KC, WR, bye 5) — board rank 34 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Split down the middle between "high-end WR1 whenever he and Mahomes are on the field" and a fade built on camp tape, availability and a shrinking scheme role.

- Availability is the first question and it has two heads. He is coming off knee surgery he rehabbed while incarcerated — "rehabbing from surgery in jail isn't the best formula for athletic success" — so conditioning as well as the knee is in doubt. One correction is worth keeping straight: he never tore an ACL. It was an LCL tear in 2024, and this year's procedure was an offseason cleanup on the repaired knee; that correction came later in the same thread as the ACL claim and supersedes it. Off-field, one commenter says he is "past the suspension scare" while others still treat proceedings as live and call him "always on the verge of not being in the lineup for one knucklehead reason or another." Nothing in the corpus names a specific pending suspension.
- Camp reports on the knee have not been good, but check the sourcing. Commenters relaying beat writers say he has lost a step, is not separating and is getting clamped one-on-one; those relays are a couple of weeks old and name no outlet. The actual named reporting is milder and trends the other way: Jesse Newell (The Athletic) wrote on 11 August that Rice started camp favouring the right knee and unsure he could trust himself to accelerate, but that the burst had looked better over the past week, he was back in full team drills and quickly one of Mahomes' favourite targets again. Charles Goldman (A to Z Sports) had him in a load-management ramp-up at the same point.
- Role is No. 1 receiver, with a live question about how much of it survives. The room calls him the clear Kansas City WR1 with a locked-in target share and a zone-killing YAC weapon whose short routes function as the run game; with Rice in the lineup the rookie behind him is expected to see limited snaps. The bear case is specific: Kansas City added Kenneth Walker III, who takes the jet sweeps, screens and possibly the goal-line work that produced much of that output, and the team is expected to be run-heavy. New/returning OC Eric Bieniemy went on 810 WHB to describe Rice as a player who needs a hard voice of accountability, and the sub reads him as the project of the coaching change.
- The production case is real: 53-571-5 in eight games last year, roughly 18.8-19 fantasy points per game with a slumping Mahomes. The rebuttal is that it is manufactured — one poster's numbers have 56.9% of his 2025 points coming from trick plays, busted coverages, scramble drills or screens, 10th-highest in the league, and rank him 95th of 102 receivers in separation against man coverage over three years.
- Market: round 3-4 in 12-team redraft, right at his board rank and roughly two rounds after Achane; the room unanimously refused a Michael Wilson plus Bucky Irving offer for him as too steep. Books set his receptions over/under at 99.5, which Cooper uses to rank him well ahead of DJ Moore in PPR. The analysts are mostly out: Sanderson would package him — projects well, terrifying floor, fragile and offense-dependent; Hartitz makes him his fade in the WR13-24 range, ranking him last of Waddle, McConkey, Flowers, Egbuka and Rice; Murchison is fading him relative to ADP after watching camp video and caps him at a mid-tier WR2.

**Draft take:** Fade him at a third-round price — the knee, the off-field file and Kenneth Walker all subtract from the same profile, and you are paying for the healthy-Mahomes weeks without any assurance of getting them.
