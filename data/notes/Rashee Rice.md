**Rashee Rice** (KC, WR, bye 5) — board rank 34 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Nobody argues he is the Chiefs' No. 1 target; the argument is whether the knee and the off-field record let him be on the field to use it.

- Availability is the first question and it is two questions. The knee: correcting an earlier claim in the same thread, it was an LCL tear in 2024, not an ACL, and this offseason's procedure was a cleanup — rehabbed while he was incarcerated, which the room repeatedly flags as a bad formula for conditioning. The off-field side: one commenter says he is "past the suspension scare", but no specific pending suspension is cited anywhere, and the room's framing is that his upside is conditional on being "healthy and not in court".
- The camp reports moved in the wrong direction. Jesse Newell (The Athletic) had him starting camp favouring the right knee and unsure he could trust himself to accelerate, with burst improving over the following week and a return to full team drills where he quickly became one of Mahomes' favourite targets again (8/11); Charles Goldman had him in a load-management ramp-up. But a commenter relaying beat writers roughly two weeks later says he has lost a step, is not separating and is getting clamped one-on-one, and Murchison, off camp video, says he looks slow and cannot separate at the line — capping him at a mid-tier WR2. Take the later reports as the current read.
- Role is not in doubt: the clear KC WR1 and short-route zone-beater, with the rookie behind him expected to see limited work while Rice plays. New OC Eric Bieniemy, working with him for the first time, described him on 810 WHB as a player who needs a hard voice of accountability, and the sub expects him to benefit from the pairing.
- The usage bear case is Kenneth Walker III. Commenters argue Rice's production leaned on jet sweeps, screens and manufactured touches functioning as Kansas City's run game, and that Walker plus a promising rookie absorb those — leaving what one calls "a pretty average receiver for fantasy".
- Market vs the board: the board has him 34, the room pays round 3-4 in 12-team, which is the same neighbourhood. Cooper ranks him well ahead of DJ Moore on a 99.5-reception over/under; Hartitz makes him his WR13-24 fade and ranks him behind Waddle, Ladd, Flowers and Egbuka; Murchison is fully fading him relative to ADP.
- Sentiment is genuinely split, not soft. Seven top-level nominations (+171) argue every healthy Rice-plus-healthy-Mahomes stretch has smashed and he could "Amon-Ra the season"; roughly 18.8-19 points per game across eight games last year with a slumping Mahomes is the number his backers use.

**Draft take:** Third round is the right price and the fourth is a bargain, but treat the late-camp separation reports as real and do not build a roster that needs him to be a top-five receiver.
