**Jordan Addison** (MIN, WR, bye 6) — board rank 105 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the most-nominated undervalued WR in one September thread, on the argument that he is 24 with two top-24 seasons already behind him.

- Health is clear. Will Ragatz reported on 8/9 that Addison "worked back into some team drills on Saturday after being hampered by a minor thumb issue for a couple days" and should be close to full participation. Nothing since.
- Role is settled and second: he is Minnesota's WR2 and second option behind Justin Jefferson. The bear case is that Kyler Murray has never supported a second receiver behind a true alpha; the bull case is that Addison is exactly the check-down target Murray throws to, that he has been unguardable in Vikings camp two preseasons running, and that he is a Kevin O'Connell favourite the room believes would be kept over the quarterback.
- The dynasty thesis is conditional on him leaving. Commenters expect him paid next offseason, possibly by another team with Washington floated, which is what would move him into a clear WR1 role — Minnesota cannot support two WR1s. That is a 2027 argument, not a 2026 one.
- Production cited in his defence: two top-24 finishes in his first two years and four straight double-digit games with Wentz before McCarthy returned. One poster ranks him WR30-34 on that.
- Market and board are close but drifting toward the room. The board has him around pick 105; the room reports him going in the 9th and calls that clear value, while one commenter says he has been going in the 7th lately and another likes him in the 8th or 9th. A third says he is "basically free" at current cost.

**Draft take:** take him in the 9th where the room still lets him go — a 24-year-old WR2 with a proven floor is the cheapest thing at this rank — but stop before the 7th.
