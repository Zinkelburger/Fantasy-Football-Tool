**Brock Purdy** (SF, QB, bye 8) — board rank 110 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The room's favourite late-round QB1, on scheme and a strong camp rather than on weapons.

- Health: he played through turf toe all of last season and missed eight games with toe and shoulder injuries; commenters say all reports have him fully healthy now (Aug 23). No source named, and nothing since.
- The camp evidence is actual reporting: Nick Wagoner of ESPN had Purdy carving through the Titans secondary in the Aug 11 joint practice, particularly in the second half, going 21-of-27 with multiple touchdown passes and no interceptions, capping a strong camp.
- No role claims — his job is not in question. Who he throws to is: commenters watching camp and preseason say rookie De'Zhaun Stribling is his first look, the running joke being that with the rest of the room old or hurt there is nobody else.
- The bull case is Shanahan's scheme producing regardless of weapons, QB5 per-game finishes in some 2025 scoring, and underrated rushing with 8 rushing touchdowns in 24 games — stated as conditional on Christian McCaffrey and Trent Williams staying healthy. Skeptics cite weak receivers, run-heavy scripts and tough defenses; one drafter who gets him for free and sees top-8 upside says his only worry is the schedule, facing the Davante Adams-added Rams and the Seahawks twice each.
- Market: going around QB12 despite being a top-5 quarterback in the games he played before missing time, and a 12th-round pick of him drew 35 upvotes. In 6-point-passing-TD leagues Cooper puts him at the top of the punt-QB tier, ahead of Mayfield, Willis and Shough, but adds that once he is that deep at the position he takes two arms. One in-thread study has him second in the league in three-and-out rate at 15.8%, behind only Josh Allen.

**Draft take:** The best of the punt-QB tier — take him around round 12 and pair him with a second late arm, because his upside is tied to McCaffrey and Williams staying upright.
