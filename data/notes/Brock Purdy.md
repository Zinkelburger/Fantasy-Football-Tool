**Brock Purdy** (SF, QB, bye 8) — board rank 110 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The room's most-nominated underpriced quarterback — the case is scheme and per-game history, the doubt is who he throws to.

- Health: one commenter (08-23) says he played through turf toe all last season and missed eight games with toe and shoulder injuries, and that all reports have him fully healthy now. No beat reporting either way in the corpus.
- Role: unquestioned starter, and camp went well. Nick Wagoner (ESPN) had him carving the Titans secondary in the 08-11 joint practice — 21-of-27, multiple touchdowns, no interceptions. Camp watchers say rookie De'Zhaun Stribling is his first look, the joke being that with the rest of the room old or hurt there is nobody else.
- The statistical case the room repeats: 6th in QB points per game over the past four years, a top-6 finish in 2025 and the QB1 over the final eight weeks — all while throwing to Jake Tonges and Kendrick Bourne — plus 8 rushing TDs in 24 games. Stated as conditional on Christian McCaffrey and Trent Williams staying healthy.
- The fade: the receiving corps, run-heavy scripts, the worst defense of the punt-QB candidates besides Willis, and a schedule with the Adams-added Rams and Seattle twice each.
- Market — the room agrees he is cheap and is not correcting it. Reported picks: around 112 overall, the 10th round of 12-team (10.8, next to Goff), the 12th more than once, the 7th of a 14-team, undrafted in at least one league. He was started in only 28% of Yahoo leagues. Cooper puts him top of the punt-QB tier in 6-point-passing-TD formats, ahead of Baker Mayfield, Malik Willis and Tyler Shough.

**Draft take:** Wait him out and take him as your only quarterback in round 10-12 — the room prices him as a leftover and nothing in the reporting supports that.
