**Jaylen Waddle** (DEN, WR, bye 10) — board rank 52 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The analysts are uniformly ahead of the board on him and the room is uniformly worried about his availability — those are the two things on this page.

- Injury: Ryan Edwards reported him out of Broncos practice on Aug 11; nothing since, and no current designation. The standing knock is a reputation rather than a record — he has missed only 7 games in 5 years, which the critics answer by saying his mid-game exits do not show up as missed games ("limping off into the tent every week"). One commenter notes he habitually got banged up mid-game in Miami.
- Role: Denver traded a first-round pick for him and nobody in any thread disputes that he is the Broncos' new number one. Per one commenter the team has said he will play more Z, and his preseason snap showed him getting the layup routes he rarely saw in Miami. Sutton, Mims, Franklin and Bryant are the rest of a thin room. The most upvoted framing (84 pts) is that he is a perfect skill-set fit for a Sean Payton play-action offense — the receiver Denver has been trying to turn Marvin Mims and Troy Franklin into — and he is the specific reason several posters expect Bo Nix to improve.
- The dissent on role is that he and Courtland Sutton are closer to 1A/1B than the price implies, and that there are not really vacated targets in Denver for him to absorb.
- Market: the board has him at 52 and every named analyst in the corpus is higher. Maher has him WR15 and 28th overall against an ADP of 45, one of his largest receiver-side edges and his favourite upside pick in the tier because he goes later than DeVonta Smith. Hartitz has him WR13 as a "my guy", first of the Waddle/McConkey/Flowers/Egbuka/Rice group, and wants him around $30 in auctions. Twerski pairs him with Egbuka as the WR13-24 upside plays and Murchison ranks him second in a five-receiver group. In dynasty he is the board's biggest riser, No. 10 to No. 7 in trade volume, with 48% of trades above his 5,546 market value.
- Sentiment cuts both ways at similar weight: 8 top-level "too low" nominations carrying about 276 upvotes, citing a career 2.4 YPRR on play action moving into a Payton offense as the clear 1A, against 11 top-level fades (109 upvotes) on the Sutton split and the injury history. He was also put forward as the truer Stefon Diggs comp than A.J. Brown — a receiver becoming a clear WR1 after playing second fiddle — in the most upvoted reply in its thread (434 pts), with the counter that he has never hit the projection attached to it.

**Draft take:** Buy at the board's rank and be happy — four separate analysts have him in the WR13-15 range, and the only real objection is a durability reputation his games-played record does not support.
