**Jaylen Waddle** (DEN, WR, bye 10) — board rank 52 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** every named analyst on his page is ahead of ADP on him; the room's objection is that he never finishes games.

- Nothing current on his health. The last report is Ryan Edwards having him out of Broncos practice on 2026-08-11; nothing since. The standing knock is not missed games — only seven in five years — but mid-game exits, which the critics say do not show up in that count: "questionable all week, plays two drives and aggravates something", and "limping off into the tent every week" (23 pts).
- Role: Denver traded a first-round pick for him and, per one commenter, has said he will play more Z; his preseason snap showed him getting the layup routes he rarely saw in Miami. He is described as a target hog who pulls coverage off Courtland Sutton while squeezing Evan Engram, in a thin room otherwise (Sutton, Mims, Franklin, Bryant). The most upvoted framing in that argument (84 pts) is that he is exactly what a Sean Payton play-action offense wants — the receiver Denver has been trying to turn Marvin Mims and Troy Franklin into.
- Market is where the case lives and it is one-sided. Maher has him WR15 and 28th overall against a WR22 ADP near 45 — one of his largest receiver-side edges and his favorite upside pick in that tier, since Waddle can be had later than DeVonta Smith. Hartitz has him WR13, first of a Waddle/McConkey/Flowers/Egbuka/Rice group, and wants him around $30 in auctions. Twerski pairs him with Egbuka as the WR13-24 upside plays and prefers him to Garrett Wilson on a rule of avoiding bad offenses. Murchison ranks him second in a five-receiver group. Dynasty has him as the board's biggest riser, No. 10 to No. 7 in trade volume.
- Sentiment splits on the same line: eight top-level "too low" nominations (~276 upvotes) citing a career 2.4 YPRR on play action moving into Payton's offense as the clear 1A, against 11 top-level fades (109 upvotes) arguing he and Sutton are closer to 1A/1B than his ADP implies and that Denver has no vacated targets. The most upvoted comparison in the room (434 pts) makes him the true Stefon Diggs comp — a second fiddle becoming a clear WR1 — with the counter that he has never reached the ~275 points that projection needs.

**Draft take:** take him at cost around the 4th-5th; the analysts are a round or more higher and the health objection is about in-game exits, not missed weeks.
