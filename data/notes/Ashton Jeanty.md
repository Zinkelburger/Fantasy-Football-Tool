**Ashton Jeanty** (LV, RB, bye 13) — board rank 9 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Split and unresolved — an ankle nobody has officially graded, on a bad offense, at a price that has already come down a round and a half.

- **Ankle sprain from the 8/23 practice; still no team diagnosis.** He was helped off unable to bear weight and Ryan McFadden's first report called it knee-related, which set off the panic. Schefter walked it back on 8/24 to an ankle sprain the Raiders did not consider long-term, and on 8/25 to "more of a low ankle sprain than a high ankle sprain" — Rapoport's phrasing, repeated since, and still the most specific thing in circulation. The team has never confirmed a grade and owes no injury report until the first Week 1 practice.
- **Trending up, but he has not practised with the team.** Per McFadden, 9/01 was his first day on the practice field at all, doing basic stretches off to the side. Raiders reporter Q Myers on 9/02 saw him working with trainers on a side field, said he "looks actually pretty good," and read the decision not to use short-term IR as a sign he is back sooner rather than later. He has never worn a boot or crutches, only a brace, and is on neither PUP nor IR. Ben Gretch reads the three-back 53 the same way; the top reply (71 pts) says it only rules out a long absence. Working consensus: 0-3 games missed, Week 1 at Miami the likeliest one lost.
- **Coach and GM are positive, which is what they would say either way.** Kubiak on 8/28: "we're counting on him." GM John Spytek on 9/01 said the ankle is in a "good spot" and he expects him back "maybe quicker than some people would expect" — no date from either. The room compared the messaging to the Mixon and CMC 2024 cases, where positive noise preceded IR.
- **Role: a Week 1 rotation, not a bellcow, while he is limited.** Commenters expect a snap count or drive-by-drive split with Mike Washington in his first game back and the workhorse share once he is healthy; one doubts he clears 55-60% of the touches, another argues a healthy Jeanty is a 65/35 back. Two single commenters, uncorroborated, relayed a coach describing the backfield as a committee. The receiving side has the only real source: Dan Graziano (ESPN) reported multiple people in the building expect him to be a bigger part of the passing game after 55 catches for 346 and five scores as a rookie, and that the Raiders felt they did not do enough to get him into space.
- **Market: the board rank is stale by a round or more.** He was a late-first before the injury; drafters now report 2.08, the 3rd, occasionally the 4th, and $28-41 in auctions, landing in the Kyren Williams / Javonte Williams / Breece Hall range. The room's answer is that the 2/3 turn is the right price until positive practice news lands. Maher has him 19th overall and RB11, a notch below Omarion Hampton; Murchison has moved him back to his pre-injury RB10.
- **Sentiment:** the serious fade case is not the ankle — Vegas has the Raiders around 28th in team total with a bottom-five win line, and the detailed version notes that RBs scoring 13-plus TDs almost always play on top-10 scoring offenses, so the outcome you need at pick 9 may not be available.

**Draft take:** Fine buy at the 2/3 turn where he is actually going, not at his board rank, and only if you take Mike Washington a few rounds later.
