**Ashton Jeanty** (LV, RB, bye 13) — board rank 9 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Genuinely split, and the split is honest — an elite talent at a real discount versus an unpracticed ankle on a bottom-five offense.

- Ankle sprain from an 8/23 practice injury. The initial panic was worse than the injury: he was helped off unable to put weight on the leg and the team took a knee, which drove knee/season-ending speculation, but Schefter reported on 8/24-8/25 that it is a sprain "believed to be more of a low ankle sprain than a high ankle sprain" and not considered long-term. Treat the early PCL/MCL chatter as dead.
- The status has not improved much since. Per Raiders beat writer Ryan McFadden, Jeanty was still not participating in practice on 9/1, a week-plus after the injury — that Tuesday was his first day on the practice field at all, doing basic stretches off to the side. He was never in a boot or on crutches, only a brace. A 9/2 thread notes he again did not practice. He is not on PUP and not on IR, which is the strongest thing arguing for a short absence.
- Week 1 is a live question, not a formality. One commenter notes Jeanty is absent from the Vegas Week 1 top-24 RB market while teammate Brock Bowers has a posted line, which reads as the books not projecting him to play. The team has issued no formal diagnosis and no timeline, and that silence is what keeps a high-ankle component on the table for the skeptics — those linger for months when they are real.
- Role risk beyond the ankle, all of it single-commenter and uncorroborated: one says a Raiders coach described the backfield as a committee, another expects his snap share to land nearer 65% than the 85%-plus baked into projections, both contingent on Mike Washington continuing to flash while Jeanty is out. The counterweight is that a Cousins-led checkdown offense should feed the back targets, though one commenter argues that checkdown back is Washington.
- Market: he has slid from a late-first pick to the 2/3 turn. Drafters report 2.08, the third round in 12-team, the fourth in superflex, and $32-41 in auctions after $47 pre-injury. He now goes after or alongside Chase Brown, Derrick Henry, Kenneth Walker, Barkley, Javonte Williams and Breece Hall. Analysts have not moved uniformly: Murchison put him back at his pre-injury RB10, Hartitz dropped him to RB13 and said he would not take him at pick 7.
- The most-cited fade argument is not the ankle at all: Vegas has the Raiders near the bottom of the league in team total, and the 13-plus-TD seasons that justify a top-12 RB pick almost always come from top-10 scoring offenses.

**Draft take:** Let him fall to the 2/3 turn — at that price the ankle discount is worth taking, but pair him with Mike Washington and do not pay first-round cost for a back who has not practiced.
