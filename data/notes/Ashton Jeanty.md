**Ashton Jeanty** (LV, RB, bye 13) — board rank 9 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Every update since the 8/23 practice injury has been better than the last, and the price has not fully caught back up.

- **Ankle sprain suffered at Raiders practice 8/23; no team-issued diagnosis or timeline exists, and Week 1 at Miami is not confirmed.** He was helped off unable to bear weight and the team knelt around him — beat writer Ryan McFadden's first report called it knee-related — before Schefter walked it back to a sprained ankle "not considered long-term", then to a sprain "believed to be more of a low ankle sprain than a high ankle sprain".
- Everything since has trended positive. He was never in a boot or on crutches, only a brace. Per McFadden he was back on the practice field 9/1 doing stretches off to the side, his first day out there; Raiders reporter Q Myers saw him working with trainers on a side field 9/2 and said he "looks actually pretty good". He is not on PUP and not on IR — Ben Gretch notes the Raiders kept only three backs on the 53 instead of IR-ing him, with a fair 71-point pushback that a three-back 53 only rules out a *long* absence.
- **What nobody has produced is a grade or a date.** GM John Spytek says the ankle is in a "good spot" and he expects him back "maybe quicker than some people would expect"; HC Klint Kubiak said 8/28 they are "counting on him". A 262-point thread reads that as deliberately evasive and comps it to the Joe Mixon hoodie and CMC in 2024. Working consensus is 0-3 games missed, with a minority still suspecting a high-ankle component. Vegas had not posted him among Week 1 top-24 RBs while Brock Bowers had a line. No official designation is owed until the first Week 1 practice report, around 9/9.
- Role if he plays: a limited Week 1, likely a snap count or rotation with Mike Washington, then the workhorse job once he is right. Two commenters relay a coach describing the backfield as a committee, uncorroborated; the harder evidence is that Kubiak's Seattle backfield split 221/184 between Kenneth Walker and Zach Charbonnet last year and Las Vegas spent a 4th-rounder on Washington. Counter: he was one of only six RBs above a 70% snap share in 2025.
- The bull case is situation, not talent — 1,300 scrimmage yards, 10 TDs and 55 catches as a rookie behind a line the room calls bottom-2% in fifteen years, with 84% of his yards after contact and hits behind the line on 54% of carries. Las Vegas added Linderbaum and Burford, has Miller and Powers-Johnson healthy, changed O-line coaches and hired Kubiak. Dan Graziano (ESPN) reports multiple people telling him the Raiders expect a bigger passing-game role.
- **Market: rank 9 is his pre-injury price; the room now pays the 2/3 turn** — reported at 2.08, the 3rd, occasionally the 4th, alongside Kyren Williams, Javonte Williams and Breece Hall, with one auction sliding from $47 to $28 in 36 hours. Analysts have not converged: Maher has him 19th overall, Hartitz dropped him to RB13 and is out at pick 7, Murchison moved him back to his pre-injury RB10.

**Draft take:** Buy the discount at the 2/3 turn and pair him with Mike Washington; at rank 9 you are paying full freight for an ankle nobody on the team has been willing to describe.
