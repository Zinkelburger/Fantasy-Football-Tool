**Trey McBride** (ARI, TE, bye 14) — board rank 19 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** By far the most-discussed player in the corpus's punt-TE thread (45 comments, ~1,827 upvotes) — nobody disputes the role, everyone argues about the price.

- Role: the unquestioned first read in the Arizona passing game and the fixed point every Michael Wilson and Marvin Harrison target-share argument is measured against. The fanbase calls him a clear top-3 and arguably the top TE in football, and cites his contract — signed as the highest-paid TE at the time after nearly walking away over an offer he found insulting — as evidence the team will keep feeding him.
- Production behind it: 126 catches, 1,239 yards and 11 TDs last season, roughly 100-105 points clear of TE2 Kyle Pitts in half PPR and the 9th-best overall flex scorer — a second straight 1,000-yard, 100-catch season, against a Kelce career high of 110.
- The regression case is specific and worth reading: he ran 667 routes, 162 more than Kelce and more than any receiver in the league, on a team that led the league in pass rate over expectation (7.3%) and attempts (649), with 39.9% of his points coming while trailing in the second half. Citing JJ Zacharison, that is roughly 54 points of pass-volume inflation — 18.6 PPG down to about 15.4. Risks named alongside it: a new OC, a stronger RB room, healthy target competition from Harrison and Wilson, and QB ambiguity if Jacoby Brissett is benched for Carson Beck.
- The rebuttal, at equal weight: strip four touchdowns and he still averaged 13.6 half-PPR PPG, which would have been WR10, and one commenter argues he would have been TE1 in half and full PPR with zero touchdowns. Cardinals fans add that a bad defence and a brutal schedule mean negative scripts and heavy late passing volume.
- Market: the room drafts him slightly later than the board does. Reported spots cluster at the 2/3 turn — 2.12, 3.4, 3.5, pick 29, pick 30, 3.11 — and he has gone ahead of Brock Bowers in several drafts, which surprised commenters. Cooper will not spend a 2nd or 3rd, arguing the top-5 all-time TE season needed a perfect runout (the Harrison injury, the QB change, Arizona being the only team not running 30 times a game) and that a back still outscored him by 100 PPR points. Loza is passing too, preferring Colston Loveland or Tyler Warren around round 4.
- Sentiment: even the punt-TE crowd carves him out — McBride or Bowers at the 2/3 turn is the near-consensus exception; the top comment there is a manager who won two titles with him and still hesitates to pay again.

**Draft take:** Pay the 2/3 turn if you want the positional edge, but the volume that produced last year's season is the one thing the room agrees is not coming back.
