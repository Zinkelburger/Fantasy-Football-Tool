**Amon-Ra St. Brown** (DET, WR, bye 6) — board rank 12 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the room's designated safe pick, and the only argument against him is that safety does not win championships.

- Healthy, and about as durable as the position gets: 16 or 17 games in all five NFL seasons, one or two games missed in his whole career. One commenter notes a wrist/hand injury early last season, plus a game he left after the first drive, cost him what had been a WR1-overall pace.
- The role is settled and the production is the proof: three straight top-3 WR finishes on lines of 117-1401-11, 115-1263-12 and 119-1515-10, and top-8 every year since he became a starter. Commenters point out the volume held up even when Detroit's offense was badly run for half of last season.
- Two things could cut into it, and neither is a report. New OC Drew Petzing is the only red flag the room can produce, and his Arizona record divides them — Kyler stagnated under him, but Brissett had his best passing year and Michael Wilson broke out. Secondary worry is touchdown share going to Jameson Williams, who commenters say looked like a beast in camp, and a healthy Sam LaPorta, plus Gibbs absorbing red-zone work. The counter-camp argues Detroit is favoured in 14 games with a dome schedule and a defense bad enough to force shootouts.
- Market: the room pays board rank 12 and says he will not fall further. He goes around 13.5, was 22% of budget in a 12-team superflex auction (behind Chase 26% and Nacua 23%), went 1.05 in a 12-team SF snake, and two commenters report him sliding to picks 9-11 in Yahoo and 14-team mocks — a slide the room says it would happily take. He is the standing WR alternative at picks 4-6, weighed directly against James Cook and Jonathan Taylor; Twerski has him tied with Puka Nacua as his fourth-best player and calls 1.05 no reach, Sanderson would keep him over Saquon Barkley and De'Von Achane, and Yahoo ranks him WR3.
- Sentiment is uniformly positive and therefore worth little: 10 bust nominations drew a 324-point "wait people are saying ARSB could be a bust lmao," and the room prefers him to Nacua even spotting Nacua a two-game suspension (237 points).

**Draft take:** take him anywhere from pick 5 through his board rank as the safest floor in round 1 — the only reason to pass is if you specifically need a ceiling swing.
