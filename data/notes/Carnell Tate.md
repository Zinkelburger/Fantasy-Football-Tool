**Carnell Tate** (TEN, WR, bye 9) — board rank 72 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the consensus top rookie receiver of the class and the room is actively selling him after one bad preseason series.

- Role is not in dispute: commenters expect him to lead the Titans receiver room in snaps as the primary outside WR, and note he was the 4th overall pick, so a starter by default. The counter, from mid-August, is that a rookie gets eased in and needs a year — the main argument for Wan'Dale Robinson keeping the target share, and it only holds while Robinson does.
- Camp was the high point: Austin Stanley (A to Z Sports, 8/9) had him "dominated Titans training camp on Sunday, catching three touchdown passes from quarterback Cam Ward in a single move-the-ball period," with other media on the sideline calling it his best day.
- Preseason was the reversal: 10 snaps across the 1st and 3rd possessions, 3 targets, 0 catches (8/14). A film evaluator on 8/30 says he has not separated in preseason, which does not match his college film, and dropped a pass after no drops in his final college season. No injury is cited — the concern is performance.
- The same evaluator is not worried about the talent (a very good route runner for his size, elite hands) but about the ecosystem, comping the bad case to Marvin Harrison Jr's situation. Murchison, more mildly, has him as a fine second flex in a 14-team league, especially if Tennessee trails often.
- Market: the rankers still have him first among 2026 rookie WRs — Cooper ahead of Makai Lemon and Jordyn Tyson, Hartitz highest of the class (though Hartitz would trade Tate plus a late 1st and a mid 2nd for Malik Nabers, and picks Chris Bell as the rookie likeliest to lead in points). The room pays less than that: 5th round of a 12-team, 4th in a startup, with replies calling him a bench stash rather than a plug-and-play flex, upside in the second half. Dynasty owners are trying to buy the dip and being refused — "For the 10th time, no, I will not accept your Tate for my Stribling."
- Sentiment is the fade case: "Drop Tate, he's cooked" was the top comment in the 8/14 thread at 523 points. Several pushed back that it was one game and three drives, but the room used it to sell. One commenter now argues Concepcion, Tyson and Lemon can all be taken over him in dynasty, a reversal from his post-draft standing.

**Draft take:** the price has already dropped to bench-stash and the bear case is three preseason targets — take him where the room now lets him fall and start him in the second half, not Week 1.
