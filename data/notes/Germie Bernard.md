**Germie Bernard** (PIT, WR, bye 9) — board rank 251 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Steelers fans are enthusiastic and specific about the traits; nobody argues he matters in 2026.

- No injury reported. The one health-adjacent item is that he will wear a Guardian Cap over his helmet all season, in games and not only practice — a voluntary choice very few players make, and the entire substance of that thread.
- Role: fourth in line, and the room is explicit that heavy snaps would be a bad sign. The most detailed argument is that Pittsburgh should run a lot of 12 personnel with DK Metcalf, Michael Pittman and Pat Freiermuth as the receiving options, so a high Bernard snap count would signal a problem with one of those three; in 11 personnel he could be a change-of-pace, but the room doubts the offense has the speed to use it consistently. He did take first-team reps on 08-11 per Brooke Pryor (ESPN) — with Pittman and Metcalf both out of practice. A commenter dropping Roman Wilson on 09-04 says he does not think Wilson "will ever surpass Germie".
- Aaron Rodgers, via Pryor, is the best source on him and is careful: Bernard has a "really bright future" but "just needs to learn the offense inside and out. And when he does that, he has a lot of great physical traits. He can get his feet in the ground quickly. He's very sudden. He's got natural hands."
- The traits case is a YAC case: 17 forced missed tackles at Alabama last year, then 46 of his 64 preseason receiving yards after the catch, 30 YAC on four catches against Green Bay, and a screen touchdown against Buffalo. The commenter making it concedes he still has to earn snaps.
- Sentiment: Steelers fans call him a "YAC monster" the team has not had in years (50 pts) and "our future WR2"; the tempering view calls him a good WR3, the best since Martavis Bryant, and says expecting a 2026 impact is too much. In dynasty he is named about six times as a taxi stash, and the room split hard in August over him versus Ja'Kobi Lane.

**Draft take:** a dynasty taxi stash and nothing in redraft — the snaps only arrive if Metcalf, Pittman or Freiermuth goes down.
