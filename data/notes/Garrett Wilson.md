**Garrett Wilson** (NYJ, WR, bye 13) — board rank 40 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the room thinks the Jets discount on him has gone too far, and most of the room is still taking somebody else.

- Healthy again as of 9/4, and named among the reasons the Jets passing game should be better than last season. He played only 5-7 games in 2025.
- Role is not in question. Jeremy Fowler of ESPN reported on 8/11 that Wilson is the Jets' clear-cut No. 1 receiver and is "cooking" with Geno Smith in camp. One commenter says everything out of camp points to roughly 14 targets a game. He has been a solid WR2 every year he played a full season, flirted with WR1 in 2024, and was producing at a WR5 rate before the injury last year with Justin Fields and Tyrod Taylor throwing — and when he was out, nobody else on the Jets stepped up.
- The bear case is the offense and the coaching staff, not the player: commenters call his touchdown rate unsustainable, say garbage-time bombs from Fields inflated the run, and expect Geno Smith to get hurt or the Jets to tank late. Cooper names him the receiver he is least confident in across his whole WR13-24 range for exactly that reason — the talent and targets are there but a QB change down the stretch scares him. One Ringer host is "personally very very low" on him, arguing models miss how bad the vibes get if the Jets start badly, with Glenn on the hot seat and Reich out of touch.
- Market is where the decision is. Board rank 40 has him in the fourth round; the room is letting him go a full round later than Chris Olave, into rounds 4-5, and one commenter took him in the 5th of a 12-team after punting WR. Maher has him 31st overall against an ADP of 44 and an ECR of 35-38, grouping him with Waddle and McConkey as round-3 values going in round 4. Sanderson calls him a substantial discount on talent, bigger than last year's, and says the price justifies bad coaching. Twerski is the dissent: he has not found himself willing to draft Wilson at cost, which he frames as a read on Geno Smith.
- Sentiment: the room's own highest-upvoted verdict (439 points) is that Wilson was crushing last year in a much worse environment and the fade has gone too far — but the loudest idea in the corpus is still "Somebody has to get points on the Jets. No. No they don't." at 1,802 points.

**Draft take:** take him in round 4-5 where the Jets discount actually pays you — a clear-cut No. 1 receiver at a WR3 price is the best ADP-relative bet in this range.
