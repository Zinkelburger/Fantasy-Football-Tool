**Michael Pittman** (PIT, WR, bye 9) — board rank 108 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** a dink-and-dunk receiver landing on a dink-and-dunk quarterback, and the room likes the fit more than it likes the player.

- The only injury reporting is old and thin. Brooke Pryor (ESPN) had him out of Steelers practice for a second straight day on 2026-08-11, and a commenter relaying camp chatter on 08-18 guessed a recurrence of the back problem he had in Indianapolis — unsourced, no beat writer attached, and nothing since confirms it.
- Role: traded from Indianapolis to Pittsburgh, he is the second option behind DK Metcalf in a run-first, 12-personnel offense. The recurring read is Aaron Rodgers' safety valve — Loza calls him "Aaron Rodgers' new Kenneth Gainwell", one commenter predicts he is Rodgers' No. 1 target, and five top-level nominations describe a short-to-intermediate PPR receiver going to a quarterback who can only throw short to intermediate. The ceiling gets capped in the same breath: one backer would be surprised if he clears 100 receiving yards more than twice, and another says only the Steelers' backs reach 1,000 yards because Rodgers gets rid of the ball fast.
- His old fanbase is the bear case. Colts commenters mostly endorsed letting him go — not the same since the back injury, no connection with the quarterback, and a 2025 line of 80/111/784/7 that Keenan Allen matched for a third of the cost against a backloaded $24M cap hit. A minority expects him to burn Indianapolis for 1,000 yards.
- Market and sentiment both say he is too cheap: seven top-level nominations (~37 upvotes), all "too low", pitching Pittsburgh plus Rodgers as a 120-plus target formula and a set-and-forget PPR WR2 at the price, with commenters repeatedly saying they would rather have him than Metcalf and one calling the connection the craziest sleeper on the board after a solid camp together. Dopp lists him a top-three value pick at ADP; Cooper names him a bounce-back and one of the cheap target-floor receivers he fills an auction roster with. The counterweight is a blanket Steelers fade.

**Draft take:** a target-floor PPR WR3 at board rank 108 that the room thinks is a round or two light — draft him for receptions, not yards.
