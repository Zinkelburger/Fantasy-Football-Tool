**Juwan Johnson** (NO, TE, bye 8) — board rank 117 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The room's favourite mid-round tight end, and the whole case is conditional on Jordyn Tyson being out — the standing dissent is that he was already New Orleans' No. 2 after the Shaheed trade last year and it produced no bump.

- Role right now is receiver, not tight end: commenters describe him as the Saints' WR2 in practice and the second-most targeted player in the offense throughout camp, with a floor around four receptions from Tyler Shough — **only while Jordyn Tyson and Alvin Kamara are out**. A minority argue the team cleared the way for him and he keeps the job when Tyson returns; most do not.
- Week 1 (2026-09-03): with Tyson and Kamara out, commenters put him in line for double-digit targets and a red-zone look or two against a Lions defence missing both starting safeties (Kerby Joseph and Brian Branch), in a game New Orleans is expected to trail and throw in. Hartitz and Cooper both single out that matchup; Cooper calls him his favourite mid-round TE.
- The baseline behind the hype is real: TE8 in PPR last season on 77 catches, 889 yards and 102 targets — 5th in receptions, 3rd in yards, 7th in targets among TEs — but only 3 TDs, and his 73 first-read targets ranked 3rd at the position. From Weeks 9-17, once Shough took over, he averaged 12.0 points a game, 7th among tight ends. One commenter makes the positive-TD-regression case; a reply notes his best season still only finished TE7.
- The bear case, from within the same threads: he is 30, he is not a top-two target when everyone is healthy, and New Orleans added Noah Fant plus a rookie. One commenter argues Fant's arrival is partly a positive because it lets the Saints move Johnson into the slot rather than leave him attached to the formation. Maher has him TE14-15; Hartitz has him around TE11.
- Market: consensus PPR ADP around TE16 (roughly pick 150) with a Sleeper ADP of TE22, and the room reports taking him in rounds 13-16 or finding him undrafted — later than the board has him — but the price is already moving, with two reported leagues losing him by the 10th and 13th. Vegas had him as the Week 1 TE5 at 10.4 points, higher than the room expected. Sanderson rejects the lottery-ticket framing outright: "Don't think Juwan is a lotto ticket - I think he's just a starting TE."
- Sentiment: nine top-level nominations for 208 upvotes as the room's zero-TE target, and roughly ten commenters said start him over Kittle in Week 1 with nobody arguing back. The dissent worth keeping is that his 2025 PPG was only tied for TE12, and one commenter's flat read that Johnson has been hyped repeatedly across his career and missed each time.

**Draft take:** Draft him as the starting TE you get in the 13th while Tyson is out, and accept that the role, not the player, is what you are buying — when Tyson comes back you are streaming again.
