**Juwan Johnson** (NO, TE, bye 8) — board rank 117 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The room's favourite mid-round tight end, and nearly all of it rests on Jordyn Tyson being hurt.

- Role, and its condition: with Jordyn Tyson out — commenters say roughly the first two months — and Alvin Kamara also out, the Sept 3 consensus makes Johnson the Saints' WR2 in practice rather than just their tight end, the second-most-targeted player in the offence throughout camp, with a floor around four receptions from Tyler Shough. The argument that he keeps the WR2 job once Tyson is back is a projection, not reporting.
- Week 1, under the same conditions: commenters put him in line for double-digit targets and a red-zone look or two against a Lions defence missing both starting safeties, Kerby Joseph and Brian Branch, in a game the Saints are expected to have to throw. Cooper and Hartitz both flag that matchup; Hartitz has him around TE11 in the Kincaid/Likely tier, and Maher TE14-15, noting a couple of TE1 weeks would make him a real trade piece.
- The volume case predates the injury: 77 catches and 889 yards last season, about 10 targets a game in a limited-weapon offence with a short-passing quarterback and high pace, and 73 first-read targets, third among tight ends — argued Sept 2 to be the best path to 90-plus targets of any cheap TE. The counter in the same thread: he is 30, not a top-two target when everyone is healthy, and New Orleans added Noah Fant and a rookie.
- Market: he was a last-round punt name through August, going in the 10th or 11th and paired with Kittle for $6 total in one auction — roughly where board rank 117 still has him. The room has moved past that price: most upvotes of any name in the Sept 1 AMA, "Juwan szn" the top comment at 125 points in the Tyson-injury thread, and Vegas opening him as the Week 1 TE5 at 10.4, higher than the room expected. If you are still getting the August price, the board is behind the room.
- Sentiment: roughly ten commenters say start him over Kittle in Week 1 with none arguing back, and a separate asker was told to start him over Kyle Pitts. The dissent worth keeping is that Johnson has been hyped repeatedly across his career and underperformed each time.

**Draft take:** Buy him as a starting TE while Tyson and Kamara are out and the punt price still holds, but treat the WR2 role as expiring rather than permanent.
