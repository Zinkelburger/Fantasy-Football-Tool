**Jake Ferguson** (DAL, TE, bye 14) — board rank 106 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the room agrees his 2025 line was inflated by CeeDee Lamb's injury and disagrees only on whether the touchdowns keep him startable anyway.

- Role, per beat reports: Joseph Hoyt (Dallas Morning News, 8/9) says Ferguson continues to play well in camp while backup Luke Schoonmaker has had multiple drops; Dan Graziano (ESPN, 8/11) on the Cowboys practice he attended said he did not know exactly how many balls Ferguson caught but it felt like close to 1,000. No injury claims on Ferguson himself.
- The number everyone cites (9/2): TE20 in points per game with the Dallas receivers healthy, TE2 in points per game across the five games Lamb was banged up — 17 PPR points in games where Lamb's route share was under 50% versus 9.2 otherwise, and 0.3 points per route run with both Lamb and George Pickens playing, outside the top 40. The rebuttal lists 0.5-TEP weeks where he still produced as the third option.
- The regression case (8/20): Flournoy has taken the third-target role and last year's production was inflated by Lamb's injury, though seam and red-zone looks should keep him serviceable — a fade that applies only while Lamb is healthy. Hartitz goes further, expecting routes down because of Brevyn Spann-Ford and Schoonmaker, and would start Isaiah Likely over him. Hoyt's camp report cuts directly against the Schoonmaker half of that.
- Market: called a big value in the 8th-9th round and a common last-resort tight end on the reasoning that Dak Prescott gets him touchdowns — board rank 106 is about that range, so board and room agree on price. In dynasty the bid has dried up: one manager had lots of offers for him last season and now nobody is interested, another calls him "at least rosterable" but not valuable.

**Draft take:** a touchdown-dependent TE2 whose ceiling needs a Dallas receiver to get hurt — fine in the 8th or 9th, wrong as your only tight end.
