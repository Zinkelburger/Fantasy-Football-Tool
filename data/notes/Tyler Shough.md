**Tyler Shough** (NO, QB, bye 8) — board rank 144 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the room's designated last quarterback — the pick that lets you wait all draft and still start someone.

- Rookie WR Jordyn Tyson is out roughly two months, and that is the live change to his outlook. The room argues he should slip a couple of ranking spots on it, since his 2026 projection had Tyson baked in; the rebuttal is that he proved himself last season with Tyson still in college. A commenter relaying the Fantasy Footballers (8/30) says the show has done a weekly eulogy for Tyson on the last two or three episodes and holds that Shough will not be as good until Tyson is back — while otherwise being high on him.
- Role: he took over as the Saints' starter in Week 9 last season, and the case for him is built on that weeks 9-17 sample — around 12th in points over the back half, 17-20 fantasy points with a depleted receiving corps in a fast-paced offense. Commenters say he leans heavily on Chris Olave, particularly in the red zone, and one credits instant chemistry with Olave in the new Kellen Moore scheme for Olave's own rising price.
- With Tyson out, his supporting cast is Olave, Juwan Johnson and Devaughn Vele, which several say caps him around QB10-12 rather than a locked QB1 — that read is conditional on Tyson returning and Olave staying healthy. Jeff Nowak reported from camp on 8/9 that the Shough-Tyson connection was building, which is the piece currently on hold.
- The bull case, the most-upvoted pick in its thread at 50: soft schedule, a healthy and improved offensive line, an improved receiver room and a second year with Kellen Moore.
- Market: ADP 123.9 in mid-August and reported going 14th round or undrafted in most leagues, with real picks in rounds 6 through 11 of 12-team superflex as a QB2/QB3 and one mocked 11th-rounder. Several posters plan on him specifically as the last quarterback they will accept, usually as the second half of a late double-tap with Malik Willis, Kyler Murray or Jacoby Brissett. Two commenters now say it is a good time to cash in relative to where he was drafted, and one has already sold.
- Sentiment: two commenters plan to start him over Jayden Daniels in Week 1 despite his being outside the posted top 12; one adviser prefers Drake Maye and Jameson Williams as keepers over a Shough slot. The dissent is not about the player — it is that a Saints quarterback will not out-score whatever else is on the board that late.

**Draft take:** the correct last quarterback in a superflex or a wait-on-QB build, but discount him until Jordyn Tyson is back rather than paying the pre-injury projection.
