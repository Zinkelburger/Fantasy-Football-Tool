**Brenton Strange** (JAC, TE, bye 7) — board rank 132 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** a recurring last-round sleeper whose case is red-zone usage, and whose problem is that Jacksonville spreads the ball too widely for anyone below the top to hold a weekly spot.

- Health: he is healthy again after an injury-hit 2025 that limited Jacksonville's two-TE looks all season, and his return plus two drafted tight ends is the stated reason the room expects much less 11 personnel. John Shipley (SI) had him not practising on 8/11; nothing since suggests that carried.
- Role: the room's consensus (five nominations, 21 upvotes) is that he is clearly targeted in the red zone and therefore TD-dependent — with the qualifier the room adds itself, that Jakobi Meyers arrived to take some of those looks. A Jaguars fan who rosters him says he is playable only when the matchup says so.
- The bear reads are blunter: one commenter puts him third at best in Jacksonville's pecking order, and Cooper has him off his board entirely, saying Strange would need multiple receiver injuries to become a top-two target on his own team.
- Market is thin. Hartitz's framing is the useful one: a Juwan Johnson plus Brenton Strange tight end room can still eke out low-end TE1 production, because there is only about 1.1 PPR points per game between TE9 and TE16. He is otherwise a 15th-round, last-pick name, taken because Jacksonville paid him and because there are questions around the rest of that pass-catching group. One outlying prediction has him a top-three tight end.

**Draft take:** a last-pick TD-streamer with a real red-zone role and a new mouth in Meyers ahead of him — draft him at the end or not at all.
