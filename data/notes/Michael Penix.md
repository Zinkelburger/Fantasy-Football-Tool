**Michael Penix** (ATL, QB, bye 11) — board rank 236 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A knee and a job, neither of them settled — this is an availability question wearing a quarterback debate.

- Availability first: he is coming off ACL rehab with three career ACL tears (two in one knee, one in the other) plus a season-ending college shoulder injury. He was limited to 7-on-7 from the start of camp and only cleared for 11-on-11 around 08-26, with roughly a week and a half of real practice, so the room's worry is that Atlanta starts him before he is 100%. He has no rushing upside to absorb hits, and a commenter's summary of the room is "Tua is toast and Penix is glass". Asked directly whether he is healthy, a commenter relayed that per Penix himself the ACL is "amazing" — no beat or team source behind it.
- The job: Ian Rapoport reported on 08-26 that Penix is the favorite to be named Atlanta's Week 1 starter, with Tua Tagovailoa's play making it impossible to name Tua QB1. That is the strongest source in the corpus and nothing at equal weight has overturned it — but it has not been confirmed either. As of 09-01 and 09-02, commenters were still describing Tua as the starter with the Falcons "biding their time", and Murchison twice framed Drake London's and Kyle Pitts's outlooks as conditional on Penix being declared QB1. Treat the job as unresolved and trending his way.
- The fantasy hook is the London connection: he started five of the six healthy 2025 games in which London posted top-3 target and yardage rates, and commenters doubt Tua replicates it with that receiver archetype.
- Sentiment is sharply mixed. Defenders point to 13 career games, a 2-1 TD/INT ratio and a massive offensive upgrade when he started; critics call him one of the least accurate quarterbacks in the league, among the worst from a clean pocket, and a bottom-10 starter — 4-8 as a starter, 12 TDs in 12 games, 60% completions. The most-upvoted line in the biggest Atlanta thread was that the Falcons "don't have a QB" (2,753 pts).

**Draft take:** Not a draftable fantasy quarterback at any cost; his real value on your board is as the swing factor under Drake London, so track whether Atlanta actually names him before you price London.
