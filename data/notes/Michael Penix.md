**Michael Penix** (ATL, QB, bye 11) — board rank 236 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Atlanta will not commit, the room does not trust the knee, and the highest-voted verdict in the corpus is that the Falcons "don't have a QB" (2,753 points).

- Availability is the whole story. He is still recovering from an ACL tear — three ACL tears in total, two in one knee, plus a season-ending college shoulder injury — with no 16-game season in two years as a pro. He was limited to 7-on-7 from the start of camp and only cleared for 11-on-11 in late August with roughly a week and a half of real practice, which the room says could force Atlanta to start him before he is fully ready. A commenter on Sept 1 says he was medically cleared two to three weeks earlier, and another relays that per Penix himself the ACL is "amazing" — his own words, with no beat or team source behind either. He is not particularly mobile, which commenters say compounds the risk; the room's shorthand is "Tua is toast and Penix is glass."
- The job is unresolved, and the reporting moved the wrong way. Rapoport reported on Aug 26 that Penix was the favourite to be named Week 1 starter, with Tua Tagovailoa's play making it impossible to name Tua QB1. The Sept 3 report is softer: Atlanta plans for him to start eventually, with the next week of practices deciding whether he opens or Tua goes in as the "placeholder." The room read that second report as a non-endorsement of both quarterbacks rather than news. Several expect him starting by around Week 3.
- Why he matters beyond his own line: he targets Drake London at a league-leading rate and started five of the six healthy 2025 games in which London posted top-3 target and yardage rates. Commenters doubt Tua replicates that connection, having not worked with London's archetype before, and Murchison twice frames London's and Pitts's outlook as dependent on Penix being declared QB1.
- The player himself is a genuine split. Defenders: only 13 career games, a 2-1 TD/INT ratio, and a "massive upgrade" to the offense when he started — people sleep on him because he has been hurt, not bad. Critics: one of the least accurate quarterbacks in the league and among the worst from a clean pocket, 4-8 as a starter with 12 TDs in 12 games at 60% completions, and a bottom-10 starter unlikely to be the long-term answer.

**Draft take:** Not a fantasy quarterback, and nothing in the corpus contests his board rank — the only reason to track him is as the switch on Drake London and Kyle Pitts, and every timeline in this file is a commenter's, not a beat writer's.
