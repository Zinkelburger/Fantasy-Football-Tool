**Jeremiyah Love** (ARI, RB, bye 14) — board rank 21 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A top-three pick with a high ankle sprain on a bad team — the price has come down and the room still thinks it has not come down enough.

- High ankle sprain, Week 1 unsettled. He was hurt on a roll-up/hip-drop tackle in his first preseason game after roughly 14-20 touches. Schefter reported on 2026-08-17 that he would not play again in the preseason with Arizona hoping for the Sept 13 opener against the Chargers. The most recent report is the one to use: Mike Garafolo, citing HC Mike LaFleur (2026-09-01), says Love is progressing but a Week 1 return is unsettled, with a source calling it "about 50/50" and the team wanting another week-plus of progress.
- Read the coach-optimism against the practice record. A physical-therapist analyst cites research putting isolated high ankle sprains around 27 missed days, which lands roughly at Week 1, and expects a return to pre-injury form after a few weeks of ramp-up. The room is far more pessimistic — several independently put him at Week 3 to Week 6 or October, citing an 8-12 week recovery and lost explosiveness after.
- Role is a committee more than a bellcow, at least early. Arizona kept James Conner and signed Tyler Allgeier, and commenters expect Allgeier to hold the goal-line and touchdown work behind a weak offensive line — the Bijan/Allgeier rookie-year comparison recurs. Missing two weeks of camp is expected to cost a rookie passing-down trust and force a snap count. Against that, he was the third overall pick with the most guaranteed money at the position, and the counter-view is that healthy, he takes the clear majority of touches.
- Market vs the board: the board has him 21 and the room now pays a round or two later. Reported picks after the news run from 15 and 24-28 down to the third round and 4.10/4.11, with a loud do-not-draft faction. Dopp names him his favourite RB2 behind Gibbs precisely because the ankle made a Gibbs-WR-Love start affordable; Murchison has him RB17, a slight bump down; Twerski would not spend a top pick on him.
- Sentiment is the negative kind worth weighting: the most-upvoted bust nomination in one thread (14 top-level comments, ~1,324 upvotes) and a 12-nomination "too high" read elsewhere, all on the same premise — an unproven rookie behind a bad line on a bad team with the hardest schedule.

**Draft take:** Buy the discount at the 3/4 turn but not before it — the 50/50 Week 1 is real and Allgeier owns the goal line either way.
