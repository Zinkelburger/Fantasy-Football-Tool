**Jeremiyah Love** (ARI, RB, bye 14) — board rank 21 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The most-nominated bust of the summer and a favourite value of half the analysts in the corpus — the ankle is why both are true.

- High ankle sprain, and Week 1 is genuinely unsettled. Schefter reported on 17 August that Love had a high ankle sprain, was unlikely to play again in preseason, and that Arizona hoped he would return for the 13 September opener against the Chargers; he was hurt on an awkward roll-up tackle after roughly 20 snaps of his first preseason game. The latest word is Mike Garafolo on 1 September, citing HC Mike LaFleur: Love is progressing but a Week 1 return is unsettled, with a source putting it "about 50/50" and the team wanting another week-plus of progress.
- The room never believed the Week 1 target. Several commenters independently put him at Week 3 to Week 6 or October, citing an 8-12 week recovery and lost explosiveness afterwards. The physical therapist working these threads is the most concrete voice against them: research has NFL players missing about 27 days with an isolated high ankle sprain — roughly Week 1 for him — with pre-injury form back after a few weeks of ramp-up. Durability doubts run past this ankle: a Notre Dame follower says he fought ankle injuries throughout college, so expect recurring questionable tags. Cardinals fans would rather sit him than rush him; the top comment there (31 points) says keep him out unless he is 100%, and notes high ankle sprains are frequently re-injured. Fantasy Points' Jeff Mueller said in August the ankle may linger and that it was already priced into his rankings.
- Role is the other half of the argument. He went third overall with more guaranteed money than Bijan Robinson; the bull case projects at least a 60% snap share plus the checkdown work in the negative game scripts Arizona will play in, and calls him the team's best pass-catching back. The bear case is a real committee — Tyler Allgeier was signed before the draft and is repeatedly named the goal-line and touchdown vulture behind a weak Arizona line, James Conner is still on the roster, and the Bijan/Allgeier rookie-year comparison gets made constantly. His college usage against Price is the strongest thing on his side: roughly 200 carries at 6.9 yards per carry with 18 touchdowns to Price's 100 at 6.0 and 11, plus essentially all the receiving work. Missing two weeks of camp is expected to cost him passing-down trust and force an eased return, and a projected four-win team has no reason to rush him.
- Market: he cost about 20th overall — right at his board rank — before the injury, as ESPN's RB13,; one drafter took him at 19 and another paid $45 in auction the night before the news. The room's post-news landing spot is mid-3rd at best, with reported picks scattered from 15 down to 4.10, and that gap is the opportunity. Dopp calls him his favourite RB2 behind Gibbs precisely because the ADP fell; Sanderson gives him the same treatment as Breece Hall — an R1/R2 talent profile at a round-3 cost and his recommended round-3 back after a WR-heavy start. Murchison has him RB17; Twerski would not spend a top pick on him.
- Sentiment: the most-upvoted bust nomination in the corpus, the room leaning "too high" for an unproven rookie on a bad team with the hardest schedule. The high-vote disagreement is worth the space: a 110-point comment calls him the far better prospect with a top-5 ceiling, an 81-point reply says no Arizona back has a top-5 ceiling in that offense, and a 47-point reply answers that Achane did it on a bad team last year.

**Draft take:** Do not pay the first-round price the board implies — wait for him at the 3rd-round cost the ankle created, and plan to start the season without him.
