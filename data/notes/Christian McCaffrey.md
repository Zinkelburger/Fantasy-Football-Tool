**Christian McCaffrey** (SF, RB, bye 8) — board rank 4 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Nothing is wrong with him right now; the entire argument is whether last year's touch count is a bill that comes due.

- **The August "tightness" is resolved.** He sat out early-August practices in street clothes; Kyle Shanahan (via Nick Wagoner, 8/11) said there was no worry and that he would be more concerned if McCaffrey tried to play through it. Rapoport reported 8/18 it was genuine tightness rather than a hold-in for a new contract. By 8/25 he had practised two straight days with player and coach calling him 100%, and on 9/1 he went through drills to a chorus of "football player practices". No designation is live.
- **The workload case against him is real but the room cannot agree on the number.** Claims variously cite 413 regular-season touches (449 with the playoffs), 440 as 311 carries plus 129 receptions, and 450 as 337 plus 113. Take the direction, not the digit: it was the league's heaviest load, at age 30. Twerski's version is the sharpest — since 2010, RBs with 330-plus touches in a season averaged over 100 fewer PPR points the following year, and McCaffrey himself has twice topped 330 and then played three and four games. He will not touch him inside the top 10. The room adds that only LaDainian Tomlinson ever survived a 370-plus season without a fantasy decline.
- Volume is not in question. Shanahan runs everything through him and commenters say he has never used a committee with a healthy lead back; with Pearsall out, Aiyuk and Jennings gone and Kittle back from an Achilles, there is nobody else to feed. He was one of only six RBs above a 70% snap share in 2025 and led the listed top PPR backs at 18.3 carries a game. Kittle's return is read as a small net positive — better run blocking, fewer forced targets. The one flag is efficiency: his 2025 rushing metrics were poor on that volume.
- **Market: board rank 4 is roughly where he is priced, but he keeps falling past it.** Drafters report him at 5, 6, 7, 1.07, 1.08, 1.09, the 1/2 turn and once pick 12 in a 10-teamer; the room's rule is that a fall to 12 is an automatic take. Cooper takes him at 6 in full PPR on 417 PPR points last year; Hartitz has him third overall and has taken him at 1.03; a keeper-league manager put his worth at 9th overall.
- He was the room's runaway pick for 2026 first-round bust (33 top-level comments, ~1,709 upvotes) and, in another thread, a 338-point "league-winner too good to pass at this cost". Both sides concede the same rebuttal: if he plays 90% of games there is no bust case.

**Draft take:** Take him if he slips past the top six and roster the handcuff the same night; paying rank 4 means paying full price for the heaviest workload in the league at 30.
