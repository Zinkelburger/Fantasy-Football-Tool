**Kaleb Johnson** (GB, RB, bye 11) — board rank 244 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** A bust label and a new address — the room reads him as a near-free depth add whose only path to touches is an injury ahead of him.

- His own status is boring and his case is entirely someone else's: he is healthy, rostered and third in line, and he only matters while Josh Jacobs is on the commissioner exempt list and MarShawn Lloyd is banged up. Green Bay traded for him the same day as the Jacobs news, and the room reads the low price as a signal the team does not expect a long Jacobs absence — so the window may be short. The August cut speculation is dead.
- The trade: Steelers beat writers had him about to be released; instead Green Bay acquired him, confirmed by Jeremy Fowler (ESPN) on Aug 30 after it first surfaced on his own Instagram. Gutekunst said the move was Green Bay's response to losing Josh Jacobs and that there was little on the market — nobody would trade a proven back. Compensation is a sixth-round pick per both clubs; a 2028 seventh appeared on one wire and the room treats it as a typo.
- He is not the starter. Consensus places him third in line behind MarShawn Lloyd, Jacobs aside. He is new to the system and expected to need a week or two, but is named the better back to stash there and a candidate for goal-line carries. Nobody in the corpus knows whether his pass protection holds up, which is the gate on any third-down work.
- Why Pittsburgh moved on, per the room: he was drafted as an outside-zone fit for Arthur Smith, and with Tomlin and Smith gone the new 11-personnel offense does not run that scheme. He also never separated from Warren, Dowdle, Nichols and Homer in preseason despite out-snapping every other Steelers back. The 2025 line the room keeps citing: 10 games, 28 carries, 69 yards, 2.5 yards per carry, no touchdowns, fumbles and a kick-return blunder that cost a game. Cooper puts it harder — two different coaching staffs looked at him and decided he was not a player who would be active on game day.
- Analysts disagree on how much to care. Sanderson calls him the more interesting Green Bay option behind Lloyd, one of his two favourite very late darts, and worth rostering to see if he works into a role over the first couple of weeks. Twerski names him the best Packers back after Lloyd and a must-grab late handcuff. Cooper would roster Chris Brooks over him in a deep league.
- Market: essentially undrafted at 251st. In dynasty, three commenters say they had offers right after the rookie draft — one was George Pickens straight up — and should have taken them; another says to sell now while "there's some hype again."
- Sentiment is overwhelmingly negative — 16 top-level mentions worth 206 upvotes in one thread, mostly "Kaleb Johnson sucks" and "stay far away," with a suggested FAAB cap of $5 as a Lloyd handcuff. The one bull scenario, at about 46 points, is an RB1 finish if Jacobs does not play this year.

**Draft take:** A $5 dart that only pays while Jacobs is on the exempt list and Lloyd is banged up, and he is third in line even then — roster him in deep leagues only, and cut him the week Jacobs is back.
