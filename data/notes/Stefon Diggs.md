**Stefon Diggs** (WAS, WR, bye 7) — board rank 134 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** the one Washington player the room likes at cost, with the age and the offense as the two standing objections.

- Only injury note in the corpus: John Keim (ESPN) had him not practising on 8/11. Nothing since, and no one has reported a follow-up — treat him as healthy but unconfirmed.
- Role is Washington's WR2 behind Terry McLaurin, signed at 33 off an 85/1,013 season. One commenter calls him the clear number two with a chance at heavy volume early while Antonio Williams ramps up; another projects him to absorb roughly half of Washington's 209 vacated targets, including 12 in the end zone, and argues he is more likely than McLaurin to be the team's best fantasy WR.
- The sharpest bear argument is scheme, not age: the room's worked example of a Joe Brady offense throttling a WR1 is Diggs himself in 2023 — through ten games under Ken Dorsey he averaged 10.2 targets and 86.8 yards a game, and after Brady took over play-calling that fell to 8.3 targets and 45 yards, with Shakir and Kincaid leading the team in targets. A counter-commenter says that split was a contract and personality rift rather than scheme.
- The rest of the fade is environment: a Commanders line the room expects to be bad, a run-first quarterback after years with an elite passer, and several saying his best days are behind him. The Ringer host's version — "liked Diggs a lot but is worried about the Washington offense".
- Market has moved past the board. His ADP rose sharply after he signed: one commenter has him going around pick 90, another passed on him at a 7.01 in the early eighth, and the room repeatedly calls him a steal as a WR4/5 at the 9/10 turn. The board rank still has him in the eleventh. The room is paying one to two rounds earlier than the board says, and one commenter attributes that specifically to TikTok sleeper hype.

**Draft take:** fine at the 9/10 turn as a WR4 with real target volume, but the room has already bid him into the 7th-8th in places and that is where the value stops.
