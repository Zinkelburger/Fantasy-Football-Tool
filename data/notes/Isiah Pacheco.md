**Isiah Pacheco** (DET, RB, bye 6) — board rank 151 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** Detroit signed him as a cheap complement, he went on IR without playing a snap, and Lions fans mostly shrugged.

- On injured reserve with a back injury, announced by the Lions on Sept 1; he misses at least four games. Detroit deliberately kept him on the initial 53 at cutdowns before making the move so he stays return-eligible rather than being lost for the season — which is why five running backs made the roster.
- Detroit hosted Kareem Hunt on a free-agent visit on Sept 3, which commenters tie directly to the absence.
- Durability is disputed rather than settled. One tally has four straight years of significant missed time — three games in 2023, ten in 2024 with a broken leg, four last year with an MCL, at least four now; the counter is that outside the leg break he has missed only four games in four years.
- The role was small before the injury and is smaller now. He signed as Detroit's RB2 on a one-year deal around $1.8M to be the short-yardage and early-down complement behind Jahmyr Gibbs, which the room pegged at roughly five to eight touches a game. Even then, several had him no higher than second and possibly behind undrafted back Jacob Saylors, who is the next man up while he is out, with Vaki and Smalls behind.
- The handcuff value is gone for now — owners report IR-stashing or dropping him outright, and Cooper moved him from first to last in his handcuff ranking unless you have an IR spot. Only eight top-level comments named him in the thread that broke the news.
- Sentiment is flatly negative: Lions fans call the signing an unreliable placeholder and the injury predictable (34 upvotes), and Chiefs commenters said back in August that he lost his burst and showed no juice in his last two years. The minority defense is that a sub-$2M bargain bet is not something to be angry about.

**Draft take:** Do not draft him — he is a waiver name for whenever the Lions confirm a return date, and only then if Gibbs's workload has actually opened up.
