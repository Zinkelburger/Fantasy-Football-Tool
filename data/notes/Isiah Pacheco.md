**Isiah Pacheco** (DET, RB, bye 6) — board rank 151 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The handcuff value is gone for at least a month, and Lions fans were not sold on him even before the IR move.

- Detroit placed Pacheco on injured reserve on 1 September with a back injury, out a minimum of four games. The Lions deliberately kept him on the initial 53-man roster at cutdowns before making the move so that he stays return-eligible after four games rather than being lost for the season — that is why five backs made the 53. Jacob Saylors is next man up, with Vaki and Smalls behind him; Detroit also hosted Kareem Hunt on a free-agent visit.
- Durability is genuinely disputed in the room. One side counts four straight years of significant missed time — 3 games in 2023, 10 in 2024 with a broken leg, 4 last year with an MCL, at least 4 now. The other side says that outside the leg break he has missed only 4 games in 4 years. Both are stated as fact in the same thread and neither is sourced further.
- Role when he returns is Detroit's RB2 on a one-year deal around $1.8M — the short-yardage and early-down complement behind Jahmyr Gibbs, which the room pegs at roughly 5-8 touches a game, or about 30% of the backfield's work. Even before the IR move commenters had him no higher than second and possibly behind undrafted back Jacob Saylors, who reporting cited in the thread had as the backup. Dissent says Gibbs is the better goal-line back anyway and that Pacheco is not David Montgomery.
- Market: essentially nothing left. Owners report IR-stashing or simply dropping him — "won't touch him with a 2ft pole" — and Cooper edited his handcuff ranking to move Pacheco from first to last unless you have an IR spot. Hartitz is fine cutting him outright if you do not. Only eight top-level comments named him across the IR threads, for 15 upvotes total.
- Sentiment: Lions fans call the signing an unreliable placeholder and say the injury was predictable (34 upvotes), with "cooked anyway" and "injury prone RB on IR without ever suiting up" typical. Chiefs commenters said separately in August that he lost his burst and was already a limited back before the leg injury. The defence is only that he was a sub-$2M bargain-bin bet.

**Draft take:** Off the board in redraft unless you have a free IR slot — he is out four games minimum and returns to a 5-8 touch role that nobody in Detroit is excited about.
