**Jonah Coleman** (DEN, RB, bye 10) — board rank 133 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The camp riser the room drafted on coach signals, tempered by everyone's memory of what Sean Payton backfields do to fantasy value.

- He is a bet on J.K. Dobbins getting hurt, and Dobbins is healthy right now — no injury is reported anywhere in the corpus. Nearly every bull case carries the same rider: meaningful production most likely still takes a Dobbins injury, and the room disputes the size of even that payoff. "Coleman is in a timeshare with Dobbins healthy," and the direct comparison drawn against Emmett Johnson is that Johnson takes a whole load if Walker is hurt while Coleman still splits.
- Role: third in a Denver committee behind Dobbins and RJ Harvey, and Payton is the reason people believe he passes both. Asked why he pulled Coleman early in the first preseason game, Payton said "I didn't need to see additional evidence of what I've been seeing for 3 weeks," which the room reads as protecting a back he has already decided on. A commenter also reports Payton saying Coleman already looks like a veteran, and notes he never said anything comparable about Harvey.
- The scouting read (08-18): a good pass catcher whose pass blocking is looking really good, capable of being a three-down back, the best the Broncos have looked at the position since Javonte Williams pre-injury, with vision and a refusal to go down on contact. Commenters expect him to take pass-catching work from Harvey even with Dobbins healthy.
- The analysts split on how much that is worth. Cooper puts the odds of him running away with the job regardless of injury at "north of zero" and keeps him over other handcuffs; Hartitz is not high on him and thinks he is two injuries from mattering; Murchison likes him as a later-round snag who gives strong RB2 numbers during a Dobbins absence.
- The bear case is the coach, not the player: "You don't take Payton RBs" — a track record of ambiguous committees that never clarify. One projection has him around 10 touches a game, needing hyper-efficiency or red-zone share to matter; another calls him a jack of all trades, which is exactly what a Payton committee absorbs.
- Market is dynasty rookie-draft pricing, all self-reported: 1.12 twice, 2.06 and 2.07, sightings as high as 1.08-1.09, and one 1.05 the room calls indefensible because "the draft capital isn't there" — he was the 108th overall NFL pick. Redraft sentiment runs warmer: three top-level predictions (~86 pts) have him as the most productive rookie back this year, one as far as a top-10 finish.

**Draft take:** A late-round handcuff to a healthy J.K. Dobbins and nothing more — Payton clearly likes him, but every route to production runs through a Dobbins injury and then still through a committee.
