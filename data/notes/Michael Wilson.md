**Michael Wilson** (ARI, WR, bye 14) — board rank 74 · as of 2026-09-04

**Room sentiment:** The extension is signed and the role is defined; the whole argument is what a healthy Marvin Harrison Jr. does to his target share.

- The contract standoff is over. Schefter and Rapoport reported on 9/3 that Arizona reached an extension agreement with Wilson — three years through 2029, $75M, with Wilson expected to sign the same day; Josh Weinfuss reported the movement first. That closes out a week of "no way they reach a deal by Week 1" reporting, so it is no longer a Week 1 question. Terms land near $25M a year, short of the $30M AAV his side was said to want, with guarantees reported at $47-48M.
- Role: the room says he holds the Z job in new HC Mike LaFleur's McVay-style offense — the Robert Woods/Puka Nacua role — and LaFleur has compared him to Nacua. Wilson has said repeatedly this offseason that the previous staff misused him by only scheming for its X. One commenter tracks him moving all over the field in camp and preseason with motion, blocking and screen work, so he never comes off the field.
- The cap is Trey McBride, not the depth chart. Cardinals fans are blunt that McBride is the first receiving option, leaving Wilson the No. 2 and sometimes the No. 3, with Jeremiyah Love also drawing receiving work. They also discount 2025 as garbage time: Arizona threw a league-high 649 times because they were blown out often behind an RB5 backfield and a decimated defense, and that volume is not expected to repeat if they lead games and run.
- The splits are the load-bearing number and both sides use them: 34 yards and 6.2 half-PPR points per game in games with Marvin Harrison on the field, against 119.6 yards and 21.4 points per game without him. He was the overall WR2 across the last eight games of 2025 with Brissett throwing about 50 times a game. Full season: 126 targets, 78 catches, 1 drop, an 18.6% target share, 1.82 yards per route run, a 10.4 aDOT.
- Market: he goes around 76th overall, the 7th/8th turn, a round or two ahead of Marvin Harrison — right where the board has him — and the room mostly calls that a steal, "priced at his floor", with ADP expected to rise on the extension. The dissent is one commenter now fading him because the deal removes his contract-year motivation, heavily downvoted, and another calling last year fool's gold from game script.
- Sentiment: a 26-point comment says the majority of the sub now expects Wilson to outproduce Harrison. The equally upvoted rebuttal is that the breakout was three spike weeks in 12/13 personnel with Harrison hurt and no receiver competition. Elsewhere he is called boom-bust and too inconsistent to trade for.

**Draft take:** Take him at the 7th-round price as the Z in a pass-forced offense, but understand you are betting on Marvin Harrison Jr. missing time or failing again.
