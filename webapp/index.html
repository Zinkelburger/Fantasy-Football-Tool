What things mean

Rank
Overall board order. A blue number is a rank you imported yourself. Click the Rank / ESPN / Sleeper headers to sort by that column.
Pos (e.g. WR2)
Position plus NFL-team depth: WR2 means that team's 2nd-ranked WR — a quick read on target share and role.
ESPN / Sleeper
That site's own ranking; the column shown matches the site your draft is on (override in Settings). (+n) = the site is n spots higher on them than the board, so they'll go earlier there; (−n) = n spots lower — they may fall to you.
Bye
The week the player's team doesn't play. Don't stack your starters on one bye.
Target (last column)
Your shortlist marker — click it (or press D) to cycle ○ no opinion → ✅ want him → ❌ avoid him. It's only a note to yourself; it doesn't draft anyone.
★
On your team.   ✎ — a note you edited.
Orange rows
After "Who should I take?" on the Draft Board: players the simulation expects to be gone before your next turn.
Cost of waiting
On the Draft Board outlook: what passing on a position now costs you, in projected season points, if you wait until your following pick. Points rather than rank spots, because a 30-spot slide at tight end is worth far less than a 16-spot slide at running back.

Picked / +Team buttons are hidden by default — during a live draft the extension handles picks automatically. Turn on Manual mode in Settings (⚙) → Debug to draft by hand. You can also drag the divider between the board and this panel to resize them (double-click it to reset).

Settings
OpenAI

Stored only in this browser's localStorage. Requests go straight from your browser to api.openai.com — there is no middle server.

Local LLM (Ollama)

If this page is hosted (not opened from disk), start Ollama with OLLAMA_ORIGINS including this site's origin so the browser may call it.

Prompts
Draft

League size, rounds and your slot live on the Draft Board tab: the slot auto-detects from your first pick (or click your column header), and the Teams/Rounds controls appear there in Debug mode. Defaults: 12 teams, 15 rounds.

Rankings & your data

Import a spreadsheet (CSV) with Rank and Player columns — or just a ranked list of names — to re-order the board for the current scoring format. Tip: Download, reorder the rows in Excel/Sheets, re-import. The bundled data is never touched; you can always revert.

"My data" = target markers, picks, your team, rank overrides, edited notes, and prompts in one JSON file — move your prep to another browser or keep a backup. Your API key is never included.

Debug / manual mode

Off by default: in a live draft the extension marks picks and your roster automatically. Turning this on shows the Picked / +Team buttons on every row, the × remove buttons in Your Team, a Reset draft button in the header, and the Teams / Slot / Rounds controls on the Draft Board. "Undo" stays available on picked rows either way, and the P / T keys always work.

Clears picked players, your team, target markers, and the extension's stored draft data — use it between drafts or while testing.
